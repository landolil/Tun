
The Tardigradia Board of Visioneers Statement 2019

Dedication to the Preservation & Documentation of All Wild Life.  Earth Rejuvenation through Scientific research, and Artistic Love of Creative Imagination thereof.

Beginning aim to create an animated tiny science-fictional world about Tardigrades, including animals and situations they could Interact with.

The first vision to Franchise TV Show titled "Tardigradia 'land 'o' lil' big adventures with Maya Tana & Buju."  A story about two girls adventures in babysitting a know-it-all little boy.  The show will contain hundreds of different tardigrade characters along with animals of all sizes.  Aimed for the enjoyment of the children visually while being interesting to listen to for the parents.  It's a singalong, dancalong, fun & games, culture meets culture, size doesn't matter kinda world.  Lovers of the theatre, studying science, music and art, contrasting the importance of family friends and strangers.  Emotions are natural, finding focus in a land of confusion, these kids take on brain teasers, overcome their fears.  These three cute moss piglets may be tiny though their imaginative dreams and adventures take them everywhere.

Maya Tana & Buju's first adventure attending "Tardigradia Playhouse presents Shakespeare's A Midsummer Night's Dream" a theatrical showcase.

The second vision creating Mini-Video Games with aim in the future to create "Tardigradia 'land 'o' lil' Multi Player Universe."

The third Vision, An Open Contribute web site will be available for Creatives such as Artists, Researchers, Writers, and more can all upload their Creative Product.  If Tardigradia Utilizes the Creative's contribution within 10 years of submittal, we reimburse them.

The Future holds no limits with visions for multiple alternate franchise that share characters, provide scientific educational scholarships, restaurants, science eduporiums, and theme parks.

The Limited Tardigrade Artwork 2019 is Now Available Printed on Merchandise.  We have a Clothing line, Art Posters, and the "The Search for Seed Money, Tardigradia The Making of."  Trademarks, Patents, Internet Servers, Computers, Graphics Cards, Microscopes, and Creatives all want Money Money Money to Help Create Tardigradia.  Really help us, Purchase a Special Gold Line of exclusive seed products.  These golden stripped obscenely over-priced items directly help seed the Tardigradia Wildlife Preservation Dream!

Remember, sometimes, what's most important we can't see, and just happens to live right under out toes.  Show Earth Love.
