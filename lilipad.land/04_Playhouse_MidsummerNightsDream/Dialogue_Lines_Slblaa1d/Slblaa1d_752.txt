O, wherefore, Nature, didst thou lions frame,  Since lion vile hath here deflowered my dear,  Which is-no, no-which was the fairest dame  That lived, that loved, that liked, that looked with  cheer?  Come, tears, confound!  Out, sword, and wound  The pap of Pyramus;  Ay, that left pap,  Where heart doth hop.
