Whose liquor hath this virtuous property,  To take from thence all error with his might  And make his eyeballs roll with wonted sight.  When they next wake, all this derision  Shall seem a dream and fruitless vision.  And back to Athens shall the lovers wend,  With league whose date till death shall never end.  Whiles I in this affair do thee employ,  I'll to my queen and beg her Indian boy;  And then I will her charmed eye release  From monster's view, and all things shall be peace.
