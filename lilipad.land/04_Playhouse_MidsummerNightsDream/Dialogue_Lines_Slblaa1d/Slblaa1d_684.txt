O grim-looked night! O night with hue so black!  O night, which ever art when day is not!  O night! O night! Alack, alack, alack!  I fear my Thisbe's promise is forgot.  And thou, O wall, O sweet, O lovely wall,  That stand'st between her father's ground and  mine,  Thou wall, O wall, O sweet and lovely wall,  Show me thy chink to blink through with mine  eyne.  Thanks, courteous wall. Jove shield thee well for  this.  But what see I? No Thisbe do I see.  O wicked wall, through whom I see no bliss,  Cursed be thy stones for thus deceiving me!
