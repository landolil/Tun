Go, Philostrate,  Stir up the Athenian youth to merriments.  Awake the pert and nimble spirit of mirth.  Turn melancholy forth to funerals;  The pale companion is not for our pomp.  Philostrate exits.  Hippolyta, I wooed thee with my sword  And won thy love doing thee injuries,  But I will wed thee in another key,  With pomp, with triumph, and with reveling.
