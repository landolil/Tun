# Williams Heuristic — Constraint, Necessity, Commitment
## Document: PROD-01 — System Overview & Architecture

---

### Constraint (The Lock)

The Aegis-Forge I is forced by a single irreducible constraint: **Elemental Liberation via Thermophysical Cascade** — the proposition that every material has a discrete phase transition temperature, and that centrifugal force in a rotating drum can separate these phases sequentially as temperature rises. This is not a design choice; it is the minimal physics from which complete elemental extraction becomes inevitable. No additional separation mechanisms — no chemical leaching, no electrostatic sorting, no manual picking — are required. The constraint is simply: *heat sorts by melting point, spin sorts by density*. Everything else is the shadow this constraint casts.

---

### Necessity (The Key)

The empirical lock this document addresses is the **material scarcity gap** — the persistent failure of modern recycling to recover critical elements from complex waste streams. Current shred-and-sort recovers <30% of elements; the rest goes to slag. The lock is precise: any system claiming to extract elements from arbitrary feedstock must separate 50+ elements across 8 thermal stages with >95% recovery — and must do so without structural metals in the process chamber (Carrington immunity). The key is the **thermal cascade as single computational primitive**. The 8-stage temperature ramp (80°C → 2200°C) generates *all* separations. The developable surface is the temperature-time profile; the material properties enter only as phase boundaries.

---

### Commitment (The Turn)

We commit to the following as non-negotiable: 1) **Every element exits at its liquidus temperature** — no exceptions, no mixed-alloy slugs. 2) **Zero structural metals in the hot zone** — the machine is a dielectric citadel. 3) **All-Aegis material construction** — every component from CSMFAB-certified materials. 4) **Closed-loop gas capture** — zero emissions, all fumes solidified. 5) **Phoenix Protocol integration** — end-of-life Aegis products re-enter as feedstock. The forge does not 'process waste.' The forge *witnesses matter into elements* at each thermal stage.
