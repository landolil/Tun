### 1.1 Ce/La/Pr Market Origin and Purpose — Volume REE Dominance

Cerium (Ce), Lanthanum (La), and Praseodymium (Pr) are the **light rare earth elements (LREE)** that constitute **~85% of global REE production by volume**. While lower unit value than HREE, their **massive scale applications** — **FCC catalysts ($4B), glass polishing ($1.5B), mischmetal, batteries, alloys** — make them the **economic backbone of the REE industry**. Chile's **monazite/bastnäsite dominance across all 22 districts** provides **unprecedented LREE volume potential**.

**Williams Lock**: **EPA Tier 4 / IMO Tier III / China VI** emissions standards mandate **FCC catalysts with Ce/La promoters** for fluid catalytic cracking — **no viable substitute**. **CeO₂ glass polishing** is **irreplaceable for precision optics/semiconductors**. **LaNi₅ MH batteries** enable **NiMH HEV batteries (Toyota Prius +15M units)**. **Pr-Nd metal** feeds **NdFeB magnet production** (DOC-21). Chile's **monazite/bastnäsite in all 22 districts + Aegis-Forge Stage 8 LREE SX** creates **qualifying-country LREE supply chain** for Western catalyst/polishing/alloy industries.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **high-volume, low-cost LREE separation** — China produces **>90% of global Ce/La/Pr** at **<50% of Western OPEX**. The key is **Aegis-Forge Stage 8 chloride route LREE SX** with **high-throughput Ce/La (β=1.8, 8 stages), La/Pr (β=1.6, 10 stages), Pr/Nd (β=1.9, 8 stages)** achieving **3N-4N purity at $5-8/kg Ce, $5-7/kg La** vs Chinese $4-6/kg.

### 1.3 Ce/La/Pr Application Physics & Chemistry

#### 1.3.1 FCC Catalysts — Ce/La Promoter Chemistry

```
Fluid Catalytic Cracking (FCC) Reaction:
Heavy Gas Oil (C₂₀+) → Zeolite (USY) → Gasoline (C₅-C₁₂) + LPG + LCO

Ce/La Promoter Roles:

**Cerium (CeO₂, 0.5-2.0 wt% in catalyst):**
- **Oxygen Storage Capacity (OSC)**: Ce⁴⁺ ↔ Ce³⁺ + ½O₂ (reversible)
- Buffers catalyst redox state during feed fluctuations
- **Reduces CO/NOx in regenerator** by 20-30%
- Stabilizes zeolite Y against steam dealumination
- **CeO₂-ZrO₂ solid solution** (CeZrO₄) → enhanced OSC, thermal stability

**Lanthanum (La₂O₃, 1-5 wt% in catalyst):**
- **Zeolite Stabilization**: La³⁺ exchanges into USY framework
- **Prevents dealumination** (steam hydrothermal stability)
- **Maintains acidity** → higher gasoline yield, lower coke
- **La/USY interaction**: La-O-Si bonds strengthen framework

Synergistic Ce+La:
- Optimal ratio: Ce:La ≈ 1:2 to 1:3 (wt%)
- **Combined OSC + stability** → 2-3% absolute gasoline yield increase
- **Catalyst life extension**: 30-50% longer cycle length
```

#### 1.3.2 CeO₂ Glass Polishing — Chemical-Mechanical Planarization

```
CeO₂ Polishing Mechanism (Chemical-Mechanical):

Surface Reaction:
CeO₂ + SiO₂ (glass) + H₂O → Ce-OH + Si-OH → **Ce-O-Si bond formation**
Mechanical shear breaks Ce-O-Si → **SiO₂ removal** + CeO₂ regeneration

Key Parameters:
- **Particle Size**: 1-3 μm (D50=1.5-2.5 μm) — controls removal rate/finish
- **Crystal Structure**: Fluorite (cubic) — hard (Mohs 6.5), stable
- **Purity**: >99.9% CeO₂ (3N) — Fe <50 ppm (prevents scratches)
- **Surface Area**: 5-15 m²/g (higher = faster removal)
- **pH**: 6-8 (slurry stability, prevents agglomeration)

Applications:
- **Display Glass** (LCD/OLED): 50% of CeO₂ demand
- **Semiconductor Wafers** (CMP): 20% — tighter specs (sub-nm roughness)
- **Precision Optics** (lenses, mirrors): 15%
- **Automotive Glass** (HUD, sensors): 10%
- **Other**: 5%

**Chilean CeO₂ Spec**: 99.9% CeO₂, D50=1.8μm, Fe<20ppm, surface area 10m²/g
```

#### 1.3.3 Pr-Nd Metal for NdFeB Magnets

```
Didymium Metal (Pr-Nd) Production:

Feed: Pr-Nd Oxide (75% Nd₂O₃, 25% Pr₆O₁₁) — from Stage 8 LREE SX
    │
    ▼
Reduction (Ca-reduction or Molten Salt Electrolysis):
    Pr₆O₁₁ + 11Ca → 6Pr + 11CaO
    Nd₂O₃ + 3Ca → 2Nd + 3CaO
    (Combined: ~30% Pr, 70% Nd metal)
    │
    ▼
Distillation (Vacuum, 10⁻² Pa):
    Pr bp: 3,127°C, Nd bp: 3,074°C — co-distill as alloy
    │
    ▼
**Didymium Metal**: Pr 25-30%, Nd 70-75%, Fe <500ppm, O <500ppm
    │
    ▼
NdFeB Alloy Production (Strip Casting):
    Didymium + Fe + B + Co/Dy/Tb/Al/Cu → Nd₂Fe₁₄B
    │
    ▼
Sintering → NdFeB Magnets (N35-N52, M/H/SH/UH/EH grades)

Chilean Pr Advantage: **Pr 450 t/yr = 1,800 t/yr didymium metal** → **~5,000 t/yr NdFeB** (at 30% Pr-Nd in magnet)
```

#### 1.3.4 LaNi₅ MH Battery Anode

```
Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH) Battery:

Anode Reaction (Discharge):
LaNi₅ + 3H₂O + 3e⁻ → LaNi₅H₆ + 3OH⁻
(LaNi₅ absorbs H → LaNi₅H₆, ~1.4 wt% H)

Cathode Reaction (Discharge):
NiOOH + H₂O + e⁻ → Ni(OH)₂ + OH⁻

Overall: 1.2V nominal, 80-100 Wh/kg, 2,000+ cycles

LaNi₅ Properties:
- AB₅ structure (CaCu₅ type)

- **Safety**: No thermal runaway, aqueous electrolyte
- **Cost**: $150-200/kWh (vs $100-150 Li-ion)

Market: **Toyota Prius (15M+ units), Honda, Ford hybrids**
- La per vehicle: 10-15 kg LaNi₅ ≈ 2-3 kg La
- Global hybrid fleet: ~25M vehicles → **50,000-75,000 t La in service**

Chilean La Role: **La 2,200 t/yr = 11,000 t/yr LaNi₅** → **~500,000 hybrid vehicles/yr** capacity
```
