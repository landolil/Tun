## Chapter 1: Nd/Dy Market Fundamentals — Price History, Demand Forecasts, DFARS 2027

### 1.1 Nd/Dy Market Origin and Purpose — Strategic Magnet Elements

Neodymium (Nd) and Dysprosium (Dy) are the **critical enablers of high-performance permanent magnets** — Nd provides the magnetic moment (2.2 μB) in Nd₂Fe₁₄B, while Dy (5.0 μB) substitutes at the 4f site to **increase coercivity (Hci)** for high-temperature operation. Without Dy, NdFeB magnets lose >50% coercivity at 150°C; with 2-5% Dy, they retain >80% to 200°C.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: The **DFARS 252.225-7018** (effective Jan 2027) mandates **US melting/production of specialty metals** including NdFeB magnets for defense. This creates a **legal boundary condition**: all Nd/Dy feeding US defense magnets must originate from qualifying sources (US, qualifying country, or compliant processing). Chile qualifies via CPTPP + proposed US-Chile Critical Minerals Agreement.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **Chinese monopoly breaking** — China controls **>85% of global Nd/Dy separation** and **>90% of NdFeB manufacturing**. The key is **Aegis-Forge Stage 8 separation** producing **Chilean-sourced Nd/Dy oxides** that enter US-allied supply chains at the **metal/alloy stage** (qualifying processing).

**SHIELD Horizon**: Nd/Dy is the **tactical deployment element** (0-5 years) — DFARS 2027 deadline forces immediate supply chain qualification. Decisions here cascade to 2032 SmCo/Tb and 2036 FeN autonomy.

## Chapter 3: Aegis-Forge Nd/Dy Separation — Stage 8 Chemistry

### 3.1 Separation Chemistry Overview

Aegis-Forge **Stage 8 (Mineral Residue, 1676°C)** processes the **post-Stage 7 residue** (zircon, monazite, xenotime, apatite, REE-phosphates) through **high-temperature chlorination** followed by **fractional condensation** and **solvent extraction** to produce **individual REE oxides ≥99.9%**.

#### 3.1.1 Stage 8 Process Flow

```
Stage 7 Residue (ZrO₂ + REE-phosphates + silicates)
    │
    ▼
Chlorination Reactor (1000°C, Cl₂ + C) → REECl₃ + ZrCl₄ + SiCl₄ + FeCl₃
    │
    ▼
Fractional Condensation (Temperature-Zoned Condensers)
    ├── Zone 1 (650°C): ZrCl₄, SiCl₄, FeCl₃ (waste/byproduct)
    ├── Zone 2 (450°C): LaCl₃, CeCl₃, PrCl₃, NdCl₃ (LREE fraction)
    ├── Zone 3 (300°C): SmCl₃, EuCl₃, GdCl₃ (MREE fraction)
    ├── Zone 4 (200°C): TbCl₃, DyCl₃, HoCl₃ (HREE fraction)
    └── Zone 5 (<100°C): YCl₃, LuCl₃ (Y+HREE fraction)
    │
    ▼
Aqueous Dissolution → Chloride Solution Feed to SX
    │
    ▼
Solvent Extraction (SX) Circuit — 120 stages
    ├── SX-1: LREE Group Separation (La-Ce-Pr-Nd)
    ├── SX-2: Nd/Pr Separation (β = 1.8-2.2)
    ├── SX-3: MREE Group Separation (Sm-Eu-Gd)
    ├── SX-4: HREE Group Separation (Tb-Dy-Ho)
    ├── SX-5: Dy/Ho Separation (β = 1.3-1.5)
    └── SX-6: Y/Lu Separation
    │
    ▼
Oxalate Precipitation → REE₂(C₂O₄)₃
    │
    ▼
Calcination (900°C) → REE₂O₃ ≥99.9%
```
