## Brokerage Mandate & Legal Basis
## 1. Brokerage Mandate & Legal Basis

This chapter establishes the legal foundation for CSM's facilitator mandate over Chilean magnetic mineral assets and defines the scope, authority, and termination conditions of the brokerage relationship.

### 1.1 CSM Facilitator Authority — Origin and Scope

Carrington Storm Motors (CSM), through its Materials Science Division and ACS Materials Processing Division, operates as a **facilitator** — not a principal — in the acquisition, development, and transition of Chilean magnetic mineral assets to Tardigradia Holding Company. This mandate derives from:

| Authority Source | Legal Basis | Scope |
|------------------|-------------|-------|
| CSM Board Resolution 2024-03 | Corporate governance | Authorize facilitator role for Chilean magnet strategy |
| ACS-Aegis Technology License | License Agreement 2024-AF-001 | Grant Aegis-Forge processing rights to Tardigradia |
| CSM-DOE CMI Partnership | Cooperative Agreement DE-AC02-05CH11231 | Critical minerals research collaboration |
| Chilean Mining Code Concessions | Law 18,248 (Código de Minería) | Concession staking, exploration, exploitation rights |

**Mandate Scope:**
- **Asset Identification**: Staking and securing mining concessions for Fe, REE, and magnetic mineral occurrences identified in DOC-01 (IOCG belt, IOA/Kiruna-type, porphyry byproducts, Atacama REE)
- **Community Engagement**: Executing community benefit agreements per DOC-03 frameworks (Royalty Minera Law 21,210 compliance, FBC trusts, CDC development corporations)
- **Government Liaison**: Securing RCA environmental permits, water rights (DGA), indigenous consulta (Law 19,253), municipal permits
- **Technology Deployment**: Licensing Aegis-Forge thermal cascade (E172 specs: 17-stage, 500 kg/cycle, $4.32/kg OPEX) to Tardigradia Chile SpA
- **Transition Execution**: Managing facilitator-to-owner transition per this protocol (DOC-04)

**Explicit Exclusions from Mandate:**
- Capital deployment for mine development (Tardigradia responsibility)
- Operational management of Aegis-Forge clusters (Tardigradia opcos)
- Magnet manufacturing operations (Magnet Chile SpA)
- Motor integration (Motor Integration Chile SpA)
- Long-term community trust administration (FBC trustees)

### 1.2 Chilean Legal Framework — Facilitator Status

Under Chilean law, CSM's facilitator role is structured through three complementary legal instruments:

#### 1.2.1 Mandato (Agency Mandate) — Civil Code (Código Civil) Articles 2126-2185

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| Mandatario (Agent) | CSM Chile SpA (to be formed) |
| Mandante (Principal) | Tardigradia Holding Company (Delaware LP) |
| Object | Staking concessions, community engagement, permit acquisition, Aegis-Forge license negotiation |
| Remuneration | Facilitator fee: 2.5% of asset value at transition + 1% gross revenue royalty (Years 1-10) |
| Duration | Until transition triggers met (Chapter 3) or 5 years, whichever earlier |
| Termination | By mandante with 90 days notice; by mandatario with 180 days notice |

#### 1.2.2 Corredor de Propiedades Mineras (Mining Property Broker) — Law 18,045 & 20,190

Chilean securities law (Law 18,045) and SpA law (Law 20,190) do not explicitly regulate mining property brokerage. However, **Corredores de Bienes Raíces** regulations (Law 19,799) apply by analogy to mining concessions as real property interests. CSM registers as:

- **Corredor de Bienes Raíces** (Real Estate Broker) — Registro de Corredores, Ministerio de Vivienda
- **Specialization annotation**: "Concesiones Mineras y Derechos Mineros"
- **Bond requirement**: UF 500 (~$19,000) fidelity bond

#### 1.2.3 Foreign Investment Facilitator — DL 600 & Chapter XIV Compendium

As facilitator for foreign capital (Tardigradia), CSM operates under:

| Provision | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| DL 600 Art 3 | Foreign investor (Tardigradia) receives non-discriminatory treatment |
| DL 600 Art 10 | Remittance freedom for capital, profits, royalties |
| Chapter XIV Central Bank | Foreign exchange formalities for capital inflows |
| Law 20,848 (2015) | Simplified foreign investment registration (replaces DL 600 for new investments) |

CSM's role: Prepare and submit foreign investment registration for Tardigradia Chile SpA capital contributions; coordinate with Central Bank and Ministerio de Hacienda.

### 1.3 Mandate Termination Conditions

The facilitator mandate terminates automatically upon **Transition Completion** (defined in Chapter 5) or upon occurrence of any **Termination Event**:

| Termination Event | Notice Period | Consequence |
|-------------------|---------------|-------------|
| Transition Completion (all Chapter 3 triggers met) | Automatic | Facilitator fee earned; transition to owner governance |
| Mandante (Tardigradia) voluntary termination | 90 days written | Facilitator fee prorated; assets transferred to Tardigradia designee |
| Mandatario (CSM) voluntary termination | 180 days written | CSM forfeits unearned facilitator fee; mandate transfers to Tardigradia designee |
| Force Majeure > 180 days | Automatic | Mutual release; facilitator fee for work completed |
| Material Breach (uncured 60 days) | 60 days cure period | Non-breaching party may terminate; damages per SHA |
| Chilean Regulatory Prohibition | Immediate | Mandate void ab initio; facilitator fee for work completed |

**Post-Termination Survival:**
- Confidentiality: 5 years post-termination
- Community agreements (DOC-03): Survive mandate; assigned to Tardigradia
- Aegis-Forge license: Survives; assigned to Tardigradia Chile SpA
- Facilitator fee royalty (1% gross revenue): Survives 10 years regardless of termination

### 1.4 Facilitator Fee Structure — Mathematical Formulation

The facilitator fee compensates CSM for origination, due diligence, community engagement, government liaison, and transition execution.

#### 1.4.1 Upfront Fee — Asset Value Percentage

$$F_{upfront} = 0.025 \times V_{asset}$$

Where:
- $F_{upfront}$ = Upfront facilitator fee (USD)
- $V_{asset}$ = Independent valuation of magnetic mineral concessions at transition (USD)
- 2.5% = Market rate for mineral property origination (benchmark: S&P Global Mining M&A fee schedule 2023)

#### 1.4.2 Ongoing Royalty — Gross Revenue Percentage

$$F_{royalty}(t) = 0.01 \times R(t) \quad \text{for } t \in [1, 10]$$

Where:
- $F_{royalty}(t)$ = Annual royalty in Year $t$ (USD)
- $R(t)$ = Consolidated gross revenue of Tardigradia Chile SpA opcos in Year $t$ (USD)
- 1% = Gross revenue royalty (Years 1-10 only)
- Cap: $F_{royalty}(t) \leq 0.5 \times F_{upfront}$ per year

#### 1.4.3 Total Facilitator Compensation

$$F_{total} = F_{upfront} + \sum_{t=1}^{10} \min(F_{royalty}(t), 0.5 \times F_{upfront})$$

## Owner Transition Trigger Conditions
## 3. Owner Transition Trigger Conditions

This chapter defines the precise, binary milestones that must be satisfied before the facilitator-to-owner transition executes. Each trigger is objectively verifiable with documented evidence.

### 3.1 Transition Philosophy — Milestone-Based, Not Time-Based

The transition from CSM (facilitator) to Tardigradia (owner) is **not** triggered by a calendar date. It is triggered by the satisfaction of **all** conditions in Section 3.2. This ensures Tardigradia assumes ownership only when assets are de-risked, permitted, and community-aligned.

$$T_{transition} = \min\{t : \bigwedge_{i=1}^{n} C_i(t) = \text{TRUE}\}$$

Where $C_i(t)$ are the binary trigger conditions defined below.

## Valuation Methodology for Transition
## 4. Valuation Methodology for Transition

This chapter establishes the independent valuation framework for determining the asset value ($V_{asset}$) that drives the facilitator upfront fee (2.5% × $V_{asset}$) and informs Tardigradia's capital deployment decisions.

### 4.1 Valuation Standards & Principles

| Standard | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| **NI 43-101** (Canada) | Mineral resource/reserve reporting for concessions |
| **JORC Code 2012** (Australia) | Alternative reporting standard (Chile recognizes both) |
| **IVS 2022** (International Valuation Standards) | Business/asset valuation methodology |
| **CIM Definition Standards** | Mineral resource classification |
| **Chilean Mining Code Law 18,248** | Legal framework for concession valuation |

**Core Principles:**
1. **Independent Valuer**: Big 4 mining valuation practice or specialist (SRK, Golder, Wood Mackenzie) — not CSM or Tardigradia affiliated
2. **Multiple Methods**: DCF primary, comparable transactions secondary, option value tertiary
3. **Conservative Bias**: P50 reserves only; 10% WACC; no exploration upside in base case
4. **Chilean Context**: Royalty Minera Law 21,210, 27% corporate tax, 1.5-2% municipal royalty

### 4.2 Valuation Scope — What Is Being Valued

| Asset Component | Included? | Valuation Basis |
|-----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Constituted mining concessions (Fe, REE) | YES | DCF of mineable reserves |
| Exploration concessions (unconstituted) | YES | Option value (Black-Scholes) |
| Community agreements (FBC/CDC) | YES | Intangible asset — reduces permitting risk |
| Aegis-Forge Chile license | YES | Royalty savings vs. acid processing |

| Tardigradia equity / brand | NO | Separate valuation |
| Magnet manufacturing facilities | NO | Post-transition CAPEX |

### 4.3 Primary Method: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

#### 4.3.1 DCF Structure — Concession Portfolio Level

$$V_{DCF} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \frac{FCF_t}{(1 + WACC)^t} + \frac{TV}{(1 + WACC)^T}$$

Where:
- $FCF_t$ = Free Cash Flow in Year $t$ (after tax, after Royalty Minera, after community royalties)
- $WACC$ = Weighted Average Cost of Capital (10% base case)
- $TV$ = Terminal Value = $\frac{FCF_{T+1}}{WACC - g}$ (perpetuity growth $g = 1.5\%$)
- $T$ = Explicit forecast period (20 years = concession life)

## Dispute Resolution & Escrow
## 7. Dispute Resolution & Escrow

This chapter establishes the dispute resolution framework and escrow mechanisms protecting all parties — especially community trusts — during and after transition.

### 7.1 Dispute Resolution Philosophy

**Principles:**
1. **Speed over perfection** — Mining operations cannot afford years of litigation
2. **Community parity** — Communities have equal access to justice as corporate parties
3. **Technical expertise** — Mining/environmental disputes decided by experts, not generalist judges
4. **Preserve operations** — Interim measures maintain production during disputes
5. **Chilean jurisdiction** — Asset disputes in Chile; holdco disputes in Delaware/NY

### 7.2 Escalation Ladder — Binding & Enforceable

| Level | Mechanism | Timeline | Binding | Cost Allocation |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **1. Direct Negotiation** | Party representatives (CEO/Community Dir) | 15 business days | No | Each bears own |
| **2. CVO Mediation** | CVO Chair facilitates (community disputes) | 20 business days | If settlement signed | Tardigradia 70% / Community 30% |
| **3. Expert Determination** | 3 experts (1 each party, 1 joint) | 45 business days | **Yes (technical)** | Tardigradia 60% / Other 40% |
| **4. ICC Arbitration** | 3 arbitrators; Spanish; Chile law (assets) / NY law (holdco) | 120 business days | **Yes (all)** | Tardigradia 65% / Other 35% |
| **5. Courts** | Chilean courts (asset disputes); Delaware/ NY (holdco) | Per court calendar | **Yes** | Loser pays (Chile); each bears own (US) |

**Community Legal Aid Fund:** $200k/year from FBC Holding Trust (DOC-03 Ch 7) covers community costs at Levels 2-5.

### 7.4 Expert Determination — Detailed Procedure

**Applicable to:** Technical disputes (production, environmental metrics, valuation, earnout milestones)

**Panel Composition:**
- Expert 1: Appointed by Claimant (mining engineer / geochemist / accountant)
- Expert 2: Appointed by Respondent (same discipline)
- Expert 3 (Chair): Jointly appointed; if no agreement in 10 days → ICC Court appoints

**Qualifications:**
- 15+ years relevant experience
- No conflicts (independence declaration)
- Spanish fluency (Chile-seated) / English (NY-seated)
- Recognized by: ICMM, SME, AusIMM, or Chilean Colegio de Ingenieros

**Procedure:**
1. Written submissions (30 pages max each) — 15 days
2. Site visit / data room access — 10 days
3. Hearing (virtual or Santiago/NY) — 5 days max
4. Written determination (reasoned) — 20 days
5. **Determination is final and binding** — no appeal on merits

**Cost Cap:** Total expert fees capped at $500k per determination (shared per cost allocation table)

**Rules:** ICC Arbitration Rules 2021
**Language:** Spanish (Chile-seated) / English (NY-seated)
**Seat:** Santiago (asset disputes) / New York (holdco disputes)
**Law:** Chilean law (assets) / New York law (holdco)
**Arbitrators:** 3 (1 each party, 1 Chair jointly/ICC)
**Confidentiality:** ICC Rules Art 22(3) + explicit agreement
**Interim Measures:** Available from arbitral tribunal + Chilean courts (Art 35)
