### 1.3 Lu/Y/Sc Application Physics

#### 1.3.1 Sc in Al Alloys (Grain Refinement & Strengthening)

```
Sc-Al Phase Diagram:
- Al₃Sc (L1₂, cubic) — coherent precipitate
- Solubility: 0.31% at 660°C, 0.03% at 300°C
- Precipitation: 250-350°C → Al₃Sc (5-20 nm, coherent)

Mechanisms:
1. **Grain Refinement**: Al₃Sc nucleates α-Al grains → **grain size 10-50 μm** (vs 1-5 mm w/o Sc)
   - Eliminates hot cracking in welding
   - Enables **weldable high-strength alloys** (no post-weld heat treat)

2. **Precipitation Strengthening**: Al₃Sc coherent precipitates
   - Δσ = 0.81 × G × b × √(f/r)  (Orowan bypassing)
   - f = volume fraction (~0.5% at 0.2% Sc), r = precipitate radius
   - **Yield strength +50-100 MPa per 0.1% Sc**

3. **Recrystallization Resistance**: Al₃Sc pins subgrain boundaries
   - **Thermal stability to 400°C** (vs 250°C for Al-Zn-Mg-Cu)

Key Alloys:
- **Al-Sc-Zr (Scalmalloy®)**: 0.12% Sc, 0.12% Zr → 520 MPa UTS, weldable
- **Al-Mg-Sc (AA5024 mod)**: 0.3% Sc → marine/shipbuilding
- **Al-Li-Sc**: 0.1% Sc + 1.5% Li → aerospace (density 2.55 g/cm³)
```

#### 1.3.2 Y₂O₃ in SCR Catalysts (NOx Reduction)

```
Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) Reaction:
4NO + 4NH₃ + O₂ → 4N₂ + 6H₂O  (V₂O₅/WO₃-TiO₂ catalyst)

Y₂O₃ Role (5-10 wt% in catalyst):
1. **TiO₂ Stabilization**: Prevents anatase→rutile transition (>600°C)
   - Y³⁺ substitutes Ti⁴⁺ → oxygen vacancies → stabilizes anatase
   - Surface area retention: **>80 m²/g after 800°C aging** (vs <20 w/o Y)

2. **SO₂/SO₃ Resistance**: Y₂O₃ captures sulfur as Y₂(SO₄)₃
   - Prevents V₂O₅ sulfation (activity loss)
   - **Regenerable** at 650°C (Y₂(SO₄)₃ → Y₂O₃ + 3SO₂ + 1.5O₂)

3. **V₂O₅ Dispersion**: Y-O-V bonds enhance V⁵⁺/V⁴⁺ redox
   - Low-temperature activity (200-250°C) improved 30%

EPA Tier 4 / Euro 6 / IMO Tier III Compliance:
- **Y₂O₃-doped SCR = only proven technology** for 90%+ NOx reduction
- Catalyst life: 24,000-40,000 hrs (vs 8,000 w/o Y)
- **Y demand per MW**: 50-100 kg Y₂O₃/MW (power plant SCR)
```

- Density: 7.4 g/cm³ → **high stopping power** (511 keV γ)

- Light yield: 32,000 ph/MeV
- **Preferred for premium TOF-PET** (Siemens Biograph Vision, GE Discovery MI)

LuAG:Ce (Lu₃Al₅O₁₂:Ce):
- Density: 6.7 g/cm³
- Decay: 50 ns, Light yield: 25,000 ph/MeV
- **No intrinsic radioactivity** (vs LSO/LYSO Lu-176)

Chilean Lu Advantage: **4N Lu₂O₃ from Alpha-17** → **lowest U/Th/K impurities** → **lowest background** → **premium scintillator grade**.
