## Chapter 1: Norte Chico — IOCG/REE Extension, Diaguita Territories, FCAB/Guayacán Logistics

### 1.1 Norte Chico Origin and Purpose — IOCG/REE Extension, Diaguita Territories

The Norte Chico region (Atacama Region southern communes + Coquimbo Region) is the **geological and cultural bridge** between the Norte Grande IOCG epicenter and the Zona Central Cu-Mo-REE belt. This region hosts the **southern IOCG extension** (Los Bronces/Andina, El Romeral, El Algarrobo, Dominga) and the **Diaguita Indigenous territories** — the most complex consent landscape in Chilean mining.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: The Chilean State owns all mineral resources (Constitution Art. 19§24). Norte Chico contains **~25% of Chile's IOCG resources** and **~30% of REE potential in IOCG systems** (DOC-01 refinement). The Diaguita people hold **constitutional territorial rights** (Law 19.253, CONADI recognition) across Elqui, Limarí, Choapa valleys — every district requires Consulta Indígena.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **district-level consent logistics** — DOC-03 provides national FBC/CDC framework; Phase 4 requires valley-specific consent pathways for 5 districts across 3 provinces.

**SHIELD Horizon**: Norte Chico is the **strategic deployment zone** (2-7 years) for Aegis-Forge Alpha/Bravo clusters. Decisions here cascade to 2032 Puerto Exterior operations and 2036 CRMA strategic project maturity.

#### 1.1.2 Document Purpose

This document translates national-scale intelligence (DOC-01..16) into **regional deployment intelligence** for Norte Chico:

- **District-level resource quantification** for Aegis-Forge sizing (refining DOC-01 national data)
