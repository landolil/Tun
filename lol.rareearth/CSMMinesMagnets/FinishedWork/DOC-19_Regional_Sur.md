## Chapter 1: Sur — Historical Iron, Biobío Corridor, Mapuche-Lafkenche, Ion-Adsorption REE

### 1.1 Sur Origin and Purpose — Historical Fe, Biobío Corridor, Mapuche-Lafkenche, Ion-Adsorption REE

The Sur region (Maule, Ñuble, Biobío, Araucanía) is Chile's **historical mining and industrial heartland** — hosting the **Maule Iron District** (magnetite skarns), the **Biobío industrial corridor** (coal, steel, port), and the **novel Coastal Cordillera ion-adsorption REE clays**. This region bridges the central porphyry belt and austral frontier, with established infrastructure and complex Mapuche territorial dynamics.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: The Chilean State owns all mineral resources (Constitution Art. 19§24). Sur contains **~15% of Chile's historical magnetite resources** and **~20% of novel ion-adsorption REE potential** (DOC-01 refinement). The Mapuche-Lafkenche/Pewenche/Wenteche hold **constitutional territorial rights** (Law 19.253) across 4 regions — every district requires Consulta Indígena with distinct cultural protocols.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **brownfield redeployment** — DOC-01/DOC-16/17/18 address greenfield/operating mines; Phase 4 requires historical district repurposing for Aegis-Forge (zero tailings, zero water, heritage compatibility).

**SHIELD Horizon**: Sur is the **legacy integration zone** (5-10 years) for Aegis-Forge Alpha clusters at historical sites + Bravo ion-adsorption clusters. Decisions here define 2032 circular economy closure and 2036 austral frontier opening.

#### 1.1.2 Document Purpose

This document translates national-scale intelligence (DOC-01..18) into **regional deployment intelligence** for Sur:

- **Historical district repurposing** for Aegis-Forge (Maule Fe, Biobío coal/metal)
- **Ion-adsorption REE deployment** — novel low-impact extraction for Aegis-Forge Stage 4

- **Urban mining integration** — Concepción/Talcahuano industrial recycling as Bravo cluster
