### 1.3.3 Detailed Community Water Profiles

#### Atacameño Territory (DOC-26) — The Water Sovereignty Benchmark

The Atacameño (Lickanantay) people hold **territorial sovereignty over the Salar de Atacama basin** — 11 ayllus, ~4,200 territorial population, 30,000 km². Their **water cosmology distinguishes puri (freshwater, sacred, vetoed) from llipi (brine, negotiable)**. 

**Hydrological Infrastructure**:
- **Community Hydrology Monitoring Network (RMHC-Atacama)**: 45 piezometers (freshwater), 12 brine monitoring wells, 8 lagoon level loggers (Chaxa, Puilar, Tebenquiche, Tuyajto, La Punta, Tuyajto Sur, Barros Negros, Burro Muerto), 3 flamingo colony cameras, 5 water quality sondes (pH, conductivity, As, B, Li, REE), 1 satellite telemetry hub
- **Alert Thresholds**: Puri level drop >0.3m/yr → Yellow alert; >0.5m/yr → Red alert (operation suspension); Brine level drop >1.0m/yr → CPA review; As >0.01 mg/L in puri → Immediate suspension; Flamingo breeding <70% baseline → Ecosystem emergency
- **Veto Power**: CPA Mining Committee → Unanimous vote = binding suspension (CPA Art. 12)

**Cumulative Impact**: SQM + Albemarle extract 220 Mm³/yr brine → modelled drawdown 0.8m/yr at lagoon margin, approaching red alert threshold. **Zero freshwater extraction** by mining (all Cu/REE operations use desalination) — Atacameño veto enforced.

#### Calama Mining Community (DOC-31) — The Loa Basin Crisis

**Loa Basin Hydrology**: Codelco holds senior rights (1,850 L/s allocation, 1,620 L/s actual use). Municipal supply (Aguas Antofagasta) has 650 L/s allocation but 720 L/s actual use — **deficit 70 L/s**. Agriculture holds 2,200 L/s allocation (1,800 L/s actual). Indigenous ancestral allocation 120 L/s (60 L/s actual). **Ecological flow mandate 350 L/s, actual <50 L/s dry season**. DGA scarcity declarations: 2015, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023 (5 of last 10 years).

**Desalination Solution**: Codelco Desal I (Mejillones→Calama, 1,200 L/s, operational 2022, US$1.1B) serves Chuqui UG (100%) and RT (50%). Codelco Desal II (800 L/s, construction 2025, US$750M) serves MH (100%), RT (50%), Municipal (200 L/s). **Total desal capacity 2,000 L/s by 2026**. Energy cost: desal + pumping (2,260m elevation) = 4.2 kWh/m³ — HIF H₂/solar PPA target US$0.80/m³.

**Environmental Liabilities**: Chuqui open pit lake forming (US$1.2B closure), RT heap leach pads 1,200 ha (US$450M), Loa River contamination 120 km affected (US$85M remediation), Loa aquifer depletion -1.2 m/yr drawdown (US$320M desal + MAR).

#### San Pedro de Atacama (DOC-34) — Tourism vs Fossil Aquifer

**Watershed**: Endorheic basin to Salar de Atacama. Río San Pedro (45 km, perennial, seasonal 2.5 m³/s summer peak, 0.3 m³/s winter). **Bo-fedales (High-Andean wetlands)**: ~1,200 ha, 40% of regional bofedales, carbon sink, biodiversity, water regulation. **Aquifer System Puripica-Chiu Chiu**: Confined volcanic sedimentary, Paleocene-Eocene, **recharge <0.5 mm/yr** (effectively non-renewable, 10,000+ yr residence). Extraction 120 L/s (tourism 45%, agriculture 35%, mining exploration 20%). Quality: As 0.015 mg/L (WHO limit 0.01), B 2.5 mg/L, F 1.8 mg/L.

**Water Stress**: Total supply 120 L/s vs peak demand 180 L/s (January) = **60 L/s peak deficit**. Grey water reuse 0 L/s (mandatory new builds per Ordenanza 2024). Fog capture pilot 1,000 m². Sewage treatment 50 L/s oxidation ponds (upgrade to MBR 2026, $8M).

**Governance Triad**: CPA Atacameño (Law 19.253, royalty minero 1.5%, cultural heritage co-management, CLP 12B budget), Municipalidad (Law 18.695, urban planning, patentes tourism 60%, water APR, CLP 8.5B), Cámara de Turismo (120 members, industry coordination, carrying capacity advocacy, CLP 400M). Mesa Tripartita monthly, Consulta Previa veto binding, CONAF-CPA Plan de Manejo annual, carrying capacity technical table quarterly.

#### María Elena Lithium Community (DOC-35) — The Company Town Extreme

**Zero Freshwater Access**: **No municipal water utility exists**. SQM provides all water services (potable, industrial, sewage) to 7,000 population. **SQM spends ~$15M/yr on town services** (0.4% of lithium revenue). **Eviction risk**: Automatic upon job loss (30-day notice, 90-day grace per 2023 CBA).

**Brine Operations**: SQM extracts 4,500 L/s (142 Mm³/yr) from 40 production wells (50-200m depth) — **world's largest single brine extraction**. 400 ha evaporation ponds, 18-month concentration cycle, 95% evaporation loss. Lithium carbonate plant 180k t/yr capacity, $3.8B revenue (2023), $4,500/t cash cost (lowest quartile globally).

**Price Sensitivity**: At $15k/t Li₂CO₃ (2024 current), SQM lithium revenue $2.7B, municipal patentes CLP 2.0B ($20M local capture) — **DEFICIT**. Critical threshold $20k/t — below this, SQM cuts capex, municipal budget collapses, automation accelerates.

**Fog Pilot**: 500 m² advanced mesh = 250 L/day. Potential scaling to 5,000 m² = 2,500 L/day (partial municipal supply).
