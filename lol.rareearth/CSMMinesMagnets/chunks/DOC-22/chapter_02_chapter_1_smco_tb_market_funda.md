## Chapter 1: SmCo/Tb Market Fundamentals — High-Temp Defense, Aerospace, Criticality

### 1.1 SmCo/Tb Market Origin and Purpose — High-Temperature Magnet Dominance

Samarium-Cobalt (SmCo) magnets are the **only commercial permanent magnets** retaining >80% coercivity at **350°C** — essential for **aerospace, downhole drilling, missile guidance, and nuclear-hardened systems**. Terbium (Tb) doping (0.5-1.0 at%) in Sm₂Co₁₇ **enhances Hci by 30-50%** via grain boundary diffusion, making **Tb the critical enabler** for next-gen SmCo 2:17 32+ MGOe grades.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: **DFARS 252.225-7018** and **ITAR** require **US/qualifying-country production** of SmCo for defense. **Co is listed as critical mineral** (USGS 2024, EU CRMA 2024). **Tb is the rarest commercial REE** (global production ~10 t/yr). Chile's **IOCG Co byproduct + lateritic Co + Aegis-Forge Sm/Tb separation** creates a **qualifying-country SmCo supply chain**.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **Tb supply security** — China produces **>95% of global Tb** (ion-adsorption clays). The key is **Aegis-Forge Stage 8 Tb separation** from Chilean epithermal/ion-adsorption sources + **Tb-doping technology transfer** to US/Japan SmCo sintering.

**SHIELD Horizon**: SmCo/Tb is the **strategic deployment element** (3-8 years) — DFARS 2027 + aerospace qualification cycles. Decisions here define 2032 high-temp motor autonomy and 2036 nuclear-hardened systems.

#### 1.2.2 Tb Doping Mechanism (Grain Boundary Diffusion)

```
Tb-Doping Process (Sm₂Co₁₇):
1. Tb metal/oxide coating on Sm₂Co₁₇ powder (0.5-1.0 wt%)
2. Vacuum diffusion annealing (900-1000°C, 2-4 hrs)
3. Tb diffuses along grain boundaries (not into 2:17 cell)
4. Forms Tb-rich shell (Tb₂Co₁₇ / TbCo₅) around 2:17 grains
5. Increases nucleation field for reverse domain formation
6. Hci enhancement: ΔHci/Hci₀ = 30-50% at 0.5-1.0% Tb

Thermodynamic Driver:
Tb-Co bond energy > Sm-Co bond energy at grain boundary
→ Tb preferentially segregates to boundary
→ Pins domain walls → raises Hci
```

**Critical Parameter**: **Tb diffusion depth** must be **<50 nm** to avoid degrading Br. Excess Tb forms non-magnetic Tb₂O₃.
