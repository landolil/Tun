## Chapter 1: Zona Central — Cu-Mo-REE Belt, Mapuche-Pehuenche, Urban Mining Hub

### 1.1 Zona Central Origin and Purpose — Cu-Mo-REE Belt, Mapuche-Pehuenche, Urban Mining

The Zona Central region (Valparaíso, Metropolitana, O'Higgins) is Chile's **industrial and demographic core** — hosting the **world-class Cu-Mo porphyry belt** (Los Bronces, Andina, El Teniente, Los Pelambres) and the **largest urban mining opportunity** in Santiago/Metropolitana. This region bridges the northern IOCG systems and southern volcanic belts, with infrastructure density unmatched nationally.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: The Chilean State owns all mineral resources (Constitution Art. 19§24). Zona Central contains **~40% of Chile's Cu-Mo resources** and **~35% of REE potential in porphyry systems** (DOC-01 refinement). The Mapuche-Pehuenche hold **constitutional territorial rights** (Law 19.253) in high Andean valleys — Los Bronces, Andina, El Teniente, Los Pelambres all require Consulta Indígena.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **urban mining integration** — DOC-25 addresses recycling nationally; Phase 4 requires Santiago-specific e-waste/industrial scrap REE recovery integrated with primary mining flow.

**SHIELD Horizon**: Zona Central is the **operational deployment zone** (3-8 years) for Aegis-Forge Alpha clusters at major mines + Bravo urban recycling hub. Decisions here define 2032 Puerto Exterior magnet throughput and 2036 CRMA strategic autonomy.

#### 1.1.2 Document Purpose

This document translates national-scale intelligence (DOC-01..17) into **regional deployment intelligence** for Zona Central:

- **District-level resource quantification** for Aegis-Forge sizing (refining DOC-01 national porphyry data)
- **Mapuche-Pehuenche consent pathways** per DOC-03 FBC/CDC + DOC-04 CVO veto (high Andes model)
- **Logistics corridor optimization** per DOC-08 central rail (FCAB/EFE) + ports (Valparaíso/San Antonio)
- **Regulatory navigation** per DOC-07 SEREMI Valparaíso/Metropolitana/O'Higgins, GORE interfaces
- **GIC hardening** per DOC-11 for SEN Central backbone (Polpaico 500 kV, Alto Jahuel 220 kV)
- **Urban mining integration** — Santiago e-waste REE recovery as Bravo cluster feedstock

### 1.3 Geological Overview — Porphyry Cu-Mo Belt and REE Systems

#### 1.3.1 Porphyry Belt Architecture

The **Andean Porphyry Cu-Mo Belt** in Zona Central formed by **Miocene-Pliocene magmatism** associated with flat-slab subduction:

```
Geological Cross-Section Zona Central (Simplified):
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  COASTAL CORDILLERA (Cretaceous volcanics)                 │
│  └─ El Soldado, Chagres (Cu manto-type)                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  PRECORDILLERA (Miocene porphyry centers)                  │
│  └─ Los Pelambres, Los Bronces, Andina, El Teniente        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  HIGH ANDES (Pliocene volcanic, glacial)                   │
│  └─ Mapuche-Pehuenche territories, glacial water sources   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  CENTRAL VALLEY (Cenozoic basin fill)                      │
│  └─ Santiago Metropolitan — Urban mining, industrial zone  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
