#### 6.2.2 Urban Mining Process Flow

```
Urban Feedstock (226 L/batch, mixed)
    │
    ▼

    │
    ▼
Stage 1: IR Drying (350°C, 15 min) — 0 water
    │
    ▼
Stage 2: Polymer/Organic Separation — PCB plastics, catalyst supports
    │
    ▼
Stage 3: Pyrolysis/Char — Organic destruction, carbon recovery
    │
    ▼
Stage 4: Low-Temp Metals — Sn, Pb, Zn, In, Sb, Bi, REE from catalysts
    │
    ▼
Stage 5: Aluminum Window — Al from frames, heat sinks
    │
    ▼
Stage 6: Copper Window (1085°C) — Cu from PCBs, wires, motors
    │
    ▼
Stage 7: Ferrous Cascade — Fe/Steel from casings, motors
    │
    ▼
Stage 8: Mineral Residue (1676°C) — REE oxide concentration from catalysts/magnets
```
