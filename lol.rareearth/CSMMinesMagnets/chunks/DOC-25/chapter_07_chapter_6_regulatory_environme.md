## Chapter 6: Regulatory/Environmental — CeO₂ Diesel Phaseout, ESG, Circular Economy

### 6.1 CeO₂ Diesel Additive Phaseout

#### 6.1.1 Regulatory Timeline

| Region | Regulation | Status | CeO₂ Impact |
|--------|------------|--------|-------------|
| **EU** | Euro 5/6, REACH | **Banned 2011** (ECHA SVHC candidate) | Zero EU market |
| **US** | EPA 40 CFR 79 | **Phaseout 2010** (voluntary), **Ban 2024** (final rule) | Zero US market |
| **Japan** | JIS K 2240 | **Voluntary phaseout 2008** | Zero JP market |
| **China** | GB 19592-2021 | **Restricted** (urban only, 50 ppm max) | Declining market |
| **India** | BS VI | **Under review** | Potential phaseout 2026 |
| **Latin America** | Various | **No ban yet** | **Chile: export-only market** |

**Chilean Position**: **No domestic CeO₂ diesel additive use** — **Aegis-Forge CeO₂ targeted at polishing, catalysts, alloys only**. **Export to non-ban markets** (limited, declining) with **full disclosure**.

### 6.3 Circular Economy — Catalyst & Polishing Recycling

#### 6.3.1 EU CRMA 30% Recycling Target

```
EU Critical Raw Materials Act (2024):
- **30% of annual REE consumption from recycling by 2030**
- **FCC catalysts**: 30% of Ce/La from spent E-Cat
- **Permanent Magnets**: 30% of Nd/Pr/Dy/Tb from EOL magnets
- **Batteries**: 30% of La/Co/Ni from EOL NiMH/Li-ion
- **Polishing Waste**: 20% of Ce from CMP sludge

Chilean Aegis-Forge Recycling Loops (Integrated):
1. **Spent FCC Catalyst** → Stage 4/8 SX → Ce/La/Pr/Nd oxides (DOC-21 Ch. 5, Ch. 4.4)
2. **EOL NdFeB Magnets** → Stage 4/8 SX → Nd/Dy/Pr/Tb oxides (DOC-21 Ch. 5)
3. **Polishing Sludge (CeO₂)** → Acid dissolution → CeO₂ reprecipitation (Ch. 5.1)
4. **NiMH Battery Scrap** → Hydromet → La/Ni/Co recovery (Ch. 5.3)
5. **Urban Mining (Bravo-5)** → Consolidated feed → Stage 8 SX (Ch. 2.6)
```
