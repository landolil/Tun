### 2.9 Chapter 2 Summary

Chilean Ce/La/Pr occurrences are **ubiquitous across all 22 districts** — **monazite/bastnäsite in IOCG (Norte Grande/Chico)**, **apatite/titanite in porphyry (Zona Central)**, **clay-bound REE in ion-adsorption (Sur/Austral)**, **xenotime/monazite in epithermal (Alpha-14, Alpha-17)**, and **urban mining (Santiago)**. **Annual potential: 2,500 t Ce, 2,200 t La, 450 t Pr**. **Urban mining contributes 24% Ce, 27% La, 24% Pr** — **growing with recycling mandates**. Chile delivers **qualifying-country, ESG-premium LREE** for Western catalyst, polishing, battery, and magnet supply chains.
