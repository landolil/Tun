### 4.3 FCC Unit Integration & Catalyst Life Cycle

```
FCC Unit Operation (Typical 100,000 bpd refinery):

Fresh Catalyst Addition:
- Rate: 0.5-1.0 kg/ton feed
- Composition: USY zeolite (20-25%), Matrix (70-75%), **Ce/La promoters (3-7%)**
- Ce:La ratio: 1:2 to 1:3

Catalyst Circulation:
- Riser: 500-540°C, 2-5 sec contact
- Regenerator: 680-750°C, air combustion (coke burn)
- **CeO₂ OSC**: Buffers regenerator O₂/CO/CO₂
- **La₂O₃**: Stabilizes USY vs steam (H₂O 15-25% in flue gas)

Equilibrium Catalyst (E-Cat) Properties:
- Activity: MAT (Micro Activity Test) 60-75 wt% conversion
- Metals: Ni/V/Na (poisons) <2,000 ppm combined
- **Ce/La Retention**: >80% of fresh charge after 5-10 cycles

Spent Catalyst Disposition:
- **Recycling (30% target, EU CRMA)**: Spent E-Cat → REE recovery
- **Landfill**: Non-hazardous (encapsulated metals)
- **Cement Kiln**: Alternative fuel/raw material

Chilean Advantage: **Aegis-Forge recycling loop (DOC-21 Ch. 5)** recovers **Ce/La/Pr/Nd from spent catalyst** → closes loop.
```

### 4.4 Catalyst Recycling Loop (Circular Economy)

#### 4.4.1 Spent FCC Catalyst REE Recovery

```
Spent E-Cat Feed (30% of 50,000 t/yr = 15,000 t/yr per major refinery):
    │
    ▼
Pre-treatment:
    ├── Crushing (jaw crusher, <10mm)
    ├── Magnetic separation (remove Fe/steel)
    └── Classification (cyclone, <75μm for leach)
    │
    ▼
Acid Leaching (H₂SO₄, 80°C, 2h, 20% solids):
    │
    ├── REE dissolution: >95% (Ce, La, Pr, Nd, Y)
    ├── Al/Si dissolution: <5% (zeolite framework resistant)
    └── V/Ni/Fe dissolution: 80-90% (impurity management)
    │
    ▼
Solid-Liquid Separation (CCD thickener + filter press)
    │
    ▼
Impurity Removal:
    ├── Fe/Al: pH 3.5 precipitation (Fe(OH)₃, Al(OH)₃)
    ├── V/Ni: Solvent extraction (D2EHPA) or ion exchange
    └── Si: Membrane filtration (optional)
    │
    ▼
REE Concentration (SX or IX):
    ├── SX-LREE: Ce/La/Pr/Nd separation (same circuit as primary)
    ├── Recovery: 90% Ce, 85% La, 80% Pr, 75% Nd
    │
    ▼
Oxalate Precipitation + Calcination → **Recycled CeO₂/La₂O₃/Pr₆O₁₁/Nd₂O₃**
    │
    ▼
Blend with Primary (30% recycled / 70% primary) → FCC Catalyst Mfr
```

**Economics**: Recycled REE OPEX ~$3-5/kg vs $5-8/kg primary → **$2-3/kg margin**. **EU CRMA 30% mandate** makes recycling **regulatory requirement by 2030**.
