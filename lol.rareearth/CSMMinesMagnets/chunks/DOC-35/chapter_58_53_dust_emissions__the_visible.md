### 5.3.3 Mitigation — The Dust Control Program

| Measure | Implementation | Cost | Effectiveness | Status |
|---------|----------------|------|---------------|--------|
| **Pond Crust Stabilization (Polymer)** | 50 ha/yr sprayed | $2M/yr | **70% PM10 reduction** | **Operational 2023** |
| **Haul Road Watering** | 50 trucks, 24/7 shifts | $3M/yr | 50% reduction | Operational |
| **Road Enclosure (Wind Barriers)** | 50 km, 3m high | $8M capex | 40% reduction | 2025 completion |
| **Conveyor Enclosure (Plant)** | 5 km covered conveyors | $15M | 90% point source | **2024 completion** |
| **Vegetative Buffer (Atriplex, Tamarugo)** | 100 ha perimeter | $1M + $0.2M/yr | 30% (long-term) | **2026 target** |
| **Real-time Monitoring (Beta-attenuation)** | 8 stations (PM10, PM2.5, As) | $500k capex | Data for alerts | **Operational 2024** |

**Residual Risk**: Even with full mitigation, **town PM10 ~25 μg/m³** — still >WHO guideline.
