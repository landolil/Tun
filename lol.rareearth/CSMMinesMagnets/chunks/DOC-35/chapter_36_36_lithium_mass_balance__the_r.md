## 3.6 Lithium Mass Balance — The Recovery Efficiency

### 3.6.1 Process Flow — Brine to Battery Grade

```
SQM LITHIUM MASS BALANCE (Annual, 180k t/yr Li₂CO₃)

BRINE INPUT:
├── Volume: 142 Mm³/yr (4,500 L/s)
├── Li Concentration: 1,500 mg/L (avg)
├── **Li Mass Input: 213,000 t/yr Li**
└── K Mass Input: 1,704,000 t/yr K

EVAPORATION PONDS (18 months):
├── Water Evaporated: 135 Mm³/yr (95%)
├── Halite Precipitated: 1.2 Mt/yr NaCl (harvested)
├── **Li in Concentrated Brine (6%): 190,000 t/yr Li**
├── **Li Losses (entrainment, adsorption): 23,000 t/yr (11%)**
└── K in Concentrated Brine: 1,400,000 t/yr K

PLANT FEED (7,000 L/s, 6% Li):
├── **Li to Plant: 190,000 t/yr Li**
├── Mg Removal (Lime): 95% efficiency
├── Ca Removal (Soda Ash): 98% efficiency
├── **Li to Carbonation: 175,000 t/yr Li**

CARBONATION & CRYSTALLIZATION:
├── Conversion Efficiency: 92% (Li₂CO₃ from Li)
├── **Li₂CO₃ Produced: 180,000 t/yr (99.5% → 99.9%)**
├── **Overall Recovery: 53% (Li in brine → Li in product)**
└── **Li in Waste Streams: 98,000 t/yr (tailings, mother liquor)**

BYPRODUCT RECOVERY:
├── KCl (Potash): 300,000 t/yr (90% K recovery)
├── Mg(OH)₂: 50,000 t/yr
├── Borates: 15,000 t/yr
└── **Total Byproduct Revenue: ~$200M/yr**
```
