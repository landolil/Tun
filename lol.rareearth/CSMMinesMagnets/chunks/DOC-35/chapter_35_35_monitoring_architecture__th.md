## 3.5 Monitoring Architecture — The Transparency Mandate

### 3.5.1 Multi-Party Monitoring Network

| Parameter | Sensors | Frequency | Data Access | Governance |
|-----------|---------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Brine Level (Piezometers)** | 50 (DGA) + 30 (SQM) + 10 (CPA) | 15-min | **Open portal (DGA)** | Tripartite review |
| **Freshwater Lens (Piezometers)** | 20 (DGA) + 15 (CPA) | Hourly | **CPA-controlled** | CPA veto on decline >0.5m |
| **Lagoon Levels (Radar/Lidar)** | 5 stations (CONAF/SQM) | Daily | **CONAF public** | CONAF alert |
| **Vegetation (NDVI/EVI)** | Sentinel-2 (10m, 5-day) | 5-day | **Open (Copernicus)** | CPA + CONAF analysis |
| **Subsidence (InSAR)** | Sentinel-1 (6-day) | 12-day | **Open (ESA)** | SQM + DGA + CPA |
| **Brine Chemistry** | SQM lab (weekly) + DGA audit (quarterly) | Weekly/Quarterly | **DGA public reports** | RCA compliance |
| **GRACE/GRACE-FO** | Satellite gravimetry | Monthly | **Open (NASA/GFZ)** | Basin-scale validation |

### 3.5.2 Decision Triggers — The Adaptive Management Protocol

| Trigger | Threshold | Action | Authority | Timeline |
|---------|-----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Freshwater Lens Drawdown** | >0.5m/yr sustained | **Immediate SQM pumping reduction (20%)** | DGA + CPA | 30 days |
| **Laguna Chaxa Level** | <700 ha surface | **Emergency brine reinjection (1,000 L/s)** | CONAF + DGA | 15 days |
| **Subsidence Rate** | >2 cm/yr new area | **Wellfield reconfiguration, reinjection** | DGA + SQM | 90 days |
| **Vegetation NDVI Decline** | >15% from baseline | **Dust mitigation + groundwater recharge** | CONAF + CPA | 60 days |
| **Li Grade Decline** | <1,200 mg/L sustained | **Wellfield rotation, deep well activation** | SQM (operational) | 180 days |
