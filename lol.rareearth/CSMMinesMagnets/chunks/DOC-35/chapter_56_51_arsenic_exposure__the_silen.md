## 5.1 Arsenic Exposure — The Silent Epidemic

### 5.1.1 Exposure Pathways — Multi-Route Contamination

| Pathway | Source | Concentration | Exposed Population | Duration |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Drinking Water (Bottled)** | SQM-provided | **0.015 mg/L** (WHO 0.01) | 100% (7,000) | Chronic (lifetime) |
| **Drinking Water (Tap/Well)** | Alluvial aquifer | **0.025-0.030 mg/L** | 0% (bottled only) | Historical |
| **Inhalation (Dust)** | Salar crust, haul roads, ponds | **As-PM10: 0.8-2.5 μg/m³** | 100% (ambient), workers (high) | Chronic + occupational |
| **Dermal (Soil)** | Campamento yards, playgrounds | **As-soil: 50-200 mg/kg** | Children (high contact) | Chronic |
| **Occupational (SQM Workers)** | Brine handling, pond maintenance, plant | **Air: 5-20 μg/m³, Biological: 50-150 μg/L urine** | 4,000 workers | Working life (25 yr avg) |

### 5.1.2 Arsenic Mass Balance — The Environmental Load

```
ARSENIC FLOW — SALAR DE ATACAMA / MARÍA ELENA SYSTEM (Annual)

NATURAL INPUTS:
├── Geothermal Upwelling: 1,200 L/s × 15 mg/L As = **0.57 t/hr = 5,000 t/yr As**
├── Weathering (Volcaniclastics): ~500 t/yr
└── Atmospheric Deposition: ~50 t/yr
TOTAL NATURAL INPUT: **~5,550 t/yr As**

ANTHROPOGENIC MOBILIZATION:
├── SQM Brine Extraction: 4,500 L/s × 15 mg/L = **2.1 t/hr = 18,400 t/yr As** (brought to surface)
├── Evaporation Ponds: 95% water evaporated → **As concentrated in residual brine/salts**
├── Halite Harvest: 1.2 Mt/yr × 5 mg/kg As = **6 t/yr As removed** (product)
├── KCl Harvest: 300k t/yr × 2 mg/kg As = **0.6 t/yr As removed**
├── **Pond Sediments (Accumulating)**: **~18,000 t/yr As retained** (long-term liability)
└── **Plant Waste Streams**: **~300 t/yr As discharged** (tailings, mother liquor)

ENVIRONMENTAL PARTITIONING:
├── **Atmosphere (Dust)**: 10-20 t/yr As (PM10 from pond surfaces, haul roads)
├── **Soil (Campamentos)**: 5-10 t/yr As (deposition + irrigation)
├── **Freshwater Lens**: <1 t/yr As (natural barrier, but drawdown risks mobilization)
└── **Biota**: Bioaccumulation in flamingo, microbial mats, vegetation

HUMAN EXPOSURE DOSE (Inhalation, Adult, 70 kg):
├── Ambient PM10: 50 μg/m³ (annual avg) × 1.5% As = 0.75 μg/m³ As
├── Inhalation Rate: 20 m³/day
├── Absorption: 30% (respirable fraction)
├── **Daily Dose: 0.45 μg/kg-day**
├── **WHO TDI: 0.3 μg/kg-day** → **EXCEEDS by 50%**
└── **EPA RfD: 0.3 μg/kg-day** → **EXCEEDS by 50%**
```
