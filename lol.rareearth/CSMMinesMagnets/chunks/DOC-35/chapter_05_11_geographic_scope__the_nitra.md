### 1.1.2 Hydrographic Context — Zero Freshwater, Brine Only

```
HYDROGRAPHIC REALITY — MARÍA ELENA COMMUNE

SURFACE WATER: **NONE PERENNIAL**
├── Quebradas: Ephemeral, flash flood only (El Niño years)
├── Salar de Atacama: Eastern boundary, brine lake (seasonal)
└── Fog (Camanchaca): Coastal intrusion 15-20 km inland, seasonal

GROUNDWATER — TWO DISTINCT SYSTEMS:
├── **FRESHWATER LENS (Western Margin)**
│   ├── Aquifer: Pampa del Tamarugal (Oligocene-Miocene)
│   ├── Recharge: Andean foothills (minimal, <1 mm/yr effective)
│   ├── Quality: As 0.02-0.05 mg/L, B 5-10 mg/L, F 2-4 mg/L
│   ├── Use: **Zero municipal extraction** — SQM holds all rights
│   └── Communities: Chiu Chiu, Estación San Pedro (DOC-26) depend on this
│
└── **BRINE AQUIFER (Salar de Atacama Nucleus)**
    ├── Depth: 50-200m (halite nucleus), 200-400m (deep brine)
    ├── Chemistry: Na-Cl, 280 g/L TDS, Li 1,500 mg/L, K 12,000 mg/L, Mg 8,000 mg/L
    ├── Extraction: **SQM 4,500 L/s** (400 ha evaporation ponds)
    ├── Recharge: Geothermal (deep) + marginal freshwater seepage
    └── Governance: **DGA brine rights (SQM), Indigenous consultation (CPA)**

FOG HARVESTING POTENTIAL:
├── Camanchaca frequency: 60% nights (May-Oct)
├── Elevation band: 800-1,400m optimal
├── Potential: **50-100 L/s** (100 collectors × 1,000 m²)
├── Current: **Zero operational** — pilot only
└── Barrier: Land tenure (SQM), maintenance capacity, visual impact
```
