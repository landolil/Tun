PROCESSING: Stanford Method for Persistent Scatterers (StaMPS)

RESULTS:
├── **Maximum Subsidence Rate**: **-3.2 cm/yr** (SQM wellfield NE sector)

├── **Risk**: Well casing failure, pond liner stress, infrastructure damage
└── **Mitigation**: SQM reinjection pilot (500 L/s, 2024) — early results show stabilization

GRACE SATELLITE (Mass Balance):
├── **Total Water Storage Change**: **-1.8 cm/yr equivalent water height**
├── **Attribution**: 70% brine extraction, 20% climate, 10% natural
└── **Implication**: **Salar losing mass faster than geothermal recharge**
