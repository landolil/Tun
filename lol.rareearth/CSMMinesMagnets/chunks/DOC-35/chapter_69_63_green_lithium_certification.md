## 6.3 Green Lithium Certification — The Market Mandate

### 6.3.1 Certification Landscape — The Three Pillars

| Standard | Scope | Key Metrics | SQM Status | Timeline |
|----------|-------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **IRMA (Initiative for Responsible Mining Assurance)** | 400+ criteria (Business, Social, Environmental, Closure) | Water, biodiversity, human rights, GHG, tailings, community | **Stage 2 Audit (2024)** | **Certification 2025** |
| **LMEpassport (London Metal Exchange)** | Digital passport for metals | Carbon footprint (<5 kg CO₂/kg Li), water, biodiversity, due diligence | **Data compilation 2024** | **Launch 2025** |
| **EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542)** | Mandatory for EU market (2027+) | Carbon footprint declaration, recycled content, due diligence, labeling | **Gap analysis 2024** | **Compliance 2026** |

### 6.3.2 Carbon Footprint — The Decarbonization Target

| Scope | Current (2023) | Target (2027) | Reduction Pathway |
|-------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Scope 1 (Direct)** | 120,000 tCO₂/yr (diesel, process) | **40,000 tCO₂/yr** | Electrification, biomass, efficiency |
| **Scope 2 (Grid Electricity)** | 350,000 tCO₂/yr (200 MW × 450 g/kWh) | **<50,000 tCO₂/yr** | **Solar PV + BESS (80% replacement)** |
| **Scope 3 (Upstream/Downstream)** | 180,000 tCO₂/yr (chemicals, transport) | 120,000 tCO₂/yr | Green chemicals, rail, shipping |
| **TOTAL** | **650,000 tCO₂/yr** | **<210,000 tCO₂/yr** | **-68%** |
| **Intensity (kg CO₂/kg Li₂CO₃)** | **15.3** | **<5.0** | **Meets IRMA/EU threshold** |
