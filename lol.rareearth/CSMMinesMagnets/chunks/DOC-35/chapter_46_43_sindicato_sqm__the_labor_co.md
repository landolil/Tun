## 4.3 Sindicato SQM — The Labor Counterweight

### 4.3.1 Union Structure & Power

| Dimension | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Members** | **2,200** (all direct SQM workers) |
| **Density** | **100% of direct workforce** |
| **Affiliation** | CUT (Central Unitaria de Trabajadores) |
| **Leadership** | Elected every 3 years, current: **Presidenta María González (since 2021)** |
| **Bargaining Unit** | **Single-employer (SQM)** — pattern setter for contractors |
| **Strike Fund** | CLP 500M (3 months operations) |
| **Political Influence** | **Determines local elections, regional deputies, CORE** |

### 4.3.2 Current Collective Bargaining Agreement (2023-2026)

| Clause | Specification | Innovation |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Wage Increase** | +4.5% real (IPC + 2.5%) | Above inflation |
| **Housing Guarantee** | **No eviction without 90-day notice + relocation** | **First in Chilean mining** |
| **Health Fund** | CLP 2B/yr (SQM 70%, worker 30%) | Covers family, specialists, dental |
| **Automation/Transition Clause** | **24-month notice, $5M retraining fund, early retirement 55+ (80% salary)** | **Just Transition precedent** |
| **Profit Sharing** | 1.5% net income distributed | **CLP 1.5M/worker/yr (2023)** |
| **Contractor Parity** | Subcontractor wages ≥85% direct equivalent | **Narrows precarity gap** |
| **Gender Equity** | 30% women in leadership by 2026 | **Mining sector leader** |

TENSION POINTS:
