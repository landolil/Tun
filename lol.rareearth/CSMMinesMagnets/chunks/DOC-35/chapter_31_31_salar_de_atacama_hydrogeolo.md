## 3.1 Salar de Atacama Hydrogeology — The Brine Nucleus

### 3.1.1 Geological Architecture — A Closed Basin Evaporite System

| Unit | Depth | Lithology | Hydraulic Role | Lithium Relevance |
|------|-------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Halite Nucleus (Core)** | 0-50m (surface), 50-200m (deep) | **Massive halite (95% NaCl)**, minor gypsum/ anhydrite | **Primary brine reservoir** — porosity 5-10% (fractures) | **Li 1,500 mg/L** — highest grade globally |
| **Deep Brine Aquifer** | 200-400m | Fractured volcaniclastics, ignimbrites | **Hydraulic connection to nucleus**, geothermal recharge | Li 800-1,200 mg/L |
| **Freshwater Lens (Margins)** | 0-100m | Alluvial fans, conglomerates, sands | **Recharge zone** — Andean meltwater infiltration | Li <10 mg/L (contamination indicator) |
| **Alluvial Aquifer (West)** | 50-300m | Pampa del Tamarugal Formation | **Regional freshwater** — Chiu Chiu, Estación San Pedro | As 0.02-0.05 mg/L, B 5-10 mg/L |

### 3.1.2 Water Balance — The Evaporation Engine

```
SALAR DE ATACAMA WATER BALANCE (Annual, 3,000 km² nucleus)

INPUTS:
├── **Geothermal/Deep Recharge**: 1,200 L/s (38 Mm³/yr) — Li-rich, heated
├── **Marginal Freshwater Seepage**: 300 L/s (9.5 Mm³/yr) — from alluvial fans
├── **Direct Precipitation**: 15 mm/yr × 3,000 km² = 45 Mm³/yr (instant evaporation)
└── **TOTAL INPUT**: ~92.5 Mm³/yr

OUTPUTS:
├── **Evaporation (Nucleus)**: 2,500 mm/yr × 3,000 km² = **7,500 Mm³/yr**
├── **SQM Brine Extraction**: 4,500 L/s = **142 Mm³/yr** (1.9% of evaporation)
├── **Other Extractors (Albemarle, etc.)**: ~2,000 L/s = **63 Mm³/yr**
└── **TOTAL OUTPUT**: ~7,705 Mm³/yr

NET BALANCE: **MASSIVE DEFICIT** — Sustained by:
├── **Halite Dissolution** (nucleus porosity release)
├── **Deep Geothermal Upwelling** (continuous, Li-rich)
├── **Paleowater Storage** (Pleistocene pluvial legacy)
└── **Result**: Salar surface declining ~1.5 cm/yr (GRACE/InSAR)

LITHIUM MASS BALANCE:
├── Li Input (Geothermal): 1,200 L/s × 1,500 mg/L = 1.8 t/hr = **15,800 t/yr Li**
├── Li Extraction (SQM): 4,500 L/s × 1,500 mg/L × 18-mo cycle efficiency = **~60,000 t/yr Li (180k t/yr Li₂CO₃)**
├── Li Extraction (Others): ~25,000 t/yr Li
├── **Li Accumulation in Nucleus**: Net positive (geothermal > extraction)
└── **Sustainability**: **Geothermal recharge > current extraction** — but freshwater lens at risk
```
