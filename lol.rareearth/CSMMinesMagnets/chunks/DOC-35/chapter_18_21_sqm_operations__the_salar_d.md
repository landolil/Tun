## 2.1 SQM Operations — The Salar de Atacama Brine Engine

### 2.1.1 Brine Extraction — The World's Largest Lithium Brine Operation

| Parameter | Specification | Global Context |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| **Brine Extraction Rate** | **4,500 L/s** (142 Mm³/yr) | **Largest single brine extraction globally** |
| **Wellfield** | 40 production wells (50-200m depth) | Clustered in 4 sectors (N, NE, E, SE) |
| **Pump Type** | Submersible, 150-250 kW each | Solar-assisted (20% power from PV) |
| **Brine Chemistry (Wellhead)** | Na-Cl, 280 g/L TDS, Li 1,500 mg/L, K 12,000 mg/L, Mg 8,000 mg/L, B 400 mg/L, SO₄ 8,000 mg/L | Consistent across wellfield |
| **Extraction Pressure** | 8-12 bar (artesian + pump assist) | Low energy vs hard rock |
| **Monitoring** | DGA piezometers (50), SQM SCADA (real-time), GRACE satellite (monthly) | Transparency mandate (RCA) |

### 2.1.2 Evaporation Pond Complex — Solar Concentration at Scale

| Metric | Value | Engineering |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Total Pond Area** | **400 ha** (4 km²) | Largest solar evaporation facility globally |
| **Pond Configuration** | 24 primary (pre-concentration), 12 secondary (Li enrichment), 6 final (saturation) | Gravity-fed cascade |
| **Concentration Cycle** | **18 months** (brine → 6% Li feed) | Seasonal variation ±2 months |
| **Evaporation Rate** | 2,500 mm/yr (net) | 95% of incident solar |
| **Water Loss** | **142 Mm³/yr extracted → 135 Mm³/yr evaporated** | 7 Mm³/yr to plant feed |
| **Halite Harvest** | 1.2 Mt/yr NaCl (byproduct) | Sold as industrial salt |
| **Pond Liner** | HDPE 2mm + geotextile (double) | Leak detection (sensor grid) |

### 2.1.3 Lithium Carbonate Plant — Chemical Conversion

| Stage | Process | Input | Output | Capacity |
|-------|---------|-------|--------|----------|
| **1. Pre-treatment** | Filtration, Mg removal (lime), Ca removal (soda ash) | 6% Li brine (7,000 L/s) | Purified Li brine | 180k t/yr Li₂CO₃ eq |
| **2. Carbonation** | CO₂ + LiOH → Li₂CO₃ (90°C, pressurized) | LiOH solution (from electrolysis) | Wet Li₂CO₃ slurry | 99.5% purity |
| **3. Crystallization** | Cooling crystallization (40°C → 10°C) | Wet slurry | Li₂CO₃ crystals | 99.9% battery grade |
| **4. Drying/Packing** | Rotary dryer, 150°C, N₂ atmosphere | Crystals | **Bagged Li₂CO₃ (500 kg big bags)** | 180k t/yr |
| **Byproducts** | KCl (potash), Mg(OH)₂, Borates | Waste streams | **Fertilizer/industrial grade** | 300k t/yr KCl |
