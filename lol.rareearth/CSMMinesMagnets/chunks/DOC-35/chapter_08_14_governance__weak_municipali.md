## 1.4 Governance — Weak Municipality, Dominant Company

### 1.4.1 Institutional Architecture

| Body | Authority | Capacity | Dependency |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Municipalidad María Elena** | Urban planning, health, education, housing, patentes | 120 staff, CLP 4B budget | **SQM = 80% revenue, owns 85% housing** |
| **SQM (Sociedad Química y Minera)** | Brine extraction, lithium processing, company town services | 4,000 direct, $3B+ revenue | **De facto governor** |
| **Sindicato SQM** | Collective bargaining, housing allocation, health benefits | 2,200 members | **SQM counterpart** |
| **CPA Atacameño (Peripheral)** | Consulta Previa on brine expansion, territorial rights | 2 comunidades in commune (Estación San Pedro, Chiu Chiu) | **Marginal influence** |
| **SEREMI/Government** | Environmental (RCA), water rights, labor, health | Regional | **Regulatory, not operational** |

### 1.4.2 The Company Town Governance Gap

| Function | Provider | Quality | Gap |
|----------|----------|---------|-----|
| **Housing** | SQM (85% stock) | Good (maintained) | **Zero tenure security — tied to employment** |
| **Health** | SQM Clinic + Public CESFAM | High (SQM), Basic (public) | **SQM clinic for workers only** |
| **Education** | SQM-funded schools (2) + Municipal (2) | High | **Curriculum aligned to mining** |
| **Water/Sewage** | SQM infrastructure | Good | **Brine only — no potable network expansion** |
| **Transport** | SQM buses (shift) + Public (limited) | Adequate | **No evening/weekend service** |
| **Recreation** | SQM Club House + Municipal | Good | **SQM facilities priority access** |
