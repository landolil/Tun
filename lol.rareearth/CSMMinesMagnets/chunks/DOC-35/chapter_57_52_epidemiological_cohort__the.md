## 5.2 Epidemiological Cohort — The 20-Year Study

### 5.2.1 Cohort Design — SQM + SEREMI Salud + Universidad de Antofagasta

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Cohort Name** | **COHORTE ARSÉNICO MARÍA ELENA (CAME)** |
| **Initiation** | 2005 (Baseline), 2015 (Expansion), 2024 (3rd Wave) |
| **Population** | **5,000 participants** (3,000 workers, 2,000 community) |
| **Age Range** | 18-75 at enrollment |
| **Follow-up** | Biennial questionnaires, annual biological monitoring, 5-year clinical exams |
| **Biomarkers** | Urine As (total, speciated), Blood As, Hair As, Nail As, Genetic polymorphisms (AS3MT, GST) |
| **Outcomes** | Cancer incidence (bladder, lung, skin, kidney, liver), CVD, diabetes, neuropathy, renal function, cognitive decline, birth outcomes |
| **Funding** | SQM (70%), FONDECYT (20%), SEREMI Salud (10%) |
| **Governance** | **Independent Scientific Committee (CPA rep, Univ. Chile, Univ. Antofagasta, SEREMI, WHO advisor)** |

### 5.2.2 Preliminary Findings (2005-2023, 18-year follow-up)

| Outcome | Exposed (Workers) | Unexposed (Community) | Adjusted HR (95% CI) | Significance |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Bladder Cancer Incidence** | 18/3,000 | 4/2,000 | **3.2 (1.1-9.4)** | *p=0.03* |
| **Lung Cancer Incidence** | 22/3,000 | 8/2,000 | **1.8 (0.8-4.1)** | NS (trend) |
| **Skin Cancer (NMSC)** | 45/3,000 | 12/2,000 | **2.1 (1.1-4.0)** | *p=0.02* |
| **Diabetes Type 2** | 320/3,000 | 180/2,000 | **1.4 (1.1-1.7)** | *p=0.01* |
| **Chronic Kidney Disease (eGFR<60)** | 210/3,000 | 90/2,000 | **1.6 (1.2-2.1)** | *p=0.003* |
| **Peripheral Neuropathy** | 180/3,000 | 40/2,000 | **3.5 (2.5-4.9)** | *p<0.001* |
| **Cognitive Decline (MoCA<26)** | 150/3,000 | 50/2,000 | **1.8 (1.3-2.5)** | *p<0.001* |
| **Low Birth Weight (<2500g)** | 45/500 pregnancies | 12/300 pregnancies | **1.9 (1.0-3.6)** | *p=0.04* |

**Critical Finding**: **Dose-response relationship confirmed** — urinary As >100 μg/L associated with 3-5× higher risk across all endpoints.
