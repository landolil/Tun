## 7.3 Transition Model — From Company Town to Heritage Hub

### 7.3.1 The Dual-Track Transition

| Track | Objective | Timeline | Key Actors | Milestones |
|-------|-----------|----------|------------|------------|
| **Track A: Nitrate Preservation** | Maintain Shanks system as living heritage | 2024-2030 | SQM, DIBAM, CPA, Municipality | UNESCO inscription, Phase 1 tourism operational |
| **Track B: Economic Diversification** | Reduce SQM dependency from 90% → 50% | 2024-2035 | Municipality, GORE, CORFO, SQM, Union | Tourism 15%, Solar services 10%, Logistics 10%, Other 15% |

### 7.3.2 Governance Architecture — The Heritage Co-Management Board

```
OFICINA MARÍA ELENA HERITAGE BOARD — PROPOSED STRUCTURE

COMPOSITION (9 members):
├── SQM Representative (2) — Land owner, operator, funder
├── Municipalidad Representative (2) — Urban planning, permits, community
├── CPA Atacameño Representative (1) — Territorial rights, cultural interpretation
├── DIBAM/CMN Representative (1) — Heritage standards, conservation
├── Sindicato SQM Representative (1) — Worker memory, oral history, community
├── Academic/Technical Representative (1) — Univ. Antofagasta/Chile, conservation science
└── UNESCO Focal Point (1, observer) — International standards, networking

DECISION RULES:
├── Strategic (budget >$1M, land use, UNESCO): **Consensus (all 8 voting)**
├── Operational (tours, maintenance, staffing): **Simple majority**
├── Emergency (safety, structural): **Chair (rotating annual) decides, ratifies next meeting**
└── Veto: **CPA + DIBAM joint veto on cultural integrity matters**

FUNDING:
├── SQM Annual Contribution: **$1M/yr (0.03% lithium revenue)** — 20-yr commitment
├── Municipal Allocation: **5% patentes mineras** (~CLP 160M/yr)
├── Ticket Revenue: **70% to Board, 30% to Municipal Trust**
├── Grants: **CORFO, UNESCO, GORE, International (target $10M/yr)**
└── Endowment Goal: **$50M by 2035** (perpetual maintenance)
```
