## 5.4 Brine Ecosystem — The Hidden Biodiversity

### 5.4.1 Microbial Mats — The Lithium Bio-Recyclers

| Component | Description | Ecological Role | Lithium Interaction |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Halophilic Archaea** | *Halobacterium, Haloferax* | Primary producers (photoheterotrophs) | **Li+ compatible, K+ dependent** |
| **Cyanobacteria** | *Euhalothece, Halothece* | Oxygenic photosynthesis, mat structure | Li tolerance up to 500 mM |
| **Sulfate Reducers** | *Desulfohalobium* | Anaerobic carbon cycling | Li inhibits at >200 mM |
| **Methanogens** | *Methanohalophilus* | Terminal carbon mineralization | Li tolerant |

**Biotech Potential**: **Lithium bio-recovery from waste streams** — microbial selectivity for Li+ over Mg2+, Na+. Pilot: **30% Li recovery from mother liquor** at lab scale.
