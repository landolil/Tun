## 7.1 Oficina María Elena — The Last Operating Nitrate Oficina

### 7.1.1 Historical Significance — The Shanks System Legacy

| Aspect | Specification | Global Uniqueness |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Process** | **Shanks Leaching** (caliche → nitrate via counter-current leaching) | **LAST COMMERCIAL OPERATION WORLDWIDE** |
| **Founded** | 1926 (Guggenheim Brothers / Cia. Salitrera de Tarapacá y Antofagasta) | Continuous operation since 1926 |
| **Peak Production** | 300,000 t/yr NaNO₃ (1940s) | Largest single oficina ever |
| **Peak Workforce** | 2,000 workers + families (pop ~8,000) | Classic company town at scale |
| **Current Production** | **40,000 t/yr** (specialty nitrate) | **5% of peak** |
| **Current Workforce** | **200** (nitrate division) | Aging, specialized knowledge |
| **Monumento Nacional** | Declared 1999 (Decreto Exento 743) | **Active industrial monument** |
| **UNESCO Tentative List** | Oficinas Salitreras (with Humberstone, Santa Laura) | **Serial nomination pending** |

### 7.1.2 Industrial Archaeology — The Intact Ensemble

| Component | Specification | Condition | Heritage Value |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|----------------|
| **Leaching Tanks (Shanks)** | 40 counter-current tanks, 500 m³ each | **Operational** | **Core process heritage** |
| **Caliche Crushing/Grinding** | Primary/secondary crushers, ball mills | Operational | Mechanical heritage |
| **Crystallization Plant** | Vacuum crystallizers, centrifuges, dryers | Operational | Chemical engineering heritage |
| **Power House** | 4 × 5 MW steam turbines (coal → biomass transition) | Operational | Energy heritage |
| **Railway (FCAB)** | 120 km to port, 3 locomotives, 200 wagons | **Active freight** | Transport heritage |
| **Campamento (Worker Housing)** | Grid layout, 1,200 units, plaza, church, school, theater | **Inhabited, maintained** | **Social heritage** |
| **Administration Building** | 1926 neoclassical, central courtyard | Restored 2015 | Architectural heritage |
| **Water System** | 40 km pipeline from Loa river (historical) | Replaced by SQM wells | Engineering heritage |
