#### 2.1.1 Geological Setting

**El Romeral** (Coquimbo Region, Elqui Province) is Chile's **largest magnetite-dominant IOCG deposit**, operated by CAP Mining (Compañía de Acero del Pacífico):

- **Resource**: 1,800 Mt @ 0.35% Cu, 0.08 g/t Au, 680 ppm REE, 45% Fe, 38% magnetite
- **Reserves**: 850 Mt @ 0.38% Cu, 0.09 g/t Au, 720 ppm REE, 48% Fe, 40% magnetite
- **Mine Life**: 25+ years at 12 Mt/yr current rate
- **Processing**: Magnetite concentration (LIMS/WHIMS) → pellet feed; Cu-Au flotation → concentrate
