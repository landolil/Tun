#### 3.3.2 Consent Complexity: Dominga Precedent

The **Dominga rejection (2021/2022)** by Coquimbo Environmental Commission and Council of Ministers creates **heightened scrutiny** for Huasco valley projects. Aegis-Forge must demonstrate:
- Zero aqueous discharge (vs Dominga desalination + tailings)
- Zero tailings (vs Dominga tailings dam)
- Community veto embedded in governance (vs Dominga community fund only)

**Advantage**: Thermal cascade addresses all three Dominga failure modes.
