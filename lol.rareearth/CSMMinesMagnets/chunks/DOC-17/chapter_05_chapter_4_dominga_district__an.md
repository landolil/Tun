### 4.2 Consulta Rejection — Forensic Analysis (Baker Street)

| Cause | Dominga Proposal | Aegis-Forge Resolution |
|-------|------------------|------------------------|

#### 4.2.2 Restart Pathway

```
Dominga Restart Protocol:

  2. Diaguita Elqui valley-wide Consulta (not community-by-community)
  3. FBC/CDC federation charter BEFORE EIA submission
  4. CRMA strategic project designation (REE criticality)
  5. SEA fast-track (12 months vs 24)
  6. Council of Ministers review with CRMA priority
```
