### 4.1 Watershed-Scale Trust Architecture

Unlike Diaguita (valley-specific), **Pehuenche territory is hydrologically integrated** — **four watersheds but single cosmological unit (pewén-glacier-water)**. **Trust structure: Single Butalmapu Trust with watershed sub-funds**.

**Butalmapu Pehuenche Trust — Capitalization**:

| Sub-Fund | Capitalization | Source | Governance | Purpose |
|----------|----------------|--------|------------|---------|

| **Watershed Trust (WT)** | US$60M (Year 0) + 4% FCF/yr | Alpha-7..11 FBC + Bravo-5 | 4 Lonkos + 2 Machi + 1 Hydrologist + 1 Tardigradia | Water rights defense, wetland restoration, aquifer recharge |

| **Cultural Center Fund (CCF)** | US$15M (Year 0) + 1% FCF/yr | All clusters | 3 Machi + 2 Norche + 1 Architect + 1 UNESCO | Kimün Mapuche (knowledge center), Nguillatun site, language nest |

**Total Year-0 Capitalization**: **US$200M** — **highest of all community trusts** (reflects glacier/pewén non-substitutability).

### 4.2 Glacier Protection Fund — Innovation

**GPF Unique Features**:
- **Glacier Veto**: **Any project impacting inventoried glacier (DGA 2023) = automatic GPF review + Machi veto**
- **Dust Suppression Mandate**: **Alpha-7..11 must fund haul road paving, enclosed conveyors, real-time dust monitoring** — **GPF audits compliance quarterly**
- **Glacier Monitoring Network**: **GPF funds 20 stake stations + 5 time-lapse cameras + 3 automatic weather stations** — **data public, Pehuenche-owned**
- **Legal Defense Fund**: **US$5M/yr for glacier protection litigation** — **Environmental Court, Water Tribunal, Supreme Court**

**GPF Glacier Monitoring Formula — Dust Impact**:
$$\Delta Melt = \frac{\alpha_{dust} \cdot D_{deposition} \cdot (1 - \alpha_{clean})}{\rho_{ice} \cdot L_{fusion}} \cdot SW_{in}$$
Where $\alpha_{dust}$ = dust albedo reduction factor (0.15–0.25), $D_{deposition}$ = dust flux (g/m²/day), $SW_{in}$ = incoming shortwave radiation. **Target: $\Delta Melt < 2\%$ above natural**.
