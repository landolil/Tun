## Chapter 3: Consent Architecture

### 3.1 Consulta Indígena — Pehuenche Protocol

**Legal Basis**: **Law 19.253 Art 34 + ILO 169 Art 6 + Supreme Court Dominga precedent (2021) + Environmental Court Andina 244 (2024)**. **Pehuenche-specific protocol established by Butalmapu Pehuenche 2022, validated CONADI Res 345/2023**.

**Consulta Phases (Pehuenche Protocol)**:

| Phase | Action | Timeline | Pehuenche Gate | Output |
|-------|--------|----------|----------------|--------|
| **1. Identification** | SEA + CONADI identify affected Lofs | 30 days | Butalmapu confirms Lof list | Affected Lof registry |
| **2. Information** | Proponent delivers EIA + glacier model + water balance + cultural impact study in Mapudungun/Spanish | 60 days | **Machi reviews spiritual impact section** | Information package receipt |
| **3. Lof Deliberation** | Each Lof **Kuyfi Kimün** convenes (min 3 sessions, quorum 2/3) | 90 days | **Machi spiritual reading required** | Lof Acta (consent/veto) |
| **4. Butalmapu Accord** | 8 Lonkos + 3 Machi + 2 Norche convene | 30 days | **Unanimous Lof consent required** | Butalmapu Acta |
| **5. Spiritual Ratification** | **Nguillatun ceremony** — community petitions Ngen Pewén | 15 days | **Machi interprets signs** | Spiritual Acta |
| **6. Acta de Consulta** | Notarized, CONADI witness, SEA filing | 15 days | **Binding veto — cannot be overridden** | Acta de Consulta |

**Total Minimum Timeline**: **240 days** (vs. 225 Diaguita) — **spiritual ratification adds 15 days**. **No CRMA fast-track applies** (Environmental Court 2024 confirmed).

**Pehuenche Consent Formula — Spiritual Threshold**:
$$C_{Pehuenche} = \begin{cases}
1 & \text{if } \left(\prod_{i=1}^{8} C_{Lof,i} = 1\right) \land (Machi\_veto = 0) \land (Nguillatun\_signs > 0) \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}$$
Where $C_{Lof,i} \in \{0,1\}$ = Lof consent (1) or veto (0). **All 8 Lofs must consent, zero Machi veto, positive Nguillatun signs**. **Binary, non-compensable**.

### 3.2 Pehuenche Territorial Authority — Legal Standing

**Standing in Environmental Courts (Tribunales Ambientales)**:
- **Collective water rights (derechos de agua ancestrales)** — **pre-1981, senior to mining** (Water Code Art 56 bis + Law 21.435 Art 5)
- **Pewén forest title** — **15,400 ha titled as Tierras Indígenas** (Law 19.253 Art 5)
- **Glacier protection standing** — **Environmental Court 2024 recognized Pehuenche as "glacier guardians"** (Law 21.435 Art 10)
- **Consulta veto** — **binding on RCA per Dominga + Andina 244 precedents**

**Territorial Jurisdiction Formula**:
$$J_{Pehuenche} = \bigcup_{w=1}^{4} \left( W_{headwaters,w} \cup P_{pewén,w} \cup S_{sacred,w} \cup G_{glacier,w} \right)$$
Where $W_{headwaters}$ = glacier/snowmelt catchments, $P_{pewén}$ = pewén forest extent, $S_{sacred}$ = Nguillatun/ceremonial sites, $G_{glacier}$ = inventoried glaciers (DGA 2023). **Total: ~350,000 ha**.

### 3.3 FPIC Standards — Beyond Consultation

| FPIC Element | International Standard | Pehuenche Standard | Enforcement |
|--------------|------------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Free** | No coercion | **No state/proponent presence in deliberations** | Machi oversees |
