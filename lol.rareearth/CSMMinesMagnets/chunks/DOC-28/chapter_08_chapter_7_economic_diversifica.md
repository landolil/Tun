### 7.3 Hydroponic Agriculture — Cold Climate Advantage

**High-Altitude Hydroponics (1,600–2,200 masl)**:
- **Advantages**: **Low pest pressure, high UV (nutrient density), cold nights (flavor), glacier water purity**
- **Crops**: Leafy greens, berries, herbs, seed potatoes (phytosanitary clean)
- **Energy**: **Geothermal (volcanic) + solar** — **off-grid capable**
- **Water**: **95% recycle (hydroponic) + glacier melt (mineral-rich)**

**Hydroponic Revenue Model**:

| Facility | Investment | Area | Annual Revenue | Pehuenche Jobs |

### 7.4 Data Centers — Natural Cold Climate

**Andean Data Center Advantage**:
- **Ambient temperature**: 4–12°C (year-round at 2,000 masl) — **free cooling 80% of year**
- **Renewable energy**: **Solar/wind/geothermal/hydro** — **100% carbon-free**
- **Latency**: **5ms to Santiago, 15ms to Buenos Aires, 60ms to São Paulo**
