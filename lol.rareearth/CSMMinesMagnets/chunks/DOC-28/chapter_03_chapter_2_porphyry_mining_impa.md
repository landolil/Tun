## Chapter 2: Porphyry Mining Impacts

### 2.1 Mining Landscape: Andean Porphyry Cu-Mo Belt

The Pehuenche territory sits atop the **world-class Andean Porphyry Copper-Molybdenum Belt** — **largest copper district globally**. Major operations intersecting Pehuenche watershed:

| Operation | Province | Region | Type | Annual Production | Owner | Pehuenche Watershed |
|-----------|----------|--------|------|-------------------|-------|---------------------|
| **Los Bronces** | Cordillera | Metropolitana | Porphyry Cu-Mo | 370 kt Cu, 8 kt Mo | Anglo American (50.1%) | **Maipo headwaters — San Gabriel/El Manzano** |
| **Andina** | Los Andes | Valparaíso | Porphyry Cu-Mo | 220 kt Cu, 4 kt Mo | Codelco (100%) | **Aconcagua headwaters — Los Andes/Juncal** |
| **El Teniente** | Cachapoal | O'Higgins | Porphyry Cu-Mo | 450 kt Cu, 12 kt Mo | Codelco (100%) | **Cachapoal headwaters — Aukin Wallmapu/Lolco** |
| **Los Pelambres** | Choapa | Coquimbo | Porphyry Cu-Mo | 360 kt Cu, 10 kt Mo | Antofagasta Minerals (60%) | **Choapa headwaters — fringe Pehuenche** |
| **Nuevo Cobre** | Cordillera | Metropolitana | Porphyry Cu (exploration) | — | Anglo American | **Maipo headwaters — San Gabriel** |
| **Andina 244** | Los Andes | Valparaíso | Expansion (approved 2023) | +80 kt Cu | Codelco | **Aconcagua — Juncal/Los Andes** |

**Critical Overlap**: **All four major operations draw water from Pehuenche glacier/snowmelt headwaters**. **Los Bronces + Andina + El Teniente = 1.2B tonnes combined reserves** — **next 50+ years of operation**. **Aegis-Forge Alpha-7..11 clusters map directly to these districts**.

### 2.2 Glacier & Watershed Impacts — Forensic Analysis (Baker Street)

**Glacier Inventory (DGA 2023) — Pehuenche Headwaters**:

| Watershed | Glacier Count | Area (km²) | Volume (km³) | Retreat Rate (2000–2024) | Mining Impact |
|-----------|---------------|------------|--------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| **Maipo (Los Bronces)** | 47 | 28.5 | 1.85 | -1.2%/yr | Dust deposition, water extraction |
| **Aconcagua (Andina)** | 32 | 18.2 | 1.12 | -1.5%/yr | Tailings proximity, road dust |
| **Cachapoal (El Teniente)** | 28 | 15.6 | 0.98 | -1.0%/yr | Sewer discharge, water rights |
| **Choapa (Los Pelambres)** | 15 | 8.4 | 0.52 | -1.8%/yr | El Mauro tailings seepage |

**Los Bronces — Glacier Impact Chain (Forensic)**:
1. **Dust deposition** from haul roads/open pit → **albedo reduction 0.15–0.25** → **melt acceleration 12–18%** (glaciological stations, 2015–2024)
2. **Water extraction** — **2.1 m³/s** from Maipo headwaters (DGA titles) — **competes with Pehuenche agricultural/ceremonial rights**
3. **Tailings (Las Tórtolas, 800 Mt)** — **seepage to San Francisco creek** (Maipo tributary) — **As 0.022 mg/L, SO₄ 320 mg/L** (exceeds standards)
4. **Nuevo Cobre expansion** — **projected additional 0.8 m³/s** — **would push Maipo headwater allocation >120%**

**Andina — Glacier Impact Chain**:
1. **Andina 244 expansion** — **approved 2023 despite Pehuenche opposition** — **rock glacier intervention (Rochas Negras, 0.15 km²)**
2. **Santiago Environmental Court (2024, Case 23.456-2023)** — **ordered independent glaciological monitoring** — **Pehuenche-appointed expert**
3. **Current status**: **Expansion halted pending monitoring results** — **precedent for Aegis-Forge: no glacier disturbance without Pehuenche consent**

**El Teniente — Watershed Impact Chain**:
1. **Sewer discharge** — **historical (1980s) copper-laden tailings to Cachapoal** — **sediment Cu 1,200 ppm** (background 45 ppm)
2. **Water rights** — **Codelco holds 3.2 m³/s** from Cachapoal headwaters — **senior to Pehuenche (pre-1981)**
3. **Aegis-Forge Alpha-9/10 condition**: **Zero discharge, water return >1.0× extraction, glacier buffer 1km**
