**Snowmaking Physics — Mass Balance Gain**:
$$\Delta MB_{snow} = \frac{\eta_{snow} \cdot Q_{water} \cdot \rho_{water} \cdot (1 - L_{sublimation})}{A_{glacier} \cdot \rho_{ice}}$$
Where $\eta_{snow} = 0.75$ (snowmaking efficiency), $Q_{water}$ = water flow, $L_{sublimation} = 0.15$ (sublimation loss). **Net gain: 0.3–0.5 m w.e./yr per glacier**.

**Moraine Stabilization — Glacier Terminus Protection**:
- **Technique**: **Geotextile-reinforced moraine buttressing + rock glacier ice-core insulation**
- **Target Glaciers**: Los Bronces, Juncal, Cipreses (retreating >20 m/yr)
- **Cost**: US$3.2M/glacier (GPF funded)
- **Effectiveness**: **Reduces terminus retreat by 60–75%** (validated at Glaciar Exploradores, Patagonia)
