### 4.5 Governance — Butalmapu Trust Board

**Butalmapu Trust Board (13 Members)**:
- **4 Lonkos** (one per watershed: Maipo, Aconcagua, Cachapoal, Choapa) — elected by Lof assemblies (3-yr terms)
- **3 Machi** (spiritual guides) — appointed by Machi council (lifetime, removable by 2/3 Butalmapu)
- **2 Norche** (elders) — appointed by Norche council (lifetime)
- **1 Werken** (messenger/diplomat) — elected by Butalmapu (2-yr term)
- **2 Independent Experts** — glaciologist/hydrologist, conservation biologist — jointly selected (3-yr term)
- **1 Tardigradia Representative** — appointed by Tardigradia Chile SpA board

**Decision Rules**:
- **Quorum**: 9/13 (must include ≥2 Machi + ≥3 Lonkos)
- **Glacier decisions (GPF)**: **Machi consensus required** (all 3 agree)
- **Pewén decisions (PRF)**: **Machi + Norche consensus required**
- **Cultural decisions (CCF)**: **Machi + Norche + Lonkos consensus**
- **Financial distributions**: **Lonko majority (3/4) + Machi consent**
- **Deadlock**: **Nguillatun ceremony** — **spiritual resolution binding**

**Meeting Cadence**: Monthly (operations), Quarterly (strategy), **Nguillatun-aligned** (solstices/equinoxes for major decisions).

### 4.6 Cross-Watershed Coordination — Butalmapu Protocol

**Butalmapu Protocol — Hydrological Solidarity**:
$$Q_{solidarity,w \to w'} = \frac{\max(0, CIA_{w'} - 0.25)}{\sum_{u} \max(0, CIA_u - 0.25)} \cdot Q_{surplus,w}$$
Where $CIA_w$ = watershed cumulative impact, $Q_{surplus,w}$ = water available above Pehuenche needs. **Enables Maipo/Cachapoal surplus to support Aconcagua/Choapa deficit** — **governed by Butalmapu, not market**.

**Solidarity Principle**: **"The glacier that feeds my river feeds my brother's river" (Pehuenche saying)** — **watershed boundaries are administrative; hydrological reality is shared**.
