### 6.4 Community Sentinel Authority — Legal Power

**Sentinel Legal Mandate (Butalmapu Trust Deed Schedule 4)**:
> **4.1** Designated Pehuenche Sentinels (4 per watershed, 16 total) have authority to:
> - **Access all Alpha/Bravo sites** for water/glacier/dust monitoring (unescorted, 24/7)
> - **Collect samples** at any discharge/intake point (chain of custody maintained)
> - **Issue Stop-Work Order** if: (a) Dust > 0.5 g/m²/day at glacier stake; (b) Discharge As > 0.01 mg/L; (c) Glacier mass balance annual < -0.5 m w.e.
> - **Stop-Work Order is immediately binding** — **Tardigradia must comply within 2 hours**
> - **Dispute resolution**: Butalmapu emergency session (24 hrs) → Environmental Court (72 hrs)

**Sentinel Training**: **PTI Glaciology program (18 months) + DGA certification + Machi spiritual preparation**. **Annual re-certification**.
