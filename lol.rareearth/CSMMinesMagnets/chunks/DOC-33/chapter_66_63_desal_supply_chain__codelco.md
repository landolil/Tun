## 6.3 Desal Supply Chain — Codelco Desal II & HIF Dedicated

### 6.3.1 Codelco Desal II — Mejillones to Calama (The Artery)

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Capacity** | **2,000 L/s** (Phase 1: 1,200 L/s operational 2022, Phase 2: 800 L/s 2026) |
| **Intake** | Mejillones Bay (shared with Aguas Antofagasta, submerged 2km offshore) |
| **Pipeline** | **2×48" (1,200mm) HDPE/Steel**, 180 km, 8 pump stations, 2,260m elevation gain |
| **Energy** | **4.2 kWh/m³** (pumping dominant) — **Solar PPA (Cerro Dominador) + Grid** |
| **Delivery Points** | **Chuqui UG (100%), RT (50%), MH (100%), Municipal Calama (200 L/s)** |
| **Redundancy** | **N+1 pumps**, emergency storage (50,000 m³ at each station), **interconnection with Aguas Antofagasta** |
| **Investment** | **US$1.85B** (Desal I $1.1B + Desal II $750M) |

### 6.3.2 HIF Dedicated Desal — Independent Supply

| Parameter | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|-----------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Capacity** | 1,800 L/s | +3,600 L/s | +5,400 L/s |
| **Intake** | Submerged (1.5km offshore) | Submerged (2km) | Submerged (2.5km) |
| **Outfall** | Dedicated (2km, diffuser) | Dedicated (3km) | Dedicated (4km) |
| **Energy** | **3.0 kWh/m³** (PV-coupled, BESS) | 3.0 kWh/m³ | 3.0 kWh/m³ |
| **Brine** | 1,440 L/s (70 g/L) | 2,880 L/s | 4,320 L/s |
| **Investment** | $180M | $320M | $450M |
