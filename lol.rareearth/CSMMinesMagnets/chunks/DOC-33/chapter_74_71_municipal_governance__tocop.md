## 7.1 Municipal Governance — Tocopilla vs Mejillones

### 7.1.1 Municipalidad de Tocopilla — The Declining Industrial Municipality

| Dimension | Status | Challenge |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Budget (2024)** | CLP 28.5B (~US$30M) | **-12% real since 2018** (population loss, tax base erosion) |
| **Revenue Sources** | 55% FNDR, 25% Patente Comercial (Codelco/Thermo), 15% Patente Minera, 5% Permisos | **Over-reliance on Codelco/Thermo (40% direct+indirect)** |
| **Debt** | CLP 4.2B (15% revenue) | Manageable but limits investment capacity |
| **Staff** | 380 funcionarios (200 planta, 180 contrata) | **Aging workforce (avg 48 yr), 25% retirement eligible by 2028** |
| **Key Plans** | PLADECO 2022-2026, PRC 2023 (updating), PMU 2024 | **PRC update critical: industrial transition zoning, Chango territory recognition** |

**Critical Governance Gaps**:
1. **No dedicated transition office** — Thermo closure (2025-2028) managed ad-hoc
2. **Limited technical capacity** — No environmental engineer, no mining lawyer on staff
3. **Codelco dependency** — Municipal land/housing/health historically Codelco-funded, now withdrawing
4. **Chango recognition** — **No municipal Chango affairs office** (vs Mejillones)

### 7.1.2 Municipalidad de Mejillones — The Growing Industrial Municipality

| Dimension | Status | Opportunity |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Budget (2024)** | CLP 42.3B (~US$45M) | **+38% real since 2018** (industrial patentes, HIF, port growth) |
| **Revenue Sources** | 35% FNDR, 40% Patente Comercial (Industrial), 15% Patente Minera, 10% Permisos/Port | **Diversified industrial base (6+ major companies)** |
| **Debt** | CLP 6.8B (16% revenue) | Higher absolute but lower risk (growing revenue) |
| **Staff** | 420 funcionarios (250 planta, 170 contrata) | **Younger workforce (avg 42 yr), hiring environmental/legal** |
| **Key Plans** | PLADECO 2023-2027, PRC 2022 (vigente), PMU 2023, **Estrategia Industrial 2024-2035** | **Proactive industrial transition planning, Chango affairs unit created 2023** |

**Governance Innovations**:
- **Oficina de Asuntos Chango (2023)** — 3 professionals (lawyer, anthropologist, marine biologist), direct to Alcalde
- **Mesa Industrial Comunal (Monthly)** — Alcalde + 6 major companies + Chango reps + unions + SEREMIs
- **Fondo de Desarrollo Comunitario Industrial (2024)** — 3% major project capex → community projects (Chango priority)
