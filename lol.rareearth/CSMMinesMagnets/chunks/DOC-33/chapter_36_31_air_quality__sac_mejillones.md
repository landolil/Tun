## 3.1 Air Quality — SAC Mejillones & Thermoelectric Contribution

### 3.1.1 Saturated Zone Declaration (SAC) — Legal Framework

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Decree** | **DS 45/2018 (MMA)** — Declara Zona Saturada por MP10, MP2.5, SO₂, NO₂ |
| **Area** | **Mejillones Peninsula** — 1,200 km² (Tocopilla, Mejillones, Sierra Gorda, María Elena) |
| **Pollutants** | **MP10 (Saturated)**, **MP2.5 (Saturated)**, **SO₂ (Saturated)**, **NO₂ (Latent)** |
| **Primary Sources** | **Thermoelectric (CTM/CTA): 65% SO₂, 45% NOx, 30% MP2.5**, Mining (40% MP10), Vehicles (15% NOx), Desal (5% MP2.5) |
| **Management Plan (PDA)** | **Plan de Descontaminación Atmosférica (PDA) Mejillones 2020–2030** |
| **Goal** | **Compliance with NCh 2731 (MP10 50 μg/m³ annual), WHO guidelines (MP2.5 10, SO₂ 20, NO₂ 40)** |

### 3.1.3 Air Quality Trends (2018–2023) — PDA Effectiveness

| Pollutant | Standard (Annual) | 2018 | 2020 | 2022 | 2023 | Trend | Compliance |
|-----------|-------------------|------|------|------|------|-------|------------|
| **MP10 (μg/m³)** | 50 (NCh 2731) | 68 | 58 | 52 | **49** | ↓ 28% | **Achieved 2023** |
| **MP2.5 (μg/m³)** | 20 (NCh 2731) / 10 (WHO) | 28 | 24 | 19 | **17** | ↓ 39% | **NCh: Yes, WHO: No** |
| **SO₂ (μg/m³)** | 50 (NCh 2731) / 20 (WHO) | 42 | 32 | 24 | **18** | ↓ 57% | **Both: Yes** |
| **NO₂ (μg/m³)** | 40 (NCh 2731) / 40 (WHO) | 38 | 35 | 33 | **31** | ↓ 18% | **NCh: Yes, WHO: Marginal** |

**Source Apportionment 2023 (CTM/CTA Contribution)**:
| Pollutant | CTM/CTA Share | Mining Share | Vehicles/Other | Natural (Dust) |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| **MP10** | 25% | **55%** | 10% | 10% |
| **MP2.5** | **45%** | 30% | 15% | 10% |
| **SO₂** | **65%** | 20% | 5% | 10% |
| **NOx** | **45%** | 20% | **30%** | 5% |

**Key Insight**: **Thermoelectric dominates SO₂ (65%) and MP2.5 (45%)** — Coal retirement (CTM 2028, CTA 2030) is **necessary and sufficient** for WHO compliance.
