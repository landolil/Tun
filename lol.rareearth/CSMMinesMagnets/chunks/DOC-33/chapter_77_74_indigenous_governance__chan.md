## 7.4 Indigenous Governance — Chango Councils & CPA

### 7.4.1 Consejo de Caletas Mejillones — The Bay-Scale Authority

| Body | Composition | Authority | Legal Basis |
|------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Consejo de Caletas** | **4 Loncos + 4 Voceros (1 per caleta) + 2 Technical Advisors (bio, legal)** | **Cumulative veto, consultation protocol, benefit distribution, kimün validation** | **Law 19.253 Art 34, ILO 169 Art 6, UNDRIP Art 19, CONADI Res 2022** |
| **Asamblea General de Caletas** | All Chango members (4 caletas, ~940 people) | **Ratify veto, approve benefit agreements, elect Loncos, mandate Council** | **Chango Estatuto Interno (2021), CONADI recognition** |
| **Comisión Técnica Chango** | 1 biologist, 1 oceanographer, 1 lawyer, 1 kimün holder per caleta | **CIA review, monitoring design, adaptive management, science-kimün integration** | **Decreed by Consejo, funded by HIF Chango Fund** |

### 7.4.2 CPA Atacameño — Distinct but Coordinated

| Dimension | CPA Atacameño | Consejo de Caletas Mejillones |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| **Territory** | Salar/Interior (18 comunidades) | **Mejillones Bay Marine (4 caletas)** |
| **Legal Person** | **Corporación Pública (Law 19.253)** | **Asociación Indígena (Law 19.253 Art 34)** |
| **Consultation** | **Standardized (Protocolo 2022)** | **Bay-specific cumulative (Protocol 2023)** |
| **Benefit Sharing** | **Royalty Minero 1.5% + project agreements** | **Project-specific (HIF 3%), future cumulative fund** |
| **Coordination** | **Mesa de Coordinación Intercultural (Quarterly)** | **Shared: brine, thermal, H₂ pipeline, REE shipping** |
