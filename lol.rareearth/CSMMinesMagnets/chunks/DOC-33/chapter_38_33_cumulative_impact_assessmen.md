## 3.3 Cumulative Impact Assessment — The 7-Source Problem

### 3.3.1 Seven Industrial Sources in One Bay

| Source | Type | Operational | Key Stressors | Cumulative Weight |
|--------|------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **1. CTM (540 MW Coal)** | Thermo | 2010–2028 | Thermal plume, SO₂/NOx/Hg, coal dust, ash | **High** |
| **2. CTA (260 MW Coal)** | Thermo | 2011–2030 | Thermal plume, SO₂/NOx/Hg, coal dust, ash | **High** |
| **3. GNL Mejillones (LNG)** | Regas | 2010– | Cold discharge, CH₄ slip, vessel traffic | Medium |
| **4. Desal Cluster (5 plants)** | Desal | 2006– | Brine, chemicals, intake impingement | **Very High** |
| **5. HIF H₂/NH₃ (Ph1-3)** | Green H₂ | 2027– | Brine (addl), NH₃ risk, H₂ safety, O₂ byproduct | **Emerging High** |
| **6. Port Expansion (EMPORCHA)** | Port | 2025–2030 | Dredging, noise, lighting, vessel traffic, spills | Medium |
| **7. Data Centers (ADC Cluster)** | Digital | 2026– | Energy demand, cooling water, land use | Low-Medium |

### 3.3.2 Cumulative Interaction Matrix

| Interaction | Mechanism | Magnitude | Management |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Thermo Thermal + Desal Brine** | **Stratification traps brine at bottom**, thermal plume caps mixing | **Synergistic (1.5× individual)** | **Staggered outfall depths, diffuser design, real-time ops coordination** |
| **Coal Ash + Desal Chemicals** | **Antiscalant-phosphonate + Fly ash = enhanced P binding**, alters sediment geochemistry | **Additive** | **Sediment monitoring, phosphonate alternatives (green antiscalants)** |
| **LNG Cold + Desal Intake** | **LNG cold water (-160°C) → intake pre-cooling** reduces RO energy | **Beneficial (5% energy savings)** | **GNL Mejillones + Desal operators MOU 2023** |
| **HIF O₂ Byproduct + Thermo Oxy-Combustion** | **HIF electrolyzer O₂ (80 kt/yr) → CTM/CTA oxy-fuel** reduces NOx, enables CCUS | **Transformative (if timed)** | **HIF Phase 2 + CTM retrofit feasibility study 2025** |
| **NH₃ Terminal + Artisanal Fishing** | **NH₃ toxicity (LC50 0.5 mg/L)**, exclusion zone, safety perimeter | **High risk perception** | **Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA), Chango co-monitoring, 2km buffer** |
