## 3.2 Marine Impact — Thermal Plume, Brine Plume, Sediment, Benthos, Fisheries

### 3.2.1 Thermal Plume — Coal Plants (CTM/CTA)

**Thermal Plume Characteristics (2023 Hydrodynamic Model + Satellite Validation)**:
| Parameter | CTM (540 MW) | CTA (260 MW) | Combined |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|----------|
| **Cooling Water Flow** | 60 m³/s | 30 m³/s | 90 m³/s |
| **Temperature Rise (ΔT)** | +7°C | +7°C | +7°C |
| **Plume Surface Area (>1°C)** | **4.2 km²** | **2.1 km²** | **5.8 km²** |
| **Plume Depth (>1°C)** | 8m (surface trapped) | 6m (surface trapped) | — |
| **Seasonal Variation** | Summer: 6.5 km² (stratification) | Summer: 3.2 km² | Summer: 9.0 km² |
| **Impact on Benthos** | **Seagrass loss (30% in plume)**, invertebrate community shift | Similar, smaller | **Cumulative 5.8 km² affected** |

**Regulatory Compliance (DS 90 — Emisión Líquidos)**:
| Parameter | Standard | CTM/CTA Actual | Status |
|-----------|----------|----------------|--------|
| **ΔT at 100m from outfall** | ≤2°C | 1.5°C (CTM), 1.2°C (CTA) | **Compliant** |
| **Plume Area >1°C** | <5 km² (per plant) | 4.2 km² (CTM), 2.1 km² (CTA) | **Compliant** |
| **Benthic Impact Zone** | <1 km² | 1.8 km² (CTM), 0.9 km² (CTA) | **CTM: Marginal exceedance** |
