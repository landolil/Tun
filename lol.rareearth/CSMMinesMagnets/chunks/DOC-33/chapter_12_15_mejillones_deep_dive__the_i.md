## 1.5 Mejillones Deep Dive — The Industrial Bay

### 1.5.1 Mejillones Bay — Geography & Industrial Zoning

**Mejillones Bay Specifications**:
| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Bay Type** | Semi-enclosed, north-facing, 12 km width, 8 km depth |
| **Bathymetry** | -15m (entrance) to -30m (inner), natural deep water |
| **Currents** | Humboldt Current influence, upwelling zone, seasonal reversal |
| **Sedimentation** | Low (rocky substrate), fine sediments in inner bay |
| **Ecological Value** | **Upwelling ecosystem**: anchoveta, sardine, jack mackerel, kelp forests, marine mammals |
| **Protected Areas** | **AMCP-MU Mejillones** (Multiple Use Marine Coastal Protected Area, 2020, 120 km²) |

**Industrial Zoning (EMPORCHA Master Plan)**:
| Zone | Location | Activities | Operators | Expansion Potential |
|------|----------|------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Zona Puerto Norte** | North shore | **LNG Terminal (GNL Mejillones)**, coal unloading (CTM/CTA) | GNL Mejillones, Engie, AES | Limited (naval base buffer) |
| **Zona Puerto Central** | Central bay | **Multi-purpose (EMPORCHA)**, HIF NH₃ future, desal intakes | EMPORCHA, HIF, Aguas Antofagasta | **High (HIF Phase 2-3, data centers)** |
| **Zona Puerto Sur** | South shore | **Desal cluster (5 plants)**, brine outfalls, artisanal caletas | Aguas Antofagasta, Codelco, Escondida, Spence, HIF | Moderate (brine management) |
| **Zona Caletas Chango** | 4 caletas along shore | **Artisanal fishing, algae, tourism, cultural** | Chango communities | **Protected (Law 20.249, AMCP-MU)** |

### 1.5.2 Industrial Cluster — Asset Register

| Asset | Operator | Capacity | Status | Key Specs |
|-------|----------|----------|--------|-----------|
| **GNL Mejillones (LNG Terminal)** | GNL Mejillones (Engie 60%, Codelco 20%, others) | **Regas: 8.5 Mt/yr**, Storage: 340k m³ (2 tanks) | **Operational 2010** | Jetty -18m, pipeline to CTM/CTA & mining |
| **CTM (Central Termoeléctrica Mejillones)** | Engie | **540 MW (2×270 MW coal)** | **Operational 2010/2012**, **Retire 2028** | Seawater cooling, flue gas desulfurization |
| **CTA (Central Termoeléctrica Andina)** | AES Andes | **260 MW (2×130 MW coal)** | **Operational 2011/2013**, **Retire 2030** | Seawater cooling, mercury control |
| **Desal Plants (5 Total)** | Various | **5,500 L/s combined** | **Operational 2006–2026** | See detailed table below |
| **HIF Mejillones (Green H₂/NH₃)** | HIF Global | **Phase 1: 400 MW electrolyzer, 65 kt/yr NH₃** | **Construction 2025–2027** | Dedicated desal, NH₃ terminal, O₂ byproduct |
| **Puerto Mejillones (EMPORCHA Multi-propósito)** | EMPORCHA | **3.5 Mt/yr general/breakbulk** | **Operational** | Berths -15m, rail access (FCAB spur) |
