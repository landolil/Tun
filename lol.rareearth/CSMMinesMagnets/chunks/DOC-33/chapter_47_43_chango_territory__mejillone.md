### 4.3.2 Chango Marine Consent Protocol — Mejillones Bay Specificity

**Distinct from Antofagasta Chango (DOC-32) and Atacameño (DOC-26)**:
| Dimension | Mejillones Bay Chango | Antofagasta Chango (DOC-32) | Atacameño (DOC-26) |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Territory** | **Mejillones Bay (120 km² AMCP-MU)** | Antofagasta Coast (30 km) | Salar/Quebradas Interior |
| **Caletas** | **4 (Mejillones, Hornitos, Punta Itata, Caleta Buena)** | 8 caletas + 3 urban barrios | 18 comunidades territoriales |

| **Veto Scope** | **Bay-wide cumulative** — Single project can be vetoed if cumulative threshold exceeded | Project-specific marine | Territorial terrestrial |
