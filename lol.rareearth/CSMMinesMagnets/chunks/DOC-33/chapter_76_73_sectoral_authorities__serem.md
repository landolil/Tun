## 7.3 Sectoral Authorities — SEREMIs & Services

### 7.3.1 SEREMI Matrix — Tocopilla/Mejillones Jurisdiction

| SEREMI | Key Instruments | Corridor Decisions | Capacity Gap |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **SEREMI Energía** | PPA approval, concession electricity, H₂ roadmap, thermo closure decree | **CTM/CTA closure dates, HIF PPA, H₂ pipeline concession** | **No dedicated H₂ unit, thermo closure coordination weak** |
| **SEREMI Medio Ambiente** | SEA coordination, RCA conditions, PRTR, cumulative impact | **HIF SEA, Desal SEAs, cumulative brine/thermal, Chango consultation** | **Cumulative assessment methodology immature** |
| **SEREMI Minería** | Mining concessions, safety (Sernageomin), closure plans, royalty | **Alpha-2, SQM iodine, Codelco cathode port, mineral transport** | **Strong on mining, weak on industrial symbiosis** |
| **SEREMI Economía** | CORFO/ProChile, cluster policies, SME support, foreign investment | **HIF investment, IMTA aquaculture, mineral recovery, tourism** | **Limited industrial policy tools** |
| **SEREMI Salud** | Air quality (PDA), water quality, occupational health, epidemiological surveillance | **Thermo air PDA, desal brine health, Chango health, worker health** | **Epidemiological baseline missing for corridor** |
| **SEREMI Vivienda** | Urban planning (PRC), housing policy, campamentos formalization (DS49) | **Tocopilla housing decay, Mejillones campamentos, Chango caletas** | **DS49 pipeline slow, Tocopilla retrofit unfunded** |
