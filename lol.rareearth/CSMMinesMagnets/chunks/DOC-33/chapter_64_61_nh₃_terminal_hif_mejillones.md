### 6.1.2 NH₃ Safety Case — Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA)

**Major Accident Hazards (MAH) Identified**:
| Scenario | Frequency (per yr) | Consequence | Risk Level | Mitigation |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|------------|------------|
| **NH₃ Release (Loading Arm Failure)** | 1.2×10⁻⁴ | Toxic cloud (ERPG-3 1.5 km), 50 fatalities | **High** | **ERC, double block & bleed, leak detection, water curtain, evacuation plan** |
| **Tank Overfill / Rollover** | 5×10⁻⁵ | BLEVE (if fire), 100 fatalities | Medium | **High-high level alarms, independent overflow, fire protection (deluge, foam)** |
| **Vessel Collision (Berth)** | 8×10⁻⁵ | NH₃ release, fire, 30 fatalities | Medium | **VTS, pilotage, tug assist, fendering, berthing velocity monitoring** |
| **Pipeline Rupture (HIF→Terminal)** | 3×10⁻⁴ | Jet fire, toxic cloud, 20 fatalities | Medium | **Buried pipeline, leak detection (fiber DTS), isolation valves 5km, cathodic protection** |
| **Seismic Event (M>8.0)** | 1×10⁻³ | Tank failure, cascade | **High** | **Seismic design (ASME VIII Div 2, IEEE 693), flexible connections, automatic shutdown** |

**Risk Reduction Measures (ALARP)**:
- **Inherently Safer Design**: Refrigerated storage (not pressurized), minimum inventory, double-wall tanks
- **Engineered Controls**: SIS (SIL 3), fire & gas detection (500 points), deluge/foam, ERC, vapor return
- **Procedural**: SIMOPS management, Chango Guardacostas co-monitoring, emergency drills quarterly
- **Emergency Planning**: **On-site (Tier 1), Off-site (Tier 2 - Municipality/GORE), National (Tier 3 - ONEMI/Armada)**
