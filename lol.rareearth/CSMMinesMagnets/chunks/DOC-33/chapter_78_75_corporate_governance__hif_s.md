## 7.5 Corporate Governance — HIF Steering & Industrial Coordination

### 7.5.1 HIF Steering Committee — The Symbiosis Orchestrator

| Member | Role | Voting | Key Decisions |
|--------|------|--------|---------------|
| **HIF Global (Chair)** | Project developer, capital, offtake | **3 votes** | Project phasing, capex allocation, offtake contracts |
| **GNL Mejillones** | LNG infrastructure, port, regas, H₂ blending | 2 votes | Terminal sharing, H₂ pipeline co-investment, safety |
| **Engie (CTM)** | Thermo assets, closure, waste heat, CO₂ capture | 2 votes | Waste heat supply, CO₂ capture, closure timeline, land |
| **AES (CTA)** | Thermo assets, closure, waste heat, CO₂ capture | 2 votes | Waste heat supply, CO₂ capture, closure timeline, land |
| **Codelco** | Cathode port, desal off-taker, H₂ consumer (Alpha-2) | 2 votes | Desal supply, H₂ pipeline anchor, cathode logistics |
| **EMPORCHA** | Port authority, berths, safety zones, concessionaire | 2 votes | NH₃ berth allocation, safety zone, REE berth contingency |
| **Chango Consejo (Observer)** | **Cumulative veto, monitoring, benefit audit** | **Veto only** | **CIA approval, adaptive management, revenue audit** |
| **Municipalidad Mejillones (Observer)** | Local permits, community fund, urban planning | Observer | Campamentos, housing, local employment, PRC alignment |
| **GORE DIMA (Observer)** | Regional policy, FNDR, royalty, transition | Observer | Just transition funding, cluster policy, infrastructure |

**Decision Rules**:
- **Strategic (phasing, capex >$50M)**: Unanimous minus Chango veto
- **Operational (dispatch, maintenance, contracts)**: Simple majority
- **Chango Veto**: **Absolute on cumulative thresholds** — Cannot be overridden
