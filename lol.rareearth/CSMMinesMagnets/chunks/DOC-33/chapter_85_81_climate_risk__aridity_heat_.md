## 8.1 Climate Risk — Aridity, Heat, Storm Surge, Fog Change

### 8.1.1 Climate Projections — Mejillones Bay 2030/2050 (CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5)

| Variable | Historical (1995-2014) | 2030 (SSP2-4.5) | 2050 (SSP2-4.5) | 2050 (SSP5-8.5) | Impact on Corridor |
|----------|----------------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Mean Temp (°C)** | 16.2 | +0.8 | +1.4 | +2.1 | Cooling efficiency ↓, heat stress ↑ |
| **Max Temp (°C)** | 28.5 | +1.2 | +2.0 | +3.2 | **Worker safety, equipment derating** |
| **Min Temp (°C)** | 8.1 | +0.6 | +1.1 | +1.8 | Reduced fog (camanchaca) |
| **Precipitation (mm/yr)** | 12 | -15% | -25% | -35% | **Zero recharge, desal dependence ↑** |
| **Relative Humidity (%)** | 72 | -3% | -5% | -8% | **Camanchaca frequency ↓ 30-50%** |
| **Wind Speed (m/s)** | 6.8 | +0.3 | +0.5 | +0.8 | Evaporation ↑, plume dispersion ↑ |
| **Sea Level Rise (cm)** | 0 | +8 | +18 | +32 | **Port infrastructure, caletas, campamentos** |
| **Storm Surge (100yr, m)** | 1.8 | +0.15 | +0.35 | +0.65 | **NH₃ terminal, desal intake/outfall, port** |
| **Marine Heatwave (days/yr)** | 12 | +25 | +55 | +95 | **IMTA species stress, HAB risk ↑** |

**Critical Threshold**: **Camanchaca loss >50% by 2050 (SSP5-8.5)** → Natural fog water harvesting (Atrapanieblas) non-viable → **100% desal dependence locked in**.

### 8.1.2 Adaptation Measures — Climate-Resilient Infrastructure

| Asset | Climate Risk | Adaptation Measure | Investment | Status |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|------------|--------|
| **Desal Intakes (5 plants)** | SLR + storm surge → intake damage/silting | **Submerged intakes 2-3km offshore, -25m depth, articulated risers** | $120M (all plants) | **Design phase (Aguas Antofagasta, Codelco, HIF)** |
| **Desal Outfalls** | SLR → reduced diffuser performance, brine accumulation | **Extended diffusers, adjustable nozzles, real-time salinity control** | $45M | **Pilot HIF Phase 1** |
| **NH₃ Terminal** | SLR + surge → berth flooding, tank foundation | **Berth elevation +1.5m, tank farm +2.0m, seawall upgrade** | $35M | **Included in terminal capex** |
| **Port (EMPORCHA)** | SLR + surge → operational downtime | **Master Plan 2040: +2.0m elevation, automated mooring, elevated substations** | $280M | **Phased 2025-2040** |
| **H₂ Pipeline** | Seismic + SLR (coastal section) | **Buried 2m, flexible joints, cathodic protection, leak detection** | Included | **Design standard** |
| **Chango Caletas** | SLR + surge → habitat loss, infrastructure damage | **Managed retreat plan, elevated housing, floating infrastructure pilot** | $15M (Chango Fund) | **Participatory design 2024** |
