### 5.2.2 Utilization Pathways — Circular Carbon Economy

| Pathway | Technology | CO₂ Demand | Product | Market | Status |
|---------|------------|------------|---------|--------|--------|
| **HIF E-Methanol** | CO₂ + Green H₂ → CH₃OH | 1.4 Mt/yr (Ph3) | **1.0 Mt/yr MeOH** | Shipping fuel, chemical feedstock | **HIF Phase 2 feasibility** |
| **Algae Biofuels (IMTA)** | Flue gas direct injection → Algae | 0.2 Mt/yr | 50 kt/yr biomass | Biofuel, feed, bioplastic | **Pilot 2023 (Gracilaria)** |

| **Direct Air Capture (DAC) Hybrid** | Flue gas pre-concentration → DAC | 0.1 Mt/yr | Pure CO₂ | High-purity markets | **Conceptual** |

### 5.2.3 HIF E-Methanol Integration — The Anchor Project

**Process Flow**:
```
CTM/CTA FLUE GAS → CO₂ CAPTURE (Amine, 90%) → COMPRESSION (110 bar) → PIPELINE (10 km) → HIF MEOH SYNTHESIS
                                                                                                    ↑
                                                                                          GREEN H₂ (HIF ELECTROLYZER)
                                                                                                    ↓
                                                                                    E-METHANOL (1.0 Mt/yr) → EXPORT (Mejillones Port)
```

**Economics (Phase 3, 2033)**:
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **CO₂ Capture Cost** | US$45/t (amine, 90% capture, compression) |
| **H₂ Cost (HIF)** | US$1.8/kg (2030 target) |
| **MeOH Production Cost** | US$320/t |
| **MeOH Market Price (Green Premium)** | US$550/t (shipping fuel, EU RFNBO compliant) |
| **Annual Revenue** | **US$550M** |
| **Annual CO₂ Avoided** | **2.8 Mt/yr** (vs fossil methanol) |
| **Carbon Credits (Art 6.4)** | US$35/t × 2.8 Mt = **US$98M/yr** |
