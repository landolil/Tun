## 1.8 Historical Trajectory — Five Epochs of Extraction

### 1.8.1 Epoch 1: Nitrate Port (1870–1930) — The British Salitrera

**Tocopilla as Nitrate Gateway**:
| Metric | 1870 | 1913 (Peak) | 1930 (Crash) |
|--------|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Population** | 2,000 | **15,000** | 8,000 |
| **Nitrate Export (Mt/yr)** | 0.1 | **1.2** | 0.2 |
| **Railway** | — | **FCAB Tocopilla Branch (1890)** | Operational |
| **British Companies** | 1 | **8 (Lautaro, Santa Laura, etc.)** | 2 |
| **Port Infrastructure** | Wooden pier | **Muelle Salitrero (3 berths), warehouses, cranes** | Deteriorating |

**Social Structure (1913)**:
| Class | Population | Origin | Power Base |
|-------|------------|--------|------------|
| **British Elite** | 300 | England, Scotland | Nitrate companies, banks, Club Inglés |
| **Chilean Elite** | 500 | Santiago, Valparaíso | Politics, commerce, land |
| **Middle Class** | 1,200 | Chile, Europe | Professionals, merchants, supervisors |
| **Pampa Workers (Rotational)** | 8,000 | Chile, Bolivia, Peru | **FOCh stronghold, strike 1918/1925** |
| **Urban Poor** | 5,000 | Chile, migrants | Dockworkers, domestic, informal |

**Institutional Legacy**:
- **FCAB Tocopilla Branch** — Still operates, cathode/iodine only
- **Port Infrastructure** — Muelle Salitrero → modern cathode berths
- **Urban Layout** — Valley-constrained, hillside neighborhoods
- **Labor Movement** — **FOCh founded in Tocopilla 1909**, first general strike 1918
- **British Heritage** — Club Inglés, British School, Anglican Church (still active)

### 1.8.2 Epoch 2: Copper Cathode Port (1950–1990) — The State Industrializer

**Codelco's Cathode Gateway**:
| Metric | 1950 | 1970 | 1990 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Population** | 8,000 | 18,000 | **22,000** |
| **Cathode Throughput (kt/yr)** | 50 | 200 | **350** |
| **Codelco Investment** | — | **US$200M (port, rail, warehouses)** | **US$500M (modernization)** |
| **Company Housing** | 500 units | **3,000 units** | 3,500 units |
| **Union Power** | Weak | **Strong (Sindicato Codelco Tocopilla)** | **National bargaining** |

**Key Transformations**:
1. **Chileanization (1969) → Nationalization (1971)** — Codelco formed, Tocopilla becomes **strategic cathode export port**
2. **FCAB Modernization** — Diesel-electric locomotives, welded rail, 400 kt/yr capacity
3. **Port Modernization (1980s)** — Dedicated cathode berth, covered warehouses, automated stacking
3. **Iodine Discovery (1960s)** — **SQM develops caliche leaching**, Tocopilla becomes **world iodine capital**

### 1.8.4 Epoch 4: Green H₂/NH₃ Transition (2025–2035) — The Decarbonization Pivot

**The Energy Transition Challenge**:
| Dimension | Current (2024) | Transition (2025–2030) | Target (2035) |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|---------------|
| **Power Generation** | 800 MW coal (CTM+CTA) | **CTM retire 2028, CTA retire 2030** | **0 MW coal** |
| **Replacement** | — | **HIF H₂ (3.8 GW electrolyzer)**, Solar 5.7 GW, BESS 22.8 GWh | **100% renewable** |
| **Port Cargo** | Coal import 2.1 Mt/yr, Cu cathode 380 kt, Iodine 18 kt | **Coal → 0, NH₃ export 350 kt/yr, H₂ pipeline, REE transshipment** | **Green export hub** |
| **Desal** | 5 plants, 6,100 L/s | **+ HIF 1,800 L/s (Ph1), +5,400 L/s (Ph2-3)** | **11,500 L/s total** |
