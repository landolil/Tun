**Tocopilla Population Pyramid — The "Hourglass" Shape**:
- **Base (0-14)**: Narrow — Birth rate 11/1000 (vs 14 national)
- **Middle (15-64)**: Bulging at 40-55 — **Thermo/Codelco workforce cohort**
- **Top (65+)**: Widening — **18% >65** (vs 12% national), growing +3%/yr

### 4.1.3 Outmigration & Housing Decay — The Post-Industrial Syndrome

| Flow | Annual Avg | Profile | Destination |

| **Net Migration** | **-420/yr** | — | — |

**Housing Stock Condition (Municipal Census 2023)**:
| Category | Units | % of Stock | Condition | Vacancy |
|----------|-------|------------|-----------|---------|
| **Codelco Heritage (Pre-1990)** | 2,800 | 42% | **Poor (seismic, asbestos, no insulation)** | 22% |
| **Thermo Campamentos (CTM/CTA)** | 600 | 9% | Fair (company maintained) | 15% |

| **Private/Informal** | 1,800 | 27% | Variable | 12% |
| **TOTAL** | **6,700** | **100%** | — | **16%** |

**Housing Decay Drivers**:
1. **Codelco divestment** — Sold 40% stock 2010–2020, no maintenance obligation
2. **Seismic risk** — 1995 Antofagasta earthquake (M8.0) damaged 60% heritage stock, **only 30% retrofitted**
3. **Asbestos** — **85% pre-1990 units contain asbestos** (roofing, insulation) — Removal cost US$15k/unit
3. **Vacancy → Vandalism** — Empty units stripped (copper piping, fixtures), squatters, fire risk

| **Joven Emprendedor (Youth Return Incentive)** | 50 families | US$1.5M | Municipal + CORFO |
