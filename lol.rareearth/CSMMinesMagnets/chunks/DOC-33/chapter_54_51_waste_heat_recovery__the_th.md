### 5.1.3 Desal Pre-heat — The Flagship Symbiosis

**Technical Design**:
```
WASTE HEAT → DESAL PRE-HEAT SYSTEM

SOURCE: CTM/CTA Condenser Discharge (32°C, 90 m³/s)
    ↓
HEAT EXCHANGER STATION (Plate & Frame, Titanium, 4×60 MWth)
    ├── **Codelco Desal** (2,000 L/s) — 60 MWth → Feed 22°C → 38°C
    ├── **Aguas Antofagasta Desal** (1,000 L/s) — 30 MWth → Feed 20°C → 35°C
    ├── **Escondida Desal** (2,500 L/s) — 75 MWth → Feed 21°C → 37°C
    ├── **Spence Desal** (600 L/s) — 18 MWth → Feed 20°C → 36°C
    └── **HIF Desal** (1,800 L/s) — 54 MWth → Feed 22°C → 39°C
    ↓
RETURN TO BAY (28°C, ΔT +3°C vs ambient) — **Reduced thermal plume 40%**
```
