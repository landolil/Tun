## 1.2 Economic Base — Industrial Metabolism

### 1.2.1 Sectoral Composition (2023, Combined GDP ~US$1.8B)

| Sector | % GDP | Employment | Tocopilla | Mejillones | Key Anchors |
|--------|-------|------------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **Port & Logistics** | **30%** | 2,800 | Cathode port (Codelco), iodine (SQM), FCAB rail | LNG terminal, coal import, multi-purpose, HIF NH₃ | EMPORCHA, GNL Mejillones, Codelco, SQM |
| **Thermoelectric** | **25%** | 1,500 | **CTM (540 MW) + CTA (260 MW) coal** — 800 direct jobs | Same plants (located Mejillones side) | Engie (CTM), AES (CTA) |
| **Mining Services** | **20%** | 1,800 | Contractors for Codelco/SQM, maintenance, transport | Contractors for Escondida/Spence, desal, LNG | Tier 1-3 ecosystem (Antofagasta-based) |
| **Industrial/Desal** | **15%** | 1,200 | Iodine plants (SQM), water pumping | **5 desal plants (5,500 L/s)**, HIF H₂, brine IMTA | Aguas Antofagasta, Codelco, Escondida, Spence, HIF |
| **Other (Commerce, Public, Tourism)** | **10%** | 1,000 | Municipal, health, education, heritage tourism | Naval base, artisanal fishing, Chango tourism | Municipalidades, Armada, SUBPESCA, Sernatur |

**Industrial Revenue Dependence**: **~70% municipal budgets** from industrial sources (patentes thermoelectric, port, mining, desal).
