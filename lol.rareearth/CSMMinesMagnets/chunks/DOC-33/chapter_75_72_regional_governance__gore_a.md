## 7.2 Regional Governance — GORE Antofagasta & CORE

### 7.2.1 GORE Structure — Industrial Corridor Competencies

| Division | Competencies (Corridor Relevant) | Key Actors |
|----------|----------------------------------|------------|
| **DIMA (Desarrollo Industrial y Minero)** | Mining/energy project coordination, FNDR mining allocation, cluster development | **Jefe División + 3 professionals (mining, energy, logistics)** |
| **DIPRES (Presupuesto e Inversión)** | FNDR programming, regional royalty (Royalty Minero) distribution, ZOFRI coordination | **Presupuesto Regional + FNDR unit** |
| **DIMA - Unidad Transición Justa (2024)** | **Thermo closure social plans, worker reskilling, territory diversification** | **New unit, 5 professionals, CLP 2.5B/yr** |
| **DIGEDUC** | Technical training (CFT/IP), SENCE regional coordination, mining competency framework | **Coordination with CICITEM, PTI** |
| **DISER (Servicios Públicos)** | Health, housing, sanitation, rural water (APR), indigenous affairs coordination | **Health/Housing critical for Tocopilla** |

### 7.2.2 CORE (Consejo Regional) — Corridor Representation

| Provincia | Consejeros (8 total) | Corridor Influence |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Antofagasta** | 4 | **Port, REE export, urban, HIF Phase 3** |
| **El Loa (Calama)** | 2 | **Mining, Alpha-2, Desal, H₂ pipeline** |
| **Tocopilla** | **1** | **Thermo closure, Tocopilla revival, Chango Mejillones** |
| **Mejillones** | **1** | **Industrial hub, NH₃, H₂, Chango bay, port** |

**CORE Commissions (Corridor Relevant)**:
- **Comisión Minería y Energía** — All energy/mining projects, royalty distribution, transition
- **Comisión Medio Ambiente** — SEA follow-up, RCA compliance, cumulative impact
- **Comisión Pueblos Originarios** — CONADI coordination, Chango recognition, territorial rights
- **Comisión Presupuesto** — FNDR allocation, Royalty Minero distribution, ZOFRI surplus
