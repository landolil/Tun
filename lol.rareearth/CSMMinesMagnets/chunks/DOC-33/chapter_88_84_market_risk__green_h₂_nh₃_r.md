## 8.4 Market Risk — Green H₂/NH₃, REE, Copper, Commodity Cycles

### 8.4.1 Green H₂/NH₃ Market — The Demand Uncertainty

| Scenario | 2030 Global Green H₂ Demand | 2030 Green NH₃ Demand | HIF Competitiveness | Corridor Impact |
|----------|----------------------------|----------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Base (IEA APS)** | 35 Mt | 15 Mt | **Competitive (Atacama solar $1.8/kg)** | **Phase 3 proceeds** |
| **Bull (IEA NZE)** | 65 Mt | 30 Mt | **Highly competitive** | **Phase 3 accelerated, Phase 4 (2 GW)** |
| **Bear (Delayed Policy)** | 12 Mt | 5 Mt | **Marginal (needs subsidy)** | **Phase 3 delayed 5+ yr, stranded asset risk** |

**Key Market Risks**:
1. **EU RFNBO Certification** — Additionality, temporality, geographic correlation rules (Delegated Acts)
2. **Shipping Fuel Adoption** — IMO 2030/2040/2050 targets, NH₃ engine commercialization (MAN, WinGD)
3. **US IRA 45V Tax Credit** — $3/kg H₂ → Global price floor, but additionality strict
4. **China Electrolyzer Cost** — $200/kW by 2028 → Global capex deflation
5. **Chile Domestic Demand** — Mining (Codelco, BHP, Antofagasta Minerals) + Fertilizer (SQM) + Refining (ENAP)

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Hedge |

| **Alternative Motors (Ferrite, Axial Flux, Induction)** | Low-Medium | Nd demand reduction 10-15% | **Alpha-2 flexible: REE recovery from any stream** |
