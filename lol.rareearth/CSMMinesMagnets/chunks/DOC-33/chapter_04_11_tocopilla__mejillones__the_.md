## 1.1 Tocopilla & Mejillones — The Peninsula's Industrial Heart

### 1.1.1 Geographic & Demographic Profile

| Parameter | Tocopilla | Mejillones | Combined |
|-----------|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Location** | 22°05'S, 70°12'W | 23°06'S, 70°27'W | Mejillones Peninsula |
| **Population (2024)** | **24,800** | **12,300** | **37,100** |
| **Population Growth (2002–2024)** | **-8%** (27,000 → 24,800) | **+45%** (8,500 → 12,300) | **+2%** |
| **Area (km²)** | 2,131 | 3,618 | 5,749 |
| **Density (pop/km²)** | 11.6 | 3.4 | 6.5 |
| **Median Age** | 38 years | 32 years | 36 years |
| **Indigenous Population** | **3% Chango**, 2% Aymara | **8% Chango**, 1% Aymara | **5% Chango** |

| **Elevation** | 0–500 masl (coastal valley) | 0–200 masl (bay-front) | Coastal |

### 1.1.2 Urban Form — Constrained Valley vs Bay-Front Linear

| Dimension | Tocopilla | Mejillones |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Topography** | **Narrow coastal valley** (Quebrada Tocopilla), steep hills, limited expansion | **Bay-front linear** along Mejillones Bay, industrial zoning dominant |
| **Historic Core** | Plaza Carlos Condell, Puerto Salitrero, Iglesia San Jerónimo | Caleta Mejillones, Puerto Industrial, Plaza de Armas |
| **Industrial Zone** | **Integrated in valley**: Puerto Tocopilla, CTM/CTA thermoelectric, iodine plants | **Segregated peninsula**: LNG, coal, desal, HIF H₂, multi-purpose port |
| **Residential** | Hillside neighborhoods (El Morro, Covadonga, Salitrera), dense | Linear along coast (Mejillones Centro, Punta Alcalde, Caletas) |
| **Campamentos** | 6 settlements, 1,200 families (Cerro Negro, Quebrada Honda) | 4 settlements, 800 families (Punta Alcalde, Caleta Buena) |
| **Expansion Constraint** | **Geographic lock** — valley walls, seismic risk, water scarcity | **Industrial zoning** — port/energy reserves, Chango territory, naval base |

### 1.1.3 Historical Trajectory — Four Industrial Epochs

| Epoch | Period | Tocopilla | Mejillones | Peninsula Scale |
|-------|--------|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| **1. Nitrate (Salitrera)** | 1870–1930 | **Main export port** (1.2 Mt/yr), railway to Oficina Salitrera, 15,000 pop | Minor caleta, guano/shipping | Nitrate artery to Europe |
| **2. Copper Cathode** | 1950–1990 | **Codelco cathode port** (Chuqui/RT), 400 kt/yr, company town | Industrial port planning begins | Copper replaces nitrate |
| **3. Thermoelectric + Iodine** | 1990–2020 | **CTM (540 MW) + CTA (260 MW) coal**, iodine (SQM), population peak 27k | **GNL Mejillones LNG (2010)**, CTM/CTA expansion, desal begins | **Energy hub**: coal→gas, water for mining |
| **4. Green H₂/NH₃ Transition** | 2025–2035 | **Coal retirement** (CTM 2028, CTA 2030), iodine continues, cathode port | **HIF H₂/NH₃ (350 kt/yr)**, 5 desal plants, data centers, brine IMTA | **Green export hub**: zero-carbon, circular |

**Key Insight**: The peninsula has **reinvented 4×** — each cycle more capital-intensive, fewer jobs, larger environmental footprint. The Green H₂ transition must break the **job loss + tax base erosion** pattern.
