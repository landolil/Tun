**VERRA IFM (Improved Forest Management) Revenue Model**:
- **Project Area**: **200,000 ha restored native forest** (ex-plantation + degraded native)
- **Baseline**: **Pinus radiata rotation 24yr** — **carbon stock 180 tC/ha**
- **Project**: **Native forest recovery** — **carbon stock 280 tC/ha at Year 30**
- **Additionality**: **100 tC/ha over 30yr = 3.3 tC/ha/yr**
- **Total Credits**: **200,000 ha × 3.3 tC/ha/yr × 44/12 = 2.44 MtCO₂e/yr**
- **Price Trajectory**: **US$15 (2025) → US$55 (2035) — CPTPP Art 14 + EU ETS linkage**
- **Annual Revenue (Year 10)**: **2.44M × US$35 = US$85M/yr**

### 4.5.2 Governance Structure

```
FOREST TRUST (FT) — GOVERNANCE CHARTER

BOARD OF TRUSTEES (13 members):
├── 6 Nagche Territorial Council Delegates (rotating, 3 macro-zones: North/Central/South)
├── 2 Machi Mapu (permanent, spiritual veto on forest/cultural matters)
├── 2 CONAF Representatives (co-chair, technical vote only)
├── 1 Independent Forest Ecologist (U. Austral/U. Chile)
├── 1 Independent Carbon Markets Expert (VERRA/Gold Standard accredited)
└── 1 Tardigradia Representative (non-voting)

MACRO-ZONAL COUNCILS (3 — North: Malleco, Central: Cautín, South: Araucanía):
├── Composition: All Lof in macro-zone + Machi Mapu + Norche
├── Authority: Approve restoration plans, allocate community benefits, monitor compliance
├── Technical Team: 3 forestry engineers + 2 GIS specialists + 1 cultural monitor (FT funded)

TECHNICAL COMMITTEES:
├── Native Forest Restoration Committee (species, genetics, nursery, planting, maintenance)
├── Sustainable Management Committee (Planes de Manejo Ley 20.283, certificación FSC/PEFC)
├── Carbon & Climate Committee (VERRA IFM, MRV, leakage, permanence, buffer pool)
├── Cultural Fire Committee (Kutral Mapu — prescribed fire, traditional knowledge, safety)
├── NTFP Economy Committee (mushrooms, berries, medicinal, crafts, value-added)
└── Finance & Investment Committee (portfolio, grants, community distribution)

DECISION RULES:
├── Operational: Majority Board (7/13)
├── Restoration Plan Approval: **2/3 Board + affected Macro-Zonal Council + Machi Mapu**
├── Plan de Manejo Submission (CONAF): **Macro-Zonal Council approval (2/3) + Machi Mapu**
├── Carbon Project Registration: **Board 2/3 + Carbon Committee + Machi Mapu**
├── Cultural Fire Authorization: **Machi Mapu + affected Lof Lonko + CONAF safety sign-off**
├── Emergency (wildfire, pest outbreak, illegal logging): **Machi Mapu + 1 Macro-Zonal Council = immediate authority**

DISTRIBUTION POLICY (Annual):
├── 40% Native Forest Restoration (planting, maintenance, fencing, monitoring, nurseries)
├── 20% Sustainable Management (Plan de Manejo prep, certification, harvesting, value-add)
├── 15% Cultural Fire Program (Kutral Mapu teams, training, safety, ecological monitoring)
├── 10% NTFP Economy (harvest, processing, marketing, youth enterprises)
├── 8% Community Programs (housing, health, education, language nests)
├── 5% Cultural Revitalization (ceremonies, oral history, rewe construction, Mapudungun)
└── 2% Solidarity Fund
```

### 4.5.3 Restoration Technical Protocol — Kutral Mapu Integration

**Species Selection Matrix (Nagche Territory)**:

| Zone | Elevation | Rainfall | Primary Species | Secondary | Nurse | Cultural Value |
|------|-----------|----------|-----------------|-----------|-------|----------------|
| **Coastal Range** | 200–600m | 2,000–3,000mm | Nothofagus dombeyi (coigüe) | N. nitida, Weinmannia | Embothrium | High (canoe wood) |
| **Central Valley** | 100–400m | 1,200–1,800mm | N. alpina (raulí) | N. obliqua (roble), Podocarpus | Aristotelia | High (construction) |
| **Foothills** | 400–800m | 1,800–2,500mm | N. pumilio (lenga) | N. dombeyi, Araucaria | Berberis | **Sacred (pewén)** |
| **Riparian** | All | High | Myrceugenia, Drimys | Azara, Lomatia | — | Medicinal, ceremonial |

**Kutral Mapu (Cultural Fire) Protocol**:
1. **Seasonal Window**: **March–May (autumn)** — **low intensity, high humidity, predictable winds**
2. **Area per Burn**: **5–20 ha** — **mosaic pattern, 15–20yr return interval**
3. **Pre-Burn**: **Machi Mapu spiritual authorization + CONAF safety plan + community notification**
4. **Execution**: **Nagche fire brigades (certified)** — **traditional ignition (friction) + modern safety**
5. **Post-Burn**: **Monitoring 5yr** — **regeneration, soil carbon, invasive species, cultural species**
6. **Carbon Accounting**: **Pyrogenic carbon (PyC) quantified** — **VERRA VM0049 methodology**
