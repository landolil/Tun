## 6.3 Integrated Land-Sea Restoration — Ridge-to-Reef

### 6.3.1 Hydrological Connectivity — Mountains to Sea

**Biobío-Toltén-Imperial Continuum**:
```
ANDES (Pewenche) → FOOTHILLS (Nagche/Wenteche) → CENTRAL VALLEY (Nagche) → COASTAL RANGE (Lafkenche/Nagche) → ESTUARY/COAST (Lafkenche) → MARINE (Lafkenche WCS)

Key Flows:
├── Water: Glacier melt → rivers → aquifers → wetlands → estuary → coastal upwelling
├── Sediment: Natural supply → delta maintenance → beach nourishment → kelp substrate
├── Nutrients: Forest litter → river → estuary productivity → fisheries → Lafkenche harvest
├── Carbon: Forest sequestration → soil → river DOC → marine carbon pump → blue carbon
├── Species: Diadromous fish (pejerrey, puye, lamprea) — **cultural keystone for all 4 sub-groups**
└── Cultural: Pewén harvest → river ceremony → estuary offering → marine Nguillatun
```
