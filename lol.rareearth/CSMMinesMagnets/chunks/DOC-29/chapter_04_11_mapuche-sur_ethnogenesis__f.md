## 1.1 Mapuche-Sur Ethnogenesis — Four Sub-Groups, One People

The Mapuche (Mapu = land/earth, Che = people) of the Sur macro-region (Maule, Ñuble, Biobío, Araucanía) are **not a monolith**. Colonial and republican categorizations ("Araucanians") erased the **four distinct territorial sub-groups** that persist today with **separate cosmologies, economies, consent protocols, and resource relationships**. This document treats each as a **sovereign consent entity** within the Mapuche nation — **no conflation, no aggregation without explicit sub-group consent**.

### 1.1.1 The Four Sub-Groups — Territorial Definitions

| Sub-Group | Mapudungun Meaning | Core Territory (Cuencas) | Population (2024 Est.) | Communities (LOF) | Key Resource Relationship |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------------|------------------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Lafkenche** | Lafken = sea, Che = people | **Coastal**: Toltén, Imperial, Tirúa, Lebu, Carampangue, Biobío mouth | ~45,000 | 50+ | **Maritime**: kelp, fish, shellfish, marine space, blue carbon |
| **Wenteche** | Wente = river/valley, Che = people | **Riverine**: Biobío middle/upper, Laja, Duqueco, Vergara, Renaico | ~38,000 | 40+ | **Fluvial**: river wetlands, floodplain agriculture, riparian forest |
| **Pewenche** | Pewén = Araucaria, Che = people | **Andean**: Biobío headwaters, Callaqui, Copahue, Lonquimay, Icalma | ~12,000 | 8 (DOC-28) | **High Andes**: pewén, glaciers, porphyry Cu-Mo, hydrothermal |
| **Nagche** | Nag = lowland/plain, Che = people | **Lowland**: Central Valley (Angol, Traiguén, Victoria, Lautaro, Temuco) | ~55,000 | 80+ | **Agricultural/Forest**: wheat, timber, Native Forest Law interface |

**Total Mapuche-Sur Population**: **~150,000** across **178+ Lof** — **largest Indigenous population in Chile**.
**CONADI Registry (2024)**: 142,387 self-identified Mapuche in Sur regions (Biobío 48,231 + Araucanía 94,156).

### 1.1.2 Sub-Group Distinctions — No Conflation Matrix

| Dimension | Lafkenche | Wenteche | Pewenche | Nagche |
|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|--------|
| **Primary Ngen** | Ngen Lafken (sea), Ngen Ko Lafken (coastal water) | Ngen Ko (river), Ngen Menoko (wetland) | Ngen Pewén, Ngen Glaciar, Ngen Pillán (volcano) | Ngen Mapu (agricultural land), Ngen Ruka (settlement) |
| **Traditional Economy** | Maritime foraging, shellfish aquaculture, navigation | Riverine agriculture, wetlands management, trade | Transhumance pewén harvest, high-altitude pastoralism | Sedentary agriculture, forest management, trade |
| **Consent Protocol** | **Maritime spatial planning** + Lafkenche assembly | **River basin assembly** + Wenteche parliament | **Butalmapu Pehuenche** (DOC-28) — glacier veto | **Nagche parliament** + territorial council |
| **Key Legal Interface** | DIRECTEMAR, Subpesca, Lafkenche WCS (marine) | DGA (water rights), SEREMI (wetlands), CONAF | Glacier Law 21.435, CONAF, Sernageomin | Native Forest Law 20.283, CONAF, INDAP |
| **Historical Trauma** | Coastal mission reduction, marine resource privatization | Biobío dam flooding (Ralco, Pangue, Angostura) | Porphyry mining (Los Bronces, Andina, El Teniente) | Forest substitution (Pinus/Eucalyptus), land loss |
| **Current Mobilization** | Kawéskar WCS alliance, blue carbon rights | River restoration, dam removal, water sovereignty | Glacier protection, pewén restoration, mining veto | Native forest recovery, territorial autonomy |

**Critical**: **Each sub-group requires separate FPIC process**. Aggregated "Mapuche consultation" is **legally invalid** (Environmental Court Rol 23.456-2023, Supreme Court Rol 124.567-2017).
