## 6.1 Native Forest Law 20.283 — Mapuche-Sur Implementation Framework

### 6.1.1 Legal Architecture — Indigenous Provisions

| Article | Provision | Mapuche-Sur Gap | Nagche/Lafkenche/Wenteche Protocol |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Art 4** | Indigenous preference in bonuses | **<5% bonuses to Mapuche** (CONAF 2023) | **FT/MT/RT mandatory 40% quota** for Mapuche Lof |
| **Art 15** | Native forest recovery fund | **US$12M/yr national, <US$0.5M Mapuche** | **FT capitalizes US$25M/yr additional** |
| **Art 20** | Sustainable management plans | **US$15k/plan cost barrier** | **FT funds 100% plan prep for Lof** |
| **Art 34** | Indigenous consultation | **Not implemented for forest plans** | **Nagche Protocol (Ch 3) = binding consultation** |
| **Art 45** | Protected areas (APN) | **Zero Mapuche co-management** | **Lafkenche WCS = marine precedent → terrestrial APN co-management** |

### 6.1.2 Plan de Manejo Simplificado — Nagche Community Protocol

**CONAF Resolution Exemption (Proposed — Nagche Council + CONAF Negotiation)**:
- **Threshold**: **Lof with <500 ha native forest** — **simplified plan (50% cost, 50% time)**
- **Technical Support**: **FT-funded forestry engineers (3 per macro-zone)** — **no cost to Lof**
- **Cultural Integration**: **Machi Mapu + Norche co-sign plan** — **kutral mapu prescribed fire schedule included**
- **Approval Timeline**: **90 days (vs 180 standard)** — **CONAF fast-track for Mapuche plans**

### 6.1.3 Native Forest Restoration — Landscape Scale Strategy

**Restoration Target**: **200,000 ha by 2040** (from 720,000 ha current → 920,000 ha)

**Spatial Prioritization Model (FT + CONAF + U. Austral)**:
$$\text{Priority Score} = 0.3 \cdot \text{Connectivity Index} + 0.25 \cdot \text{Degradation Level} + 0.2 \cdot \text{Cultural Value} + 0.15 \cdot \text{Carbon Potential} + 0.1 \cdot \text{Water Regulation}$$

| Priority Tier | Area (ha) | Criteria | Timeline | FT Investment |
|---------------|-----------|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Tier 1: Critical Corridors** | 35,000 | Connectivity >0.8, riparian, ceremonial sites | Year 1–5 | US$140M |
| **Tier 2: Degraded Plantation Edges** | 65,000 | Adjacent to native forest, high natural regeneration | Year 3–10 | US$195M |
| **Tier 3: Cultural Keystone Areas** | 40,000 | Machi Mapu designated, pewén/raulí/coigüe groves | Year 2–8 | US$200M |
| **Tier 4: Water Regulation Zones** | 35,000 | Aquifer recharge, wetland buffering, flood mitigation | Year 5–12 | US$140M |
| **Tier 5: Carbon Maximization** | 25,000 | High growth species, VERRA IFM eligibility | Year 4–15 | US$100M |

**Restoration Methods by Tier**:
| Tier | Primary Method | Species Mix | Cost/ha | Monitoring |
|------|----------------|-------------|---------|------------|
| 1 | **Assisted natural regeneration + enrichment planting** | 60% natural, 40% planted (keystone spp.) | US$4,000 | Annual (drone + plots) |
| 2 | **Plantation conversion (thinning + underplanting)** | 30% residual pine/eucalypt, 70% native | US$3,000 | Bi-annual |
| 3 | **Cultural fire + enrichment** | Kutral Mapu mosaic + ceremonial species | US$5,000 | Annual + cultural |
| 4 | **Wetland/riparian planting** | Myrceugenia, Drimys, Azara, Gunnera | US$4,000 | Hydrological + veg |
| 5 | **High-density native plantation** | Nothofagus, Podocarpus, Araucaria | US$4,000 | Carbon verification |
