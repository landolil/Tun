## 3.4 Pewenche High-Andes Consent — Butalmapu Protocol (DOC-28 Reference)

**Incorporated by reference from DOC-28 Chapter 3 (Butalmapu Pehuenche Consulta Protocol)**.

**Key Elements for Cross-Sub-Group Coordination**:
- **Glacier Veto** (GPF Schedule) — applies to all headwater projects affecting Pewenche glaciers
- **Pewén Restoration Veto** (PRF Schedule) — applies to any project disturbing pewén stands >1 ha
- **Watershed Integrity Veto** (WT Schedule) — applies to projects altering flow to Wenteche/Lafkenche downstream
- **Machi Triad Veto** — **all 3 Machi (Biobío, Maipo/Aconcagua, Cachapoal) must concur** for any Andean project
