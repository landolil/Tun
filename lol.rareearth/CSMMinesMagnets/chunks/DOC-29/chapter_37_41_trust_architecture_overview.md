## 4.1 Trust Architecture Overview — Sub-Group Sovereignty

**Principle**: Each sub-group controls its **own trust capital, governance, and allocation**. **No cross-sub-group pooling without explicit Wallmapu Wenuy agreement**. Tardigradia SpA cascade funds **four parallel trust streams** — Lafkenche Marine Trust, Wenteche River Trust, Pewenche Glacier/Watershed Trust (DOC-28), Nagche Forest Trust.

| Trust | Sub-Group | Capital Source | Governance | Primary Mandate |
|-------|-----------|----------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Marine Trust (MT)** | Lafkenche | 15% equity (FBC) + 20% FCF (CDC) Alpha/Bravo coastal + Blue Carbon credits | Lafkenche Assembly + Machi Lafkenche + Kalku | Marine ecosystem restoration, artisanal fisheries sovereignty, blue carbon, navigation school |
| **River Trust (RT)** | Wenteche | 15% equity + 20% FCF Alpha-12/13/14/Bravo-6/7 + Water rights leasing | Wenteche Basin Councils + Machi Ko + Ngenpin | River restoration, caudal ecológico cultural, dam removal fund, wetland recovery |
| **Glacier/Watershed Trust (GWT)** | Pewenche | **See DOC-28** — GPF, WT, PRF, CCF, HET | Butalmapu Pehuenche | Glacier protection, pewén restoration, watershed integrity, high-Andes health |
| **Forest Trust (FT)** | Nagche | 15% equity + 20% FCF Alpha-12/13/14/Bravo-6/7 + VERRA IFM carbon credits | Nagche Territorial Council + Machi Mapu + CONAF co-chair | Native forest restoration (200k ha), sustainable management, cultural fire, NTFP economy |

**Total Trust Capital Target (Year 10)**: **US$1.8B** across 4 trusts — **leveraged 4:1 via EIB/CPTPP/IRA 45X**.
