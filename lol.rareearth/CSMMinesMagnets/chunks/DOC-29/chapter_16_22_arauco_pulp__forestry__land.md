## 2.2 Arauco Pulp & Forestry — Landscape Transformation

### 2.2.1 Plantation Expansion on Mapuche Land (1974–2024)

| Metric | 1974 | 1990 | 2010 | 2024 | Change |
|--------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| **Pinus radiata (ha)** | 180,000 | 650,000 | 1,100,000 | 1,350,000 | **+650%** |
| **Eucalyptus spp. (ha)** | 20,000 | 120,000 | 350,000 | 480,000 | **+2,300%** |
| **Native Forest (ha)** | 1,800,000 | 1,400,000 | 950,000 | 720,000 | **-60%** |
| **Mapuche Land in Plantations** | 50,000 | 180,000 | 320,000 | **400,000** | **+700%** |

**Arauco S.A. (Celulosa Arauco y Constitución) — Mapuche Land Interface**:
- **Total Plantations**: **1.1M ha** (Chile + Argentina + Brazil + Uruguay)
- **Chile Plantations**: **680,000 ha** — **~280,000 ha on Mapuche-claimed territory**
- **Annual Harvest**: **18M m³** — **~40% from Mapuche-claimed land**
- **Pulp Production**: **2.1 Mt/yr** (Valdivia, Nueva Aldea, Licancel, Horcones)

### 2.2.2 Pulp Mill Impacts — Biobío & Coastal Watersheds

| Mill | Capacity | Watershed | Discharge | Key Pollutants | Mapuche Impact |
|------|----------|-----------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Nueva Aldea** | 1.6 Mt/yr | Itata/Biobío | 12 m³/s | AOX 0.8 kg/t, COD 15 kg/t, Color 250 Pt-Co | **Wenteche: river color, fish kills, ceremonial water** |
| **Valdivia** | 550 kt/yr | Cruces/Valdivia | 8 m³/s | AOX 0.6 kg/t, Dioxins 0.05 pg TEQ/L | **Lafkenche: wetland contamination, bird die-offs** |
| **Licancel** | 320 kt/yr | Mataquito | 4 m³/s | AOX 1.2 kg/t, Chlorate 2.5 mg/L | **Nagche: irrigation water, agricultural contamination** |
| **Horcones** | 450 kt/yr | Biobío | 6 m³/s | AOX 0.7 kg/t, TRS 3.5 kg/t | **Wenteche/Nagche: odor, air quality, river foam** |

**Cruces River Ecological Collapse (2004–2007) — Precedent Case**:
- **Cause**: Valdivia pulp mill black liquor spill + chronic AOX discharge
- **Impact**: **90% loss of *Egeria densa* (submerged macrophyte)** → **black-necked swan population 5,000 → 400**
- **Legal Outcome**: **Supreme Court 2013 — Arauco fined US$4.2M, mandated tertiary treatment**
- **Lafkenche Role**: **Key plaintiffs** — **Machi spiritual testimony admitted as evidence**
- **Current Status**: **Tertiary treatment operational (2018)** — **swan population recovering (1,200 in 2024)**

### 2.2.3 Native Forest Law 20.283 — Implementation Failure on Mapuche Land

| Indicator | National (2024) | Mapuche Territory | Gap |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|-----|
| **Native Forest Area** | 14.2M ha | **1.8M ha (claimed)** | 13% of national |
| **Management Plans Approved** | 4,200 (2.8M ha) | **380 (180,000 ha)** | **6.4% of Mapuche area** |
| **Restoration Bonuses Paid** | US$12M/yr | **US$480k/yr (4%)** | **96% shortfall** |
| **Sustainable Harvest Certificates** | 180 | **12 (all Nagche)** | **0 for Lafkenche/Wenteche** |
| **CONAF Technical Support** | 45 engineers | **2 assigned to Mapuche territory** | **1 per 900,000 ha** |

**Structural Barriers Identified (U. Austral + Mapuche NGOs 2023)**:
1. **Title Requirement**: Management plans require **clean title** — **70% Mapuche Lof lack title**
2. **Technical Cost**: **US$15k/plan** — **no community fund, INDAP excludes forestry**
3. **Cultural Mismatch**: Plans require **even-aged silviculture** — **incompatible with Mapuche *kutral* (fire) management**
4. **Benefit Capture**: **Large forestry companies** access bonuses via **subcontracting simulado**

**Nagche Forest Restoration Proposal (CCF-Funded)**:
- **Target**: **200,000 ha by 2040** — **native forest recovery on degraded plantation land**
- **Species**: **Nothofagus (roble, raulí, coigüe), Araucaria (pewén), Podocarpus, Weinmannia**
- **Management**: **Nagche *kutral* (cultural fire) + enrichment planting + natural regeneration**
- **Financing**: **CCF US$25M + CONAF matching + carbon credits (VERRA IFM methodology)**
- **Governance**: **Nagche Territorial Council + CONAF co-management board**
