## 4.2 Lafkenche Marine Trust (MT) — Detailed Design

### 4.2.1 Capitalization Schedule

| Year | FBC Equity (15%) | CDC FCF (20%) | Blue Carbon Credits | Total Cumulative |
|------|------------------|---------------|---------------------|------------------|
| 1–2 (Construction) | US$45M | US$0 | US$0 | US$45M |
| 3–5 (Ramp-up) | US$120M | US$80M | US$15M | US$260M |
| 6–10 (Steady) | US$180M | US$320M | US$120M | **US$780M** |
| 11–20 (Maturity) | US$200M | US$600M | US$300M | **US$1.88B** |

**Blue Carbon Revenue Model**:
- **Kelp Forest Restoration**: **2,500 ha by Year 10** — **VERRA VM0033 methodology**
- **Sequestration Rate**: **12 tCO₂e/ha/yr** (Macrocystis pyrifera, validated U. Concepción)
- **Carbon Price**: **US$25/tCO₂e (2024) → US$65/tCO₂e (2035) — CPTPP Art 14 linkage**
- **Annual Revenue (Year 10)**: **2,500 ha × 12 tCO₂e × US$45 = US$1.35M/yr**
- **Reinvestment**: **70% to restoration, 30% to community distribution**

### 4.2.2 Governance Structure

```
MARINE TRUST (MT) — GOVERNANCE CHARTER

BOARD OF TRUSTEES (9 members):
├── 4 Lafkenche Assembly Delegates (rotating annually, gender parity)
├── 1 Machi Lafkenche (permanent, spiritual veto)
├── 1 Kalku Lafkenche (permanent, navigation veto)
├── 1 Independent Marine Scientist (appointed by Assembly, U. Concepción/UMAG)
├── 1 Tardigradia Representative (non-voting, observer)
└── 1 CONADI/Subpesca Observer (non-voting)

TECHNICAL COMMITTEES:
├── Marine Ecology Committee (kelp, benthic, fisheries, mammals)
├── Blue Carbon Committee (VERRA compliance, MRV, market access)
├── Navigation & Safety Committee (wampo school, SAR, traditional knowledge)
├── Cultural Heritage Committee (sites ceremoniales, Ngen Lafken, oral history)
└── Finance & Investment Committee (portfolio, grants, community distribution)

DECISION RULES:
├── Operational: Majority Board (5/9)
├── Strategic (capital allocation >US$5M): **2/3 Board + Machi Lafkenche consent**
├── Veto Matters (new concessions, boundary changes, spiritual sites): **Machi Lafkenche OR Kalku Lafkenche veto = binding**
├── Emergency (oil spill, HAB, tsunami): **Machi Lafkenche + 1 Board = immediate action authority**

DISTRIBUTION POLICY (Annual):
├── 40% Ecosystem Restoration (kelp, wetlands, benthic, water quality)
├── 25% Community Programs (navigation school, health, education, elder care)
├── 20% Cultural Revitalization (Mapudungun marítimo, ceremonies, oral history)
├── 10% Trust Capital Preservation (endowment growth)
└── 5% Solidarity Fund (Wallmapu Wenuy redistribution)
```
