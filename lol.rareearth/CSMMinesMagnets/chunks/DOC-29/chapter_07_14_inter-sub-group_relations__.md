## 1.4 Inter-Sub-Group Relations & Collective Governance

### 1.4.1 Historical Alliances & Trade

| Axis | Sub-Groups | Exchange | Mechanism |
|------|------------|----------|-----------|
| **Andes-Valley** | Pewenche ↔ Wenteche/Nagche | Pewén (piñón) ↔ maize, quinoa, textiles, salt | **Trariwe** (seasonal trade fairs at mountain passes) |
| **Valley-Coast** | Wenteche ↔ Lafkenche | Freshwater fish, agricultural products ↔ kelp, shellfish, marine salt | **River navigation** (wampo) + **coastal trails** |
| **Andes-Coast** | Pewenche ↔ Lafkenche | Volcanic obsidian, pewén wood ↔ marine resources | **Long-distance trade** via Wenteche intermediaries |

**Trariwe System** — **Pre-colonial Mapuche economic integration**:
- **Frequency**: Annual (summer) at passes: **Pino Hachado (Pewenche-Nagche), Rahue (Wenteche-Lafkenche)**
- **Volume**: Estimated **500–800 pack animals** per fair
- **Currency**: **Barter + pewén seeds as value standard**
- **Governance**: **Neutral ground** — enforced by Machi of participating sub-groups

### 1.4.2 Contemporary Coordination — Mapuche-Sur Parliament

**Wallmapu Wenuy (Mapuche-Sur Parliament) — Established 2018**:
- **Composition**: **4 Sub-Group Assemblies** (Lafkenche, Wenteche, Pewenche, Nagche) — each sends 5 delegates
- **Total Delegates**: 20 (gender parity mandated)
- **Permanent Secretariat**: Temuco (Nagche territory)
- **Decision Rule**: **Sub-group consensus required** — no majority vote across sub-groups
- **Mandate**: **Inter-sub-group coordination, national/international representation, Aegis-Forge interface**

**Aegis-Forge Interface Protocol (2024 Agreement)**:
1. **Sub-group consent first** — each sub-group processes per their protocol
2. **Wallmapu Wenuy ratification** — only if all 4 sub-groups consent
3. **Veto power** — any sub-group veto = project blocked for that territory
4. **Benefit distribution** — per sub-group trust (Lafkenche: Marine Trust, Wenteche: River Trust, Pewenche: GPF/WT, Nagche: Forest Trust)
