## 7.2 Lafkenche — Blue Economy Diversification

### 7.2.1 Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (MTA) — Lafkenche-Led

**System Design (Lafkenche WCS Zone: Artisanal Fisheries Priority + Blue Carbon)**:
```
MTA MODULE — 10 HA COASTAL (LAFKENCHE AMRB)

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TROPHIC LEVELS                                                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LEVEL 1 (Extractive): Macrocystis pyrifera (kelp) — 2 ha      │
│    → Nutrient uptake (N, P), carbon sequestration, habitat     │
│    → Harvest: 120 t/yr (wet) → biostimulant, food, biofuel     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LEVEL 2 (Filter Feeders): Chorito (Mytilus chilensis) — 3 ha  │
│    → Filtration: 50,000 L/ha/day, water clarity                │
│    → Harvest: 180 t/yr → premium export (EU/US/Asia)           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LEVEL 3 (Deposit Feeders): Piure (Pyura chilensis) — 1 ha     │
│    → Biodeposit processing, sediment oxygenation               │
│    → Harvest: 90 t/yr → high-value domestic/export             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LEVEL 4 (Finfish): Pejerrey (Odontesthes regia) — 2 ha        │
│    → Polyculture with kelp (oxygen, shade), fed on bycatch     │
│    → Harvest: 60 t/yr → local market, cultural consumption     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LEVEL 5 (Decomposers): Sea cucumber (Athyonidium) — 1 ha      │
│    → Sediment cleaning, nutrient recycling                     │
│    → Harvest: 15 t/yr → Asian premium market                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LEVEL 6 (Cultural): Navigation corridor + ceremonial space    │
│    → Wampo transit, Machi Lafkenche access, youth training     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Economic Model (Per 10-ha Module, Year 5 Mature)**:
| Revenue Stream | Volume | Price | Annual Revenue | Lafkenche Share |
|----------------|--------|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Kelp (biostimulant)** | 120 t | US$450/t | US$54,000 | 100% (co-op) |
| **Chorito (export)** | 180 t | US$3,200/t | US$576,000 | 85% (fair trade) |
| **Piure** | 90 t | US$1,800/t | US$162,000 | 100% |
| **Pejerrey** | 60 t | US$4,500/t | US$270,000 | 100% (local + cultural) |
| **Sea cucumber** | 15 t | US$12,000/t | US$180,000 | 70% (joint venture) |
| **Blue Carbon (kelp)** | 24 tCO₂e | US$55/t | US$1,320 | 100% MT |
| **Total** | — | — | **US$1.24M** | **US$1.12M (90%)** |

**Scaling Target**: **50 modules (500 ha) by Year 10** — **US$62M/yr revenue, 600 direct jobs**.
