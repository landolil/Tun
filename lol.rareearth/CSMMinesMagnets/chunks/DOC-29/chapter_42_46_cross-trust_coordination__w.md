### 4.6.2 Solidarity Fund Formula

$$\text{Vulnerability Index}_i = 0.4 \cdot \text{CII}_i + 0.3 \cdot \frac{1}{\text{Trust Capital}_i} + 0.2 \cdot \text{Health Gap}_i + 0.1 \cdot \text{Cultural Erosion}_i$$

$$\text{Solidarity Allocation}_i = \frac{\text{Vulnerability Index}_i}{\sum_j \text{Vulnerability Index}_j} \times \text{Total Solidarity Fund}$$
