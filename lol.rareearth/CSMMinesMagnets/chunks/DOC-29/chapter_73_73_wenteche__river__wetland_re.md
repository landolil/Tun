### 7.3.2 Geothermal Direct Use — Wenteche Valley (Copahue-Callaqui)

```

1. POWER (Binary ORC): 3 MWe → Grid (synthetic inertia) + Local
   ↓ Brine 120°C

   ↓ Water 85°C

   ↓ Water 60°C

   ↓ Water 45°C

   ↓ Water 35°C

   ↓ Water 25°C → Reinjection
```
