**Recycling Plant Design (Nagche FT + Tardigradia JV)**:
```
REE RECYCLING HUB — CONCEPCIÓN/TALCAHUANO (Bravo-5 + Alpha-13 Integration)

CAPACITY: 25,000 t/yr feed → 1,800 t/yr REO (NdPr 60%, DyTb 15%, CeLa 25%)

PROCESS FLOW:
1. PREPROCESSING (Bravo-5 Robotics):
   ├── Robotic disassembly (e-waste, motors, turbines)
   ├── Magnet harvesting (NdFeB separation)
   ├── Battery dismantling (Li-ion, NiMH)
   └── Shredding + sorting (AI vision)

2. HYDROMETALLURGY (Alpha-13 Stage 8 Adapted):
   ├── Acid leach (H₂SO₄, 2M, 80°C) — magnet fraction
   ├── Bio-leach (Bravo-7 consortium) — catalyst/tailings fraction
   ├── Solvent extraction (SX): 8-stage mixer-settler
   │   ├── Circuit 1: La/Ce removal (D2EHPA)
   │   ├── Circuit 2: Nd/Pr separation (HEH/EHP)
   │   ├── Circuit 3: Dy/Tb purification (Cyanex 572)
   │   └── Circuit 4: Sc/Y/Lu polishing (TBP)
   ├── Precipitation: Oxalate → Calcination → Oxide
   └── Metal Reduction: Molten salt electrolysis (Nd, Pr, Dy, Tb)

3. PRODUCT FINISHING:
   ├── Oxide (4N) → Catalyst, polishing, glass
   ├── Metal (99.9%) → Alloy (NdFeB, SmCo, NdFeB-Dy)
   └── Master Alloy (Dy-Tb-Co) → Grain boundary diffusion

OUTPUTS:
├── NdPr Oxide: 950 t/yr → CSM + export
├── DyTb Oxide: 270 t/yr → CSM (high-temp magnets) + defense
├── CeLa Oxide: 480 t/yr → Catalyst (DOC-25 chain)
├── Sc/Y/Lu Oxide: 85 t/yr → Specialty (DOC-24 chain)
└── Byproducts: Fe/Al hydroxide → cement, Cu/Au/Ag → refinery
```
