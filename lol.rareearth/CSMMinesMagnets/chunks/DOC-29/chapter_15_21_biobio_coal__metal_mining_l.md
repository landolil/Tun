## 2.1 Biobío Coal & Metal Mining Legacy — The Industrial Sacrifice Zone

### 2.1.1 Lota-Schwager Coal Basin — 140 Years of Extraction

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Operational Period** | 1852–1997 (Chiflón 1–4, Mina El Chiflón) | ENACAR archives |
| **Total Coal Extracted** | **42 million tonnes** | Sernageomin |
| **Peak Production** | 1.2 Mt/yr (1940s) | ENACAR |
| **Workforce Peak** | 8,500 miners (1950) | Union archives |
| **Mapuche Communities Affected** | 12 Lof (Nagche/Wenteche) | CONADI |
| **Area Undermined** | 8,500 ha (subsidence zone) | Sernageomin geotechnical |

**Environmental Legacy (Sernageomin + U. Concepción 2023)**:
- **Acid Mine Drainage (AMD)**: **15 active seeps** — pH 2.8–3.5, Fe 450 mg/L, SO₄ 2,800 mg/L, Al 85 mg/L, Mn 45 mg/L
- **Subsidence Damage**: **2,300 ha** with >0.5m surface deformation — **cracked foundations, drained wetlands, altered drainage**
- **Coal Waste Piles**: **12 major piles** (8.5 Mt total) — **spontaneous combustion risk, leachate to Biobío tributaries**
- **Groundwater Contamination**: **15 km² plume** — **As 0.12 mg/L (std 0.01), Fe 15 mg/L, Mn 8 mg/L** — **affects 8 Lof water sources**

**Health Impact (Lota Hospital + U. Chile School of Public Health 2022)**:
| Indicator | Lota/Mapuche | National | Ratio |
|-----------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **COPD Prevalence** | 18.5% | 6.2% | **3.0×** |
| **Lung Cancer (age-std)** | 42/100k | 24/100k | **1.75×** |
| **Cardiovascular Mortality** | 285/100k | 198/100k | **1.44×** |
| **Birth Defects** | 4.8% | 2.9% | **1.66×** |
| **Heavy Metals (hair)** | As 3.2, Pb 8.5, Cd 1.2 μg/g | As 0.8, Pb 2.1, Cd 0.3 | **2.5–4×** |

**Remediation Status**: **US$45M allocated (2018–2028)** — **<US$8M executed (2024)** — **no Mapuche participation in design**.

**Deposition on Mapuche Territory (U. Concepción CMAQ Model 2023)**:
- **SO₂ Deposition**: **1.8–3.2 kg/ha/yr** (Nagche/Wenteche agricultural land) — **exceeds critical load (1.5 kg/ha/yr) for native forest**
- **Hg Deposition**: **12–18 μg/m²/yr** — **bioaccumulation in pejerrey, trucha, human hair**
- **PM2.5 Annual Avg**: **28–35 μg/m³** (Nagche communities) — **WHO guideline 5 μg/m³, Chile std 20 μg/m³**

**Coronel Mapuche Health Study (2021–2024, n=1,200)**:
- **Asthma (children 6–14)**: **28%** vs national 12%
- **Low Birth Weight**: **14%** vs national 7%
- **Neurodevelopmental Delay**: **OR 2.3** (95% CI 1.6–3.4) associated with Hg hair >2 μg/g
