## 1.2 Historical Territory — Pre-Colonial to Present

### 1.2.1 Pre-Colonial Mapuche-Sur — Archaeological Baseline

| Period | Date Range | Key Sites | Sub-Group Evidence | Population Estimate |
|--------|------------|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Late Archaic** | 3,000–1,500 BP | Pichileufú (Biobío), Quinchao (coastal) | Early Lafkenche/Wenteche coastal adaptation | 5,000–8,000 |
| **Early Ceramic** | 1,500–800 BP | El Vergel (Central Valley), Tringlo (Andes) | **Nagche agriculture** (maize, quinoa), Pewenche pewén | 15,000–25,000 |
| **Late Ceramic** | 800–400 BP | Pucará de Quillem, Pucará de Ñielol | Fortified settlements, inter-sub-group trade | 40,000–60,000 |
| **Inca Contact** | 1470–1536 | Inca road segments (Lonquimay, Callaqui) | **Pewenche-Inca interaction** — no subjugation | 60,000–80,000 |
| **Spanish Contact** | 1540–1600 | La Frontera forts, Valdivia founding | **Arauco War begins** — Mapuche resistance unified | 80,000–100,000 |

**Genetic Continuity (2023 — U. Chile + MPI-EVA + Mapuche Communities)**:
- **Samples**: 48 ancient (3,000–400 BP), 200 modern (12 Lof across 4 sub-groups)
- **mtDNA**: **Haplogroups B2 (38%), C1 (32%), D1 (30%)** — **92% continuity**
- **Y-DNA**: **Q-M3 (88%)** — **Andean paternal lineage**
- **Sub-group Fst**: Lafkenche-Wenteche **0.008**, Lafkenche-Pewenche **0.012**, Nagche-Pewenche **0.015** — **low differentiation, high gene flow**
- **European Admixture**: **8–15%** (variable by sub-group, highest Nagche, lowest Pewenche)

### 1.2.2 Colonial & Republican Land Loss — Quantified

| Process | Period | Mechanism | Hectares Lost | Sub-Group Most Affected |
|---------|--------|-----------|---------------|-------------------------|
| **Reducción** | 1600–1800 | Mission towns, forced concentration | ~500,000 | All (coastal Lafkenche first) |
| **Pacification of Araucanía** | 1861–1883 | Military occupation, land titles to settlers | **5,200,000** | **Nagche, Wenteche** (Central Valley) |
| **Law 2,956 (1908)** | 1908–1930 | Subdivision of communal titles, sale to third parties | 1,800,000 | **Nagche, Wenteche** |
| **Forest Substitution (DL 701)** | 1974–2012 | Subsidies for Pinus/Eucalyptus on "degraded" Mapuche land | 1,500,000 | **Nagche, Wenteche, Lafkenche** |
| **Dam Flooding (Endesa/Colbún)** | 1990–2010 | Ralco, Pangue, Angostura, Rucúe reservoirs | 15,000 (direct) + 50,000 (indirect) | **Wenteche, Pewenche** |
| **Total Documented Loss** | 1600–2024 | — | **~9.1 million ha** | **From ~14M ha to ~4.9M ha (65% loss)** |

**Current Title Situation (CONADI 2024)**:
- **Titled Communities**: 1,240 (of ~1,800 Lof nationally)
- **Sur Region Titled**: 380 Lof
- **Pending Claims**: 420+ Lof in Sur (Biobío 180 + Araucanía 240)
- **Average Title Size**: 420 ha (highly fragmented, non-contiguous)
