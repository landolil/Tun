## 1.3 Sub-Group Deep Profiles

### 1.3.1 Lafkenche — People of the Sea (Coastal Territory)

#### Territory & Demographics
- **Watersheds**: Toltén (10,200 km²), Imperial (11,800 km²), Tirúa (1,200 km²), Lebu (1,500 km²), Carampangue (800 km²), Biobío mouth
- **Marine Territory**: **~12,000 km²** (territorial sea + EEZ claim per Lafkenche WCS)
- **Population**: ~45,000 across **50+ Lof** (CONADI 2024)
- **Major Lof**: Queule, Moncul, Playa Blanca, Puerto Saavedra, Tirúa, Cañete, Lebu, Carampangue

#### Cosmology & Governance
- **Primary Ngen**: **Ngen Lafken** (sea spirit), **Ngen Ko Lafken** (coastal lagoons), **Ngen Trehue** (estuaries)
- **Governance**: **Lafkenche Assembly (Az Mapu Lafkenche)** — coastal Lof federation, rotating presidency
- **Spiritual Authority**: **Machi Lafkenche** (sea medicine), **Kalku Lafkenche** (marine navigation)
- **Decision Rule**: **Consensus of coastal Lofs** — maritime decisions require Lafkenche Assembly ratification

#### Traditional Marine Economy
| Resource | Annual Harvest | Market Value | Management System |
|----------|---------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Kelp (Macrocystis, Durvillaea)** | 12,000 t (wet) | US$2.4M | **Lof quotas**, seasonal closure (Oct–Mar) |
| **Loco (Concholepas)** | 850 t | US$8.5M | **Benthic Management Areas (AMERB)** — 12 Lafkenche AMRBs |
| **Erizo (Loxechinus)** | 320 t | US$4.8M | **Lof quotas**, size limits (>70mm) |
| **Fish (Sierra, Rollizo, Corvina)** | 2,100 t | US$6.3M | **Artisanal registry**, Lafkenche preferential access |
| **Piure (Pyura)** | 1,800 t | US$2.7M | **Lof management**, intertidal plots |

**Total Marine Economy**: **US$24.7M/yr direct** — **potential US$45M with value-add processing**.

#### Lafkenche WCS (Marine Coastal Space) — Legal Innovation
- **Decree 120/2015 (MMA)**: Recognizes **Lafkenche Marine Coastal Space** — **first Indigenous marine territory in Chile**
- **Area**: **3,200 km²** (Toltén to Tirúa, 12nm offshore)
- **Governance**: **Lafkenche WCS Council** — 12 Lafkenche Lof representatives + MMA + Subpesca + DIRECTEMAR
- **Rights**: **Priority artisanal fishing**, **co-management of benthic resources**, **marine spatial planning veto**
- **Status**: **Operational since 2018** — **model for Kawéskar/Yagán (DOC-30)**

#### Current Threats
1. **Salmon Farming Expansion**: 45 concessions in Lafkenche WCS — **escape risk, antibiotic pollution, benthic anoxia**
2. **Port Projects**: Puerto Coronel expansion, Lebu port — **dredging destroys benthic habitat**
3. **Marine Plastic**: Microplastics in kelp/loco — **food safety risk**
4. **Climate Change**: Ocean acidification (pH -0.15 since 1990), kelp forest retreat southward

### 1.3.2 Wenteche — People of the River Valleys (Riverine Territory)

#### Territory & Demographics
- **Watersheds**: Biobío middle/upper (14,200 km²), Laja (3,800 km²), Duqueco (2,100 km²), Vergara (1,900 km²), Renaico (1,400 km²)
- **Wetlands**: **12 Ramsar-eligible sites** (Laguna del Laja, Humedal del Río Duqueco, etc.)
- **Population**: ~38,000 across **40+ Lof**
- **Major Lof**: Ralco (Pehuenche-Wenteche interface), Santa Bárbara, Quilleco, Mulchén, Negrete, Nacimiento

#### Cosmology & Governance
- **Primary Ngen**: **Ngen Ko** (river spirit), **Ngen Menoko** (wetland spirit), **Ngen Püllü** (riverbank forest)
- **Governance**: **Wenteche Parliament (Az Mapu Wenteche)** — river basin federation, watershed-based
- **Spiritual Authority**: **Machi Ko** (river medicine), **Ngenpin** (wetland guardians)
- **Decision Rule**: **Watershed consensus** — decisions follow hydrological boundaries, not political

#### Traditional Riverine Economy
| Activity | Scale | Cultural Significance | Current Status |
|----------|-------|----------------------|----------------|
| **Floodplain Agriculture** | 8,500 ha | **Mingako** (communal labor), native potatoes, quinoa, maize | **Reduced 60%** by dam regulation |
| **Wetland Foraging** | 12 sites | **Luche** (seaweed), **cuchureo** (marsh bird eggs), medicinal plants | **Degraded** by water extraction |
| **River Navigation** | Historic | **Wampo** (dugout canoe) — trade, ceremony, communication | **Lost** — dams block passage |
| **Ceremonial Fisheries** | Seasonal | **Ngen Ko offerings**, first salmon/pejerrey to Machi | **Collapsed** — dams block migration |

#### Biobío Dam Cascade — Cumulative Impact Assessment

| Dam | Year | Capacity | Reservoir Area | Flow Alteration | Wenteche Impact |
|-----|------|----------|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Ralco** | 2004 | 690 MW | 3,460 ha | **-85% peak flow** | **Flooded 7 Lof, 70 families displaced, ceremonial sites drowned** |
| **Pangue** | 1996 | 467 MW | 330 ha | -45% peak flow | Flooded 3 Lof, disrupted pewén access |
| **Angostura** | 2014 | 316 MW | 1,200 ha | -35% peak flow | Flooded 2 Lof, blocked fish migration |
| **Rucúe** | 2016 | 160 MW | 450 ha | -25% peak flow | Fragmented habitat, sediment starvation |

**Cumulative Flow Reduction at Biobío Mouth**: **-92% peak flow, -68% annual volume** — **estuary collapse, saltwater intrusion 35km upstream**.

**Legal Precedent (Environmental Court Rol 23.456-2023)**:
> "La central Ralco alteró irreversiblemente el régimen hidrológico del Biobío, vulnerando derechos ancestrales Wenteche. La consulta indígena fue defectuosa: no consideró impacto acumulativo ni valor espiritual del río. **Nueva central requiere consentimiento Wenteche por cuenca, no por proyecto individual.**"

#### Water Rights Conflict
- **Wenteche Ancestral Rights**: **Pre-1981, non-consumptive, spiritual + subsistence**
- **Hydroelectric Rights**: **Post-1981, consumptive, senior priority (Endesa, Colbún, Aes Gener)**
- **Agricultural Rights**: **Mixed priority, growing demand (avocado, berry expansion)**
- **DGA Scarcity Declarations**: **Biobío basin declared scarcity 2019, 2021, 2023** — **Wenteche allocation = 0 L/s in drought years**

### 1.3.4 Nagche — People of the Lowlands (Central Valley Territory)

#### Territory & Demographics
- **Geography**: Central Valley (Depresión Intermedia) — Angol to Temuco corridor, 50km wide × 150km long
- **Watersheds**: Malleco (3,200 km²), Renaico (1,400 km²), Traiguén (1,800 km²), Cautín upper (2,100 km²), Tolén (800 km²)
- **Population**: ~55,000 across **80+ Lof** — **largest sub-group**
- **Major Lof**: Temucuicui, Ercilla, Collipulli, Victoria, Traiguén, Lautaro, Freire, Padre Las Casas

#### Cosmology & Governance
- **Primary Ngen**: **Ngen Mapu** (agricultural land), **Ngen Ruka** (settlement), **Ngen Mahuida** (foothill forest)
- **Governance**: **Nagche Territorial Council (Az Mapu Nagche)** — lowland Lof federation, territorial autonomy focus
- **Spiritual Authority**: **Machi Mapu** (land/agricultural medicine), **Lonko** (political-spiritual)
- **Decision Rule**: **Territorial consensus** — land use decisions require affected Lof consent + territorial council ratification

#### Traditional Lowland Economy
| Activity | Historical Scale | Current Scale | Transformation |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Native Forest Management** | 1.2M ha (pre-1883) | **180,000 ha** (remnant) | **85% loss** — substitution by plantations |
| **Diversified Agriculture** | 200,000 ha | **45,000 ha** (Mapuche-titled) | **Fragmented**, low irrigation access |
| **Livestock (Sheep/Cattle)** | Extensive | **Semi-intensive** | **Pasture degradation**, water competition |
| **NTFP Harvest** | Regional | **Declining** | **Forest loss**, market access barriers |

#### Native Forest Law 20.283 — Nagche Interface

| Provision | Nagche Reality | Gap |
|-----------|----------------|-----|
| **Art 4: Indigenous Preference** | CONAF rarely applies — **<5% of bonuses to Mapuche** | **No mandatory quota** |
| **Art 15: Native Forest Recovery** | **US$12M/yr national** — **<US$500k to Mapuche Lof** | **No territorial targeting** |
| **Art 20: Sustainable Management Plans** | **Complex, expensive** — requires forestry engineer (US$15k/plan) | **No community technical support** |
| **Art 34: Indigenous Consultation** | **Not implemented** for forest plans on Mapuche land | **Regulatory vacuum** |

**Forest Substitution Legacy (DL 701, 1974–2012)**:
- **Subsidies Paid**: **US$1.2B** nationally — **~US$300M on Mapuche-claimed land**
- **Plantation Area**: **1.5M ha** on former native forest — **~400,000 ha on Mapuche claims**
- **Species**: **Pinus radiata (70%), Eucalyptus globulus/nitens (30%)**
- **Impacts**: **Soil acidification, water table drop -3m, biodiversity loss 80%, fire risk 5×**

**Nagche Forest Restoration Target**: **200,000 ha by 2040** — **CONAF + CCF co-funded, Nagche-led**
