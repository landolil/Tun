**Water Rights Leasing Revenue**:
- **Mechanism**: **Lease ancestral water rights (non-consumptive) to agricultural/industrial users** — **market rate + cultural premium**
- **Volume**: **1,200 L/s total ancestral rights** — **lease 400 L/s at US$0.15/L/s/day = US$22M/yr**
- **Legal Basis**: **Water Code Art 129 bis + Wenteche Protocol Art 3** — **priority rights recognized**

### 4.3.2 Governance Structure

```
RIVER TRUST (RT) — GOVERNANCE CHARTER

BOARD OF TRUSTEES (11 members):
├── 5 Basin Council Representatives (1 per basin: Biobío, Laja, Duqueco, Vergara, Renaico)
├── 2 Machi Ko (Biobío basin + Laja/Duqueco basin)
├── 2 Ngenpin (wetland guardians, appointed by Basin Councils)
├── 1 Independent Hydrologist (U. Chile/Centro de Estudios Avanzados)
└── 1 Tardigradia Representative (non-voting)

BASIN COUNCILS (5 — one per watershed):
├── Composition: All Lof in basin + Machi Ko + Ngenpin
├── Authority: Define CEC, approve projects, appoint sentinels
├── Meetings: Monthly (ordinary), Emergency (any sentinel trigger)

TECHNICAL COMMITTEES:
├── Hydrology & Caudal Ecológico Committee (MHIC maintenance, CEC modeling)
├── Water Quality Committee (AMD, thermal, nutrients, emerging contaminants)
├── River Restoration Committee (dam removal, floodplain reconnection, fish passage)
├── Wetland Restoration Committee (Menoko recovery, peatland rewetting)
└── Cultural Flows Committee (Ngen Ko sites, ceremonial access, wampo navigation)

DECISION RULES:
├── Operational: Majority Board (6/11)
├── CEC Modification: **Unanimity Basin Council + Machi Ko consent**
├── Dam Removal/Decommission: **2/3 Board + all affected Basin Councils + Machi Ko**
├── Water Rights Lease: **Basin Council approval (2/3) + Machi Ko consent**
├── Emergency (spill, fish kill, drought): **Machi Ko + 1 Basin Council = immediate authority**

DISTRIBUTION POLICY (Annual):
├── 35% River Restoration (dam removal fund, floodplain, fish passage, sediment)
├── 25% Caudal Ecológico Cultural (CEC enforcement, monitoring, legal defense)
├── 20% Wetland & Aquifer Restoration (Menoko, peatlands, recharge zones)
├── 10% Community Programs (water sovereignty education, health, agriculture)
├── 5% Cultural Revitalization (ceremonial access, wampo building, oral history)
└── 5% Solidarity Fund
```
