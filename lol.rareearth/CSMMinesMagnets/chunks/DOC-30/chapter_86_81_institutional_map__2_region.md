## 8.1 Institutional Map — 2 Regions, Multiple Sovereignties

### 8.1.1 Chilean Administrative Structure

| Region | Capital | SEREMI Minería | SEREMI MMA | SEREMI Economía | GORE | Kawéskar/Yagán Presence |
|--------|---------|----------------|------------|----------------|------|------------------------|
| **Aysén** | Coyhaique | SEREMI XI | SEREMI XI | SEREMI XI | GORE Aysén | **Kawéskar** (Puerto Edén, Tortel, Andrade) |
| **Magallanes** | Punta Arenas | SEREMI XII | SEREMI XII | SEREMI XII | GORE Magallanes | **Kawéskar** (Natales, Punta Arenas) + **Yagán** (Puerto Williams) |

### 8.1.2 Antarctic & Maritime Authorities — Overlapping Jurisdiction

| Authority | Mandate | Kawéskar/Yagán Interface |
|-----------|---------|--------------------------|
| **INACH** (Instituto Antártico Chileno) | Antarctic science, logistics, Madrid Protocol compliance | **MOU 2023** — Yagán/Kawéskar as logistics partners, TK co-design |
| **DIRECTEMAR** (Dirección General del Territorio Marítimo) | Maritime concessions, navigation safety, pollution, SAR | **WCS Council + CCY veto** on concessions; **Sentinels = inspectors** |
| **Subpesca** | Fisheries, aquaculture, AMRBs | **5 Kawéskar AMRBs + 2 Yagán AMRBs** — priority access |
| **MMA** (Ministerio Medio Ambiente) | WCS (Decree 120), MPA, climate, biodiversity | **Kawéskar WCS Council + Yagán CCY** as co-managers |
| **CONAF** | National parks, biosphere reserves, native forest | **CHBR co-management (Yagán)**, **Kawéskar WCS islands** |
| **Sernageomin** | Mining concessions, safety, closure | **Alpha-17 consent = Yagán/Kawéskar veto** |
| **Cancillería** (Foreign Ministry) | Antarctic Treaty, CCAMLR, Madrid Protocol, ISA | **Kawéskar/Yagán as Consultative Parties** — observer status sought |

### 8.1.3 International Bodies — Binding on Chilean Austral Territory

| Body | Instrument | Relevance to Kawéskar/Yagán |
|------|------------|----------------------------|
| **ATS (Antarctic Treaty System)** | Treaty, Madrid Protocol, CCAMLR | **Chile = Consultative Party** — Kawéskar/Yagán seek **Observer status** |
| **CCAMLR** | Convention on Antarctic Marine Living Resources | **Toothfish/krill in WCS/Beagle** — Kawéskar/Yagán data providers |
| **ISA** (International Seabed Authority) | UNCLOS Part XI, seabed minerals | **Area beyond EEZ** — Kawéskar/Yagán as **Indigenous stakeholders** |
| **IMO** (International Maritime Org) | Polar Code, MARPOL, SOLAS | **Strait/Drake shipping** — Yagán/Kawéskar pilotage, SAR |
| **UNESCO** | World Heritage, Biosphere Reserves, UCH | **CHBR (Cape Horn)** — Yagán co-management model |
