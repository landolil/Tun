## 4.1 Trust Architecture — Two Peoples, Two Trusts, One Antarctic Gateway

**Principle**: **Kawéskar and Yagán maintain distinct trusts** reflecting distinct territories, cosmologies, and governance. **Antarctic Gateway Fund (AGF)** created as **joint venture** for shared logistics revenue.

| Trust | People | Capital Source | Governance | Primary Mandate |
|-------|--------|----------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Marine Territory Trust (MTT)** | Kawéskar | 15% FBC equity + 20% CDC FCF (Alpha-17, HIF, ports) + Blue Carbon credits | CECMK (5 communities + Kalku) | Kelp restoration, AMRB expansion, halakha culture, marine sentinels, blue carbon |
| **Yagán Heritage Trust (YHT)** | Yagán | 15% FBC equity + 20% CDC FCF (Alpha-17, HIF, Antarctic logistics) + Cape Horn tourism | CCY (3 families + Yekamush) | Language revitalization, awa navigation, Cape Horn stewardship, Antarctic gateway culture |
| **Antarctic Gateway Fund (AGF)** | **Joint (50/50)** | 10% FCF from Austral clusters (Alpha-15/16/17, Bravo-8/9) + Port/Logistics revenue | **Joint Board** (MTT Chair + YHT Chair + INACH + ENAP) | Puerto Williams/Punta Arenas logistics, Antarctic science support, tourism management, search & rescue |

**Total Trust Capital Target (Year 10)**: **US$1.2B** (MTT $550M + YHT $350M + AGF $300M)
