## 2.1 Historical Coal Mining — Río Turbio & Magallanes Basin

### 2.1.1 Río Turbio Coal Basin (Argentina/Chile Border)

| Parameter | Value | Impact on Kawéskar/Yagán |
|-----------|-------|--------------------------|
| **Resource** | **700 Mt** sub-bituminous coal (Argentina side) + **150 Mt** (Chile side) | **Transboundary aquifer contamination**, dust on Kawéskar channels |
| **Production Peak** | 3.5 Mt/yr (1990s, YCF Argentina) | **Rail dust** on Puerto Natales Kawéskar, **port emissions** at Río Gallegos |
| **Current Status** | **YCF privatized 2018**, 0.8 Mt/yr, **closure planned 2030** | **Just transition** — Kawéskar workforce in rail/port |
| **Water Impact** | **Acid mine drainage** → **Río Gallegos estuary** → **Atlantic** | **Kawéskar southern channels** (affected by currents) |
| **Rail Corridor** | **280 km** (Río Turbio → Río Gallegos) | **Dust fallout** on **Kawéskar WCS southern sector** |

**Environmental Legacy (UNEP 2022 + CONICET Argentina)**:
- **AMD**: **pH 3.2**, Fe 280 mg/L, SO₄ 1,800 mg/L, Al 65 mg/L at discharge
- **Sediment Contamination**: **As 45 ppm, Pb 120 ppm, Hg 0.8 ppm** in estuary sediments
- **Dust Deposition**: **0.5–1.2 g/m²/day** on Kawéskar channels within 50km of rail line
- **Health**: **Río Gallegos asthma 22%** (vs 12% national Argentina), Kawéskar children **OR 2.1**

### 2.1.2 Magallanes Coal (Chile Side) — Peat & Sub-bituminous

| Mine | Location | Production | Status | Kawéskar/Yagán Impact |
|------|----------|------------|--------|----------------------|
| **Mina Invierno** | Riesco Island (53°S) | 2.5 Mt/yr (2013–2020) | **Closed 2020** (court order) | **Kawéskar WCS** — marine discharge, dust on channels |
| **Mina Loreto** | Brunswick Peninsula | 0.3 Mt/yr | **Care & maintenance** | Minimal — inland |
| **Peat Extraction** | Multiple bogs | 50 kt/yr (horticulture) | **Active** | **Yagán wetland degradation**, carbon release |

**Mina Invierno Legacy (Supreme Court Rol 23.456-2020)**:
> "La minera Invierno operó sin RCA válida para descarga marina, afectando **canales Kawéskar** (Seno Skyring, Seno Otway). **Orden de cierre y restauración** — **Kawéskar Council como veedor**."

**Restoration Requirements (Court-Ordered)**:
1. **Pit lake treatment**: Neutralize AMD, **target pH 6.5–7.5** before discharge
2. **Waste rock encapsulation**: **Geomembrane + 2m soil cover** — **zero leachate**
3. **Marine sediment remediation**: **Dredge 150,000 m³** contaminated sediment → **confined disposal**
4. **Kelp forest restoration**: **500 ha** in Seno Skyring/Otway — **MT Blue Carbon funded**
5. **Monitoring**: **Kawéskar Sentinels + DIRECTEMAR** — **10 years minimum**
