## 7.4 Kelp Farming & Blue Carbon — Industrial Scale

### 7.4.1 Kelp Farming Technology — Subantarctic Optimization

| Parameter | Standard (Temperate) | Austral (Kawéskar/Yagán) | Advantage |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------------|-----------|
| **Species** | *Macrocystis pyrifera* | **M. pyrifera + D. antarctica + Lessonia spp.** | **Multi-species resilience** |
| **Growth Rate** | 30 cm/day (summer) | **45 cm/day** (long photoperiod, high nutrients) | **50% faster** |
| **Carbon Sequestration** | 12 tCO₂e/ha/yr | **18 tCO₂e/ha/yr** (colder water, higher alkalinity) | **50% higher** |
| **Seasonality** | 9 months | **11 months** (winter growth continues) | **22% longer** |
| **Storm Risk** | Moderate | **High** (80kt winds) | **Submerged longlines (15m depth)** — survival >95% |
| **Harvest Method** | Surface cutting | **Selective frond harvest** — **regenerative, no holdfast damage** | **Perennial crop** |

### 7.4.2 Industrial Kelp Farm Design — MTT/YHT

```
KELP FARM MODULE — AUSTRAL INDUSTRIAL (KFM-AI-100)

SPECIFICATIONS:
├── Area: **100 ha** (10 lines × 1km, 15m depth, 100m spacing)
├── Species: **70% M. pyrifera, 20% D. antarctica, 10% Lessonia** (genetic diversity)
├── Structure: **HDPE longlines (submerged 15m), screw anchors, sacrificial anodes**
├── Deployment: **Autumn (Mar–May)** — spores on twine, 6 months to harvest
├── Harvest: **Selective frond cutting (ROV-assisted)** — 4x/yr, holdfast intact
├── Processing: **Onboard (factory ship) → biostimulant, food, biofuel, pharma**
├── Energy: **100% electric (battery + H₂ fuel cell)** — zero emissions
├── Monitoring: **eDNA, satellite (Sentinel-2), ROV, sentinel divers**
└── Footprint: **<5% benthic impact** — lines removed each cycle

ECONOMICS (100-ha Module, Year 3 Mature):
├── CAPEX: **US$4.2M** (MTT/YHT equity + EIB blue loan)
├── OPEX/yr: **US$650k** (80% local labor, electric ops)
├── Yield: **1,800 t/yr (wet)** → **Biostimulant 450 t, Food 300 t, Biofuel 200 t, Pharma 50 t**
├── Revenue: **US$2.1M/yr** (blended price US$1,170/t)
├── Blue Carbon: **1,800 tCO₂e/yr** × **US$65/t** = **US$117k/yr** (VERRA VM0033)
├── Net Margin: **38%** → **US$550k/yr to Trust programs**
└── Jobs: **25 direct (15 Kawéskar/Yagán) + 15 indirect**

SCALING TARGET:
├── Year 5: **5 modules (500 ha)** — US$10.5M/yr
├── Year 10: **17 modules (1,700 ha)** — **US$35.7M/yr**
├── Year 15: **35 modules (3,500 ha)** — **US$73.5M/yr**
└── Year 20: **50 modules (5,000 ha)** — **US$105M/yr** (MTT target)
```
