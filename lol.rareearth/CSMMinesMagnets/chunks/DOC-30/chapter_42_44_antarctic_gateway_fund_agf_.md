## 4.4 Antarctic Gateway Fund (AGF) — Joint Venture

### 4.4.1 Structure & Capitalization

```
ANTARCTIC GATEWAY FUND (AGF) — JOINT VENTURE CHARTER

STRUCTURE: SpA (Chile) — **50% MTT + 50% YHT**
CAPITALIZATION:
├── MTT Contribution: 5% FCF Austral clusters (Alpha-15/16/17, Bravo-8/9)
├── YHT Contribution: 5% FCF Austral clusters
├── Port Revenue: 1% Punta Arenas + Puerto Williams gross
├── HIF Revenue: 0.5% HIF Magallanes gross
├── INACH Contracts: Direct billing + 10% margin to AGF
└── Tourism Concessions: 15% net revenue (cruise, expedition, fly-in)

GOVERNANCE BOARD (6 members):
├── 1 MTT Chair (rotating annual)
├── 1 YHT Chair (rotating annual)
├── 1 INACH Director (permanent, technical vote)
├── 1 ENAP Representative (energy/logistics, non-voting after Year 5)
├── 1 Independent Antarctic Logistics Expert (COMNAP/SCAR affiliated)
└── 1 Tardigradia Austral Representative (non-voting)

OPERATIONS:
├── **Puerto Williams Logistics Hub** — fuel (H₂), provisions, waste, medical, comms
├── **Punta Arenas Antarctic Terminal** — cargo, pax, science equipment, ice-class vessels
├── **Search & Rescue (SAR)** — 2 helicopters (Ice-class), 1 fixed-wing, 24/7 — **Yagán/Kawéskar crew**
├── **Science Support** — lab space, sample cold chain, data uplink, field camp logistics
└── **Tourism Management** — visitor permits, biosecurity, guide certification, carrying capacity
```
