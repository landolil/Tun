## 9.1 Methodology

Each technical, legal, economic, and cultural claim in DOC-30 is traced to:
1. **Primary Source** — Original data, legislation, court decision, community protocol, scientific study
2. **Cross-Document Reference** — Link to DOC-01..29 where applicable
3. **Verification Status** — Verified (V), Community-Validated (CV), Modeled (M), Projected (P)

**Traceability Format**: `[Claim ID] | Claim Summary | Primary Source | Cross-Ref | Status`
