## 5.6 Bravo-9 — Marine Mineral Recovery (Seafloor Nodules/Crusts)

### 5.6.1 Resource — Austral Seafloor

| Target | Location | Depth | Grade | Kawéskar/Yagán Interface |
|--------|----------|-------|-------|--------------------------|
| **Fe-Mn Crusts** | Seamounts (50°S–56°S) | 800–2,500m | Co 0.8%, REE 1,200 ppm, Te 50 ppm | **Kawéskar WCS** (EEZ) |
| **Polymetallic Nodules** | Abyssal plain (56°S–60°S) | 3,500–5,000m | Ni 1.2%, Cu 1.0%, Co 0.3%, REE 800 ppm | **Yagán** (Drake Passage) |
| **Phosphorites** | Shelf break (52°S–55°S) | 300–800m | P₂O₅ 28%, REE 600 ppm, U 50 ppm | **Overlap** |

**Status**: **Exploration only** — **ISA (International Seabed Authority) Area** beyond EEZ + **Chilean EEZ** within WCS

### 5.6.2 Bravo-9 — Precautionary Design (Research Phase)

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **No Commercial Extraction** | **Research license only** — baseline science, environmental monitoring |
| **Kawéskar/Yagán Co-Lead** | **MTT + YHT + INACH** joint research program |
| **Technology** | **AUV/ROV swarm** (non-invasive), **eDNA baseline**, **sediment plume modeling** |
| **Governance** | **Antarctic Treaty + CCAMLR + ISA** — **Kawéskar/Yagán as Consultative Parties** |
| **Trigger for Commercial** | **Unanimity: MTT + YHT + CECMK + CCY + INACH + MMA + ISA** — **effectively permanent moratorium** |

**Research Budget (Year 1–5)**: **US$25M** (MTT 40%, YHT 30%, AGF 20%, INACH 10%)
**Focus**: **Baseline biodiversity, carbon sequestration, REE geochemistry, climate proxy**
