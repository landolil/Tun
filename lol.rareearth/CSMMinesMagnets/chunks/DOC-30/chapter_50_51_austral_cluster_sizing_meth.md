## 5.1 Austral Cluster Sizing Methodology — Extreme Environment Factors

**Aegis-Forge Cluster Sizing Formula (Austral Adaptation)**:
$$\text{Cluster Capacity} = \frac{\text{Resource Grade} \times \text{Tonnage} \times \text{Recovery} \times \text{Operating Days}_{\text{austral}}}{\text{Target Output} \times \text{Contingency}_{\text{extreme}}}$$

**Austral Adjustment Factors (vs Standard)**:
| Factor | Standard | Austral | Multiplier | Rationale |
|--------|----------|---------|------------|-----------|
| **Operating Days** | 330 | **210** (7 months) | **0.64×** | Winter shutdown (May–Sep), -20°C, 80 kt winds |
| **Logistics Cost** | 1.0× | **2.5×** | **2.5×** | Helicopter/ship only, no roads, ice-class vessels |
| **Energy Cost** | 1.0× | **0.6×** | **0.6×** | World-class wind (12 m/s), HIF H₂ at $1.8/kg |
| **Cooling Efficiency** | 1.0× | **1.4×** | **1.4×** | Ambient 2–8°C, free cooling 95% of operating hours |
| **Construction Cost** | 1.0× | **1.8×** | **1.8×** | Modular, helicopter transport, permafrost foundations |
| **Workforce Cost** | 1.0× | **1.5×** | **1.5×** | Rotational 21/21, hazard pay, PTI training |
| **Composite Factor** | 1.0 | — | **1.7×** | **Net: 70% CAPEX premium, 40% OPEX discount (energy)** |
