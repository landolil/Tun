## 6.2 Yagán Cape Horn — Biosphere Reserve & MPA Integration

### 6.2.1 Cape Horn Biosphere Reserve (CHBR) — UNESCO 2005

| Element | Detail | Yagán Role |
|---------|--------|------------|
| **Area** | **49,000 km²** (marine 35,000 + terrestrial 14,000) | **Co-management** (CONAF + INACH + GORE + **Yagán Community**) |
| **Zoning** | Core (15%), Buffer (35%), Transition (50%) | **Yagán defines Core boundaries** (ceremonial sites) |
| **Governance** | **Consejo de la Reserva** (12 members: 3 Yagán + 3 CONAF + 2 INACH + 2 GORE + 1 MMA + 1 Navy) | **Yagán veto** on Core zone decisions |
| **Strategic Plan** | **2023–2033** — Climate adaptation, sustainable tourism, science, culture | **Yagán TK integrated** — Yekamush co-authors |

### 6.2.2 Cape Horn Marine Protected Area (CHMPA) — Proposed

**Yagán Community Proposal (CCY Acta 2024-02) — Submitted to MMA**:

| Zone | Area | IUCN Category | Protection Level | Yagán Governance |
|------|------|---------------|------------------|------------------|
| **Cape Horn Ceremonial Core** | 5,000 km² | **Ia** | **No entry** except Yekamush + Yagán ceremony | **Absolute Yagán** |
| **Bahía Wulaia Cultural Sanctuary** | 200 km² | **III (Natural Monument)** | Ceremony, research (Yagán-approved), no extractive | **Yekamush + CCY** |
| **Beagle Channel Kelp Corridor** | 8,000 km² | **IV** | Kelp restoration, centolla nursery, blue carbon | **YHT Blue Carbon + CONAF** |
| **Drake Passage Whale Corridor** | 12,000 km² | **IV** | Whale migration (blue, sei, humpback), zero noise | **Yagán Sentinels + CCAMLR** |
| **Sustainable Use Zone** | 9,800 km² | **VI** | Yagán artisanal fisheries, tourism, research | **CCY manages** |
| **Total CHMPA** | **35,000 km²** | **Network** | **20% no-take, 80% sustainable Yagán use** | **CCY = MPA Authority** |

### 6.2.3 CHBR-CHMSA Integration — Governance Model

```
CAPE HORN GOVERNANCE ARCHITECTURE (2025+)

├── UNESCO CHBR Consejo de la Reserva (Estratégico, 12 miembros, 3 Yagán + veto)
├── CHMPA Autoridad Yagán (Operativo, CCY + Yekamush, 100% Yagán control)
├── Kawéskar WCS CECMK (Vecino, coordinación Seno Almirantazgo / Cabo de Hornos)
├── Comisión Binacional Kawéskar-Yagán (Zona superposición, unanimidad 6)
├── INACH Comité Científico Antártico (Ciencia, logística, protocolo Madrid)
├── DIRECTEMAR/Armada (Seguridad, navegación, SAR, soberanía)
├── MMA/CONAF/Subpesca (Técnicos, permisos, fiscalización)
└── GORE Magallanes (Desarrollo regional, turismo, infraestructura)

DECISIONES TRANSFRONTERIZAS (Cabo de Hornos / Seno Almirantazgo):
├── Requieren: **Consenso Kawéskar (CECMK) + Consenso Yagán (CCY) + Aprobación INACH**
├── Veto: **Kalku O Yekamush = Bloqueo**
├── Mediación: **MMA + CONADI + Cancillería** → **Tribunal Ambiental Punta Arenas**
```
