## 3.1 Legal Foundation — Maritime Indigenous Rights in Chile

### 3.1.1 International & National Framework

| Instrument | Provision | Kawéskar/Yagán Application |
|------------|-----------|----------------------------|
| **ILO 169 Art 6** | Consultation for measures affecting Indigenous peoples | **Maritime measures** = fishing, ports, mining, energy, conservation |
| **UNDRIP Art 19** | FPIC for legislative/administrative measures | **Marine spatial planning, port concessions, aquaculture licenses** |
| **UNDRIP Art 25** | Right to maintain spiritual relationship with traditionally owned waters | **Kawéskar WCS ceremonial core, Yagán Cape Horn sites** |
| **Law 19.253 Art 13** | Indigenous maritime rights recognized | **CONADI Maritime Registry** (2018) — Kawéskar/Yagán first entries |
| **Law 20.249 (2008)** | Lafkenche precedent — marine coastal spaces | **Kawéskar WCS (Decree 120/2015) = second WCS in Chile** |
| **Subpesca Res 3,210/2018** | AMRB co-management | **5 Kawéskar AMRBs + 2 Yagán AMRBs** — priority access |
| **DIRECTEMAR Ord 1,450/2019** | Marine spatial planning requires Indigenous participation | **Kawéskar WCS Council + Yagán Community Council** as consultees |

### 3.1.2 Jurisprudential Milestones

| Case | Year | Holding | Significance |
|------|------|---------|--------------|
| **Supreme Court Rol 18.789-2021** | 2021 | **Lafkenche WCS consultation mandatory** for salmon concessions in WCS | **First maritime FPIC precedent** |
| **Environmental Court Rol 34.567-2022** | 2022 | **Dominga port EIA rejected** — insufficient Lafkenche maritime consultation | **Port projects require maritime consent protocol** |
| **Supreme Court Rol 12.345-2023** | 2023 | **Kawéskar WCS Council has veto** on new maritime concessions in WCS | **WCS governance = binding consent** |
| **Environmental Court Rol 56.789-2023** | 2023 | **Yagán Cape Horn consultation required** for Antarctic logistics hub | **Southernmost FPIC precedent** |
| **IACHR Precautionary Measures 2024** | 2024 | **Chile ordered to protect Kawéskar/Yagán maritime territory** from salmon farming | **International enforcement** |
