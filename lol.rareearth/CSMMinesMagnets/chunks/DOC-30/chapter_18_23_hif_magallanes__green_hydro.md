## 2.3 HIF Magallanes — Green Hydrogen Mega-Project (1.2 GW)

### 2.3.1 Project Overview

| Parameter | Value | Significance |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| **Name** | **HIF (Highly Innovative Fuels) Magallanes** | **World's largest commercial e-fuels project** |
| **Location** | **Punta Arenas / Cabo Negro** (53°S) | **Windiest continental site** (12 m/s avg) |
| **Capacity** | **1.2 GW electrolysis** → **550,000 t/yr e-methanol** → **130,000 t/yr e-gasoline** | **Carbon-neutral fuels** for shipping/aviation |
| **CO₂ Source** | **Biogenic** (pulp mill waste, future DAC) | **Circular carbon** — not fossil |
| **Wind Resource** | **4,500 full-load hours/yr** | **World-class** (vs 2,500 EU offshore) |
| **Investment** | **US$8.5B** (Phase 1: US$2.5B, 300 MW by 2026) | **EIB, CPTPP, IRA 45V, Chilean CORFO** |
| **Partners** | **HIF Global, Porsche, Siemens Energy, ExxonMobil, ENAP, Enel** | **Global offtake** (EU, US, Chile, Japan) |
