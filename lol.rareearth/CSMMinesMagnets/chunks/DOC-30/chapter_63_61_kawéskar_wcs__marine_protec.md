## 6.1 Kawéskar WCS — Marine Protected Area Network Design

### 6.1.1 Legal Basis for MPA in WCS

| Instrument | Provision | Kawéskar Application |
|------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Law 20.249 (Lafkenche precedent)** | Marine Coastal Spaces (ECMPO) | **Kawéskar WCS = ECMPO Type 2** (Indigenous governance + conservation) |
| **Decree 120/2015 Art 7** | Marine Spatial Planning mandatory | **MSP = MPA zoning + sustainable use zones** |
| **Law 21.435 (Glacier Law) Art 10** | Glacial-marine interface protection | **Tidewater glaciers in WCS** — no disturbance buffer 5km |
| **CBD (Chile Party)** | Aichi Target 11 / GBF Target 3 | **30% marine protection by 2030** — Kawéskar WCS contributes 120,000 km² |
| **CCAMLR** | Antarctic MPA framework | **WCS as sub-Antarctic MPA model** — CCAMLR observer status sought |

### 6.1.2 Kawéskar MPA Network — Zoning & Objectives

| MPA Zone | Area | IUCN Category | Primary Objective | Kawéskar Governance |
|----------|------|---------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Ceremonial Core (Ngen Kaxás)** | 15,000 km² | **Ia (Strict Nature Reserve)** | Spiritual integrity, zero extractive | **Absolute Kawéskar control** — Kalku only access |
| **Kelp Forest Sanctuary** | 12,000 km² | **IV (Habitat/Species Management)** | Kelp restoration, blue carbon, nursery habitat | **MTT Blue Carbon Committee** — co-mgmt CONAF |
| **Seabird & Mammal Reserve** | 18,000 km² | **II (National Park)** | Albatross/petrel colonies, whale calving, seal haulouts | **Kawéskar Sentinels + CONAF** — research only |
| **Artisanal Fisheries Reserve** | 45,000 km² | **VI (Protected Landscape/Seascape)** | Sustainable Kawéskar harvest, cultural continuity | **CECMK manages quotas** — Subpesca technical advice |
| **Navigation & Culture Corridor** | 8,000 km (linear) | **V (Protected Seascape)** | Halakha routes, ceremonial access, intergenerational teaching | **Kalku authority** — DIRECTEMAR coordination |
| **Climate Refugia Zone** | 22,000 km² | **IV** | Acidification-resistant kelp, cold-water corrals, future-proofing | **MTT Climate Committee** — adaptive management |
| **Total MPA** | **120,000 km²** | **Network** | **30% no-take (Core + Sanctuary + Reserve), 70% sustainable use** | **CECMK = MPA Authority** |
