## 3.5 Cross-Border Coordination — Kawéskar/Yagán/Argentina

### 3.5.1 Beagle Channel Binational Commission (Chile/Argentina)

| Issue | Kawéskar/Yagán Position | Mechanism |
|-------|------------------------|-----------|
| **Salmon Farming (Argentina side)** | **Opposed** — escapes, disease, benthic impact in shared waters | **Binational Commission + CCAMLR** — Yagán/Kawéskar observers |
| **Port Ushuaia Expansion** | **Consult Yagán** — Beagle Channel shared, navigation routes | **DIRECTEMAR + Prefectura Naval Argentina + CCY** |
| **Antarctic Tourism** | **Shared governance** — Puerto Williams/Ushuaia as twin gateways | **INACH + IAA (Argentina) + CCY + Yagán Ushuaia** |
| **Marine Protected Areas** | **Yaganes MPA (Argentina) + Cape Horn (Chile)** — connected | **CCAMLR + CCY + Kawéskar Council** |

### 3.5.2 Yagán Diaspora — Ushuaia Community

- **Population**: **~300 Yagán** in Ushuaia (Argentina)
- **Organization**: **Comunidad Yagán Ushuaia** — recognized by INAI Argentina
- **Protocol**: **Binational Yagán Council** (Puerto Williams + Ushuaia) — **meets quarterly**
- **Consent**: **Projects affecting Beagle Channel require BOTH communities' consent**
