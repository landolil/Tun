## 1.3 Communities — Five Kawéskar + One Yagán

### 1.3.1 Kawéskar Communities (5 Recognized)

| Community | Location | Population | Key Characteristics |
|-----------|----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Puerto Edén** | Wellington Island (49°08'S) | **~280** | **Only Kawéskar-majority town**, isolated (no road, ship/air only), halakha building active |
| **Puerto Natales** | Continental (51°44'S) | **~1,100** | Urban Kawéskar, tourism gateway, cultural center, language nest |
| **Punta Arenas** | Continental (53°10'S) | **~850** | Largest urban Kawéskar, ZOFRI workers, university students |
| **Caleta Tortel** | Continental (47°48'S) | **~450** | Northernmost Kawéskar, cypress boardwalks, tourism, logging history |
| **Caleta Andrade** | Madre de Dios Island (50°25'S) | **~120** | Remote, traditional economy (kelp, loco, centolla), halakha culture strong |

**Total Kawéskar**: **~2,800** in recognized communities + **~400** diaspora (Santiago, Concepción, abroad)

### 1.3.2 Yagán Community (1 Recognized)

| Community | Location | Population | Key Characteristics |
|-----------|----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Puerto Williams** | Navarino Island (54°56'S) | **~2,500** (1,523 Yagán) | **Southernmost town**, Chilean Navy base, INACH, tourism, Yagán cultural revival |

**Total Yagán**: **~1,800** (1,523 Puerto Williams + 277 diaspora)
