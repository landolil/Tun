## 6.5 Climate Adaptation — Austral Frontline

### 6.5.1 Observed Changes (2000–2024)

| Parameter | Trend | Kawéskar Impact | Yagán Impact |
|-----------|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Sea Surface Temperature** | **+1.2°C** | Kelp retreat southward, species shift | Beagle warming, centolla range shift |
| **Ocean Acidification (pH)** | **-0.15 units** | Loco/centolla larval survival -25% | Piure/kipa shell thinning |
| **Glacier Retreat (Cordillera Darwin)** | **-18% area** | Freshwater input change, fjord stratification | Beagle salinity drop, circulation change |
| **Permafrost Thaw** | **Active layer +0.5m** | Coastal erosion, infrastructure risk | Cape Horn ceremonial site instability |
| **Wind Intensity** | **+15% >80kt events** | Navigation danger, halakha/awa safety | Tourism disruption, logistics delay |
| **Sea Level Rise** | **+3.2mm/yr** | Low island inundation, Puerto Edén risk | Puerto Williams coastal erosion |

### 6.5.2 Adaptation Strategies — Trust-Led

| Strategy | MTT (Kawéskar) | YHT (Yagán) | AGF (Joint) |
|----------|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Kelp Assisted Migration** | Plant **Durvillaea antarctica** (more resilient) at southern edge | **Kipa restoration** in Beagle cooler refugia | **Seed bank** (MTT+YHT) — genetic diversity |
| **Infrastructure Relocation** | **Puerto Edén** — elevated platforms, floating modules | **Puerto Williams** — coastal setback, elevated utilities | **Ports** — adaptive design, H₂-ready |
| **Cultural Site Protection** | **3D scanning** all ceremonial sites, **digital twins** | **Cape Horn sites** — physical stabilization + digital archive | **Antarctic gateway** — heritage protocol |
| **Food Sovereignty** | **Community freezers** (solar/H₂), **kelp/protein processing** | **Native species aquaculture** (merluza, róbalo) | **AGF supply chain** — resilient logistics |
| **Health Surveillance** | **Respiratory/cardiovascular** (cold, damp, mold) | **Vector-borne** (new mosquito range), mental health | **SAR medical** — telehealth network |
