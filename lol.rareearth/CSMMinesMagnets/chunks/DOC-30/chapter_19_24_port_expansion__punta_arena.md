## 2.4 Port Expansion — Punta Arenas & Puerto Williams

### 2.4.1 Punta Arenas Puerto Exterior (ENAP/EMPORCHA)

| Parameter | Value | Kawéskar/Yagán Interface |
|-----------|-------|--------------------------|
| **Investment** | **US$450M** (Phase 1: US$180M by 2028) | **Kawéskar WCS Council veto** on dredging plan |
| **New Berths** | **3× 350m** (Post-Panamax) + **2× 200m** (feeder) | **Dredging: 2.5M m³** — sediment plume modeling required |
| **Capacity** | **2.5M TEU/yr** (vs 180k current) | **Strait transit increase** — **Yagán navigation safety** |
| **Green Corridor** | **Shore power (100%)**, **H₂ bunkering**, **ammonia-ready** | **HIF H₂ supply**, **MT/RT blue carbon offset** |
| **REE Handling** | **Certified Class 9** (IMDG) — Alpha-17 xenotime export | **MT/Yagán Trust co-certification** — **sentinel oversight** |

**Dredging Impact Assessment (EIA 2023 — Kawéskar Council Review)**:
- **Sediment Plume**: **Modelled 15km dispersion** — **affects 3 Kawéskar AMRBs**
- **Mitigation**: **Silt curtains (double layer)**, **tidal window dredging (slack tide only)**, **real-time turbidity monitoring**
- **Compensation**: **US$15M to MT** — **kelp restoration 1,000 ha**, **AMERB enhancement**
