### 4.3.2 Governance Structure

```
YAGÁN HERITAGE TRUST (YHT) — GOVERNANCE CHARTER

BOARD OF TRUSTEES (7 members):
├── 3 Family Trustees (linajes Calderón, González, Vargas — rotación 2yr, paridad género)
├── 1 Yekamush Principal (permanent, spiritual veto)
├── 1 Independent Cultural Anthropologist (U. Magallanes/UMAG, appointed by CCY)
├── 1 Tardigradia Austral Representative (non-voting)
└── 1 CONADI/INACH Observer (non-voting)

TECHNICAL COMMITTEES:
├── Language Revitalization Committee (Yagán dictionary, school curriculum, digital archive, AI speech)
├── Awa Navigation & Culture Committee (awa building, youth training, ceremonial routes, Hain adaptation)
├── Cape Horn Stewardship Committee (Ceremonial sites, biosphere reserve co-mgmt, tourism carrying capacity)
├── Antarctic Gateway Committee (INACH coordination, science support, tourism protocol, search & rescue)
├── Climate Witness Committee (Permafrost monitoring, glacier retreat, Beagle warming, species shifts)
└── Finance & Investment Committee (portfolio, grants, community distribution, AGF coordination)

DECISION RULES:
├── Operational: Majority (4/7)
├── Strategic (capital >US$2M, Cape Horn zonification, Antarctic protocol): **2/3 (5/7) + Yekamush consent**
├── Veto Matters (Cape Horn ceremonial sites, Bahía Wulaia, awa routes, Ngen Wata/Kipa): **Yekamush veto = binding**
├── Emergency (ship grounding, oil spill, tourist death, extreme weather): **Yekamush + 1 Trustee = immediate authority**

DISTRIBUTION POLICY (Annual):
├── 30% Language Revitalization (school, nests, digital, AI, teacher training, Cristina Calderón legacy)
├── 25% Awa Navigation & Cultural Transmission (awa building, Hain adaptation, youth expeditions)
├── 20% Cape Horn Stewardship (site protection, biosphere co-mgmt, tourism management, research)
├── 10% Antarctic Gateway Programs (guide training, science support, logistics, search & rescue)
├── 8% Community Programs (housing, health, education, connectivity, elder care)
├── 5% Cultural Revitalization (ceremonies, crafts, oral history, museum co-curation)
└── 2% AGF Solidarity Contribution
```

### 4.3.3 Yagán Language Revitalization — Comprehensive Program

**Status**: **Critically Endangered** — **1 fluent speaker (Cristina Calderón † 2022)**, **~50 semi-speakers**, **0 child speakers (2024)**

**Revitalization Strategy (YHT Funded, 20-yr Horizon)**:

| Pillar | Action | Budget (5-yr) | Target (Year 10) |
|--------|--------|---------------|------------------|
| **Documentation** | Complete dictionary (digital, audio, video), grammar, ethnobotany, place names | US$3M | **100% documented**, open access (Yagán-controlled) |
| **Education** | **Escuela Yagán Puerto Williams** (immersion K–8), **Nidos de Lengua** (0–5), teacher certification | US$12M | **150 child speakers**, 10 certified teachers |
| **Technology** | **Yagán AI** (speech recognition, translation, synthesis), **App Kawésqar/Yagán** (offline), VR Hain ceremony | US$5M | **Functional translator**, 5,000 app users |
| **Intergenerational** | **Abuelos Maestros** (semi-speakers + children), **Expediciones Awa** (language + navigation), **Hain adaptado** (ceremonia + lengua) | US$4M | **50 semi-speakers active**, 20 youth fluent |
| **Institutional** | **Official status** (Chile: Language Law 2025), **municipal services in Yagán**, **INACH protocols bilingual** | US$2M | **Legal recognition**, 5 municipal bilingual staff |

**Budget Total (5-yr)**: **US$26M** — **YHT 70%, MINEDUC 20%, UNESCO 10%**
