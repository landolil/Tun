## 5.2 Alpha-15 — Aysén Coal/Fe Remediation (Río Cisnes Basin)

### 5.2.1 Resource Base — Historical Mining Legacy

| Waste Stream | Volume | Magnetic/REE Content | Recovery Potential |
|--------------|--------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Río Cisnes Coal Waste** | 12 Mt | Magnetite 8–12%, REE 60–100 ppm | **Fe concentrate + REE carbonate** |
| **Aysén Peat Bogs (Drained)** | 5 Mt dry | REE 200–400 ppm (ion-adsorption) | **HREE-enriched REE** |
| **Río Baker Sediment** | 200 kt/yr | Magnetite 15%, REE 120 ppm | **Fe + REE + glacial flour** |

**Environmental Legacy**: **AMD from historic coal (pH 3.5, Fe 180 mg/L)**, **peat oxidation CO₂ emissions 50 kt/yr**

| Component | Specification | Austral Adaptation |

| **Footprint** | **55 ha** (modular, barge-transportable) | **No roads** — Río Cisnes barge access |
| **Workforce** | **220 direct** (rotational 21/21) | **60% Kawéskar**, PTI Puerto Aysén campus |
