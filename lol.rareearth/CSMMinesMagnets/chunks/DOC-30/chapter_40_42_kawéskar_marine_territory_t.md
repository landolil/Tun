## 4.2 Kawéskar Marine Territory Trust (MTT) — Detailed Design

### 4.2.1 Capitalization Schedule

| Year | FBC Equity (15%) | CDC FCF (20%) | Blue Carbon Credits | Port/HIF Revenue Share | Total Cumulative |

**Blue Carbon Revenue Model (Kawéskar WCS Kelp Restoration)**:
- **Restoration Target**: **5,000 ha by Year 10** (Macrocystis pyrifera, Durvillaea antarctica, Lessonia spp.)
- **Sequestration Rate**: **15 tCO₂e/ha/yr** (validated U. Magallanes — subantarctic kelp higher than temperate)
- **Carbon Price**: **US$30/tCO₂e (2025) → US$85/tCO₂e (2035)** — **CPTPP Art 14 + EU ETS linkage**
- **Annual Revenue (Year 10)**: **5,000 ha × 15 tCO₂e × US$55 = US$4.1M/yr**
- **Cumulative (20yr)**: **~US$85M** — **reinvested in restoration expansion**

**HIF/Port Revenue Share**:
- **Punta Arenas Puerto Exterior**: **0.5% gross revenue** → **US$2.5M/yr by Year 8**
- **HIF Magallanes**: **0.3% gross revenue** → **US$1.8M/yr by Year 6**
- **Antarctic Logistics (AGF)**: **20% AGF net** → **US$1.2M/yr by Year 10**

### 4.2.2 Governance Structure

```
MARINE TERRITORY TRUST (MTT) — GOVERNANCE CHARTER

BOARD OF TRUSTEES (9 members):
├── 5 Community Trustees (1 per community: Edén, Natales, Punta Arenas, Tortel, Andrade — elected 3yr)
├── 1 Kalku Principal (permanent, spiritual veto on Ngen matters)
├── 1 Independent Marine Scientist (U. Magallanes/UMAG, appointed by CECMK)
├── 1 Tardigradia Austral Representative (non-voting, observer)
└── 1 CONADI/Subpesca Observer (non-voting)

TECHNICAL COMMITTEES:
├── Kelp Forest & Blue Carbon Committee (VERRA VM0033, restoration, MRV, markets)
├── Fisheries & AMRB Committee (quotas, species, gear, enforcement, centolla/loco/erizo)
├── Marine Sentinel Committee (20 sentinels, real-time buoys, eDNA, satellite, DIRECTEMAR link)
├── Navigation & Culture Committee (halakha building, Kalku training, youth programs, ceremonial sites)
├── Climate Adaptation Committee (acidification, warming, kelp migration, permafrost coast)
└── Finance & Investment Committee (portfolio, grants, community distribution, AGF coordination)

DECISION RULES:
├── Operational: Majority Board (5/9)
├── Strategic (capital >US$3M, new zones, MSP changes): **2/3 Board (6/9) + Kalku consent**
├── Veto Matters (ceremonial sites, Ngen Kaxás/Kálak/Pájaro, halakha routes): **Kalku veto = binding**
├── Emergency (oil spill, HAB, salmon escape, tsunami): **Kalku + 1 Trustee = immediate authority**

DISTRIBUTION POLICY (Annual):
├── 35% Kelp Forest & Blue Carbon Restoration (planting, monitoring, MRV, expansion)
├── 25% AMRB & Fisheries Sovereignty (gear, vessels, processing, market access, youth)
├── 15% Halakha Culture & Navigation School (Escuela Halakha — 30 students/yr)
├── 10% Marine Sentinel Network (equipment, training, stipends, satellite data)
├── 8% Community Programs (health, education, elder care, housing, connectivity)
├── 5% Cultural Revitalization (Kawésqar language nests, oral history, Kalku apprenticeship)
└── 2% AGF Solidarity Contribution
```
