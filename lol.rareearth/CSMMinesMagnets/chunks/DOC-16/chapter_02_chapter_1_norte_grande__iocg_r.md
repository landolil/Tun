## Chapter 1: Norte Grande — IOCG/REE Epicenter of the Chilean Magnetic Flow

### 1.1 Norte Grande Origin and Purpose — IOCG/REE Epicenter

The Norte Grande region (Antofagasta and Atacama Regions) is the **geological and logistical epicenter** of Chile's magnetic mineral endowment. This region hosts the world-class **IOCG (Iron Oxide-Copper-Gold) belt** and the **Salar de Atacama lithium-REE brine system** — the two primary source engines for the Carrington Storm Motors (CSM) magnetic mineral supply chain.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: The Chilean State owns all mineral resources (Constitution Art. 19§24). Norte Grande contains **>60% of Chile's IOCG resources** and **>40% of REE potential** (DOC-01). This concentration forces the brokerage pathway: Norte Grande → Aegis-Forge → CSM.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **regional specificity** — DOC-01 provides national mapping; Phase 4 requires district-level resolution for Aegis-Forge deployment decisions.

**SHIELD Horizon**: Norte Grande is the **tactical deployment zone** (0-5 years) for first Aegis-Forge Alpha clusters. Decisions here cascade to 2036 Puerto Exterior operations.

#### 1.1.2 Document Purpose

This document translates national-scale intelligence (DOC-01..12) into **regional deployment intelligence** for Norte Grande:

- **District-level resource quantification** for Aegis-Forge sizing
- **Community-specific consent pathways** per DOC-03 FBC/CDC model

### 1.3 Geological Overview — IOCG Belt and REE Systems

#### 1.3.1 IOCG Belt Architecture

The **Chilean IOCG Belt** extends ~600 km north-south through Norte Grande, formed by **Mesozoic subduction-related magmatism** and **hydrothermal overprint**:

```
Geological Cross-Section (Simplified):
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  COASTAL CORDILLERA (Jurassic-Cretaceous volcanics)        │
│  └─ IOCG Deposits: Candelaria, Manto Verde, Mantoverde    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  PRECORDILLERA (Paleozoic basement + Mesozoic intrusions)  │
│  └─ IOCG Deposits: El Salvador, Potrerillos, Spence       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SALAR BASINS (Cenozoic evaporites + brines)               │
│  └─ REE-Li-K: Salar de Atacama, Salar de Maricunga        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
