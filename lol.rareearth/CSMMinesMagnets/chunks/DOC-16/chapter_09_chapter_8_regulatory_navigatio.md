### 8.1 SEREMI Mining — Concession Management

#### 8.1.1 Regional Workflow

```
Concession Application → SEREMI Antofagasta/Atacama
    │
    ├── Technical Review (30 days)
    ├── Overlap Check (Catastro Minero)
    ├── Environmental Pre-screen (SEREMI MA)
    └── Grant/Deny (60 days total)
```

**CRMA Fast-Track**: Strategic projects → 30-day total (DOC-07 Ch. 7).
