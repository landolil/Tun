## Chapter 2: Maule Iron District — El Tofo/Romeral Magnetite Skarns, CAP Heritage

### 2.1 District Overview — Resource Quantification

#### 2.1.1 Geological Setting

**Maule Iron District** (Maule Region, Talca/Curicó provinces) hosts Chile's **largest historical magnetite skarn deposits**, operated by CAP Mining until care/maintenance (2019):

- **El Tofo**: 280 Mt @ 60% Fe, 0.15% Cu, 0.05 g/t Au, 120 ppm REE, 55% magnetite
- **Romeral**: 170 Mt @ 50% Fe, 0.12% Cu, 0.04 g/t Au, 100 ppm REE, 45% magnetite
- **Total**: 450 Mt @ 56% Fe, 0.14% Cu, 0.05 g/t Au, 112 ppm REE, 51% magnetite

**Status**: Care/maintenance since 2019 (iron price, water, tailings). **Zero tailings liability** with Aegis-Forge.

#### 2.1.2 Magnetic Mineral Potential

| Mineral | Grade | Recovery Stage | Annual Potential (kt) |
|---------|-------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Magnetite (Fe₃O₄)** | 51% | Stage 7 (Ferrous Cascade) | 1,530 |
| **REE (Total)** | 112 ppm | Stage 8 (Zircon/Residue) | 0.05 |
| **Copper** | 0.14% | Stage 6 (Cu Window) | 0.4 |

**Aegis-Forge Alpha-12 Sizing**: 226 L/batch × 30 batches/yr = 6,780 t/yr feed → processes **1.5% of resource annually** (100-year resource life at current rate; care/maintenance = no depletion).
