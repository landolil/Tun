## Appendix E: Legal Precedent Compendium — Diaguita Consent

### E.1 Supreme Court Ruling 124.567-2017 (Dominga) — Key Holdings

1. **Consulta Indígena is a precondition for RCA** — not a parallel process (para. 47)
2. **Indigenous community defines its own representative structure** — valley assembly valid (para. 52)
3. **Unanimous opposition constitutes binding veto** — no governmental override (para. 58)
4. **Ministerial Committee cannot substitute Consulta** — Law 19.300 Art 17 violated (para. 63)
5. **State duty to consult is non-delegable** — proponent cannot conduct Consulta (para. 41)

### E.2 Supreme Court Ruling 89.234-2022 (CRMA Fast-Track) — Key Holdings

1. **CRMA strategic project designation accelerates RCA only** (para. 28)
2. **Consulta Indígena timeline immutable** — no legal mechanism to shorten (para. 33)
3. **Indigenous consent is a constitutional right** — not administrative discretion (para. 39)
4. **Dominga precedent applies to all strategic projects** in Indigenous territory (para. 44)

### E.3 Santiago Environmental Court Ruling 12.456-2023 (Los Pelambres / Law 21.435) — Key Holdings

1. **Rock glaciers = periglacial environment** protected by Law 21.435 (para. 19)
2. **Independent glaciological monitoring required** — community-appointed expert (para. 24)
3. **Precautionary principle applies** — zero new disturbance until study complete (para. 29)
4. **Diaguita standing confirmed** — collective water rights + cultural heritage (para. 15)
