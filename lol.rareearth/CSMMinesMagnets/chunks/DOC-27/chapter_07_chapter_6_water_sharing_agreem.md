## Chapter 6: Water Sharing Agreements

### 6.1 Surface Water + Groundwater Conjunctive Management

Diaguita valleys operate **integrated surface-groundwater management** — unique in Chilean water law (typically separated). **DGA Resolution 1238/2018** recognizes **valley aquifer management plans (PGA)** for all four valleys:

**Conjunctive Allocation Formula**:
$$Q_{alloc,v} = Q_{surface,v} \cdot (1 - r_{eco}) + Q_{recharge,v} - Q_{mining,v} - Q_{domestic,v}$$
Where $r_{eco} = 0.15$ (ecological reserve), $Q_{recharge,v}$ = managed aquifer recharge (MAR) from WSF projects, $Q_{mining,v}$ = mining/Aegis-Forge allocation (subject to veto), $Q_{domestic,v}$ = human consumption priority (Law 21.435 Art. 5).

**Current Allocations (2024)**:
| Valley | Q_surface (m³/s) | Q_recharge (m³/s) | Q_mining (m³/s) | Q_domestic (m³/s) | Q_alloc_agriculture (m³/s) |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Elqui** | 18.5 | 0.8 (MAR) | 1.2 (Los Pelambres) | 0.6 | **16.7** |
| **Limarí** | 22.3 | 1.2 (MAR) | 2.1 (CAP + Los Pelambres) | 0.8 | **19.6** |
| **Choapa** | 14.7 | 0.5 (MAR) | 1.8 (Los Pelambres) | 0.5 | **11.9** |

**Aegis-Forge Additional Demand**: Alpha-3/4/5/7 + Bravo-3/4 = **2.3 m³/s total** — **requires WSF desalination co-investment** (no surface water allocation available).

### 6.2 Agricultural Priority Protocol

**Law 21.435 (Glacier Protection) Article 5** and **Water Code Article 56 bis** establish **human consumption > agriculture > mining** priority. Diaguita **Estatutos Comunales** reinforce: **agriculture = cultural survival**. Protocol for new water requests:

1. **Hydrological Impact Assessment (HIA)** — independent consultant, Diaguita-approved
2. **Valley Assembly Review** — 90-day deliberation
3. **WSF Co-Investment Test** — project must fund desalination/MAR equivalent to 2× water demand
4. **Veto Gate** — unanimous valley assembly opposition = **automatic denial**

**Aegis-Forge Water Commitment**: **Zero net valley water consumption** — all process water from **desalination (WSF co-invest) + AWG + ZLD recycle**. **Written into FBC/CDC trust deeds** as covenant.

### 6.3 Mining Efficiency & Water Recycling Standards

**Aegis-Forge Water Efficiency Standards** (exceeds Chilean norms):
- **IOCG/IOA clusters (Alpha-3..7)**: **0.8 m³/t ore** (vs. industry avg 2.5 m³/t) — thickened tailings (70% solids), ZLD circuit
- **Ion-adsorption clusters (Bravo-3/4)**: **0.05 m³/t ore** — ambient leach, 95% reagent recycle, AWG supplement
- **Monitoring**: **Real-time flow meters** at every intake/discharge, **community sentinel access** to data (SCADA read-only), **quarterly third-party audit** (DGA + Diaguita hydrologist)
