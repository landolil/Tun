**Capitalization Formula — Per Valley**:
$$NPV_{FBC,v} = \sum_{t=0}^{20} \frac{CF_{FBC,v,t} + CF_{WSF,v,t} + CF_{CRF,v,t}}{(1+r)^t}$$
Where $CF_{FBC}$ = equity distributions, $CF_{WSF}$ = water security returns (avoided cost), $CF_{CRF}$ = cultural value (non-monetary, shadow priced), $r=0.08$. **Choapa highest NPV** due to **highest equity (20%) + highest WSF need (desalination)**.

### 4.6 Governance — Valley Trust Boards

**Each Valley Trust Board (7 members)**:
- **3 Diaguita representatives** — elected by valley assembly (3-yr terms, max 2 terms)
- **2 Elder Council members** — appointed by valley elder council (lifetime, removable by 2/3 assembly)
- **1 Tardigradia representative** — appointed by Tardigradia Chile SpA board
- **1 Independent expert** — hydrologist/economist/governance, jointly selected (2-yr term)

**Decision Rules**:
- **Quorum**: 5/7 (must include ≥2 Diaguita + ≥1 Elder)
- **Ordinary decisions**: Simple majority
- **Water decisions (WSF)**: **Diaguita majority (3/4 Diaguita+Elder) required**
- **Cultural decisions (CRF)**: **Elder consensus required**
- **Equity decisions (FBC)**: **Unanimous Diaguita+Elder required**
- **Deadlock**: Referred to **Nación Diaguita Council** (binding)

**Board Meeting Cadence**: Monthly (operations), Quarterly (strategy), Extraordinary (water/cultural emergencies).

### 4.7 Cross-Valley Coordination: Nación Diaguita Council

**Nación Diaguita Council** — **supra-trust coordination body**:
- **Composition**: 4 valley trust presidents + 3 elder council representatives (rotating)
- **Mandate**: Inter-valley water sharing, regional Consulta coordination, FBC/CDC policy alignment, CRMA engagement
- **Authority**: **Binding on valley trusts for cross-valley matters** (water, Consulta, CRMA)
- **Meeting**: Monthly (virtual), Quarterly (in-person, rotating valley)
- **Secretariat**: Funded by **1% of total FBC distributions** (US$1.6M/yr at maturity)

**Cross-Valley Water Sharing Protocol**:
$$Q_{share,v \to v'} = \max\left(0, \frac{WSI_{v'} - 1.0}{\sum_{u: WSI_u < 1.0} (1.0 - WSI_u)} \cdot Q_{surplus,v}\right)$$
Where $WSI_v$ = Water Stress Index, $Q_{surplus,v}$ = water available above valley needs. **Enables Limarí/Elqui surplus to support Choapa/Huasco deficit** — **governed by Nación Council, not market**.
