## Chapter 8: Regulatory Navigation

### 8.1 Multi-Region SEREMI/GORE Interface

Diaguita territory spans **2 regions (Atacama, Coquimbo) × 6 provinces × 15 communes**. Regulatory matrix:

| Authority | Jurisdiction | Diaguita Interface |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| **SEREMI Minería Atacama** | Huasco Province | RCA for Alpha-4/7, Bravo-4 |
| **SEREMI Minería Coquimbo** | Elqui, Limarí, Choapa Provinces | RCA for Alpha-3/5/6, Bravo-3 |
| **GORE Atacama** | Regional development | FBC/CDC trust approval, WSF co-invest |
| **GORE Coquimbo** | Regional development | FBC/CDC trust approval, WSF co-invest, DTI funding |
| **DGA Atacama/Coquimbo** | Water rights, PGA approval | Water allocation, WSF MAR projects, veto oversight |
| **CONADI Coquimbo/Atacama** | Consulta Indígena, land title | Consulta process, Estatutos validation, CRF oversight |
| **Sernageomin** | Mine safety, tailings, closure | Alpha/Bravo safety permits, tailings design review |
| **CRMA** | Strategic project designation | Aegis-Forge cluster fast-track (RCA only, not Consulta) |

**Critical Path**: **Consulta Indígena (225 days minimum) → RCA (120 days) → Sectoral Permits (60 days) → Construction**. **Total: 14–16 months** before groundbreaking. **CRMA designation accelerates RCA only** — Consulta timeline immutable.

### 8.2 Glacier Law 21.435 & Choapa Headwaters

**Law 21.435 (2022)** protects **glaciers and periglacial environment** — **Los Pelambres operates in periglacial zone** (Cerro El Plomo rock glaciers). **Diaguita Choapa Assembly** invoked Law 21.435 in **2023 protection action** — **Santiago Environmental Court ordered** (Case 12.456-2023):
1. **Independent glaciological monitoring** (Diaguita-appointed expert)
2. **Zero new disturbance** in periglacial zone
3. **Tailings expansion freeze** until glacier impact study complete

**Aegis-Forge Compliance**: Alpha-5/7 clusters **exclude periglacial zones** — **500m buffer** from inventoried rock glaciers (DGA Inventory 2023). **Written into FBC/CDC covenants**.

### 8.3 CRMA Strategic Project Designation

**EU Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA) 2024** — Chile candidate for **Strategic Project** status for **REE separation (Aegis-Forge Stage 8) and magnet manufacturing**. **Benefits**:
- **RCA fast-track**: 120 days → 60 days
- **Permitting coordination**: Single window (SEREMI + SEA + DGA)
- **EU offtake guarantees**: 10-year offtake at benchmark pricing
- **EIB/CPTPP financing**: Preferred terms

**Diaguita Condition**: **CRMA designation does not waive Consulta** — **Supreme Court 2023 confirmed**. Aegis-Forge **applies for CRMA** but **budgets full Consulta timeline**.
