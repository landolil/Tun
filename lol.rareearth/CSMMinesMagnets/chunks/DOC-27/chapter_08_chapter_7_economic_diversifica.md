## Chapter 7: Economic Diversification

### 7.1 Agro-Export Value Chain Deepening

Diaguita valleys **already export US$1.4B/yr** — Aegis-Forge FBC/CDC trusts invest in **moving up value chain**:

| Investment | Valley | Capital | IRR Target | Diaguita Employment |
|------------|--------|---------|------------|---------------------|
| **Pisco Denomination of Origin (DO) Facility** | Elqui | US$8M | 18% | 45 (production, tourism) |
| **Table Grape Cold Chain Hub** | Limarí | US$12M | 22% | 60 (logistics, QC) |
| **Citrus Essential Oil Distillery** | Choapa | US$6M | 20% | 35 (processing, lab) |
| **Avocado/Ice Cream Plant** | Huasco | US$5M | 19% | 30 (processing, retail) |

**Agro-Export Diversification Formula**:
$$\Delta GDP_{agro,v} = \sum_i \left( V_{product,i} \cdot \eta_{value\_add,i} \cdot A_{available,i} \right) - C_{facility,i}$$
Where $V_{product}$ = base commodity value, $\eta_{value\_add}$ = value-add multiplier (pisco DO: 3.5×, essential oil: 8×, cold chain: 1.8×, ice cream: 4×), $A_{available}$ = hectares convertible to high-value crop, $C_{facility}$ = facility capex + opex. **Projected ΔGDP: US$340M/yr across 4 valleys by Year 10**.

### 7.2 Astro-Tourism: Cerro Tololo, La Silla, Gemini

Elqui Valley hosts **world-class observatories** — **Cerro Tololo (CTIO), La Silla (ESO), Gemini South** — **+300 clear nights/yr, Bortle Class 2–3**. Current astro-tourism: **45,000 visitors/yr, US$12M revenue**. **Diaguita-led expansion**:

| Project | Investment | Capacity | Revenue/yr | Diaguita Jobs |
|---------|------------|----------|------------|---------------|
| **Diaguita Cosmovision Observatory** (cultural astronomy) | US$3M | 15,000 visitors | US$2.5M | 25 (guides, educators) |
| **Dark Sky Lodge Network** (4 valleys) | US$8M | 80 rooms | US$3.2M | 40 (hospitality) |
| **Astro-Photography Workshops** | US$0.5M | 500 participants | US$0.8M | 10 (instructors) |

**Astro-Tourism Revenue Formula**:
$$R_{astro} = \sum_v \left( N_{visitors,v} \cdot \bar{S}_{spend} \cdot \eta_{local\_capture} \right)$$
Where $N_{visitors}$ = annual visitors, $\bar{S}_{spend}$ = avg spend (US$180/day), $\eta_{local\_capture}$ = local retention (target 65% vs. current 35%). **Target: US$25M/yr by Year 8**.
