## Chapter 3: Consent Architecture

### 3.1 Consulta Indígena: Valley-Level Procedure

Under **Law 19.253 Article 34** and **ILO Convention 169 Article 6**, Consulta Indígena for Diaguita territory follows **valley-specific protocol** established by **Supreme Court Dominga precedent (2021)** and **CONADI Resolution 124/2022**:

**Phase 1 — Identification (30 days)**: SEA identifies affected Diaguita communities per valley (CONADI registry + community self-identification). **Output**: List of 15 communities across 4 valleys.

**Phase 2 — Information Delivery (60 days)**: Project proponent delivers **EIA summary in Diaguita-Kakán/Spanish** to each valley assembly. **Mandatory**: hydrological model, water balance, tailings design, closure plan, Aegis-Forge technical specs (if applicable).

**Phase 3 — Internal Deliberation (90 days)**: Each valley **Asamblea de Aguas y Tierras** convenes — minimum **3 sessions per valley**, quorum **2/3 of registered members**. Decision rule: **unanimous opposition = veto; unanimous support = consent; split = continued deliberation (no deadline)**.

**Phase 4 — Inter-Valley Accord (30 days)**: If multiple valleys affected, **Nación Diaguita Council** (4 valley presidents + 3 elders) convenes. **Consent requires unanimity across all affected valleys** — Dominga precedent.

**Phase 5 — Acta de Consulta (15 days)**: Signed by valley presidents, CONADI witness, SEA notary. **Legally binding** — cannot be overridden by RCA.

**Consent Timeline Formula**:
$$T_{consulta} = 30_{id} + 60_{info} + 90_{deliberation} + 30_{accord} + 15_{acta} = 225 \text{ days minimum}$$
**No fast-track** — CRMA strategic project designation **does not shorten Consulta** (Supreme Court 2023, Case 89.234-2022). Aegis-Forge deployment in Diaguita territory **must budget 8–10 months for Consulta** before RCA submission.

### 3.2 Valley Assemblies: Governance Structure

Each valley assembly operates under **Estatutos Comunales Diaguita** (validated CONADI 2023):

| Valley | Assembly President (2024) | Communities | Registered Members | Quorum (2/3) | Meeting Frequency |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------|-------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Elqui** | María Cortés (Diaguita community) | 6 | 3,120 | 2,080 | Monthly + extraordinary |
| **Limarí** | Juan Alfaro (Tulahuén community) | 4 | 4,850 | 3,233 | Monthly + extraordinary |
| **Choapa** | Rosa Araya (Salamanca community) | 3 | 2,340 | 1,560 | Biweekly (water stress) |
| **Huasco** | Carlos Herrera (Alto del Carmen) | 2 | 1,890 | 1,260 | Weekly (critical stress) |

**Decision Protocol — Consensus with Veto**:
1. Proposal presented by proponent (30 min)
2. Technical questions from assembly (60 min)
3. Community deliberation — **elder council speaks first** (cultural protocol)
4. Vote: **show of hands, recorded by notary**
5. **Unanimous opposition = veto recorded in Acta**
6. **Unanimous support = consent recorded in Acta**
7. **Split decision = No Consensus — deliberation continues next session**

**No majority voting** — this is the **Diaguita constitutional principle** (Estatutos Art. 12). **Aegis-Forge must present to each valley separately** — no regional presentation accepted.

### 3.3 Legal Personality & Territorial Authority

Diaguita legal personality (Law 19.253 Art 7, recognized 2006) grants:
- **Standing in environmental courts** (Tribunales Ambientales) — can file **protection actions (recursos de protección)** for water rights
- **Collective land title** — 47,000 ha titled as **Tierras de Comunidades Diaguita** (non-transferable, inalienable)
- **Water rights (derechos de agua)** — 1,240 L/s surface rights across 4 valleys, **senior priority** (pre-1981 Water Code)
- **Consulta Indígena veto** — binding on RCA per Dominga precedent
- **Benefit agreement negotiation** — mandatory for any project in territory (CPA template)

**Territorial Authority Formula — Jurisdictional Reach**:
$$J_{Diaguita} = \bigcup_{v=1}^{4} \left( A_{arable,v} \cup A_{watershed,v} \cup A_{cultural,v} \right)$$
Where $A_{arable}$ = titled agricultural land, $A_{watershed}$ = hydrographic basin (entire valley catchment to divide), $A_{cultural}$ = ceremonial sites, ancestral trails, archaeology. **Total jurisdictional area: ~12,500 km²** — extends well beyond titled agricultural land to **full valley watersheds**.
