### 1.5 Diaguita Social Organization: Kinship, Authority, Decision-Making

The Diaguita social structure is **matrilineal kinship-based** with **territorial segmentation by valley** — distinct from Atacameño ayllu (patrilineal, high-altitude pastoral) and Mapuche lof (patrilineal, forest/riverine). Core units:

| Unit | Scale | Authority | Function |
|------|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Familia Extendida** | 15–50 persons | Madre Mayor (eldest woman) | Land use, water shares, ritual |
| **Comunidad** | 200–5,000 persons | Presidente + Consejo de Ancianos | Valley assembly delegate, water governance |
| **Valle (Cuenca)** | 2,000–12,000 persons | Asamblea de Aguas y Tierras | Consulta Indígena, territorial defense, FBC/CDC governance |
| **Nación Diaguita** | 29,000+ persons | Consejo Nacional (4 valley presidents + 3 elders) | Inter-valley coordination, national policy, CONADI liaison |

**Decision-Making Protocol — Consensus with Elder Veto**:
1. Proposal enters at **comunidad level** — discussed in **reunión familiar** (family meeting)
2. Comunidad president presents to **valley assembly** — **elder council speaks first** (cultural protocol)
3. Deliberation continues until **consensus or clear opposition**
4. **Elder veto** (any 2 of 3 elders per valley) = **automatic rejection** — cannot be overridden
5. **Unanimous consent** = recorded in **Acta de Asamblea** — legally binding

This **multi-scale consensus protocol** is the **operational expression of El Segundo's Participation heuristic** — the inside operator (elder, water manager, family head) **witnesses the decision into existence** at each scale. No decision is valid without **witness at all four scales**.
