### N.1 DGA Resolution 1238/2018 — Ecological Flow Regime

**Key Provisions Applied to Diaguita Valleys**:

| Valley | River | Q_mean (m³/s) | Q_eco (15%) | Q_eco (m³/s) | Monitoring Point |
|--------|-------|---------------|-------------|--------------|------------------|
| **Elqui** | Río Elqui | 18.5 | 15% | **2.78** | La Laguna (DGA) + Community sentinel |
| **Limarí** | Río Limarí | 22.3 | 15% | **3.35** | Recoleta (DGA) + Community sentinel |
| **Choapa** | Río Choapa | 14.7 | 15% | **2.21** | Salamanca (DGA) + Community sentinel |
| **Huasco** | Río Huasco | 8.9 | 15% | **1.34** | Huasco Bajo (DGA) + Community sentinel |

**Compliance Formula**:
$$C_{eco} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbb{I}(Q_{measured,i} \geq Q_{eco}) \geq 0.95$$
Where $\mathbb{I}$ = indicator function (1 if true, 0 if false), $N$ = number of 15-min intervals in month. **95% compliance required monthly**.

**Diaguita Sentinel Role**: **Community data = official compliance evidence** — **DGA Resolution 1238/2018 Article 7** recognizes **community monitoring as valid for enforcement**.

### N.2 Water Code Article 56 bis — Human Consumption Priority

**Text (Law 21.435 Amendment)**:
> En caso de sequía declarada, la Dirección General de Aguas podrá restringir o suspender el ejercicio de los derechos de aprovechamiento de aguas, dando prioridad al consumo humano, al saneamiento y a la subsistencia de ecosistemas, sobre cualquier otro uso.

**Diaguita Legal Argument (Water Tribunal Case 45.678-2023)**:
1. **Agriculture = subsistence** for Diaguita (cultural survival, not commercial only)
2. **Mining = commercial** — lowest priority
3. **Pre-1981 rights not absolute** — subject to Art 56 bis in declared drought
4. **Megadrought (2010–present) = declared drought** (DGA Decree 124/2021, renewed annually)
5. **Therefore**: **Diaguita agricultural rights > CAP mining rights** in drought

**Expected Precedent**: **If Diaguita prevail, all Chilean mining water rights subordinated to Indigenous agriculture in drought** — **transformational**.

### N.3 Aegis-Forge Zero Net Water Covenant — Legal Form

**Trust Deed Schedule 3 — Water Covenant**:

> **3.1 Zero Net Valley Water Withdrawal**: The Project shall not withdraw any net volume of surface water or groundwater from the Valley watershed. All process water shall be sourced from: (a) Desalination plants funded by the WSF; (b) Atmospheric Water Generation (AWG) units; (c) Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) recycle within the Project.
>
> **3.2 Real-Time Monitoring**: The Project shall install and maintain SCADA-connected flow meters at all water intake and discharge points. Data shall be transmitted in real-time to: (a) DGA monitoring platform; (b) Diaguita Valley Trust dashboard (public access for community members); (c) Sernageomin environmental compliance system.
>
> **3.3 Community Sentinel Access**: The Valley Trust shall designate up to 5 Community Sentinels per valley who shall have: (a) Read-only access to Project SCADA water data; (b) Right to collect monthly grab samples at Project intake/discharge; (c) Authority to trigger automatic Project water suspension upon documented exceedance of trigger levels (As > 0.01 mg/L, SO₄ > 250 mg/L, flow < Q_eco for >7 consecutive days).
>
> **3.4 Breach Consequences**: Any breach of Section 3.1 shall result in: (a) Automatic 5% equity dilution from Project SPA to Valley Trust FBC; (b) Mandatory independent audit at Project expense; (c) Suspension of Project operations until cure verified by DGA + Diaguita hydrologist.
>
> **3.5 Force Majeure**: Only **Act of God (earthquake >Mw 8.0, tsunami)** excuses breach. **Drought, equipment failure, regulatory change = not force majeure**.
