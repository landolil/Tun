### 3.4 Consulta Indígena: Step-by-Step Procedural Guide

**For Aegis-Forge Proponents — Diaguita Consulta Checklist**:

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Deliverable | Diaguita Gate |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **1** | Identify affected communities (CONADI + self-ID) | SEA + Proponent | Day 1–30 | Community list (15) | Valley presidents confirm |
| **2** | Prepare Information Package (EIA summary, water model, Aegis-Forge specs, FBC/CDC terms) | Proponent | Day 31–60 | Package in Spanish + Diaguita-Kakán | Elder council reviews translation |
| **3** | Deliver to each valley assembly | SEA | Day 61 | Receipt signed by valley president | Assembly acknowledges receipt |
| **4** | Valley Assembly deliberation (min 3 sessions) | Asamblea de Aguas y Tierras | Day 62–151 | Minutes, attendance, vote record | **Unanimous opposition = veto** |
| **5** | Nación Diaguita Council inter-valley accord | 4 valley presidents + 3 elders | Day 152–181 | Acta de Acuerdo Inter-Valle | **Unanimity across affected valleys** |
| **6** | Acta de Consulta finalization | SEA notary + CONADI witness | Day 182–225 | **Acta de Consulta (legally binding)** | **Cannot be overridden** |
| **7** | Submit Acta with RCA | Proponent | Day 226+ | RCA application + Acta | SEA validates Acta authenticity |

**Critical Path Variants**:
- **Single valley affected**: Steps 1–4 + 6 (skip 5) = **195 days minimum**
- **All 4 valleys affected**: Full 225 days
- **Split decision in any valley**: **Deliberation continues indefinitely** — no statutory deadline (Estatutos Art 12)

### 3.6 Consent Architecture — El Segundo Mirror/Participation/Protocol Analysis

**Mirror (Self-Reflection)**: **How does the Diaguita community observe its own consent process?**
- **Acta de Asamblea** notarized, digitized, archived in **Diaguita Digital Archive (Universidad de La Serena)**
- **Real-time attendance tracking** — QR code check-in at assembly sessions
- **Decision dashboard** — public (community-only) portal showing proposal status, deliberation history, votes
- **Elder council audit** — annual review of all Actas for procedural integrity

**Participation (Witness)**: **How does the inside operator witness the phenomenon?**
- **Water managers (regantes)** witness flow measurements at canal intakes — **daily ritual**
- **Elders witness** consent deliberations — **physical presence mandatory** (no proxy)
- **Youth witnesses** — **apprenticeship requires attendance at 10+ assemblies**
- **Cultural monitors** witness construction — **on-site, real-time authority to stop work**

**Protocol (Recursive)**: **What recursive sequence guarantees fidelity?**
1. **Family meeting** → 2. **Comunidad assembly** → 3. **Valley assembly** → 4. **Nación Council** → 5. **Acta notarized** → 6. **Archive digitized** → 7. **Annual elder audit** → **back to 1**
- **Self-similar at each scale**: consensus rule, elder veto, notarized record
- **Fractal QC**: family consensus mirrors valley consensus mirrors national consensus
