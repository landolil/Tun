**MAR Effectiveness Formula**:
$$\eta_{MAR} = \frac{Q_{recovered}}{Q_{injected}} \cdot \frac{C_{target}}{C_{ambient}} \cdot (1 - L_{seepage})$$
Where $Q_{recovered}$ = water recovered at downstream wells, $C_{target}$ = target quality (irrigation standard), $C_{ambient}$ = ambient aquifer quality, $L_{seepage}$ = conveyance losses. **Target $\eta_{MAR} > 0.75$** for all projects.

### 6.5 Desalination Co-Investment Model — WSF

**WSF Desalination Co-Investment Structure**:
- **WSF provides**: 60% capex (equity), land, permits, community liaison
- **Private partner provides**: 40% capex (debt), EPC, O&M (15-yr contract)
- **Offtake**: **Valley agricultural users + Aegis-Forge clusters** — **take-or-pay at marginal cost**
- **Governance**: **Diaguita majority board** — sets tariff, approves expansions
- **Brine management**: **Zero ocean discharge** — brine crystallized, salts sold (NaCl, MgCl₂, CaCl₂)

**Choapa Desal (Puerto Los Vilos) Financial Model**:
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Capacity | 500 L/s (43,200 m³/day) |
| Capex | US$180M (WSF: US$108M equity, Partner: US$72M debt) |

| Tariff (agriculture) | US$0.35/m³ (subsidized by WSF equity return) |
| Tariff (Aegis-Forge) | US$0.65/m³ (full cost recovery) |
| IRR (WSF equity) | 12% (20-yr concession) |
| Water Security Value | **Avoided agricultural loss: US$28M/yr** (Choapa valley) |

**Sentinel Protocol**:
1. **Community members trained** (DTI Hydrology program) — **certified water samplers**
2. **Monthly grab samples** — lab analysis (DGA accredited) — **cross-check telemetry**
3. **Quarterly community data review** — valley assembly presentation
4. **Trigger levels** — exceedance → **automatic alert to valley president + DGA + Sernageomin**
5. **Veto trigger** — **sustained exceedance (7 days) = automatic project water suspension**
