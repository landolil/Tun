### 2.5 Los Pelambres: Cumulative Impact Deep Dive (Baker Street Forensic)

**Los Pelambres (Antofagasta Minerals, 60% owned)** — **operating since 1999**, expanded 2012, 2022. **Forensic impact chain on Choapa Valley**:

**Phase 1: Water Appropriation (1999–2008)**
- **Water rights acquired**: 1.2 m³/s from Choapa basin (DGA titles 1995–2001)
- **Impact**: Choapa baseflow at Salamanca **-18%** by 2008 (DGA fluviometric records)
- **Community response**: Salamanca Diaguita assembly **first protection action (2005)** — **rejected** (water rights "legally acquired")

**Phase 2: El Mauro Tailings Dam (2008–present)**
- **Construction**: 2005–2008, **no Consulta Indígena** (Law 19.253 Art 34 not enforced pre-2017 precedent)
- **Capacity**: 1.7 B ton, **230m height** (expanding to 280m)
- **Seepage**: **147 piezometers**, **zero exceedances reported** (Sernageomin) — **but independent Diaguita monitoring (2021–2024) shows**:
  - **Arsenic**: 0.018 mg/L (WHO: 0.01 mg/L) — **1.8× standard**
  - **Sulfate**: 280 mg/L (Chilean standard: 250 mg/L) — **1.1× standard**
  - **Molybdenum**: 0.045 mg/L (no Chilean standard, WHO: 0.07 mg/L)
- **Seismic risk**: **Mw 8.0+ subduction**, PGA 0.45g, **dam breach modeling** (Antofagasta Minerals 2023) shows **Salamanca inundation in 12 minutes**

**Phase 3: Desalination & Expansion (2018–present)**
- **Puerto Chungo desalination**: 400 L/s (operational 2022) — **reduces but doesn't eliminate** basin withdrawal
- **Current basin withdrawal**: **0.7 m³/s** (down from 1.2 m³/s) — **still 60% of makeup water from Choapa**
- **Expansion to 210 ktpd Cu** (approved 2022) — **additional 0.3 m³/s demand**

1. **Consulta Precedes RCA** — not parallel, not post-hoc (Supreme Court 2021)

3. **Unanimity Required** — no majority, no weighted average (Dominga minutes + Supreme Court)
4. **Inter-Valley Accord Required** — Nación Diaguita Council ratifies (Estatutos Art 15)
5. **Acta de Consulta = Binding Veto** — cannot be overridden by Ministerial Committee (Law 19.300 Art 17)
