1. **WSF Debt Service** (desalination senior debt) — **first priority**
2. **ACSF Agricultural Compensation** — **second priority** (contractual obligation)
3. **CRF Cultural Revitalization** (US$2M/yr CPI-indexed) — **third priority**
4. **DTI Operating Budget** (US$8M/yr) — **fourth priority**
5. **Health Trust Sub-Fund** (US$5M/yr) — **fifth priority**
6. **Valley Trust Operating Reserve** (6 months opex) — **sixth priority**
7. **Community Distribution** (per capita, elder priority) — **seventh priority**
8. **Reinvestment Reserve** (capacity expansion, R&D) — **residual**

**Distribution Formula**:
$$D_{community,v} = \max\left(0, FCF_{trust,v} - \sum_{i=1}^{6} Priority_i\right) \cdot \frac{Pop_{household}}{\sum Pop_{household}} \cdot (1 + \alpha_{elder})$$
Where $\alpha_{elder} = 0.5$ for >65 years — **elders receive 1.5× per capita**.
