## Appendix BB: Social License — Longitudinal Tracking Framework

### BB.1 Social License Index (SLI) — Quarterly Measurement

**SLI Formula** (validated by Diaguita Asamblea + Universidad Católica del Norte):
$$SLI_v(t) = 0.30 \cdot W_{trust} + 0.25 \cdot E_{benefit} + 0.20 \cdot C_{integrity} + 0.15 \cdot H_{health} + 0.10 \cdot G_{governance}$$

**Component Indicators**:

| Component | Indicator | Measurement | Target |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **W_trust (Water Trust)** | % community believing water is protected | Quarterly survey (n=200/valley) | >80% |
| **E_benefit (Economic Benefit)** | % households with Aegis-income > poverty line | Annual household survey | >70% |
| **C_integrity (Cultural Integrity)** | Kakán speakers + ceremony participation | CONADI + CRF reports | >60% participation |
| **H_health (Health)** | Silicosis/arsenic biomarkers vs baseline | MINSAL + community screening | <50% baseline |
| **G_governance (Governance)** | Assembly attendance + trust transparency | Notarized Actas + financial audits | >75% attendance |

**Baseline (2024, Pre-Aegis)**:
| Valley | W_trust | E_benefit | C_integrity | H_health | G_governance | **SLI** |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------------|----------|--------------|---------|
| **Elqui** | 35% | 28% | 45% | 40% | 55% | **0.40** |
| **Limarí** | 30% | 32% | 42% | 38% | 52% | **0.38** |
| **Choapa** | 15% | 25% | 38% | 20% | 45% | **0.28** |
| **Huasco** | 18% | 22% | 40% | 35% | 48% | **0.31** |

**Target (Year 5)**:
| Valley | W_trust | E_benefit | C_integrity | H_health | G_governance | **SLI** |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------------|----------|--------------|---------|
| **All** | >80% | >70% | >60% | >60% (improving) | >75% | **>0.70** |

**Social License Gate**: **SLI < 0.50 for 2 consecutive quarters = automatic project pause** — **written into FBC/CDC trust deeds**.

### BB.2 Grievance Mechanism — Diaguita-Led

**Three-Tier Grievance Process**:

| Tier | Forum | Timeline | Authority | Appeal |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **1** | **Valley Assembly** (monthly) | 30 days | Asamblea de Aguas y Tierras | To Tier 2 |
| **2** | **Nación Diaguita Council** | 60 days | 4 valley presidents + 3 elders | To Tier 3 |
| **3** | **Independent Mediator** (Chilean Arbitration Center) | 90 days | Binding arbitration | Courts of Justice |

**Grievance Categories & SLA**:
- **Water quality/quantity**: 15 days (emergency: 48 hours)
- **Cultural site impact**: 30 days (immediate stop-work if sacred site)
- **Employment discrimination**: 30 days
- **Trust financial transparency**: 60 days (quarterly audit cycle)
- **Health impact**: 60 days (epidemiological investigation)

**No corporate-controlled grievance mechanism** — **Diaguita-owned, Diaguita-operated, Diaguita-funded (CRF)**.
