## Chapter 1: Austral — Historical Iron, Coal, Volcanic, Ion-Adsorption, Kawéskar/Yagán Gateway

### 1.1 Austral Origin and Purpose — Historical Fe, Coal, Volcanic, Ion-Adsorption, Kawéskar/Yagán

The Austral region (Aysén, Magallanes) is Chile's **southern frontier** — hosting **historical magnetite skarns** (Aysén), **Patagonian coal measures** (Magallanes), **Fuegian volcanic epithermal systems**, **novel glacial/fluvial ion-adsorption REE clays**, and the **Antarctic gateway logistics hub** at Punta Arenas. This region bridges Chilean territory and Antarctic operations, with unique maritime Indigenous peoples and emerging green hydrogen corridors.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: The Chilean State owns all mineral resources (Constitution Art. 19§24). Austral contains **~8% of Chile's historical magnetite resources** and **~15% of novel Patagonian ion-adsorption REE potential** (DOC-01 refinement). The **Kawéskar (canoe nomads), Yagán (canoe nomads), Aonikenk (foot nomads), and Selk'nam (foot nomads)** hold **constitutional territorial rights** (Law 19.253) — Kawéskar/Yagán maritime, Aonikenk/Selk'nam terrestrial. Every district requires differentiated Consulta Indígena.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **maritime nomad consent logistics** — DOC-03/DOC-17/DOC-19 address terrestrial communities; Phase 4 requires canoe-based territorial consent models for Kawéskar/Yagán fjord/archipelago territories.

**SHIELD Horizon**: Austral is the **frontier integration zone** (8-15 years) for Aegis-Forge Alpha clusters at historical sites + Bravo ion-adsorption clusters. Decisions here define 2032 Antarctic Treaty consultative status leverage and 2036 green hydrogen-mineral corridor maturity.

#### 1.1.2 Document Purpose

This document translates national-scale intelligence (DOC-01..19) into **regional deployment intelligence** for Austral:

- **Historical district repurposing** for Aegis-Forge (Aysén Fe, Magallanes coal)
- **Fuegian volcanic epithermal deployment** — Au-Ag-REE in Tierra del Fuego

- **GIC hardening** per DOC-11 for SEN Austral (Charrúa 500 kV → Punta Arenas 220 kV isolation)
- **Green hydrogen-mineral integration** — ENAP HIF project, Puerto Exterior Magallanes, Antarctic supply
