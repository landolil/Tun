**Community Relations Mandate (Law 19.542 Art. 14)**:
- **Consejo de Desarrollo Puerto-Ciudad** — Quarterly, 15 members (municipal, GORE, unions, NGOs, indigenous, academia, neighbors)
- **Plan de Relacionamiento Comunitario** — Annual, public consultation, mitigation commitments
- **Fondo de Desarrollo Portuario (1% revenue)** — US$1.1M/yr → community projects (noise, air, traffic, heritage)
