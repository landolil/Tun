## 4.2 Technology Cluster — Innovation in the Desert

### 4.2.1 Institutional Architecture — The Antofagasta Mining Tech Ecosystem

```
ANTOFAGASTA MINING TECH CLUSTER — 2024 MAP

ANCHOR INSTITUTIONS
├── **CICITEM** (Centro de Investigación Científica y Tecnológica para la Minería)
│   ├── Budget: US$8.5M/yr (CORFO 60%, Private 30%, Regional 10%)
│   ├── Researchers: 42 PhD, 28 MSc, 15 technicians
│   ├── Labs: Autonomous Systems, Sensor Networks, Tailings Reprocessing, Water Efficiency
│   ├── Pilot Plant: 50 t/d concentrator (test new reagents, circuits, automation)
│   └── Spin-offs: 7 (2015–2024) — **Automin, SensorTech, HydroMet, TailingsRe, WaterSense, FiberMine, DataRock**
│
├── **PTI Minería** (Programa Transforma Industria — CORFO)
│   ├── Budget: US$5.2M/yr
│   ├── Focus: Tech transfer, supplier development, startup acceleration
│   ├── Programs: **Proveedores 4.0, Minería Verde, Capital Humano, Internacionalización**
│   ├── Startup Portfolio: 24 active (12 mining, 6 water, 6 energy)
│   └── Metrics: 340 suppliers upgraded, US$180M new contracts, 1,200 jobs
│
├── **Universidad de Antofagasta (UA) — Facultad de Ingeniería**
│   ├── Mining Engineering: 420 students, 35 faculty
│   ├── Research Centers: **CEMES (Mining-Metallurgy), CIEAM (Water), CIGIDEN (Disaster)**
│   ├── Industry Chairs: **BHP Chair, Codelco Chair, Antofagasta Minerals Chair**
│   └── Continuing Education: 450 professionals/yr (diplomas, masters)
│
├── **Universidad Católica del Norte (UCN) — Escuela de Ingeniería**
│   ├── Geology: 280 students, **Atacama Desert focus**
│   ├── Metallurgy: **Hydrometallurgy Lab (REE, Li, Cu)**
│   ├── Research: **CEDES (Sustainable Development), CICYT (Tech Transfer)**
│   └── Community Link: **Chango/Atacameño co-research protocols**
│
└── **CCTVal (Valparaíso) — Antofagasta Node**
    ├── Particle Physics → **Muon Tomography for Ore Body Imaging**
    ├── Radiation Detection → **Tailings/Environmental Monitoring**
    └── Computing → **AI/ML for Process Optimization**

COLLABORATION PLATFORMS
├── **Mesa Minería 4.0** (Monthly: Codelco, BHP, Antofagasta Minerals, CICITEM, PTI, UA, UCN)
├── **Clúster Minero Antofagasta** (120 firms, monthly networking, joint R&D)
├── **Expomin / Exponor** (Biennial — Latin America's largest mining expo, Antofagasta)
└── **International Partnerships**: **CSIRO (Aus), Fraunhofer (Ger), NRCan (Can), US DOE, JOGMEC (Jpn)**
```
