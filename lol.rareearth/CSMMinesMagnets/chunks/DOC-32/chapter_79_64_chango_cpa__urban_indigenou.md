## 6.4 Chango CPA — Urban Indigenous Consent Authority

### 6.4.1 Consejo de Comunidades Costeras Chango — Structure

| Body | Composition | Authority |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| **Asamblea General Chango** | **8 Loncos Costeros (caletas) + 3 Voceros Urbanos (barrios)** | Sovereign — Consent decisions |
| **Consejo Directivo** | Lonco Principal + Vice + Secretario + Tesorero + 4 Vocales | Executive — Implements consent |
| **Comisión Mar** | 3 pescadores mayores + 2 buzos + 1 alguero + 1 navegante | Marine impact assessment |
| **Comisión Territorio** | 2 abogados + 1 antropólogo + 1 ingeniero costero + 1 comunicador | Legal/technical analysis |
| **Comisión Cultura** | 3 kimün costero + 1 educador + 1 artesano | Cultural heritage, language, education |
| **Guardacostas Chango** | 12 monitores (2 por caleta/barrio) | **Participatory monitoring (SUBPESCA validated)** |

### 6.4.2 Consent Protocol — Coastal Specificity (Recap from Ch 3)

**Trigger**: Any coastal/marine project in SEA (RCA), DGA marine permit, SUBPESCA concession, EMPORCHA concession, DIRECTEMAR authorization.

**Timeline**: **55 days** (Notification 10d + Deliberation 30d + Assembly 15d + Legal 10d)

**Veto Power**: **Absolute** — Project cannot proceed without Chango consent. Only reversible by new Assembly with new marine facts.

**Condition Precedents (Standard)**:
1. **Marine Baseline** — 2 years pre-project (fisheries, benthos, water quality, mammals, birds)
2. **Participatory Monitoring** — Guardacostas Chango on all surveys, real-time data access
3. **Benefit Sharing** — Minimum 2% project revenue to Chango Development Fund
4. **Cultural Protection** — Sacred sites (caletas ancestrales, ceremonial zones) exclusion buffers
5. **Cumulative Impact Assessment** — Not just project, but **port + desal + H₂ + cable + aquaculture combined**
