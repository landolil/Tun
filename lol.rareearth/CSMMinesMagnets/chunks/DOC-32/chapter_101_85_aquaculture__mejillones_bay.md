**Environmental Benefit**:
- **Brine Dilution**: 1,000 L/s brine → **1:50 dilution** in bay (vs 1:10 direct discharge)
- **Nutrient Removal**: Algae uptake N/P from bay (mining/urban runoff)
- **Carbon Sequestration**: 15,000 t algae = **~22,000 t CO₂/yr**
- **Habitat Creation**: Shellfish reefs → biodiversity, fisheries enhancement
