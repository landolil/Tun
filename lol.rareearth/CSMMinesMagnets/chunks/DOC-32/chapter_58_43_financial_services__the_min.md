### 4.3.2 Supply Chain Finance — The Contractor Lifeline

**Factoring Flow (Monthly Average)**:
```
MINING COMPANY (Codelco/BHP/Antofagasta Minerals)
    │
    ├── Pays Tier 1 at 30 days (mandated)
    │
    ├── Pays Tier 2 at 45 days (contractual)
    │
    └── Pays Tier 3 at 65 days (reality) ← **PAIN POINT**
            │
            ▼
    FACTORING COMPANY (Banco Estado / FinTech)
    ├── Advances 85% invoice value at Day 5
    ├── Cost: 1.2–1.8%/month (15–22% APR)
    ├── Recourse: **Non-recourse (mining company credit risk)**
    └── Volume: **US$35M/month** (Tier 2/3 invoices)
            │
            ▼
    TIER 2/3 CONTRACTOR
    ├── Receives cash at Day 5 (vs Day 65)
    ├── Funds payroll, fuel, supplies, taxes
    ├── Cost absorbed in bid (margin compression 2–3%)
    └── **Enables survival/growth**
```
