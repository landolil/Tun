## 6.5 Unions — Port, Mining Contractors, Municipal

### 6.5.1 Port Unions — The Dockworker Power

| Union | Affiliation | Members | Coverage | Key Agreements |
|-------|-------------|---------|----------|----------------|
| **Sindicato N°1 Trabajadores Portuarios ATI** | CUT | **1,100** | ATI terminal | **Contrato Colectivo 2023–2026** — 4.2% real, safety bonus, automation clause |
| **Sindicato N°2 Trabajadores Portuarios ATI** | CUT | 450 | ATI terminal (admin, maintenance) | Contrato Colectivo 2024–2027 — career path, remote ops training |
| **Sindicato Trabajadores Portezuelo** | CUT | 750 | Portezuelo terminal | Contrato Colectivo 2022–2025 — dust hazard pay, medical surveillance |
| **Sindicato EMPORCHA (Directos)** | CUT | 320 | Port authority direct | Contrato Colectivo 2023–2026 — port governance participation |
| **Federación Portuaria Antofagasta** | CUT | **2,620** | All port workers | **Negociación Ramal Portuaria** — industry standards |

**Port Union Power**: **Strategic choke point** — 48hr strike = **US$45M/day** export losses. **Automation clause (2023)**: No forced displacement, retraining guarantee, early retirement option.
