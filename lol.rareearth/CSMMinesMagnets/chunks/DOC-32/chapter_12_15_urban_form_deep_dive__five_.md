## 1.5 Urban Form Deep Dive — Five Sectors, One Fragmented Coast

### 1.5.1 Sectoral Morphology & Spatial Inequality

| Sector | Area (km²) | Density (pop/km²) | Housing Type | Green Space (m²/cap) | Commute Time (avg) | Land Value (UF/m²) |
|--------|------------|-------------------|--------------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Centro** | 8.2 | 10,365 | Mixed: historic, apartments, commercial | 2.1 | 25 min | 85–120 |

**Spatial Gini Coefficient**: **0.52** (higher than Santiago's 0.46) — **extreme spatial segregation**

### 1.5.2 Centro — The Historic Core Under Pressure

**Morphology**: Grid plan (1870s), **Victorian/Neoclassical heritage** (Customs House, Intendencia, Teatro Municipal, British Club), **pedestrian streets** (Prat, Baquedano, Matta).

**Current Dynamics**:
| Indicator | Value | Trend |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Residential Population** | 12,000 (declining -2%/yr) | Conversion to offices/short-term rental |
| **Commercial Vacancy** | 18% (post-COVID) | Mall Plaza dominance, e-commerce |
| **Heritage Buildings at Risk** | 47 (Category 1-2) | Seismic vulnerability, maintenance cost |
| **Daytime Population** | 85,000 (swells 7×) | Transport collapse 8-9am, 6-7pm |
| **Parking Deficit** | 3,200 spaces | Surface lots → tower pressure |

**Revitalization Plan (Municipal 2024–2028)**:
- **Peatonalización**: 12 blocks car-free by 2026
- **Vivienda Centro**: 1,500 units adaptive reuse (subsidized 30%)
- **Patrimonio Activo**: Tax credit for seismic retrofit + heritage preservation
- **Ciclovía Costera**: 8 km connecting Coloso → Centro → La Portada

### 1.5.3 Norte — The University & Services Hub

**Anchors**: **Universidad de Antofagasta (UA) 18,000 students**, **Universidad Católica del Norte (UCN) 12,000 students**, **Hospital Regional**, **Mall Plaza Antofagasta**, **CICITEM** (mining tech), **PTI** (tech transfer).

**Demographic Profile**:
- **Student Population**: 30,000 (40% from other regions) — **transient, rental demand**
- **Academic/Research Workers**: 4,500 (high-income, 65% own housing)
- **Service Workers**: 12,000 (commute from Sur/La Chimba)

**Land Use Conflict**: **Student housing vs. family housing** — rents +45% since 2019, displacement to La Chimba.

### 1.5.4 Sur — The Industrial-Port Interface

**Facilities**:
| Facility | Operator | Capacity | Workers | Environmental Impact |
|----------|----------|----------|---------|---------------------|
| **Terminal ATI** | Antofagasta Terminal Internacional | 2.5M TEU/yr | 1,200 direct | Noise, PM10, light, traffic |
| **Terminal Portezuelo** | Portezuelo S.A. | 1.8M ton/yr bulk | 800 direct | Dust (Cu concentrate), runoff |
| **Terminal Multi-propósito** | EMPORCHA | 3.5M ton/yr general | 600 direct | Mixed cargo, chemical risk |
| **FCAB Rail Yard** | FCAB | 12M ton/yr throughput | 400 direct | Diesel, noise, barrier effect |
| **Fuel Terminals** | ENAP, Copec, Shell | 800k m³ storage | 200 direct | Spill risk, vapor emissions |
| **Desal Plants (3)** | Aguas Antofagasta | **1,800 L/s total** | 150 direct | Brine discharge, energy use |

**Community Impact (Sur Residents, n=2,400 survey 2023)**:
- **Air Quality Concern**: 78% (PM10 24-hr avg 65 μg/m³ vs WHO 45)
- **Noise Complaints**: 62% (port 24/7 ops, rail shunting)
- **Tsunami Anxiety**: 85% (no vertical evacuation, single route)
- **Employment Access**: 34% work in port/logistics, **but 68% contractor (precarious)**

### 1.5.6 Coloso — The New Elite Enclave

**Development Pattern**: **Gated communities (12), country clubs (3), golf course (1), marina (planned)** — **1990s onward**.

**Demographics**:
- **Population**: 50,000 (12% commune)
- **Mining Executive/Professional**: 55%
- **Expat/Retiree**: 15%
- **Service Workers (live-in)**: 20%
- **Migrant**: 10%

**Resource Consumption (Per Capita vs Commune Avg)**:
| Resource | Coloso | Commune Avg | Ratio |
|----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Water (L/day)** | **850** | 220 | **3.9×** |
| **Electricity (kWh/mo)** | **1,200** | 380 | **3.2×** |
| **Vehicle Trips/day** | **4.2** | 1.8 | **2.3×** |
| **Waste (kg/day)** | **2.8** | 1.1 | **2.5×** |

**Governance Tension**: **Coloso Juntas Vecinales** demand same municipal services (security, streets, parks) but **pay 40% less property tax** (agricultural land valuation loophole).
