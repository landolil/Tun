**Logistics Chain — Calama to Asia**:
```
CALAMA (Chuqui/RT/MH) → FCAB RAIL (900 km, 12 Mt/yr cap) → ANTOFAGASTA PORT
    ↓
CONCENTRATE PIPELINE (Escondida) → PORTESUELO TERMINAL
    ↓
REE CONTAINERS (Aegis-Forge Calama) → ATI TERMINAL (reefer/secure)
    ↓
NH₃ ISO TANKS (HIF Mejillones) → MEJILLONES TERMINAL
    ↓
VESSEL LOADING → PACIFIC ROUTES:
    ├── **China (Qingdao, Shanghai)** — Cu cathode, REE oxides (45 days)
    ├── **Japan (Tokyo, Nagoya)** — High-grade Cu, REE metals (40 days)
    ├── **Korea (Busan, Gwangyang)** — Cu concentrate, NH₃ (38 days)
    ├── **Europe (Rotterdam, Antwerp)** — REE magnets, specialty (35 days)
    └── **USA (Houston, Long Beach)** — DFARS-compliant REE (32 days)
```
