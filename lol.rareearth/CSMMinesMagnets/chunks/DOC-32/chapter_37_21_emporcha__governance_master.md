## 2.1 EMPORCHA — Governance, Master Plan & 2030 Capacity Targets

### 2.1.1 Institutional Architecture — The Port Authority Model

**EMPORCHA Legal Framework**:
| Legal Basis | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Law 19.542 (1997)** | Modernización del Estado — Empresas Portuarias del Estado |
| **DFL 1.302 (1998)** | Estatuto Orgánico EMPORCHA |
| **Concession Law (1995)** | Ley de Concesiones Marítimas — private terminal operation |
| **Port State Control** | DGTM (Dirección General del Territorio Marítimo) — SOLAS, MARPOL, IMSBC |

**Governance Structure (2024)**:
```
EMPORCHA GOVERNANCE

PRESIDENTE DE LA REPÚBLICA
    ↓ (appoints 3)
DIRECTORIO EMPORCHA (7 members)
├── 3 President Appointees (technical: port engineering, logistics, finance)
├── 2 GORE Antofagasta Representatives (regional development, environment)
├── 1 Worker Representative (elected by port workers unions)
└── 1 User Representative (elected by shipping lines, cargo owners)
    ↓
GERENTE GENERAL (appointed by Directorio, 4-yr term)
    ↓
GERENCIAS:
├── Operaciones (terminals, berths, yards, equipment)
├── Planificación y Desarrollo (master plan, capex, concessions)
├── Comercial y Concesiones (contracts, tariffs, business development)
├── Seguridad, Salud y Medio Ambiente (SSO, PCA, ISO 45001/14001)
├── Administración y Finanzas (budget, accounting, procurement)
└── Asuntos Corporativos (comunidades, gobiernos, prensa, sostenibilidad)
```

### 2.1.2 Master Plan 2030 — The $1.1B Transformation

**Strategic Objectives (Approved 2023)**:
1. **Capacity**: 14 Mt/yr total throughput (from 9.5 Mt/yr 2023)
2. **Diversification**: Cu concentrate <55%, REE 25%, NH₃/H₂ 15%, Containers 800k TEU
3. **Decarbonization**: Shore power 100% berths, electric yard equipment, H₂ tugboats
4. **Digitalization**: Port Community System (PCS), digital twin, predictive maintenance
5. **Resilience**: Tsunami vertical evacuation, climate adaptation, cyber-security

**Capital Investment Program (2024–2030)**:
| Project | Capex (US$M) | Timeline | Capacity Added | Strategic Driver |
|---------|--------------|----------|----------------|------------------|
| **Berths 5-7 (New Multi-Purpose)** | 320 | 2025–2028 | 3.5 Mt/yr | REE, NH₃, project cargo, cruise |
| **Dredging to -18m (Access Channel)** | 180 | 2025–2027 | Post-Panamax, Capesize | Larger vessels, NH₃ carriers |
| **Rail Access Upgrade (FCAB Link)** | 150 | 2024–2026 | 15 Mt/yr rail | Concentrate, REE, containers |
| **Shore Power (All 12 Berths)** | 85 | 2025–2029 | Zero hoteling emissions | IMO 2030, community health |
| **REE Secure Terminal (ATI Zone)** | 65 | 2026–2027 | 500k TEU secure | High-value, security, cold chain |
