## 1.7 Historical Trajectory — Four Cycles of Extraction, One City

### 1.7.1 Cycle 1: Nitrate Monopoly (1879–1930) — The British Engine

**The Nitrate City**:
| Metric | 1879 | 1913 (Peak) | 1930 (Crash) |
|--------|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Population** | 5,000 | **90,000** | 75,000 |
| **Nitrate Exports (Mt/yr)** | 0.3 | **2.8** | 0.4 |
| **Foreign Capital** | £2M | **£45M** | £8M |
| **Port Tonnage** | 0.5 Mt | **4.2 Mt** | 0.8 Mt |
| **Railway (km)** | 0 | **900 (FCAB)** | 900 |

**Social Structure (1913)**:
| Class | Population | Origin | Power |
|-------|------------|--------|-------|
| **British Elite** | 800 | England, Scotland | Nitrate companies, banks, clubs |
| **Chilean Elite** | 1,200 | Santiago, Valparaíso | Politics, land, commerce |
| **Middle Class** | 8,000 | Chile, Europe | Professionals, merchants, supervisors |
| **Working Class** | 45,000 | Chile, Bolivia, Peru | **Pampa nitrate workers (rotational)** |
| **Urban Poor** | 35,000 | Chile, migrants | Dockworkers, domestic, informal |

**Institutional Legacy**:
- **FCAB Railway** (1888) — **still operates**, 900 km, only private railway in Chile
- **Port Infrastructure** — Muelle Salitrero → modern terminals
- **Urban Grid** — Centro plan, Plaza Colón, civic buildings
- **Labor Movement** — **FOCh founded 1909** (Antofagasta), first general strike 1918
- **British Schools/Clubs** — Still operating (The British School, Club Hípico)

**The Bust (1930)**: **Haber-Bosch synthetic nitrate** → price -90% in 5 years. **Unemployment 60%**, population -17%, **radicalization → Communist Party stronghold**.

### 1.7.2 Cycle 2: Copper Industrialization (1950–1990) — The State Engine

**The Copper City**:
| Metric | 1950 | 1970 | 1990 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Population** | 90,000 | 180,000 | **230,000** |
| **Cu Production (kt/yr)** | 120 | 650 | **1,800** |
| **Major Mines** | Chuqui | Chuqui, Exotica | **Chuqui, Escondida, Spence, Zaldívar** |
| **State Share** | 0% | 51% (1969) | **100% (Codelco 1976)** |
| **Port Tonnage** | 1.2 Mt | 3.5 Mt | **6.8 Mt** |

**Key Transformations**:
1. **Chuquicamata Expansion (1950s–70s)** — Open pit scaling, company towns (Chuqui campamento)
2. **Chileanization (1969) → Nationalization (1971)** — Codelco formed, **Antofagasta becomes Codelco Norte headquarters**
3. **Escondida Discovery (1981) → Production (1990)** — **World's largest Cu mine**, 100% private (BHP/Rio Tinto), **changed revenue model** (royalty vs profit share)
4. **ZOFRI Creation (1994)** — **Zona Franca**, duty-free import/export, logistics hub, **attracted Asian commerce**

**Social Contract Shift**:
| Dimension | Nitrate Era | Copper Era (Codelco) | Copper Era (Private) |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Employment** | Rotational pampa camps | **Permanent, unionized, benefits** | Contractor model, precarious |
| **Housing** | Company camps | **Codelco housing (6,000 units)** | Market/self-built |
| **Health/Education** | Company clinics/schools | **Codelco integral (clinics, schools, clubs)** | Public/private mix |
| **Community Power** | Weak (company towns) | **Strong (unions, Codelco social)** | Fragmented |

### 1.7.3 Cycle 3: Lithium/REE Services (1990–2024) — The Private Services Engine

**The Service City**:
| Metric | 1990 | 2010 | 2024 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Population** | 230,000 | 320,000 | **412,000** |
| **Salar de Atacama Li** | 0 | 45 kt LCE | **180 kt LCE (SQM+Albemarle)** |
| **Mining Services Firms** | 50 | 320 | **1,200+** |
| **Port REE Tonnage** | 0 | 0.2 kt | **1.2 kt** |
| **Desal Capacity** | 0 | 400 L/s | **1,800 L/s** |
| **Solar Capacity (Region)** | 0 | 200 MW | **4.2 GW** |

**Structural Changes**:
1. **Contractorization** — Codelco direct employment 8,000 → 4,500; contractor 35,000 → 45,000
2. **Service Specialization** — Engineering, geotech, metallurgy, environmental, logistics, IT
3. **Knowledge Institutions** — **CICITEM (2004)**, **PTI Minería (2010)**, UA/UCN mining faculties
4. **Migrant Influx** — 2010: 4% foreign → 2024: **18%** (Venezuelan crisis, Peruvian/Colombian/Bolivian economic)
5. **Water Transition** — 100% desal for municipal, mining mixed (Escondida 100% desal, others partial)

**Revenue Model Shift**:
| Source | 1990 | 2024 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| **Codelco Profit Share** | 100% | 0% (Law 20.392) | **-100%** |
| **Royalty Minera** | 0% | **35% municipal revenue** | **New** |
| **Patentes Mineras** | 5% | 12% | +140% |
| **FDC Private Mines** | 0% | 28% | **New** |
| **ZOFRI Concessions** | 0% | 15% | **New** |
| **Other (commerce, services)** | 95% | 10% | **-90%** |

**Result**: **Municipality more mining-dependent than ever** — but revenue **volatile** (royalty tied to Cu price, not production).
