## 3.4 Chango Urban Indigenous — Coastal Guardians in the City

### 3.4.1 Chango Population & Territory — Urban Coastal People

| Dimension | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Legal Recognition** | **Law 20.249 (2008)** — Pueblo Chango distinct from Atacameño |
| **Population (Antofagasta Commune)** | **12,200** (3.0% commune, 28% of national Chango) |
| **Territorial Units** | **8 Caletas (coves)** + **3 Barrios Urbanos** (La Chimba Costa, Coloso Norte, Centro Histórico) |
| **Caletas (North to South)**: | **Mejillones, Hornitos, Punta Itata, Caleta Buena, Caleta Coloso, Caleta Constitución, Caleta El Cobre, Caleta Huanillo** |
| **Urban Barrios**: | **La Chimba Costa (4,200)**, **Coloso Norte (3,100)**, **Centro Histórico (1,800)** |

### 3.4.2 Chango Livelihoods — Marine-Coastal Economy

| Activity | Participants | Annual Value (US$) | Status | Threats |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|--------|---------|
| **Artisanal Fishing (Benthic)** | 1,800 | 18M | **Sustainable (quotas)** | Port expansion, pollution, climate |
| **Algae Harvesting (Pelillo, Luga, Chascón)** | 650 | 4.2M | **Managed (Áreas de Manejo)** | Overharvest, desal brine, warming |
| **Camanchaca Harvesting (Fog Water)** | 120 | 0.3M | **Ancestral, declining** | **Urbanization, fog decline (-30% since 1980)** |
| **Shellfish (Locos, Machas, Choros)** | 400 | 6.8M | **Co-managed (SUBPESCA + Chango)** | Red tide, acidification, poaching |
| **Marine Tourism (Diving, Kayak, Birdwatching)** | 180 | 1.5M | **Growing** | Port traffic, water quality |
| **Cultural Services (Ceremonies, Education, Crafts)** | 300 | 0.5M | **Revitalization** | Language loss, youth migration |

**Total Marine Economy**: **US$31.3M/yr** — **2.5% commune GDP** — **Chango: 60% of value capture**

### 3.4.3 Chango Consent Protocol — Marine-Coastal Specificity

**Distinct from Atacameño (DOC-26) and Mapuche (DOC-28/29)**:
| Dimension | Chango Protocol | Atacameño Protocol | Mapuche Protocol |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Territory** | **Marine-coastal (water column, seabed, shore)** | Terrestrial (salar, quebradas, highlands) | Terrestrial + forest + river + marine |
| **Authority** | **Consejo de Comunidades Costeras (8 caletas + 3 barrios)** | CPA (18 comunidades) | Lonko + Werken + Parlamento |
| **Episteme** | **Kimün costero** (navigation, tides, species, fog) | Kimün de altura (water, volcanoes, pasture) | Kimün mapuche (ñuke mapu, spirits) |
| **Veto Scope** | **Coastal/marine projects: port, desal, aquaculture, H₂ terminal, cables** | Terrestrial mining, water, energy, tourism | All projects in territory |
| **Institutions** | **SUBPESCA, DGTM, EMPORCHA, DIRECTEMAR, SERNAPESCA** | DGA, Sernageomin, CONAF, SEA | CONADI, SEA, CONAF, DGA |
| **Language** | **Chango costero (variant)** | Kunza (revitalizing) | Mapudungun |

**Chango Consent in Practice — Recent Cases**:
| Project | Year | Chango Decision | Conditions Imposed |
|---------|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Desal Sur Expansion (Aguas Antofagasta)** | 2021 | **Consent w/ Conditions** | Brine diffuser redesign, marine monitoring, 2% revenue to Chango fund |
| **HIF Mejillones NH₃ Terminal** | 2023 | **Consent w/ Conditions** | Marine baseline 2yr, emergency fund, Chango observers on safety committee |
| **Submarine Cable Landing (Valparaíso-Antofagasta)** | 2024 | **In Process** | Route avoids kelp beds, Chango marine monitors, revenue share |
| **Aquaculture Concessions (Mejillones Bay)** | 2024 | **Veto (2 caletas)** | **Cumulative impact** — algae, fishing, navigation, cultural sites |
