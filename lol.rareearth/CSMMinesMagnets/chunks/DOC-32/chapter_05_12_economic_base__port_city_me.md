## 1.2 Economic Base — Port City Metabolism

### 1.2.1 Sectoral Composition (2023, % of Commune GDP ~US$12.5B)

| Sector | % GDP | Employment | Key Anchors | Growth Trend |
|--------|-------|------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Port & Logistics** | **35%** | 18,000 | EMPORCHA, ATI, Portezuelo, FCAB, trucking | +3%/yr (REE, H₂ volume) |
| **Mining Services** | **25%** | 22,000 | Contractors (Tier 1-3), CICITEM, PTI, labs, engineering | +4%/yr (automation, green mining) |
| **Commerce & Services** | **20%** | 35,000 | Mall Plaza, ZOFRI, banking, retail, tourism | +2%/yr (population-driven) |
| **Tourism & Heritage** | **10%** | 8,000 | La Portada, Mano del Desierto, astronomy, cruises, nitrate heritage | +6%/yr (post-COVID recovery) |
| **Public Admin/Other** | **10%** | 15,000 | GORE, Municipalidad, Seremis, armed forces, health, education | Stable |

**Mining Revenue Dependence**: **~40% municipal budget** from mining-linked sources (patentes, royalty, FDC Escondida/Spence/Zaldívar).
