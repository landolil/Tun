## 6.6 Migrant Representation — From Invisibility to Voice

### 6.6.1 Mesa Migrante Antofagasta — The Coordinating Body

| Structure | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Member Organizations** | **18** (Venezuela 6, Colombia 4, Peru 3, Bolivia 2, Haiti 2, Multi 1) |
| **Coordination** | **Rotating trimester presidency** (by nationality consensus) |
| **Comisiones** | Regularización, Vivienda, Salud, Trabajo, Integración, Infancia, Género, Cultura |
| **Institutional Links** | Municipalidad (Oficina Migrantes), SERMIG, OIM, IOM, UNHCR, SSA, Serviu |
| **Legal Status** | **Decreto Alcaldía 2023** — Formal recognition, budget allocation, participation rights |

### 6.6.2 Consejería Migrante Municipal — The Political Breakthrough

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Legal Basis** | **Law 21.325 Art. 64** — Migrant participation in local gov |
| **Seats** | **3 Consejeros Migrantes** (elected by residents >5 years, 18+ years old) |
| **First Election** | **2025 (municipal elections)** — Historic first in Chile |
| **Powers** | Voice in Concejo (no vote), commission participation, budget advocacy, program design |
| **Electorate** | ~18,000 eligible migrants (5+ years residence) |
