## 2.3 REE Handling Certification — The Competitive Moat

### 2.3.1 Regulatory Stack — IMSBC, RADSAFE, IAEA, Chilean Law

| Standard | Scope | Antofagasta Status | Gap to World-Class |
|----------|-------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **IMSBC Code (IMO)** | Solid bulk cargo safety (Group B — REE) | **ATI certified 2022** | None — **full compliance** |
| **RADSAFE (Chile)** | Radioactive material transport/port handling | **Level 2 (Sernageomin)** | **Level 3 target 2026** (full automation) |
| **IAEA SSR-6** | Radioactive material transport regulations | **Applied (Nd/Dy oxides low-level)** | Full integration with customs |
| **Chilean Nuclear Safety Law (18.302)** | Nuclear materials control | **CCHEN licensing** | Streamline for REE (non-fissile) |
| **Customs (DNA)** | Valuation, origin, HS codes (2846.xx) | **Standard** | **Green lane REE (target 2025)** |
| **Port State Control (DGTM)** | SOLAS, MARPOL, ISPS, STCW | **Regular inspections** | **Specialized REE inspectors needed** |

**Competitive Positioning**:
- **Vs. Mejillones**: Antofagasta = **containerized REE + general cargo**; Mejillones = **bulk NH₃ + industrial**

- **Vs. Chilean Ports (Valparaíso, San Antonio)**: Antofagasta = **mining-specialized, REE-ready, northern gateway**
