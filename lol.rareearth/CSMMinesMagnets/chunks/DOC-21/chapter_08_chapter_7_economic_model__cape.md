### 7.4 Chapter 7 Summary

Aegis-Forge Nd/Dy clusters deliver **22-38% IRR (base case)** with **2.3-4.2 year payback**. Urban recycling clusters (Bravo-5,7,9) are **most profitable** (38% IRR) due to negative feedstock cost. **Primary IOCG clusters** (Alpha-1..11) provide **Dy volume** essential for high-grade magnets. **Price floor**: Nd $60/kg, Dy $300/kg maintains >12% IRR.
