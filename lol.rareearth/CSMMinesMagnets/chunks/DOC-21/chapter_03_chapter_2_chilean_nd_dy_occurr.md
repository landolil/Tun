Chilean Nd/Dy occurrences span **5 geological systems** across **22 districts** with **12,280 t/yr Nd** and **1,113 t/yr Dy** potential — covering **82%/56% of 2030 global deficits**. **Dy bottleneck** (11:1 to 18:1 Nd/Dy ratio in Chilean sources vs 20:1+ needed for N52EH+) requires either **epithermal HREE-enriched sources** (Fuegian 7.6:1) or **Dy-substitution technology** (Tb-doping, grain boundary diffusion).
