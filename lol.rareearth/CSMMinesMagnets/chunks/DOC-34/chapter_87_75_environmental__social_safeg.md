## 7.5 Environmental & Social Safeguards — The CPA Standard

### 7.5.1 Arsenic Management — The Critical Path

| Stage | As Concentration | Treatment | Removal Efficiency | Residual |
|-------|------------------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| **Production Fluid** | 15 mg/L (El Tatio), 8 mg/L (Puchuldiza) | — | — | — |
| **After Separator** | 12 mg/L / 6 mg/L | — | — | — |
| **Co-precipitation (FeCl₃ + Ca(OH)₂)** | 12 → 0.5 mg/L | **96%** | 0.5 mg/L |
| **Adsorption (TiO₂/Fe-oxides)** | 0.5 → **0.01 mg/L** | **98%** | **0.01 mg/L (WHO compliant)** |
| **Reinjection Fluid** | 0.01 mg/L | — | — | **Safe for aquifer** |

**Sludge Management**: As-Fe sludge → **Stabilized in cementitious matrix** → Disposal in licensed hazardous waste facility (Antofagasta) — **$120/ton, 500 ton/yr**.

### 7.5.2 Seismic Monitoring — Induced Seismicity Protocol

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Network** | 12 broadband seismometers (surface + borehole) |
| **Detection Threshold** | **ML 0.5** (local magnitude) |
| **Traffic Light System** | Green: <ML 1.5, Yellow: ML 1.5-2.5 (reduce rate), Red: >ML 2.5 (shut-in) |
| **CPA Role** | **Real-time data access, veto on Yellow/Red, kimün correlation** |
| **Baseline** | 2 years pre-operation (2022-2024) — **ML <0.8 background** |

### 7.5.3 Bofedales Protection — The Hydrological Shield

| Measure | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Hydrological Buffer** | **No extraction within 5km of bofedales** (Chaxas, Puilar, Miscanti) |
| **Monitoring** | Piezometers (20), vegetation NDVI (Sentinel-2), spring flow (monthly) |
| **Trigger** | Drawdown >0.5m OR NDVI decline >10% → **Immediate extraction reduction** |
| **CPA Authority** | **Absolute veto on any well within buffer zone** |
