### 1.1.2 Watershed — Endorheic Basin to Salar de Atacama

```
HYDROGRAPHIC NETWORK — SAN PEDRO DE ATACAMA COMMUNE

RÍO SAN PEDRO (Principal)
├── Source: Volcán Licancabur glaciers (5,920m) + snowmelt
├── Flow: Perennial but highly seasonal (summer peak 2.5 m³/s, winter 0.3 m³/s)
├── Course: 45 km through San Pedro oasis → Quebrada de Vilama
├── Termination: **Salar de Atacama (endorheic)** — no ocean outlet
└── Tributaries:
    ├── Río Vilama (north, seasonal)
    ├── Río Toconce (south, thermal springs)
    └── Quebrada de Kari (flash flood conduit)

BOFEDALES (High-Andean Wetlands) — CRITICAL ECOSYSTEM
├── Locations: Tulor, Chaxas, Puilar, Miscanti, Miñiques
├── Area: ~1,200 ha (commune), 40% of regional bofedales
├── Function: Carbon sink, biodiversity (vicuña, flamingos), water regulation
├── Threat: Water extraction, tourism trampling, climate change
└── Governance: CPA Atacameño Zona de Protección + CONAF

AQUIFER SYSTEM — PURIPURA-CHIU CHIU (Fossil)
├── Type: Confined volcanic sedimentary, Paleocene-Eocene
├── Recharge: **<0.5 mm/yr** (effectively non-renewable, 10,000+ yr residence)
├── Extraction: 120 L/s (2024) — Tourism 45%, Agriculture 35%, Mining exploration 20%
├── Quality: As 0.015 mg/L (WHO limit 0.01), B 2.5 mg/L, F 1.8 mg/L
└── Governance: DGA + CPA Atacameño (Indigenous Law Art 13 priority)
```

### 1.1.3 Climate Zones — Altitude Gradient (5 Zones in 21 km)

| Zone | Elevation | Climate (Köppen) | Temp Range | Precip | Key Features |
|------|-----------|------------------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Oasis Floor** | 2,400-2,500m | **BWk (Cold Desert)** | -5°C to 28°C | 35 mm | San Pedro town, agriculture, tourism base |
| **Precordillera** | 2,500-3,500m | BWk → ET (Tundra) | -10°C to 22°C | 50 mm | Ayllus, pastoralism, archaeological sites |
| **Altiplano** | 3,500-4,200m | **ET (Tundra)** | -15°C to 15°C | 100 mm | Bo-fedales, lagunas, vicuña, geothermal |
| **Volcanic Peaks** | 4,200-5,920m | **EF (Ice Cap)** | -25°C to 5°C | 150 mm (snow) | Licancabur, Lascar, Sairecabur — glaciers retreating |
| **Salar Margin** | 2,300-2,400m | BWk (hyperarid) | 0°C to 30°C | 15 mm | Salar de Atacama edge, lithium brine |
