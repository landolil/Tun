## 8.5 Governance Resilience — The Triad Under Stress

### 8.5.1 Stress Test — The Triad in Crisis

| Crisis Scenario | CPA Response | Municipal Response | Cámara Response | Triad Coordination | Outcome |
|-----------------|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Flash Flood (2022)** | Kimün observers activated, ayllu evacuation led | Emergency decree, shelters, ONEMI liaison | Tourist evacuation, guide coordination | **EXCELLENT** — Mesa Tripartita convened 2hr post-event | 0 fatalities, 90% tourists evacuated |
| **COVID-19 (2020)** | Community lockdown (stricter than national), food sovereignty | Health protocols, border control | **Collapse** — 95% revenue loss, layoffs | **POOR** — No triad meetings Mar-Jun 2020 | Recovery led by CPA + Muni, Cámara lagged |
| **Water Crisis (Ongoing)** | **Leading** — Adaptation Horizons, veto mining, fog harvesting | **Following** — Leak reduction, grey water ordinance | **Passive** — Efficiency voluntary | **IMPROVING** — Monthly technical table since 2023 | Horizon 1 funded, Horizon 2 in design |
| **Overtourism Backlash (2023)** | **Leading** — Cap enforcement, cultural protocol | **Following** — PRC update, Ordenanza lumínica | **Resistant** — Revenue defense | **TENSION** — Cámara sued CONAF over caps | Court upheld caps, Cámara adapting |
