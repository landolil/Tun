### 8.3.2 CPA Cultural Defense Protocol

```
CPA CULTURAL COMMODIFICATION DEFENSE — OPERATIONAL RULES

RULE 1: SACRED SITES (Zona Sagra) — ABSOLUTE PROTECTION
├── No tourism infrastructure within 500m
├── No guided access without kimün holder + Nivel 4 guide
├── Ceremonial closure: 60 days/yr (solstices, festivals, mourning)
├── Drone/satellite imaging: CPA approval required
└── Violation: **Immediate closure + $10,000 fine + license revocation**

RULE 2: CEREMONIAL INTEGRITY — NO PERFORMANCE
├── Authentic ceremonies: Community-only, no observers
├── Educational demonstrations: Separate, clearly labeled, kimün-led
├── Photography: **Prohibited** during authentic ceremonies
├── Audio recording: **Prohibited** without Asamblea consent
└── Commercial use of ceremony imagery: **CPA veto + benefit share**

RULE 3: KUNZA LANGUAGE — REVITALIZATION NOT DECORATION
├── Tourism use: Place names, greetings, basic phrases (approved list)
├── Marketing: **No Kunza in advertising without CPA review**
├── Guide certification: **Nivel 2+ requires Kunza proficiency test**
├── Digital archive: **CPA-controlled access, community benefit**
└── AI training on Kunza: **Explicit CPA consent + revenue share**

RULE 4: ARTISAN ECONOMY — FAIR TRADE NOT EXTRACTION
├── Cooperative structure: 80% artisans in CPA cooperatives
├── Pricing: **Minimum floor prices (CPA-set, annual review)**
├── Intellectual Property: **Collective Atacameño design registry**
├── Tourist market: **Direct sales preferred, intermediary capped at 15%**
└── Revenue transparency: **Quarterly reporting to Asamblea**

ENFORCEMENT:
├── CPA Cultural Monitors (4, one per macrozone) — Power to halt tours
├── Municipal alignment: Ordenanza references CPA protocol
├── SENATUR: Guide license tied to CPA compliance
├── CONAF: Park ranger training includes cultural protocol
└── Judicial: CPA legal unit + Defensoría Comunitaria
```
