## 8.4 Social License — The CPA Veto as Governance Anchor

### 8.4.1 Social License Index — Quarterly Measurement

```
SOCIAL LICENSE INDEX (SLI) = 0.3×Trust + 0.25×Benefit + 0.2×Voice + 0.15×Environment + 0.1×Culture

COMPONENTS (0-100 each):
├── Trust (CPA-Government-Private): Survey quarterly, n=400
├── Benefit Perception: Revenue share fairness, job quality, infrastructure
├── Voice: Consulta Previa satisfaction, grievance resolution time
├── Environment: Water quality, carrying capacity compliance, biodiversity
├── Culture: Sacred site protection, language vitality, ceremony integrity

CURRENT (2024 Q3): SLI = 72/100
├── Trust: 68 (Municipal-CPA tension on ZUIT)
├── Benefit: 78 (Royalty transparent, tourism fees improving)
├── Voice: 75 (Consulta Previa working, but slow)
├── Environment: 65 (Water crisis, overcapacity sites)
└── Culture: 72 (Language growing, but commodification pressure)

THRESHOLDS:
├── SLI > 80: **STRONG** — Projects proceed with standard Consulta
├── SLI 60-80: **MODERATE** — Enhanced Consulta, CPA monitoring mandatory
├── SLI 40-60: **FRAGILE** — **Moratorium on new projects**, trust rebuilding
├── SLI < 40: **COLLAPSE** — **Territorial defense mode**, legal action, international appeal
```
