## 4.3 Territorial Zoning — The CPA Spatial Plan

### 4.3.1 Zonas de Uso — CPA Territorial Ordering (2023 Update)

| Zone | Area | Comunidades | Permitted Uses | Prohibited | Governance |
|------|------|-------------|----------------|------------|------------|
| **Zona Sagra (Sacred)** | 45,000 ha | All (transversal) | **Ceremonial access only**, kimün transmission, guided cultural visits (Nivel 3/4 guides) | **All extraction, infrastructure, tourism infrastructure, vehicles** | CPA Directorio + kimün holders (absolute) |
| **Zona de Protección (Conservation)** | 120,000 ha | Camar, Talar, Socaire, Pejine | **Bofedales restoration, vicuña management, scientific research (CPA permit), controlled tourism (carrying cap)** | Mining, water extraction > monitoring, permanent infrastructure | CPA + CONAF co-management |
| **Zona Productiva (Pastoral/Tourism)** | 65,000 ha | Coyo, Sequitor, Solcor, Toconao | **Llama/alpaca pastoralism, agriculture (traditional), tourism services (capped), crafts, geothermal direct-use** | Industrial mining, large-scale energy, water bottling | Comunidad + CPA technical unit |
| **Zona Urbana de Interés Turístico (ZUIT)** | 2,500 ha | San Pedro town (municipal) | **Hotels, restaurants, agencies, housing, municipal services** | **Height >2 stories, neon signs, groundwater extraction >APR allocation** | **Municipal PRC + CPA veto on height/extraction** |

**Key Innovation**: **ZUIT is the ONLY zone where Municipal PRC prevails** — but CPA retains veto on height (visual impact on sacred landscape) and groundwater extraction (aquifer protection).
