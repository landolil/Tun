## 2.6 Dark Sky Astronomy — The Premium Differentiator

### 2.6.1 Market & Economics

| Metric | Value | Trend |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Clear Nights/Year** | 300+ | Stable |
| **SQM (Zenith)** | 21.8 mag/arcsec² | Degrading -0.02/yr (LED creep) |
| **Active Observatories** | 15 (public + private) | +3/yr |
| **Astro-Tours/Night (Peak)** | 50+ | Growing |
| **Astro-Tourism Revenue** | **$15M/yr (33% of total)** | **+25%/yr** |
| **Avg Spend/Astro-Tourist** | $2,200 | 2.2× general tourist |
| **Length of Stay** | 5.5 nights | 1.5× general |

### 2.6.2 Light Pollution Ordinance — The Protection Imperative

| Measure | Current Status | IDA Requirement | Investment Needed |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Ordenanza Lumínica Municipal** | Draft (2024), not enacted | Enacted + enforced | Legal process CLP 50M |
| **Municipal Streetlight Retrofit** | 35% shielded 2200K LED | 100% shielded ≤2200K | **CLP 1.2B ($1.3M)** |
| **Commercial Curfew (22:00-06:00)** | Voluntary (20% compliance) | Mandatory | Enforcement CLP 80M/yr |
| **New Construction Standard** | None | Full cutoff, 2200K, motion sensor | Building code update |
| **ALMA/APEX Buffer Monitoring** | Annual SQM survey | Continuous (3 stations) | **CLP 150M (sensors + telemetry)** |

**Economic Case**: $1.3M retrofit protects $15M/yr revenue stream → **Payback < 2 months**. Without protection, SQM degradation to 21.0 = **$5M/yr loss** (astro-tourist decline).
