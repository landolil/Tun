### 2.4.2 Guide Certification — The Quality Filter

| Level | Requirements | Validated By | Guides Certified | Market Access |
|-------|--------------|--------------|------------------|---------------|
| **Nivel 1 (Basic)** | 40hr course, first aid, driving license | SENATUR | 280 | All sites except archaeological |
| **Nivel 2 (Cultural)** | Nivel 1 + 20hr Kunza/culture (CPA), archaeology basics | **SENATUR + CPA** | 145 | **All sites including Pukará, Tulor, Petroglyphs** |
| **Nivel 3 (Specialty)** | Nivel 2 + astronomy/geology/flora-fauna (Univ. Católica) | SENATUR + CPA + Univ. | 45 | **Premium (astro-tours, photo workshops, scientific)** |
| **Nivel 4 (Master)** | Nivel 3 + 5yr exp, trainer certification, emergency response | SENATUR + CPA + ONEMI | 12 | **Expedition leader, film crews, VIP** |

**Gap**: **15% informal guides operate without any certification** — safety risk, cultural insensitivity, no insurance.
