## 4.2 Consulta Previa Protocol — The 2022 Standardized Process

### 4.2.1 Legal Framework — Beyond ILO 169

| Instrument | Article | Requirement | CPA Implementation |
|------------|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **ILO 169** | Art 6 | Prior, free, informed consultation | **Exceeded** — CPA protocol adds kimün integration, veto power |
| **UNDRIP** | Art 19, 32 | FPIC for projects affecting territories | **Operationalized** — 60-day timeline, technical equivalence |
| **Law 19.253** | Art 13, 34 | Indigenous water priority, territorial rights | **Binding** — DGA must deny rights if CPA objects |
| **Law 21.435** | Art 7 | Consulta Previa for energy projects | **Applied** — Geothermal, solar, transmission |
| **CONADI Res 2022** | — | Standardized methodology | **CPA co-authored** — 60 days, 3 phases, kimün integration |

### 4.2.2 The 60-Day CPA Consulta Previa Protocol

```
CPA CONSULTA PREVIA — STANDARDIZED 60-DAY PROCESS

PHASE 1: NOTIFICATION & SCOPING (Days 1-10)
├── Proponent delivers: Project description, EIA/EIS, baseline studies, maps
├── CPA Unidades Técnicas review: Completeness check (5 days)
├── Directorio issues: Ámbito de Consulta (territorial scope), comunidades affected
└── Communities receive: Sumario ejecutivo (Spanish + Kunza), plenary scheduled

PHASE 2: INFORMATION & DELIBERATION (Days 11-40)
├── Week 1-2: Technical workshops (CPA professionals + proponent experts)
│   ├── Hydrogeology (water), Archaeology (heritage), Tourism (carrying capacity)
│   ├── Kimün holders present: Territorial memory, sacred sites, water spirits
│   └── Independent experts (Univ. Católica, CICITEM) available to communities
├── Week 3: Comunidad plenaries (each affected comunidad, quorum 50%+1)
│   ├── Deliberation guided by Vocero + kimün holder
│   ├── Minutes recorded (audio + written, Spanish + Kunza)
│   └── Preliminary position: Apoyo / Apoyo con condiciones / Rechazo
└── Week 4: Macrozone assemblies → Synthesis document

PHASE 3: DECISION & AGREEMENT (Days 41-60)
├── Day 41-45: Asamblea General de Comunidades (36 votes)
│   ├── Quorum: 12 comunidades (2/3 of 18)
│   ├── Voting: Simple majority for conditions, 2/3 for veto
│   └── Outcome: Resolución de Consulta (binding)
├── Day 46-55: If Apoyo con condiciones → Negotiation of Acuerdo de Beneficios
│   ├── Economic: Royalty %, employment quotas, local procurement
│   ├── Environmental: Monitoring by CPA, adaptive management triggers
│   ├── Cultural: Sacred site protection, ceremonial access, language
│   └── Social: Health, education, housing, water security
├── Day 56-60: Final signature → Resolución + Acuerdo → SEA / DGA / CONAF
└── POST-APPROVAL: CPA Monitoring Unit tracks compliance (quarterly reports)
```
