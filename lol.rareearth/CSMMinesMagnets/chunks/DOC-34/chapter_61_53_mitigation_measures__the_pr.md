## 5.3 Mitigation Measures — The Preservation Toolkit

### 5.3.1 Physical Infrastructure

| Measure | Sites Applied | Cost | Effectiveness (TDI Reduction) | Status |
|---------|---------------|------|-------------------------------|--------|
| **Elevated Boardwalks** | Tulor, Pukará, Yerbas Buenas | $2.5M | **65%** (eliminates direct foot erosion) | **Tulor done, Pukará 80%, YB 2025** |
| **Forced Ventilation (CO₂ control)** | Tulor (enclosed) | $450k | **80%** (chemical damage) | **Operational 2023** |
| **Sacrificial Mud Plaster** | Pukará, Iglesia San Pedro | $200k/yr | **40%** (weathering protection) | **Annual cycle** |
| **Seismic Retrofit (Adobe)** | Iglesia San Pedro, Toconao, Socaire | $3.2M | **90%** (collapse prevention) | **San Pedro 2024, others 2025-26** |
| **Viewing Platforms (10-15m)** | Yerbas Buenas, Río Grande, Chullpas | $1.1M | **95%** (eliminates contact/vibration) | **YB 2024, RG 2025** |
| **CCTV + AI Monitoring** | Pukará, Tulor, Iglesia | $600k | **70%** (theft/vandalism deterrence) | **Pukará done, others 2025** |

### 5.3.2 Operational Controls

| Control | Mechanism | Enforcement | Compliance |
|---------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Timed Entry Slots** | Online booking (Sernatur platform), 2-hr windows | QR code at entrance, guide verification | 92% (2023) |
| **Guide Mandatory (Nivel 2+)** | SENATUR + CPA certification, 1:15 ratio | Guide ID check, penalty $500 + license suspension | 88% |
| **Visitor Cap Enforcement** | Real-time counter (IR sensors), auto-lock at cap | CONAF rangers + CPA monitors | 85% (peak days challenging) |
| **No-Flash Photography** | Signage + guide enforcement, sensor-triggered alerts | Guide responsibility | 75% |
| **Ceremonial Closure** | CPA-declared (solstices, ceremonies, mourning) | **Absolute — no exceptions** | 100% |
