## 7.1 El Tatio Geothermal Field — Chile's Premier Geothermal Asset

### 7.1.1 Resource Characterization

| Parameter | Value | Significance |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| **Location** | 4,320m ASL, 90 km N of San Pedro | **Highest geothermal field in the world** |
| **Surface Manifestations** | **80 geysers**, 120 hot springs, 200 fumaroles, sinter terraces | Tourist attraction #1 (120K visitors/yr) |
| **Reservoir Temperature** | **220-250°C** (at 1,500-2,500m depth) | **Binary cycle power generation viable** |
| **Thermal Power (Surface)** | **80 MWth** (measured heat discharge) | **Electric potential: 20-30 MWe (binary)** |
| **Reservoir Volume** | ~15 km³ (fractured volcanic) | Sustainable with reinjection |
| **Fluid Chemistry** | Na-Cl type, TDS 8,000 mg/L, As 15 mg/L, B 45 mg/L, SiO₂ 450 mg/L | **Scaling/corrosion challenge, As treatment needed** |
| **Recharge** | Meteoric (Andean snowmelt) + magmatic | Estimated 50-80 kg/s natural recharge |
| **Concession** | **CONAF (RN Los Flamencos) — Termas de Puritama operator** | Tourism-only current, energy potential untapped |

### 7.1.2 Tourism Operation — Termas de Puritama

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pools** | 8 thermal pools (32-38°C), natural rock, river-fed |
| **Flow** | 25 L/s geothermal + 15 L/s river mixing |
| **Reinjection** | 80% of extracted fluid (20 L/s) → sustainability |
| **Visitors** | 65,000/yr (cap 350/day enforced) |
| **Revenue** | $3.2M/yr (entry $45, transport $25) |
| **Employment** | 45 local (30 Atacameño), guide + maintenance |
| **Concession Term** | 2018-2038 (20 yr, renewable) |
| **CPA Benefit** | 3% revenue + employment priority + environmental monitoring |
