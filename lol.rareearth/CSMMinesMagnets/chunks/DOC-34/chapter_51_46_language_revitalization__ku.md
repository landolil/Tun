### 4.6.2 Revitalization Strategy — The Intergenerational Bridge

```
KUNZA REVITALIZATION PROGRAM — CPA + UNIV. CATÓLICA + UNESCO

PILLAR 1: EDUCATION (Formal)
├── Curriculum: MINEDUC-approved (2022), 3 hrs/week K-8
├── Teachers: 12 Atacameño educators trained (Universidad Católica)
├── Materials: Textbooks, apps, games, AR (petroglyph translation)
└── Assessment: Annual proficiency (CEFR-aligned A1-B1)

PILLAR 2: COMMUNITY (Informal)
├── Talleres de Abuelos: Kimün holders teach youth (oral tradition)
├── Radio Kunza: 2 hrs/day (Radio Ayllu 107.5 FM, streaming)
├── Turismo Lingüístico: Guides use Kunza place names, greetings
└── Festival Anual: Kunza Vive (cultural pride, intergenerational)

PILLAR 3: DIGITAL (Preservation + Access)
├── Corpus: 450 hrs audio/video (elders, ceremonies, place names)
├── Dictionary: 3,200 entries (Spanish-Kunza, with audio)
├── GIS Layer: 1,200 toponyms with Kunza names, meanings, stories
├── API: Open for apps, tourism platforms, education
└── Blockchain: Immutable record of speakers, stories, rights

PILLAR 4: LEGAL (Rights)
├── Law 21.435 Art 12: Indigenous language rights in public services
├── Municipal Ordenanza: Kunza co-official in San Pedro commune
├── CONAF: Park ranger Kunza certification (Nivel 2 requirement)
└── SENATUR: Guide certification requires Kunza basics (Nivel 2+)
```
