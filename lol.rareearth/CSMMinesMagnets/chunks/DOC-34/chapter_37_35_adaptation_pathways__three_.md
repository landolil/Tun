## 3.5 Adaptation Pathways — Three Horizons

### 3.5.1 Horizon 1: Demand Management (2024-2026) — Immediate

| Measure | Potential Savings | Cost | Implementation | Status |
|---------|-------------------|------|----------------|--------|
| **Leak Reduction (35% → 15%)** | **24 L/s** | $4M | Pipeline replacement 15km/yr | **Started 2024 (2km done)** |
| **Grey Water Mandate (New Builds)** | 8 L/s (by 2030) | $2M (subsidies) | Ordenanza 2024, applies to permits | **Enacted Jan 2024** |
| **Tourism Water Efficiency** | 5 L/s | $500k | Low-flow fixtures, linen reuse, pool covers | **Voluntary program 60% uptake** |
| **Smart Metering (All Connections)** | 3 L/s (behavioral) | $1.5M | 3,500 meters, telemetry | **Tender 2024** |
| **TOTAL HORIZON 1** | **40 L/s** | **$8M** | — | **Achievable by 2026** |

### 3.5.2 Horizon 2: Alternative Sources (2026-2030) — Bridge

| Source | Potential | Quality | Cost | Timeline | Barriers |
|--------|-----------|---------|------|----------|----------|
| **Fog Harvesting (Atrapanieblas)** | **50 L/s** (100 collectors × 500 m²) | Excellent (As <0.001) | $6M | 2026-2028 | Land (CPA), maintenance, visual impact |
| **Wastewater Reuse (MBR Upgrade)** | **30 L/s** (from 50 L/s sewage) | Excellent (As <0.001) | $8M | 2026-2027 | Public acceptance, regulatory (NCh 1333) |
| **Aquifer Storage Recovery (ASR)** | 20 L/s (seasonal) | Good | $4M | 2028-2030 | Geological uncertainty, clogging |
| **TOTAL HORIZON 2** | **100 L/s** | — | **$18M** | — | **Closes 2030 baseline gap** |

### 3.5.3 Horizon 3: Desalination Pipeline (2030-2035) — Structural

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Source** | Pacific Ocean (Mejillones or Tocopilla) — DOC-33 corridor |
| **Capacity** | 200 L/s (Phase 1), 400 L/s (Phase 2) |
| **Pipeline** | 200 km, 600mm steel, 3 pump stations, 2,400m elevation gain |
| **Energy** | 4.5 kWh/m³ (pumping dominant) — Solar PPA (Atacama) + BESS |
| **Cost** | **$500M (Phase 1), $850M (Full)** |
| **Governance** | **Tripartite: CPA (water rights), Muni (distribution), GORE (funding)** |
| **CPA Condition** | **CPA owns 51% of water rights, sets allocation priority** |
