## 7.3 Direct-Use Applications — Cascading Heat for Local Value

### 7.3.1 Cascade Design — Maximizing Exergy Efficiency

```
GEOTHERMAL CASCADE — EL TATIO / PUCHULDIZA DIRECT USE

SOURCE: 220°C (El Tatio) / 200°C (Puchuldiza) — Geothermal Fluid
    │
    ├── STAGE 1: POWER GENERATION (Binary ORC)
    │   ├── Inlet: 220°C / 200°C
    │   ├── Outlet: 120°C
    │   ├── Power: 20 MWe (El Tatio) / 10 MWe (Puchuldiza)
    │   └── Efficiency: 12-14% (Carnot 22%)
    │
    ├── STAGE 2: DISTRICT HEATING (San Pedro Town)
    │   ├── Inlet: 120°C
    │   ├── Outlet: 70°C
    │   ├── Demand: 3 MWth (peak winter)
    │   ├── Network: 8 km insulated pipeline
    │   └── Consumers: Hotels, municipal, residential (500 connections)
    │
    ├── STAGE 3: GREENHOUSE AGRICULTURE
    │   ├── Inlet: 70°C
    │   ├── Outlet: 45°C
    │   ├── Area: 5 ha (tomato, strawberry, herbs)
    │   ├── Production: 200 ton/yr tomato, 50 ton/yr strawberry
    │   └── Market: Local hotels, Calama, export (premium geothermal grown)
    │
    ├── STAGE 4: AQUACULTURE (Trout)
    │   ├── Inlet: 45°C → Heat exchanger → 18°C tank
    │   ├── Flow: 50 L/s
    │   ├── Production: 100 ton/yr trout (high-value, sashimi grade)
    │   └── Effluent: Nutrient-rich → Spirulina ponds (Stage 5)
    │
    ├── STAGE 5: SPIRULINA / MICROALGAE
    │   ├── Inlet: 35°C (from aquaculture)
    │   ├── Area: 2 ha raceways
    │   ├── Production: 20 ton/yr dry spirulina
    │   └── Market: Nutraceutical export ($150/kg)
    │
    └── STAGE 6: REINJECTION
        ├── Temperature: 30°C
        ├── Flow: 90% of extracted
        ├── Location: Peripheral injection wells
        └── Monitoring: Pressure, temperature, chemistry, seismicity

TOTAL CASCADE EFFICIENCY: **65% exergy utilization** (vs 12% power-only)
```
