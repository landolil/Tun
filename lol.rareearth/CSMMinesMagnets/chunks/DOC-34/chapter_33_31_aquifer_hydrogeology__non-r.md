### 3.1.2 Isotope Hydrology — The Fossil Water Proof

| Isotope | Puripica Wellfield | Chiu Chiu Wellfield | Modern Recharge (Andes) | Interpretation |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------|------------------------|----------------|
| **δ¹⁸O (‰ VSMOW)** | -12.5 to -11.8 | -12.2 to -11.5 | -8.5 to -6.0 | **Depleted = paleoclimate (colder)** |

| **¹⁴C (pmC)** | **2.5-4.5 pmC** | **3.0-5.0 pmC** | 100 pmC (modern) | **Age: 10,000-25,000 years BP** |

**Conclusion**: **Zero modern recharge** — Water extracted today fell as rain/snow **10,000-25,000 years ago** during last glacial maximum. This is **mining fossil water**, not managing a renewable resource.
