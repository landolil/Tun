## 4.4 Benefit Distribution — The Indigenous Fiscal Architecture

### 4.4.1 Revenue Streams & Allocation Rules (2024)

| Revenue Source | Annual Amount | CPA Share | Distribution Formula |
|----------------|---------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Royalty Minero (1.5% Ley 21.435)** | CLP 8.2B (~$8.8M) | **100%** | 40% direct to 18 comunidades (per capita + territorial), 30% CPA operations, 20% health/education fund, 10% emergency reserve |
| **Entrance Fees (CONAF Sites)** | $1.9M | **40-50% per site** | Per site agreement: Valle de la Luna 0% (CONAF 100%), Geysers 40%, Lagunas 50%, Pukará 80% → Cultural preservation, guide training, bofedales |
| **Tourism Agreements (Private)** | ~$0.5M | **Negotiated** | Voluntary agreements with hotels/lodges: 1-2% revenue → CPA cultural fund |
| **Project Agreements (Mining/Energy)** | CLP 3.5B (~$3.8M) | **100%** | Per Acuerdo de Beneficios: 50% comunidad affected, 25% CPA ops, 25% territorial monitoring |
| **International Cooperation** | CLP 800M (~$0.85M) | **100%** | GEF, UNESCO, IDB grants → Language revitalization, bofedales, climate adaptation |

### 4.4.2 Distribution Mechanics — Transparency & Accountability

```
CPA BENEFIT DISTRIBUTION — QUARTERLY CYCLE

INPUTS (Quarterly):
├── Royalty Minero: DGA transfer → CPA tesorería (automated)
├── Entrance Fees: CONAF transfer → CPA tesorería (monthly)
├── Project Agreements: Proponent transfer → CPA tesorería (per milestone)
└── Grants: Donor transfer → CPA project accounts

ALLOCATION (Directorio resolution + Asamblea ratification):
├── 1. COMUNIDAD DIRECT TRANSFER (40% Royalty)
│   ├── Formula: Base $500K/comunidad + $50/hectare + $100/persona
│   ├── Transfer: Direct to comunidad bank account (2 signatories: Presidente + Tesorero comunidad)
│   └── Reporting: Comunidad Asamblea quarterly → CPA auditoría
├── 2. CPA OPERATIONS (30% Royalty + 100% Project Agreements ops)
│   ├── Unidades Técnicas salaries (25 professionals)
│   ├── Consulta Previa costs (workshops, experts, translation)
│   ├── Legal defense (water rights, territorial integrity)
│   ├── Communications (radio, web, Kunza materials)
│   └── Governance (Asambleas, Directorio, travel)
├── 3. HEALTH/EDUCATION FUND (20% Royalty)
│   ├── Intercultural health (CPA + SEREMI Salud convenio)
│   ├── Bilingual education (Kunza-Spanish, 8 escuelas)
│   ├── Scholarships (universidad, técnico, turismo)
│   └── Elderly care (vivienda tutelada, telemedicina)
└── 4. EMERGENCY RESERVE (10% Royalty)
    ├── Flash flood response
    ├── Drought emergency (water trucks)
    ├── Health crisis (pandemic)
    └── Legal emergency (territorial defense)

AUDIT & TRANSPARENCY:
├── Quarterly: Estados financieros → Asamblea General (public)
├── Annual: Auditoría externa (Big 4) → CONADI + Contraloría
├── Real-time: Portal transparencia CPA (blockchain-registered transfers)
└── Grievance: Defensoría Comunitaria (independent, 3 members elected)
```
