## 1.4 Governance Triad — CPA + Municipal + Tourism Chamber

### 1.4.1 Three-Legged Governance Model

| Body | Legal Basis | Composition | Authority | Budget (2024) |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| **CPA Atacameño** (Corporación Pueblo Atacameño) | Law 19.253, CONADI Res 2022 | 18 comunidades (8 in SPA commune), Directorio (Presidente, VP, Secretario, Tesorero + 4 Voceros) | **Indigenous territory (18 comunidades), Consulta Previa veto, Royalty Minero 1.5%, Cultural heritage co-management** | CLP 12B (Royalty + fees) |
| **Municipalidad San Pedro de Atacama** | Law 18.695 (Orgánica Municipal) | Alcalde + 6 Concejales, 180 funcionarios | **Urban planning (PRC), Patentes (tourism 60%), Water (municipal APR), Health, Education, Campamentos (DS49)** | CLP 8.5B (FNDR 55%, Patentes 30%) |
| **Cámara de Turismo San Pedro** | Private non-profit (Law 20.500) | 120 members (agencies, hotels, guides, restaurants), Directorio 9 | **Industry coordination, Guide certification (with SENATUR), Marketing, Carrying capacity advocacy** | CLP 400M (Membership + projects) |

### 1.4.2 Coordination Mechanisms — The Triad in Practice

| Mechanism | Frequency | Output | Binding? |
|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Mesa Tripartita (CPA + Muni + Cámara)** | Monthly | Actas with commitments | **Yes (political)** |
| **Consulta Previa (CPA veto)** | Per project (SEA) | Resolución vinculante | **Yes (Legal - Law 19.253, ILO 169)** |
| **Plan de Manejo CONAF-CPA** | Annual (protected areas) | Visitor caps, trail maintenance, monitoring | **Yes (CONAF resolution)** |
| **PRC Update (Municipal + CPA input)** | 5-year cycle | Zoning, height limits, density | **Yes (Ordenanza Municipal)** |
| **Carrying Capacity Technical Table** | Quarterly | Site-specific caps, monitoring data | **Advisory → CONAF binding** |
