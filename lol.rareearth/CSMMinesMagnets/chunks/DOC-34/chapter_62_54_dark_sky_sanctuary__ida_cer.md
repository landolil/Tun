## 5.4 Dark Sky Sanctuary — IDA Certification 2025

### 5.4.1 Technical Requirements & Current Status

| IDA Requirement | Specification | San Pedro Current | Gap | Action |
|-----------------|---------------|-------------------|-----|--------|
| **SQM (Zenith)** | >21.5 mag/arcsec² | **21.8** | **PASS** | Monitor quarterly |
| **SQM (Horizon)** | >21.0 mag/arcsec² | **21.2** (NE glow from Calama) | **MARGINAL** | Calama lighting ordinance |
| **Lighting Management Plan** | Approved + enforced | **Draft (Municipal)** | **MISSING** | Enact Ordenanza 2024 |
| **Shielded Fixtures** | 100% full cutoff | **35%** (municipal only) | **65%** | Retrofit $1.2M |
| **Color Temperature** | ≤2200K (amber) | **3000K** (70% LED) | **FAIL** | Replace 2,800 fixtures |
| **Curfew (Commercial)** | 22:00-06:00 | **Voluntary 20%** | **FAIL** | Mandate + enforcement |
| **Public Education** | Annual program | **CPA + ALMA outreach** | **PARTIAL** | Expand to schools/tourism |
| **Monitoring Network** | 3+ SQM stations continuous | **1 (ALMA)** | **2 MISSING** | Deploy 2 CPA stations |

### 5.4.2 Economic Value of Dark Sky — The $15M Asset

| Metric | Value | Methodology |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Astro-Tourism Revenue** | $15M/yr | 30% of 150K visitors × $2,200 spend × 5.5 nights |
| **Premium over General Tourism** | 2.2× spend, 1.5× stay | Survey 2023 (n=1,200) |
| **Job Multiplier** | 1.8 (vs 1.3 general) | Input-output (tourism satellite account) |
| **Light Pollution Cost (Projected)** | **$5M/yr loss by 2030** | SQM degradation 0.02/yr → 21.4 by 2030 → 30% astro-tourist decline |
| **Retrofit Investment** | $1.3M | Municipal + private (50/50) |
| **ROI** | **1,000%+ over 10 years** | $1.3M protects $150M cumulative revenue |
