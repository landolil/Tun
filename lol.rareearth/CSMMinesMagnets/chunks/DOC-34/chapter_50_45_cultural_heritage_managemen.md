## 4.5 Cultural Heritage Management — Co-Management Model

### 4.5.1 The 380+ Sites — Governance by Category

| Category | Count | Management Authority | Visitor Access | Monitoring |
|----------|-------|---------------------|----------------|------------|
| **Monumentos Nacionales (DIBAM/CMN)** | 12 | **CPA + DIBAM + CONAF (tripartite)** | Nivel 2+ guide mandatory, caps | CPA technical unit + CONAF park rangers |
| **Sitios Arqueológicos CMN** | 380 | **CPA (Zona Sagra/Productiva)** | Nivel 2 guide, caps per site | CPA territorial monitors (1 per macrozone) |
| **Potenciales (LiDAR 2023)** | 800+ | **CPA (preventive protection)** | **Closed until survey** | CPA + Univ. Católica LiDAR monitoring |
| **Patrimonio Inmaterial (UNESCO)** | 7 domains | **CPA + UNESCO Chile** | Community-led transmission | CPA cultural unit + UNESCO reporting |

### 4.5.2 Visitor Impact Mitigation — The Archaeological Protocol

| Site | Threat | Mitigation | Cost | Status |
|------|--------|------------|------|--------|
| **Tulor (3000 BC)** | CO₂/humidity from visitors, vibration | **Visitor cap 50/day, boardwalk, forced ventilation, CO₂ sensors** | $1.2M | **Implemented 2023** |
| **Pukará de Quitor** | Wall erosion, graffiti, artifact theft | **Cap 200/day, Nivel 2 guide, CCTV, sacrificial plaster, community guards** | $800k | **Implemented 2022** |
| **Petroglifos Yerbas Buenas** | Touching, chalk tracing, dust | **Cap 100/day, viewing platform 10m, laser scanning baseline** | $450k | **2024 implementation** |
| **Iglesia San Pedro (17th C)** | Candle soot, humidity, seismic | **LED candles, humidity control, seismic retrofit (2024)** | $1.5M | **In progress** |
| **Chullpas (Funerary Towers)** | Looting, climbing, wind erosion | **Sacred zone (no access), drone monitoring, community ceremonies** | $200k | **CPA enforced** |
