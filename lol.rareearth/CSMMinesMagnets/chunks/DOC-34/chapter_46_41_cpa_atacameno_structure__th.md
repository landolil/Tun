## 4.1 CPA Atacameño Structure — The 18 Comunidades

### 4.1.1 Organizational Architecture

| Level | Body | Composition | Authority | Legal Basis |
|-------|------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Supreme** | **Asamblea General de Comunidades** | 18 comunidades × 2 delegates = 36 votes | **Strategic direction, budget approval, Directorio election, Consulta Previa mandate, territorial zoning** | Law 19.253 Art 34, CPA Estatutos |
| **Executive** | **Directorio (Board)** | Presidente, Vicepresidente, Secretario, Tesorero + 4 Voceros (rotating by macrozona) | **Daily management, legal representation, fund execution, external relations, Consulta Previa coordination** | Law 19.253, CPA Estatutos |
| **Territorial** | **8 Macrozonas** (grouping 18 comunidades) | Each: 2-3 comunidades, 1 Vocero in Directorio | **Local implementation, community consultation, benefit distribution, territorial monitoring** | CPA Reglamento Interno |
| **Technical** | **Unidades Técnicas** (5) | Professionals: Legal, Hydrogeology, Archaeology, Tourism, Administration | **Evidence generation, project evaluation, monitoring, capacity building** | CPA Orgánico |
