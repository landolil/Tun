### 3.2.2 Indigenous Law Art 13 — The CPA Priority Right

> **Law 19.253 Art 13**: *Las comunidades indígenas tienen derecho preferente al uso de las aguas que nacen o cruzan sus tierras...* (Indigenous communities have preferential right to waters originating or crossing their lands)

**Operational Conflict**:
1. **CPA holds 25 L/s priority right** (Chiu Chiu) — but ayllus need 35 L/s for pastoralism + tourism
2. **Municipal APR holds 45 L/s** (human consumption) — but 60% serves tourism, not residents
3. **Mining exploration holds 17 L/s** — **CPA veto active** on all new mining rights in aquifer
4. **Tourism demand growing 5%/yr** — no new rights available without reallocation

**Legal Precedent (2023)**: *CPA Atacameño vs DGA — Corte Suprema* — **Art 13 preference is substantive, not procedural — DGA must deny mining rights if aquifer sustainability threatened**. Result: **2 mining rights denied, 3 suspended**.
