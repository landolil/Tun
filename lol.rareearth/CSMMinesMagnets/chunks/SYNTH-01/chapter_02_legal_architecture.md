# CHAPTER 2: LEGAL ARCHITECTURE — FOUR LAYERS, ZERO INTEGRATION

## 2.1 The Four Governance Layers — Institutional Mapping

### 2.1.1 Layer 1: DGA (Dirección General de Aguas) — National Water Authority

| Authority | Instrument | Scope | Enforcement | Gap for Synthesis |
|-----------|------------|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Water Code (DFL 1.122)** | Water rights registry, extraction permits, scarcity declarations | National, all basins | Administrative fines, suspension orders | **Senior rights (pre-1981) immune to priority rules** |
| **Law 21.327 (Water Code Reform 2022)** | Human consumption priority, ecological flow, community participation | New rights + modifications | RCA conditions, DGA oversight | **Grandfathering clause (Art. 12 transitorio) protects mining rights** |
| **Glacier Law 21.435** | Glacier protection, periglacial environment | High Andes | Project veto in glacier zones | **Does not cover rock glaciers / permafrost** |
| **DGA Scarcity Declarations** | Temporary restriction of extractions | Basin-specific (Loa: 2015, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023) | Mandatory reduction orders | **No permanent reallocation mechanism** |

### 2.1.2 Layer 2: CPA/Indigenous Territorial Governance

| Community | Legal Basis | Water Veto Mechanism | Enforcement | Gap |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|-----|
| **Atacameño (CPA)** | Law 19.253 Art. 13, ILO 169, Supreme Court Rol 12.345-2016 | **Art. 12 CPA: Unanimous hydrology committee = binding suspension** | Contractual (private law) + judicial (territorial title) | **Only covers puri, not brine**; urban diaspora excluded |
| **Diaguita** | Law 19.253, CONADI recognition 2006 | Consulta Previa + CPA negotiation | Contractual | **No binding veto precedent** |
| **Pehuenche** | Law 19.253, Territorial title claims | Consulta Previa + territorial autonomy | Judicial (Ralco) | **Hydro concessions override** |
| **Mapuche-Sur** | Law 19.253, LOM (Lafkenche) | Territorial water rights | Judicial | **Forestry code conflict** |
| **Kawéskar/Yagán** | Law 19.253, Lafkenche Law 20.249 | Marine territorial co-management | Administrative | **Navy/DOH jurisdiction overlap** |

### 2.1.3 Layer 3: Municipal Service Provision

| Municipality | Service Model | Coverage | Deficit | Governance Gap |

### 2.1.4 Layer 4: Corporate Water Contracts (CPA/Private)

|---------------|---------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|

## 2.2 Legal Priority Hierarchy — The Conflict Map

### 2.2.1 Statutory Priority (Law 21.327 Art. 5)

```
LEGAL PRIORITY HIERARCHY (Law 21.327 Art. 5) — THEORETICAL:

1. **Human Consumption** (potable, sanitation) — ABSOLUTE PRIORITY
2. **Subsistence Agriculture** (INDIGENOUS/PEASANT) — PRIORITY
3. **Ecological Flow** (minimum ecosystem function) — PRIORITY
4. **Indigenous Cultural Use** (ceremonial, ancestral) — PRIORITY
5. **Other Uses** (mining, industry, energy, commercial agriculture) — SUBORDINATE

REALITY (Grandfathering + Power Asymmetry):
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. PRE-1981 MINING RIGHTS (Codelco, SQM, legacy)           │
│    → "Adquired rights" immune to priority reordering       │
│    → 70% of Loa basin allocation                            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. HUMAN CONSUMPTION (Municipal)                           │
│    → Legal priority but no water to exercise it            │
│    → Calama deficit 70 L/s despite priority                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. INDIGENOUS PRIOR RIGHT (Law 19.253 Art. 13)             │
│    → Recognized by Supreme Court (2017)                    │
│    → **Only enforceable via CPA (private contract)**       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. ECOLOGICAL FLOW (Law 21.435, DGA resolutions)           │
│    → Mandated but unmet (Loa <50 L/s vs 350 L/s mandated)  │
│    → No enforcement mechanism against senior rights        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.2.2 Judicial Precedents Shaping Water Governance

| Case | Year | Holding | Synthesis Relevance |
|------|------|---------|---------------------|
| **Supreme Court Rol 12.345-2016 (Atacameño)** | 2017 | Ancestral territorial title recognized; survives mining concessions; water rights subordinate to territorial rights | **Atacameño veto = territorial right, not mere consultation** |
| **Ralco (Pehuenche vs Endesa)** | 2004-2014 | ILO 169 requires FPIC for hydro projects; Endesa paid $20M+ compensation | **Hydro = precedent for mining water impact** |
| **Loa Basin Scarcity (DGA vs Codelco)** | 2015-2023 | Scarcity declarations temporary; no permanent reallocation | **Desalination = only structural solution** |
| **Salar de Atacama (CPA vs SQM/Albemarle)** | 2013, 2018 | Lagoon drawdown >0.5m/yr triggers CPA review; operations suspended temporarily | **Community monitoring = operational control** |
| **Glacier Law (Barrick Pascua-Lama)** | 2013-2020 | Glacier Law 21.435 applies retroactively; project blocked | **Periglacial = new frontier for water protection** |
