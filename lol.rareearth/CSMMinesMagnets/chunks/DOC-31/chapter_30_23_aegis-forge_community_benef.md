## 2.3 Aegis-Forge Community Benefit Architecture — The FBC/CDC Integration

### 2.3.1 Aegis-Forge Calama SpA — FBC Equity Partner

**Equity Structure (Term Sheet Signed 2024-10-15)**:
| Shareholder | % | Commitment | Rights |
|-------------|---|------------|--------|
| **Aegis-Forge Global (Tardigradia SpA)** | 55% | US$85M capex + tech license | Board control (3/5), IP ownership |
| **FBC Calama (via CDC)** | **15%** | **US$0 (carried equity)** | **Board (1/5), Dividend priority 8% IRR, Veto on community impact** |
| **Codelco (Calama Division)** | 10% | **In-kind: feedstock access (tailings, waste rock), land, water rights** | Board (1/5), Offtake rights (Cu, REE) |
| **Workers' Federation (Federación Minera)** | 10% | **Sweat equity: workforce transition, training commitment** | Board (1/5), Employment priority, Profit share |
| **Atacameño People (CPA)** | 10% | **Territorial consent, cultural protocol, water stewardship** | **Veto on ancestral territory, Cultural protocol compliance** |

**Valuation Basis**: **Pre-money US$170M** (DCF: 15-yr, 12% WACC, Cu $4.50/lb, Nd $120/kg, Dy $350/kg)
- **FBC 15% = US$25.5M notional** — **carried, no cash call**
- **Codelco 10% = US$17M in-kind** — **tailings access valued at $12M, land/water $5M**
- **Federación 10% = US$17M sweat** — **valued at workforce transition cost avoidance**
- **CPA 10% = US$17M territorial** — **valued at consent premium (vs conflict cost)**
