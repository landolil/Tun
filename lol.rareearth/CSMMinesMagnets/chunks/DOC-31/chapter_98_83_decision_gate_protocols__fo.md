## 8.3 Decision Gate Protocols — Formal Governance

### 8.3.1 Gate Review Package (Each Gate)

| Document | Prepared By | Reviewed By | Approval Threshold |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Technical Performance Report** | Aegis-Forge Engineering | Independent Engineer | Meets spec ±5% |
| **Financial Model Update** | Aegis-Forge Finance | FBC Financial Advisor | IRR > hurdle, DSCR >1.5× |
