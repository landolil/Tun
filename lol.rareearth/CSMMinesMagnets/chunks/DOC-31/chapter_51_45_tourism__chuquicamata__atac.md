| Metric | 2019 (Pre-COVID) | 2023 | 2024 Target | 2030 Vision |
|--------|------------------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Annual Visitors (Calama Commune)** | 45,000 | 38,000 | 55,000 | **120,000** |
| **Chuquicamata Mine Tours** | 12,000 | 8,500 | 15,000 | **30,000** |
| **Atacama Desert Tours (San Pedro base)** | 30,000 | 25,000 | 35,000 | 60,000 |
| **Average Spend/Visitor** | US$180 | US$210 | US$240 | **US$350** |

| **Direct Jobs** | 320 | 280 | 400 | **1,000** |
