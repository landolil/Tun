### 1.6.2 Arsenic — The Invisible Contaminant

**Arsenic Exposure Pathways in Calama**:
```
ARSENIC EXPOSURE MODEL — CALAMA RESIDENTS

PATHWAY 1: INHALATION (Mine Dust)
├── Source: Chuqui/RT/MH dust emissions (PM10: 45 μg/m³ annual avg)
├── As concentration in dust: 120–350 mg/kg (ore grade dependent)
├── Deposition: 0.8–2.1 μg/m²/day (residential zones)
├── Inhalation dose: 0.15–0.45 μg/kg/day (adult, 20 m³/day breathing)
└── Contribution to urinary As: **35–45%**

PATHWAY 2: WATER (Loa River)
├── Raw Loa River As: 45 μg/L (upstream geogenic + mining)
├── Treatment plant removal: 82% → 8 μg/L at tap (std 10 μg/L)
├── Consumption: 2.5 L/day × 8 μg/L = 20 μg/day
├── Absorption: 80% → 16 μg/day systemic
└── Contribution to urinary As: **30–40%**

PATHWAY 3: DIET (Local Agriculture)
├── Loa Valley crops (alfalfa, corn, citrus): As 0.8–2.5 mg/kg dry
├── Meat/dairy (cattle watered with Loa): As 0.05–0.15 mg/kg
├── Daily intake: 15–35 μg/day
└── Contribution to urinary As: **15–25%**

PATHWAY 4: GEOGENIC DUST (Natural)
├── Atacama soils: As 15–40 mg/kg (volcanic origin)
├── Wind resuspension: PM10 25 μg/m³ (non-mining days)
└── Contribution to urinary As: **5–10%**

TOTAL URINARY AS PREDICTED: **38–52 μg/g creat** (matches measured 42 μg/g)
```
