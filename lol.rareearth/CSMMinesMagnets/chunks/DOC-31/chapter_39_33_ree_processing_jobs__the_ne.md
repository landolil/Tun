### 3.3.2 Workforce Transition — From Contractor to Aegis-Forge

**Transition Pathway (Codelco Contractor → Aegis-Forge Employee)**:
```
TRANSITION PROGRAM — 18 MONTHS (2025-2027)

PHASE 1: IDENTIFICATION & COMMITMENT (Months 1-3)
├── Codelco + Federación + Aegis-Forge joint committee
├── 600 contractor workers pre-selected (seniority, skills, interest)
├── Medical screening (spirometry, As, Pb, Hg, audiometry)
├── Skills assessment (SENCE competency framework)
└── Commitment signing: 3-yr employment guarantee post-training

PHASE 2: FOUNDATIONAL TRAINING (Months 4-9) — PTI Calama + Aegis Academy
├── Module A: Aegis-Forge Process Fundamentals (80 hrs) — ALL
├── Module B: Specialization Tracks (240 hrs each):
│   ├── Track 1: Alpha-1 Operator (liberation, sorting, drying)
│   ├── Track 2: Alpha-2 Operator (inductive cascade, FBG monitoring)
│   ├── Track 3: Maintenance Tech (mech/elect/instrumentation)
│   ├── Track 4: Process Control (DCS, Digital Twin, ML optimization)
│   └── Track 5: HSE + Quality (ISO 45001, 14001, 9001)
├── Module C: Cross-Cutting (80 hrs): Data literacy, English, safety leadership
├── Certification: **Aegis-Forge Certified Operator (AFCO)** — SENCE + Universidad Antofagasta
└── Stipend: **US$1,200/mo** (Codelco transition fund + Aegis-Forge + SENCE)

PHASE 3: ON-THE-JOB TRAINING (Months 10-15) — Alpha-1/2 Ramp-Up
├── Shadow shifts with commissioning team (vendor experts)
├── Progressive responsibility: Assistant → Junior → Certified Operator
├── Mentor pairing: 1 experienced : 2 trainees
├── Performance gate: **AFCO Level 2** (independent operation)
└── Salary: **70% → 85% → 100%** of target (US$2,800/mo base + benefits)

PHASE 4: STEADY STATE & UPSKILLING (Month 16+)
├── Continuous learning: 40 hrs/yr (paid)
├── Career ladder: Operator → Senior → Lead → Specialist → Supervisor
├── Rotation program: Alpha-1 ↔ Alpha-2 ↔ Training Center ↔ Clinic
├── Equity vesting: **Federación 10% equity → worker trust → annual allocation**
└── Pension: **AFP + Aegis-Forge supplemental (3% match)**
```
