### 1.1.2 The Three Giants — Codelco's Calama Division

| Mine | Type | Start Year | Current Capacity | Workforce | Key Metrics |
|------|------|------------|------------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Chuquicamata (Chuqui)** | Open pit → **Underground transition** | 1915 | **320 kt/yr Cu** (UG target 140 kt) | 4,200 direct | **World's largest open pit** (4.3km × 3km × 1km deep), UG block cave since 2019 |
| **Radomiro Tomic (RT)** | Open pit + **Heap leach / SX-EW** | 1997 | **280 kt/yr Cu** (cathode) | 2,800 direct | **World's largest SX-EW**, oxide ore, low cost |
| **Ministro Hales (MH)** | Open pit + **Concentrator** | 2014 | **220 kt/yr Cu** (concentrate) | 1,900 direct | **High-grade sulfide**, modern automation, desal water |

**Total Calama Division**: **820 kt/yr Cu** = **~10% world production** — **Codelco's largest division**
