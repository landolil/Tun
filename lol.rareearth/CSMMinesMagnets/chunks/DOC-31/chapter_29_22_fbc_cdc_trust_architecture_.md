## 2.2 FBC/CDC Trust Architecture — The Aegis-Forge Integration Layer

### 2.2.1 Fondo de Bienes Comunes (FBC) — Calama Territorial Trust

**FBC Calama — Legal Structure (Law 21.435 Art. 42 + DS 118)**:
```
FBC CALAMA — GOVERNANCE & CAPITAL STRUCTURE

SETTLOR (Disponente): Codelco Chile (Calama Division assets)
TRUSTEE (Fiduciario): BancoEstado Fideicomisos (independent)
BENEFICIARIES (Beneficiarios):
├── Primary: Calama Commune (territorial) — 60%
├── Secondary: Atacameño People (CPA) — 25%
├── Tertiary: Mining Workers (Federación) — 10%
└── Quaternary: Migrant Communities (recognized orgs) — 5%

TRUST COMMITTEE (Comité Fiduciario) — 11 Members:
├── 3 Calama Municipalidad (elected: Mayor + 2 Concejales)
├── 2 CPA Atacameño (elected: Asamblea General)
├── 2 Federación Minera Calama (elected: Congreso Sindical)
├── 1 GORE Antofagasta (appointed: Gobernador)
├── 1 Migrant Representative (elected: Mesa Migrante)
├── 1 Academic/Technical (Universidad de Antofagasta)
├── 1 Environmental NGO (Fundación Tantí)
└── 1 Independent Expert (rotating, 3-yr term)

DECISION RULES:
├── Ordinary: Simple majority (6/11)
├── Strategic (asset allocation, >US$10M): 2/3 (8/11) + CPA veto on ancestral territory
├── Emergency (disaster, health crisis): Trustee + 2 Committee (fast-track 48hr)
└── Amendment: 3/4 (9/11) + Codelco consent (while contributor)

INVESTMENT POLICY (IPS):
├── Mandate: **Perpetual real return ≥3.5%** (CPI + 3.5%)
├── Asset Allocation:
│   ├── 40% Chile Sovereign (BCU, Bonos) — liquidity, safety
│   ├── 25% Infrastructure Concessions (tolls, desal, transmission) — yield + strategic
│   ├── 20% Green Bonds (HIF, solar, wind, H₂) — mission alignment
│   ├── 10% Private Equity (mining tech, water tech, REE processing) — alpha
│   └── 5% Cash/Equivalents — operations
├── ESG Screen: **Exclude** — thermal coal, tobacco, weapons, labor violations
├── Impact Target: **≥40% investments in Calama/Antofagasta Region**
└── Distribution Rule: **4% of 3-yr rolling avg NAV** → annual grant budget
```
