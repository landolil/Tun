## 1.2 Labor Structure — The Union Fortress

### 1.2.1 Codelco Union Landscape (Calama Division)

| Union | Affiliation | Members | Mine Coverage | Key Agreements |
|-------|-------------|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **Sindicato N°1 Chuquicamata** | CUT | **4,200** | Chuqui UG | **Contrato Colectivo 2023–2026** — 4.5% real wage increase, health fund |
| **Sindicato Radomiro Tomic** | CUT | **2,800** | RT | **Contrato Colectivo 2024–2027** — productivity bonus, remote work clauses |
| **Sindicato Ministro Hales** | CUT | **1,900** | MH | **Contrato Colectivo 2023–2026** — automation transition fund |
| **Federación Minera de Calama** | CUT | **8,900** | All 3 mines | **Negociación Ramal** — industry-wide standards |
| **Sindicato Contratistas** | CUT | **6,200** | Contractor companies | **Equal pay for equal work** (Law 21.327 compliance) |
| **Sindicato Profesionales** | ANEF | **1,500** | Engineers, geologists, technicians | **Career path, remote rotation, mental health** |

**Total Unionized Direct Workforce**: **~10,900** (Codelco direct) + **~6,200** (contractor unionized) = **17,100**

### 1.2.2 Contractor vs Direct Employment — The Dual Structure

| Category | Direct (Codelco) | Contractor | Total | Ratio |
|----------|------------------|------------|-------|-------|
| **Operations** | 4,200 | 8,500 | 12,700 | 1:2 |
| **Maintenance** | 2,100 | 7,200 | 9,300 | 1:3.4 |
| **Projects/Construction** | 300 | 12,000 | 12,300 | 1:40 |
| **Services/Logistics** | 800 | 5,500 | 6,300 | 1:6.9 |
| **Total** | **7,400** | **33,200** | **40,600** | **1:4.5** |

**Critical Issue**: **Contractor workers earn 35–45% less** for same tasks, **higher accident rate (2.3×)**, **no career path**, **housing insecurity**.

- **Sernameg + Codelco**: Childcare centers at 3 campamentos (capacity 300)
- **Diversity Clause in Collective Agreements**: 2023 — harassment protocol, parental leave equity
