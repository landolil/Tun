## 6.2 Deep Dive — Top 5 Risks

### 6.2.1 R-01: Loa Basin Aquifer Collapse — The Existential Threat

**Scenario**: **Consecutive mega-drought (2025-2028) + glacier loss + demand growth → Loa River dry 8 months/yr, aquifer drawdown >3m/yr, municipal wells fail, agriculture collapses, indigenous territories uninhabitable**

**Causal Chain**:
```
CLIMATE FORCING
├── Temperature +2.5°C (2050, RCP 4.5) → Evapotranspiration +18%
├── Precipitation Altiplano -15% → Recharge -25%
├── Glacier Cerro Toco/Licancabur → **Gone by 2045** (current -3%/yr)
├── Snowpack duration -40 days → Spring pulse lost
└── Extreme events: **Flash floods (destructive) + Mega-droughts (prolonged)**

DEMAND PRESSURE
├── Codelco: Desal I+II online 2026 → **Codelco Loa extraction 0.2 m³/s** (from 1.62)
├── Municipal: Population +2%/yr → Demand 0.9 m³/s by 2030
├── Agriculture: **No desal allocation** → Curtailed 60% in drought
├── Indigenous: **Zero allocation guarantee** → Cultural survival threat
├── Mining Others (SQM, Albemarle): **Own desal** but brine discharge impacts
└── Ecological Flow: **<10% mandated** → Ecosystem collapse

TIPPING POINT: **Aquifer storage <20% capacity** → **Irreversible compaction, land subsidence, water quality collapse (As, B, Li ↑ 10×)**

IMPACT QUANTIFICATION (Aquifer Collapse Scenario):
├── Municipal Water Failure: **185,000 people** — emergency tankers (cost $12M/mo)
├── Agriculture Loss: **12,000 ha** — US$45M/yr revenue, 3,500 jobs
├── Indigenous Displacement: **8 communities (2,400 people)** — cultural genocide risk
├── Codelco Operations: **Forced curtailment** — US$2.1B/yr revenue at risk
├── Health Crisis: **Water-borne disease, As exposure 10×** — 5,000+ cases
├── Social Unrest: **Protests, roadblocks, mine shutdowns** — US$500M+ losses
└── **TOTAL ECONOMIC LOSS: US$3.2B/yr** (2.5% Chile GDP)
```

**Resilience Measures — Layered Defense**:
| Layer | Measure | Status | Capacity | Gap |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|-----|
| **1. Demand Reduction** | Codelco Desal I+II (2,000 L/s) | **Operational 2026** | Codelco 100% covered | Municipal 52% gap |
| **2. Supply Augmentation** | MAR (Managed Aquifer Recharge) | **Pilot 2024** | 500 L/s target | **Scale 10× needed** |
| **3. Efficiency** | Municipal leak reduction (45% → 20%) | **In progress** | 150 L/s saved | Funding gap $25M |
| **4. Allocation Reform** | Water Code reform → prioritize human/eco | **Legislative** | Structural | Political resistance |
| **5. Emergency** | Aquifer strategic reserve (deep wells) | **Planned** | 6 months supply | Capex $80M |
| **6. Regional** | **Loa Basin Water Authority** (new governance) | **Proposed** | Integrated mgmt | Requires Law |
