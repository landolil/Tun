|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|---------------------|

| **COPD Prevalence** | **18.2%** | 12.5% | 6.2% | **2.9×** |

**Silicosis Registry (SUSESO 2024)**:
- **Active Cases**: **1,240** (Calama Division)

- **Compensation Paid (2023)**: **US$28M** (Codelco + contractors)
- **Undiagnosed Estimate**: **300–500** (informal/contractor, no surveillance)
