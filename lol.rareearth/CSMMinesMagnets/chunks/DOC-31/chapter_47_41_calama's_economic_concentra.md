## 4.1 Calama's Economic Concentration Risk — The Copper Mono-Culture

### 4.1.1 Copper Dependency Metrics

| Indicator | Calama | Antofagasta Region | Chile | OECD Mining Regions (Avg) |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|-------|---------------------------|
| **Mining % of Regional GDP** | **58%** | 42% | 12% | 15–25% |
| **Codelco % of Calama Employment** | **45% direct + 35% indirect** | 28% | 3% | 10–20% |
| **Fiscal Revenue Mining-Linked** | **64% municipal** | 38% | 18% | 20–30% |
| **Export Concentration (Cu)** | **92% of commune exports** | 78% | 52% | 40–60% |
| **Economic Complexity Index (ECI)** | **-0.82 (low)** | -0.45 | 0.12 | >0.5 (diversified) |
| **HHI (Employment)** | **0.38 (high concentration)** | 0.22 | 0.08 | <0.15 |

| Cu Price | Royalty Revenue | FDC Revenue | Budget Gap | Austerity Required |

**Conclusion**: **Calama cannot survive a copper downcycle without structural diversification.**
