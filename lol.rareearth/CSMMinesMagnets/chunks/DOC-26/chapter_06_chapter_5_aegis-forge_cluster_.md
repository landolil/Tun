### 5.4 Zero-Water Operations — Aegis-Forge Design Verification

#### 5.4.1 Water Balance (Alpha-1/2 Combined)

```
Water Balance (Annual, Steady State):

INPUTS:
├── Seawater (Mejillones desal): 1.8 Mm³/yr (RO permeate)
├── Salar Brine (valorization feed): 27 Mm³/yr (not extracted, processed in situ)
├── Recycled Process Water: 88 Mm³/yr (98% recycle rate)
└── Rainfall capture (cluster footprint): 0.02 Mm³/yr (negligible)
TOTAL INPUT: 116.82 Mm³/yr

OUTPUTS:
├── Product Moisture (REE oxides, concentrates): 0.15 Mm³/yr
├── Evaporation (cooling, ponds): 1.2 Mm³/yr (air-cooled primary)
├── Brine Return (to Salar, post-REE extraction): 25.5 Mm³/yr
├── Crystallized Salts (byproduct, sold): 0.05 Mm³/yr
└── Losses (leaks, inefficiency): 0.4 Mm³/yr
TOTAL OUTPUT: 27.3 Mm³/yr

**Net Puri Consumption: 0 Mm³/yr** (contractual zero)
**Net Brine Consumption: 1.5 Mm³/yr** (REE removal concentrates brine slightly)
**Recycle Rate: 98%** (industry best practice: 85-90%)
```

#### 5.4.2 Zero-Aqueous-Discharge (ZAD) Verification

| Stream | Treatment | Discharge | Compliance |
|--------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Process Water** | Closed-loop RO + evaporation | **Zero** (recycled) | ZAD certified |
| **Cooling Blowdown** | Air-cooled (primary) + seawater HX | **Zero** (seawater return) | ZAD certified |
| **Tailings** | **None** (Aegis-Forge solid residues → construction aggregate) | **Zero** | ZAD certified |
| **Sanitary** | Containerized treatment → reuse (dust suppression) | **Zero** | ZAD certified |
| **Stormwater** | Capture → process water makeup | **Zero** | ZAD certified |

**Verification**: **Independent ZAD audit (quarterly)** + **Community water sentinel real-time monitoring** + **CPA Art. 12 veto trigger on any puri detection**.
