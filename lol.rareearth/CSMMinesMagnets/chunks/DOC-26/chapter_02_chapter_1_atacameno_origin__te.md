### 1.1 Atacameño Origin and Purpose — Territorial Sovereignty in the Hyperarid Core

The Atacameño (Lickanantay) people are the **indigenous inhabitants of the Salar de Atacama basin and surrounding Andean foothills** in what is now **Antofagasta Region, Chile**. Their territory spans **~30,000 km²** from the **Salar de Atacama (2,300 masl)** to **volcanic peaks >5,500 masl** (Licancabur, Lascar, Sairecabur). This document establishes the **operational intelligence baseline** for Aegis-Forge deployment in Alpha-1/2 clusters (Candelaria, Ojos del Salado, Manto Verde, Punta Cobre) within Atacameño territory.
