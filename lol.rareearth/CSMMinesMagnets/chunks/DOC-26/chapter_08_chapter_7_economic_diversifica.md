## Chapter 7: Economic Diversification — Tourism, Agriculture, Value-Added REE Processing

### 7.1 Tourism — High-Value, Low-Impact, Community-Owned

#### 7.1.1 Current Tourism Baseline

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2030 Target |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| **International Visitors** | 185,000 | 210,000 | 350,000 |
| **Domestic Visitors** | 95,000 | 110,000 | 180,000 |
| **Average Stay** | 3.2 nights | 3.5 nights | 4.5 nights |
| **Daily Spend (USD)** | $180 | $200 | $280 |
| **Total Revenue** | $150M | $185M | $380M |
| **Community Capture** | 15% | 22% | **40%** |

**Key Attractions**:
- **ALMA Observatory** (50% of international draw) — Chajnantor plateau (5,000m)
- **Geysers del Tatio** (30%) — 4,300m, third-largest geyser field globally
- **Valle de la Luna / Muerte** (20%) — Atacama Desert landscapes
- **Lagunas Altiplánicas** (15%) — Flamingo watching, photography
- **Cultural Tourism** (growing) — Ayllu stays, Kunza workshops, ceremonies

#### 7.1.2 Community-Owned Tourism Development

```
CDC Tourism Investment Portfolio (2025-2030):

1. **Ayllu Lodge Network** ($12M investment):
   - 11 lodges (1 per ayllu, 8-12 rooms each)
   - Community-owned, professionally managed
   - Cultural immersion: Kunza lessons, weaving, cooking, ceremonies
   - **Revenue**: $8M/yr by 2030, 60 jobs

2. **ALMA Visitor Center + Astronomy Tourism** ($8M):
   - San Pedro base: Planetarium, tours coordination, astrophotography
   - Community guides trained (ATTI astronomy module)
   - **Revenue**: $5M/yr, 25 jobs

3. **Geysers del Tatio Community Concession** ($5M):
   - Negotiate concession from National Assets Ministry
   - Community operation: access control, guiding, café, crafts
   - **Revenue**: $4M/yr, 20 jobs

4. **High-Altitude Wellness Retreats** ($3M):
   - Luxury segment: altitude acclimatization, stargazing, silence
   - Partnerships: international wellness brands
   - **Revenue**: $3M/yr, 15 jobs

5. **Cultural Festival Circuit** ($2M/yr operating):
   - Lickanantay New Year (June), Solstices, Pachamama (August)
   - Kunza language festival, traditional music, craft fairs
   - **Revenue**: $1.5M/yr direct, 10x multiplier

**Total Tourism Investment**: $30M capex + $2M/yr operating
**Total Tourism Revenue (2030)**: $21.5M/yr direct + $150M/yr indirect
**Community Jobs**: 120 direct, 300 indirect
```

### 7.2 Agriculture — Water-Efficient, High-Value, Climate-Resilient

#### 7.2.1 Current Agricultural Baseline

| Crop/Livestock | Area (ha) | Production (t/yr) | Water Use (m³/ha/yr) | Revenue ($/ha/yr) |
|----------------|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Alfalfa** | 1,200 | 18,000 | 12,000 | $1,200 |
| **Quinoa** | 300 | 450 | 3,500 | $4,500 |
| **Corn (choclo)** | 200 | 1,600 | 8,000 | $2,500 |
| **Fruit (citrus, figs, pomegranate)** | 150 | 1,200 | 10,000 | $8,000 |
| **Llama/Alpaca** | 15,000 head | 300 t fiber | 500 (per head) | $200/head |
| **Vicuña (wild, managed)** | 8,000 | 2 t fiber | Natural | $400/kg fiber |
| **TOTAL** | **~2,000 ha + grazing** | — | **~25 Mm³/yr puri** | **~$8M/yr** |

#### 7.2.2 Diversification Strategy (CDC + Water Trust Funded)

```
Agricultural Transformation Plan (2025-2030):

1. **Water Efficiency Revolution** ($15M investment):
   - Drip irrigation conversion: 100% of crops by 2028
   - Soil moisture sensors + automated scheduling (IoT)
   - **Water savings: 40%** (25 → 15 Mm³/yr puri)
   - Hydroponic greenhouses (5 ha): 90% water savings, 10x yield/ha

2. **High-Value Crop Transition** ($8M):
   - Quinoa expansion: 300 → 800 ha (organic, export)
   - Ancient grains: Kañiwa, amaranth, tarwi
   - High-altitude berries: Maqui, calafate, murtilla
   - Medicinal herbs: Rica-rica, pingo-pingo, chachacoma
   - **Revenue/ha target: $15,000-25,000** (vs $1,200 alfalfa)

3. **Camelid Value-Add** ($5M):
   - Community-owned fiber processing (spinning, weaving, felting)
   - Vicuña fiber certification (CITES, community quota)
   - Llama meat (gourmet, low-fat, high-protein)
   - **Revenue target: $2M/yr** (vs $0.5M raw fiber)

4. **Agro-Ecological Restoration** ($7M):
   - Wetland/vega restoration (water trust funded)
   - Native species reforestation (queñoa, yareta, tola)
   - Carbon sequestration credits (verra/gold standard)
   - **Carbon revenue: $1M/yr by 2030**

**Projected Agriculture (2030)**:
- Puri use: **15 Mm³/yr** (40% reduction)
- Revenue: **$25M/yr** (3x current)
- Jobs: **400 direct** (vs 150 current)
- **Community capture: 85%** (vs 60% current)
```

#### 7.3.2 Local Processing Zone — San Pedro de Atacama Industrial Park

```
Parque Industrial REE Atacama (PIREA):

Location: 500 ha, 15km north of San Pedro (outside lagoon buffer, flat, grid-connected)
Zoning: **Industrial light (REE processing), supported by CPA + municipal ordinance**

Infrastructure (CDC + FBC + GORE + CORFO funded):
- **Electrical**: 50 MW substation (GFM-ready, solar + battery)
- **Water**: Seawater pipeline (Mejillones, 200 L/s) + brine return line
- **Gas**: Green H₂ pipeline (HIF Magallanes, future)
- **Rail**: FCAB spur (concentrate in, product out)
- **Digital**: Fiber optic (5G, low latency for process control)
- **Waste**: Zero discharge — solid residues to construction aggregate plant

Anchor Tenants (CDC equity participation):
1. **CeO₂ Polishing Plant** (CDC 30%, JM/BASF 70%)
2. **La₂O₃ Catalyst Plant** (CDC 25%, Umicore/Topsoe 75%)

4. **Mischmetal/Sc-Al Plant** (CDC 35%, local/regional 65%)
5. **Lu₂O₃ Scintillator Plant** (CDC 40%, Siemens/GE 60%)

Community Benefits:
- **Priority hiring**: ATTI graduates, Atacameño families
- **Procurement**: 50% local (CDC suppliers)
- **Tax base**: Municipal revenue (San Pedro, Calama)
- **Technology spillover**: ATTI curriculum alignment, R&D partnerships
```
