## Chapter 4: FBC/CDC Trust Design — 20% Equity, 15% FCF, Water Trust, Cultural Heritage Fund, Governance

### 4.1 FBC (Foundational Benefit Corporation) — 20% Equity Structure

#### 4.1.1 FBC-Atacama Design

```
FBC-Atacama (Fundación de Beneficio Comunitario Atacama):

Legal Form: **Corporación de Derecho Privado sin Fines de Lucro** (Law 20.500)
Tax Status: **Donataria (Art. 33 LIR)** — tax-exempt, donor deductions
Equity Holding: **20% of Alpha-1 SpA + 20% of Alpha-2 SpA** (voting shares)

Capitalization:
- Initial: 20% equity at SpA formation (pre-revenue valuation)
- Anti-dilution: **Full ratchet** — FBC% maintained at each capital raise
- Dividend Rights: **Pro-rata** (20% of dividends declared)
- Liquidation Preference: **1x invested capital + accrued dividends**
- Put Option: FBC can put shares to Tardigradia at **fair market value (3rd party)** after Year 10
- Call Option: Tardigradia can call FBC shares at **1.5x FMV** after Year 15

Governance Board (7 Members):
├── 3 Atacameño Appointees (CDA Council selection)
├── 2 Independent Experts (hydrology, finance, governance)
├── 1 Tardigradia Appointee (non-voting on community matters)
└── 1 CONADI Observer (non-voting, oversight)

Decision Thresholds:
- Routine: Simple majority (4/7)
- **Water Veto: Unanimous Atacameño appointees (3/3)**
- Equity decisions: 5/7 (including ≥2 Atacameño)
- Budget >$5M: 5/7
- Amendment: 6/7 (including all 3 Atacameño)
```

#### 4.1.2 FBC Financial Flows

```
FBC-Atacama Revenue Streams (Projected 2030):

1. **Dividends (20% of Alpha-1/2 FCF)**:
   - Alpha-1 FCF (2030): $180M/yr → FBC share: $36M/yr
   - Alpha-2 FCF (2030): $120M/yr → FBC share: $24M/yr
   - **Total Dividends: $60M/yr**

2. **Capital Gains** (put/call exercise, secondary sales):
   - Projected: $15-25M/yr (lumpy)

3. **Management Fees** (from CDC for shared services):
   - $2M/yr

**Total FBC Annual Resources (2030): ~$75-85M/yr**

Allocation Mandate (FBC Bylaws):
- 40% → **Water Trust** (hydrology monitoring, aquifer recharge, legal defense)
- 25% → **Community Development** (infrastructure, housing, health, education)
- 15% → **Cultural Heritage** (Kunza language, ceremonies, sites, elders)
- 10% → **Economic Diversification** (tourism, agriculture, REE value-add ventures)
- 10% → **Endowment** (perpetual fund, 4% spending rule)

Reserves: **Minimum 3 years operating budget** ($225M by 2030)
```

### 4.2 CDC (Community Development Corporation) — 15% FCF

#### 4.2.1 CDC-Atacama Design

```
CDC-Atacama (Corporación de Desarrollo Comunitario Atacama):

Legal Form: **Corporación de Derecho Privado** (Law 20.500) — **for-profit enabled**
Tax Status: **Standard corporate** (pays taxes, reinvests)
Revenue Right: **15% of Free Cash Flow** from Alpha-1 SpA + Alpha-2 SpA

FCF Definition (Per CPA):
FCF = EBITDA - Capex - Interest - Taxes - Working Capital Change
     - Environmental Bond Contributions
     - FBC Dividend Preference (if any)

Projected CDC Revenue (2030):
- Alpha-1 FCF: $180M → CDC 15% = $27M
- Alpha-2 FCF: $120M → CDC 15% = $18M
- **Total CDC Revenue: $45M/yr**

Governance Board (9 Members):
├── 4 Atacameño Appointees (Ayllu assembly election, 4-year staggered)
├── 2 Independent Directors (business, development)
├── 1 Tardigradia Representative
├── 1 Municipal Representative (San Pedro de Atacama)
└── 1 Regional Government Representative (GORE Antofagasta)

Investment Mandate (CDC Bylaws):
- **Minimum 60% in Atacameño territory** (communes: San Pedro, Calama, Antofagasta)
- **Maximum 20% outside territory** (diversification)
- **Prohibited**: Fossil fuels, gambling, weapons, extractive (non-Aegis)
- **Priority Sectors**: 
  ├── Renewable energy (solar, green H₂)
  ├── Sustainable agriculture (quinoa, camelids, hydroponics)
  ├── Tourism infrastructure (ALMA, geysers, cultural)
  ├── REE value-add (polishing, catalysts, alloys)
  ├── Education/training (technical, intercultural)
  └── Health (traditional + western integration)
```

### 4.3 Water Trust — Hydrological Sovereignty Vehicle

#### 4.3.1 Water Trust Structure

```
Water Trust Atacama (Fideicomiso Hidrológico Atacama):

Legal Form: **Fideicomiso (Trust) under Law 19.303**
Trustee: **BancoEstado (fiduciary) + CDA (beneficiary representative)**
Protector: **Independent hydrologist (rotating, 3-year term)**

Trust Assets (Funded by FBC 40% allocation = $24M/yr by 2030):
1. **Monitoring Endowment**: $150M (perpetual, funds RMHC-Atacama operations)
2. **Legal Defense Fund**: $50M (water rights litigation, DGA challenges)
3. **Aquifer Recharge Fund**: $100M (managed aquifer recharge, wetland restoration)
4. **Contingency Reserve**: $200M (climate adaptation, drought resilience)

Trust Mandate:
- **Absolute priority**: Puri aquifer protection (zero drawdown, zero contamination)
- **Monitoring sovereignty**: Community owns data, controls access
- **Veto enforcement**: Trust funds legal injunctions for CPA Art. 12 veto
- **Intergenerational**: Perpetual trust, spending rule 3% of corpus

Governance:
- **Beneficiary Committee**: 11 Ayllu representatives (water authorities)
- **Technical Advisory**: 3 independent hydrologists + 1 Atacameño knowledge keeper
- **Annual Report**: To CDA Council, CONADI, DGA, SEA
```

### 4.4 Cultural Heritage Fund — Kunza, Ceremonies, Sites, Elders

#### 4.4.1 Fund Structure & Programs

```
Cultural Heritage Fund (Fondo Patrimonio Cultural Atacameño):

Source: FBC 15% allocation = $9M/yr (by 2030)
Governance: **Council of Elders (Consejo de Sabios)** + CDA Cultural Committee

Programs:
1. **Kunza Language Revitalization** ($3M/yr):
   - Immersion schools (3: Toconao, Peine, Socaire)
   - Adult classes (Calama, Antofagasta, Santiago)
   - Digital archive (recordings, dictionary, grammar)
   - Teacher training (intercultural pedagogy)

2. **Ceremonial Practice Support** ($2M/yr):
   - Annual ceremonies: Lickanantay New Year, Solstices, Water ceremonies, Pachamama
   - Ceremonial materials (textiles, ceramics, instruments)
   - Site access & protection (licancabur, lascar, lagoons, petroglyphs)
   - Intergenerational transmission (elders-youth camps)

3. **Cultural Site Protection** ($2M/yr):
   - Archaeological monitoring (pre-construction, ongoing)
   - Site documentation (3D scanning, oral history mapping)
   - Legal protection (National Monument requests, UNESCO)
   - Community archaeology (Atacameño-led research)

4. **Elder Support & Knowledge Keeping** ($1M/yr):
   - Elder stipends (knowledge keepers, ceremonial leaders)
   - Health integration (traditional medicine + western)
   - Oral history recording (video, audio, transcript)
   - Succession planning (apprentice knowledge keepers)

5. **Cultural Innovation** ($1M/yr):
   - Contemporary Atacameño art (residencies, exhibitions)
   - Digital media (VR territory, language apps)
   - Cultural tourism products (community-owned)
   - Research partnerships (universities, co-design)
```
