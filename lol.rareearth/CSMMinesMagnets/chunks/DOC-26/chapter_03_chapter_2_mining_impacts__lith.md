### 2.5 Chapter 2 Summary

**SQM/Albemarle brine operations** (220 Mm³/yr) drive **lagoon drawdown (0.3-0.7m/yr)** — **approaching CPA red alert thresholds**. **Cu/REE operations (Alpha-1/2) use zero puri** — **desalination only**. **Aegis-Forge Alpha-1/2 adds +27 Mm³/yr brine valorization** (not extraction) with **zero puri, zero tailings, zero aqueous discharge**. **CIA identifies brine→lagoons→flamingos as critical pathway** — **community hydrology monitoring + CPA veto** is the operational control. **Aegis-Forge zero-tailings design eliminates D5 driver** — **net improvement vs status quo**.
