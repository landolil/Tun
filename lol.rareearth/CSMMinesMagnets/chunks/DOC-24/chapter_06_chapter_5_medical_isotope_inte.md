### 5.2 Y-90 Radioembolization Supply Chain

#### 5.2.1 Y-90 Production Routes

```
Route 1: Sr-90/Y-90 Generator (Standard)
    Sr-90 (T½=28.8 yr, β⁻) → Y-90 (T½=64.1h, β⁻ max 2.28 MeV)
    - Sr-90 from fission (Hanford, Mayak, La Hague)
    - Generator: Sr-90 on TiO₂/SrCO₃ column → Y-90 elution (0.9% NaCl)
    - **Chilean role**: Y₂O₃ 4N for generator column matrix

Route 2: Direct (n,γ) on Y-89 (NCA)
    Y-89 (100% nat.) + n → Y-90 (T½=64.1h)
    - Specific activity: ~500 GBq/mg (CA)
    - Lower purity than generator route
```

#### 5.2.2 Chilean Y₂O₃ for Y-90 Generators

```
Generator Column Matrix:
- Y₂O₃ or Y-doped TiO₂ / ZrO₂ / SnO₂
- Requirements: High surface area, radiation stability, Sr/Y separation factor >10⁶
- Chilean Y₂O₃ 4N (Aegis-Forge) → Qualified for generator matrix production
- **Annual Y-90 demand**: ~50,000 Ci/yr → ~10 kg Y₂O₃/yr for generators
```
