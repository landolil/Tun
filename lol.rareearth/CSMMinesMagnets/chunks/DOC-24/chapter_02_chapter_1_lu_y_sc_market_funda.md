## Chapter 1: Lu/Y/Sc Market Fundamentals — Aerospace, SCR, Scintillators, Isotopes

### 1.1 Lu/Y/Sc Market Origin and Purpose — High-Value Specialty Applications

Lutetium (Lu), Yttrium (Y), and Scandium (Sc) are **ultra-high-value specialty REEs** with **irreplaceable roles in aerospace alloys, environmental catalysis, nuclear medicine, and radiation detection**. Unlike bulk REEs (Ce, La, Nd), Lu/Y/Sc command **premium pricing ($300-1,500/kg)** driven by **performance-critical applications** with **zero commercial substitutes**.

#### 1.1.1 Strategic Imperative

**Williams Lock**: **EPA Tier 4 / Euro 6** NOx regulations require **Y₂O₃-stabilized SCR catalysts** (heavy-duty diesel, marine, power gen). **AMS 4340 / AMS 4341** aerospace specs mandate **Sc-Al master alloy (0.1-0.5% Sc)** for **weldable high-strength airframes**. **Lu-177 (T½=6.65d)** is **standard-of-care for neuroendocrine tumors** (PRRT) — **global shortage** drives Lu₂O₃ demand. Chile's **HREE-enriched IOCG + ion-adsorption clay + Aegis-Forge Stage 8 HREE SX** creates **qualifying-country Lu/Y/Sc supply chain**.

**Keymaker Lock**: The empirical gap is **Lu/Yb separation (β=1.2)** — the **most difficult REE separation** requiring 25+ SX stages. The key is **Aegis-Forge Stage 8 fractional condensation pre-separation (LuCl₃ 190°C, YbCl₃ 185°C) + 25-stage HEH[EHP] SX** achieving **4N Lu₂O₃ at 1/3 Chinese OPEX**.

**SHIELD Horizon**: Lu/Y/Sc is **strategic deployment element** (2-5 years) — Sc-Al aerospace qualification (AMS), SCR catalyst mandate (2027), Lu-177 supply security (2026+). Decisions here define 2030 aerospace alloy autonomy and 2035 radiopharmaceutical sovereignty.

### 1.3 Lu/Y/Sc Application Physics

#### 1.3.1 Sc in Al Alloys (Grain Refinement & Strengthening)

```
Sc-Al Phase Diagram:
- Al₃Sc (L1₂, cubic) — coherent precipitate
- Solubility: 0.31% at 660°C, 0.03% at 300°C
- Precipitation: 250-350°C → Al₃Sc (5-20 nm, coherent)

Mechanisms:
1. **Grain Refinement**: Al₃Sc nucleates α-Al grains → **grain size 10-50 μm** (vs 1-5 mm w/o Sc)
   - Eliminates hot cracking in welding
   - Enables **weldable high-strength alloys** (no post-weld heat treat)

2. **Precipitation Strengthening**: Al₃Sc coherent precipitates
   - Δσ = 0.81 × G × b × √(f/r)  (Orowan bypassing)
   - f = volume fraction (~0.5% at 0.2% Sc), r = precipitate radius
   - **Yield strength +50-100 MPa per 0.1% Sc**

3. **Recrystallization Resistance**: Al₃Sc pins subgrain boundaries
   - **Thermal stability to 400°C** (vs 250°C for Al-Zn-Mg-Cu)

Key Alloys:
- **Al-Sc-Zr (Scalmalloy®)**: 0.12% Sc, 0.12% Zr → 520 MPa UTS, weldable
- **Al-Mg-Sc (AA5024 mod)**: 0.3% Sc → marine/shipbuilding
- **Al-Li-Sc**: 0.1% Sc + 1.5% Li → aerospace (density 2.55 g/cm³)
```

#### 1.3.2 Y₂O₃ in SCR Catalysts (NOx Reduction)

```
Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) Reaction:
4NO + 4NH₃ + O₂ → 4N₂ + 6H₂O  (V₂O₅/WO₃-TiO₂ catalyst)

Y₂O₃ Role (5-10 wt% in catalyst):
1. **TiO₂ Stabilization**: Prevents anatase→rutile transition (>600°C)
   - Y³⁺ substitutes Ti⁴⁺ → oxygen vacancies → stabilizes anatase
   - Surface area retention: **>80 m²/g after 800°C aging** (vs <20 w/o Y)

2. **SO₂/SO₃ Resistance**: Y₂O₃ captures sulfur as Y₂(SO₄)₃
   - Prevents V₂O₅ sulfation (activity loss)
   - **Regenerable** at 650°C (Y₂(SO₄)₃ → Y₂O₃ + 3SO₂ + 1.5O₂)

3. **V₂O₅ Dispersion**: Y-O-V bonds enhance V⁵⁺/V⁴⁺ redox
   - Low-temperature activity (200-250°C) improved 30%

EPA Tier 4 / Euro 6 / IMO Tier III Compliance:
- **Y₂O₃-doped SCR = only proven technology** for 90%+ NOx reduction
- Catalyst life: 24,000-40,000 hrs (vs 8,000 w/o Y)
- **Y demand per MW**: 50-100 kg Y₂O₃/MW (power plant SCR)
```

#### 1.3.3 Lu₂O₃ in Scintillators (TOF-PET)

```
Lu₂O₃-Based Scintillators (Time-of-Flight PET):

LSO:Ce (Lu₂SiO₅:Ce):
- Density: 7.4 g/cm³ → **high stopping power** (511 keV γ)
- Light yield: 30,000 ph/MeV (75% NaI:Tl)
- Decay time: **40 ns** (fast → TOF capability)
- **TOF resolution: 200-300 ps** → image SNR 2-3x improvement
- Lu-176 background: 2.6% (β⁻, T½=3.8×10¹⁰ yr) → manageable

LYSO:Ce (Lu₁.₈Y₀.₂SiO₅:Ce):
- Y substitution → **lower Lu-176 background**, faster decay (38 ns)
- Light yield: 32,000 ph/MeV
- **Preferred for premium TOF-PET** (Siemens Biograph Vision, GE Discovery MI)

LuAG:Ce (Lu₃Al₅O₁₂:Ce):
- Density: 6.7 g/cm³
- Decay: 50 ns, Light yield: 25,000 ph/MeV
- **No intrinsic radioactivity** (vs LSO/LYSO Lu-176)
- Emerging for **high-count-rate PET/CT**

Chilean Lu Advantage: **4N Lu₂O₃ from Alpha-17** → **lowest U/Th/K impurities** → **lowest background** → **premium scintillator grade**.
```

#### 1.5.2 Lu/Yb Separation Thermodynamics

```
Lu/Yb Separation (Most Difficult REE Pair):
- Ionic Radii: Lu³⁺ 0.861 Å, Yb³⁺ 0.868 Å (Δ = 0.007 Å, 0.8%)
- HEH[EHP] Selectivity: β = 1.15-1.25 (pH dependent)
- Aegis-Forge Optimization:
  ├── Extractant: 50% HEH[EHP] + 50% kerosene
  ├── pH: 3.8-4.0 (maximizes β)
  ├── Temperature: 25°C (lower T → higher β)
  ├── Stages: 25 (vs Chinese 30-35)
  ├── Scrub: 0.15M HCl (removes Yb from Lu org)
  ├── Strip: 6M HCl (recovers Lu)
  └── **Recovery: 95% Lu, 97% Yb, Purity: 4N**
```

#### 1.5.3 Y/La Separation (Easy, High Volume)

```
Y/La Separation:
- Ionic Radii: Y³⁺ 0.900 Å, La³⁺ 1.032 Å (Δ = 0.132 Å, 14.7%)
- β = 2.1 (very favorable)
- 8 stages sufficient for 4N Y₂O₃
- High throughput (Y is 10x Lu volume)
```

#### 1.5.4 Sc Impurity Removal

```
Sc Behavior in SX:
- Sc³⁺ ionic radius: 0.745 Å (smaller than all REE)
- Extracts **earlier** than LREE in HEH[EHP] (pH <2)
- In chloride SX: Sc reports to **raffinate of LREE circuit**
- Sc Removal Circuit:
  ├── Pre-extraction at pH 1.5 (Sc into organic)
  ├── Scrub: 0.5M HCl (removes Fe/Al)
  ├── Strip: 0.01M HCl (recovers Sc)
  ├── Oxalate precipitation → Sc₂O₃ 99.9% (3N)
  └── **Sc recovery: 85%** (lower due to low concentration)
```
