# Williams Heuristic — DOC-34 San Pedro de Atacama Tourism Gateway

## Constraint (The Lock)
The irreducible principle forcing this design is the **physical and cultural carrying capacity of a 2,400m-altitude oasis** that receives 150,000 visitors annually into a resident population of 11,200 — a 13:1 visitor-to-resident ratio concentrated in 4 summer months. The constraint is not financial or political — it is **biophysical and cultural**: the Puripica-Chiu Chiu fossil aquifer (10,000+ year water, zero recharge) cannot sustain 180 L/s peak demand; the 380+ archaeological sites (12 Monumentos Nacionales) degrade measurably at current visitation (TDI >0.7 at all open sites); the Atacameño Lickanantay cultural integrity erodes when sacred ceremonies become tourist performances and Kunza becomes marketing decoration. The lock is the **simultaneous exceeding of hydrological, archaeological, and cultural thresholds** — any single threshold breach cascades into systemic collapse.

## Necessity (The Key)
The empirical gap this resolves is the **absence of an integrated carrying capacity governance instrument** for San Pedro de Atacama. Current management is fragmented: CONAF sets site caps (unenforced), Municipalidad grants building permits (ignoring water), CPA holds veto authority (but lacks real-time monitoring), Cámara Turismo markets growth (ignoring limits). The key is the **Social License Index (SLI) coupled with the Water Security Index (WSI)** — a dual-dashboard that makes the invisible visible: real-time aquifer drawdown, arsenic levels, site degradation indices, cultural commodification metrics, and community trust scores. This transforms sustainable tourism from a marketing slogan into a **quantitative governance protocol** with automatic triggers: when WSI < 0.7 → building permit freeze; when SLI < 60 → new project moratorium; when TDI > 0.5 at any MN site → immediate visitor cap reduction. The solution is inevitable because: (a) the 2022 flash flood proved infrastructure fragility; (b) the 2020 pandemic proved economic mono-culture fatality; (c) the 2023 arsenic exceedance proved regulatory failure; (d) the CPA veto on Puchuldiza geothermal proved cultural authority is non-negotiable.

## Commitment (The Turn)
This document makes three non-negotiable commitments:
1. **Water as the Master Constraint**: No new tourism bed capacity without demonstrated water security (Horizon 1+2 adaptation delivering +100 L/s by 2028). The Puripica-Chiu Chiu aquifer is managed as a **finite cultural endowment**, not an extractive resource — CPA holds veto on all new extractions.
2. **Cultural Integrity Over Revenue**: The Zona Sagra (45,000 ha) remains **absolute no-go for tourism infrastructure**. Ceremonies are never performed for tourists. Kunza revitalization is funded at $800K/yr (not $350K) with community-controlled digital sovereignty. Artisan cooperatives receive 85% of retail price (not 15%).
3. **Governance Triad Institutionalization**: The Mesa Tripartita (CPA + Municipalidad + Cámara) becomes a **permanent secretariat with legal personality, shared data platform, and binding crisis simulation mandate**. The CPA's Consulta Previa protocol (60-day, 3-phase, kimün-integrated) is the **gold standard for all Chilean indigenous consultation** — not a local exception.
