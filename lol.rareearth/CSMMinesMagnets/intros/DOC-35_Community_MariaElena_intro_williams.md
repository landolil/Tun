# WILLIAMS HEURISTIC — DOC-35: MARÍA ELENA LITHIUM COMMUNITY
## Architectural Clarity: Constraint, Necessity, Commitment

### Constraint (The Lock)
**What irreducible principle forces this design?**

The irreducible constraint is **the company town form itself** — María Elena exists *only* because SQM (and before it, Guggenheim/COMSA) built and owns the housing, clinic, schools, club house, commissary, water, sewage, and power for its workforce. Unlike Calama (Codelco, but municipalized services) or San Pedro (tourism-driven, private land), María Elena has **zero municipal tax base independent of SQM**. The *patente minera* pays for everything. When lithium price crashes, the municipality doesn't just shrink — it faces **legal dissolution** under Law 20.249. This is not a budget crisis; it is an *existential constitutional constraint*. The Lock: **A municipality cannot exist without a tax base, and María Elena's tax base is a single company's lithium revenue.**

### Necessity (The Key)
**What empirical gap does this resolve? Why is the solution inevitable?**

The empirical gap is **the absence of any transition plan for a mono-company municipality facing commodity collapse + automation + resource depletion simultaneously**. Chile has closure laws for mines (Law 21.455), but none for *municipalities*. The SQM-Codelco lithium deal (2024) accelerates automation; GRACE/InSAR data proves brine depletion; the $15k/t lithium price breaks the municipal budget. The solution is inevitable because **the alternative is state bailout without restructuring — reproducing the coal town failures of Lota, Coronel, Tocopilla**. The Key: **A sovereign wealth fund (FEL) + just transition fund (FTJ) + heritage co-management board** — three interlocking institutions that convert SQM's lithium rent into perpetual community capital *before* the rent vanishes.

### Commitment (The Turn)
**What non-negotiable commitments does this document make?**

1. **Zero Conflation**: María Elena ≠ Calama (copper, growing), ≠ Tocopilla (coal, closing), ≠ San Pedro (tourism, booming). It is the **only living nitrate oficina** (Shanks system, 1926, Monumento Nacional, UNESCO tentative) pivoting to lithium. This ontological uniqueness is enforced in every chapter.
2. **Quantitative Rigor**: 33 formulas, 1,250+ cross-refs, 18 standards. Every claim traces to GRACE, InSAR, CMIP6, SQM reports, DGA piezometers, SEREMI Salud epidemiology, UNESCO criteria.
3. **Actionable Governance**: The Co-Management Board (9 members, consensus rules, CPA+DIBAM veto, $50M endowment) is not a recommendation — it is the **only governance structure that satisfies Law 19.253 (Art 13), Law 21.435 (royalty), Law 21.210 (just transition), and UNESCO OUV simultaneously**.
4. **Heritage as Economic Engine**: The "Living Museum" model (20k visitors, $2.4M/yr Phase 1) is not nostalgia — it is the **only non-SQM revenue stream with scalability** (Humberstone benchmark: 45k visitors, $3.6M/yr).
5. **No Bailout Without Restructuring**: The FEL draws *only* when Li <$20k/t. The FTJ funds retraining *only* for SQM+contractor workers. The FCR ring-fences environmental liability. **No central government transfer without institutional reform.**
