# SHIELD Heuristic — DOC-34 San Pedro de Atacama Tourism Gateway

## Threat Model — Adversarial Risk to Oasis Integrity
The oasis faces four threat actors with capability and intent to degrade the system:
1. **Climate Forcing (APT-Environment)**: SSP5-8.5 → Precip -30%, PET +35%, Extreme precip ×5, Frost -60%. Capability: Aquifer recharge → 0, flash flood Q_500 frequency ×3, UV stable extreme. Intent: Entropic.
2. **Tourism Mono-Culture Volatility (APT-Economic)**: Pandemic recurrence (15%/5yr), Social unrest (25%/5yr), Reputation collapse (20%/5yr). Capability: Revenue -95%, employment -80%, municipal bankruptcy. Intent: Market dynamics.
3. **Cultural Commodification (APT-Social)**: Sacred site tourism (+133%), Ceremony performance (new), Kunza decoration (+2900%), Youth exodus from pastoralism (85/15). Capability: Cultural integrity collapse, CPA legitimacy erosion, UNESCO delisting risk. Intent: Market demand + internal assimilation.
4. **Water Mining (APT-Resource)**: Fossil aquifer extraction 3.78 Mm³/yr vs <2 Mm³/yr recharge. Capability: Aquifer exhaustion 2050, arsenic >0.05 mg/L 2040, town + tourism collapse. Intent: Short-term profit (tourism, mining exploration).

## Detection & Response — The SHIELD Protocol
**DETECT**: OSV threshold crossing (VETO/ADAPT) + Cultural Monitor kimün anomaly + SEREMI As exceedance + DGA drawdown alert + ALMA/APEX flood radar + SLI quarterly drop >10 points + Visitor drop >40% (parametric insurance trigger).
**TRIAGE**: Automated severity classification (P1: VETO/Flood/As crisis → <1 hr; P2: ADAPT/SLI drop/Capacity breach → <4 hr; P3: Climate trend/Economic shift → <72 hr).
**RESPOND**: 
- P1: Emergency decree (Municipal + CPA), Evacuation (flood), Tanker water (As), Project halt (VETO), Insurance claim
- P2: Adaptive action (cap reduction, permit freeze, fog deployment), Mediation (Tripartita), Legal defense (CPA)
- P3: Strategic review (Asamblea), Scenario planning (GORE), Contract renegotiation (PPAs, concessions)
**RECOVER**: Phased reopening (safety→water→culture→tourism), Damage assessment (CPA kimün + OSV science), Lessons learned (protocol amendment), Benefit recalculation (Royalty + tourism fees).

The SHIELD protocol is **exercised quarterly** (tabletop) and **annually** (full-scale with ONEMI, Bomberos, CPA, Municipal, Cámara, ALMA). The 2024 exercise simulated Q_500 flood + As crisis + Pandemic resurgence + CPA veto on Puchuldiza — **recovery time objective: 96 hrs for safety, 180 days for full tourism operations, 5 years for aquifer stabilization**.
