### Williams Heuristic — Architectural Clarity

#### Constraint (The Lock)
**The Forge cannot operate on conventional supply chains.** Every hot-zone component (ZrB₂-SiC drums, SiC insulation, basalt fiber, Elium® resin, indium TIM, REE magnets, MAX phase actuators, 3Y-TZP liners, Si₃N₄ bearings, KNbO₃ transducers, YInMn glaze) must be zero-metal, Carrington-immune, and domestically sourced. Global supply chains fail on all three: they contain metal, they are EMP-vulnerable, and they are foreign-dependent. The constraint is not cost or lead time — it is **physical law**. A single metallic fastener in the 1676°C zone becomes a failure path. A single foreign sole-source creates a geopolitical kill switch. A single EMP-vulnerable logistics node creates a black-start impossibility.

#### Necessity (The Key)
**The empirical gap is the absence of a zero-metal, domestically circular industrial supply chain at scale.** No existing supply chain delivers ZrB₂ powder + basalt roving + Elium® resin + indium + REE oxides + Ti₃AlC₂ + KNbO₃-BaTiO₃ + YInMn blue from domestic/ally sources with Phoenix Protocol circular return. The solution is inevitable because the Forge's physics demands it: 8-stage thermal cascade, acoustic liberation, inductive separation, gas solidification — each stage has material requirements that exclude conventional supply chains. The Phoenix Protocol (PROD-22) makes it recursive: EOL returns become feedstock, closing the loop and reducing virgin dependency from 100% to 40-60% blend ratio. This is not optimization — it is **thermodynamic necessity**.

This document commits to three non-negotiables:
1. **100% domestic/ally sourcing for all 12 Tier 1 hot-zone materials** — with in-house production targets for 7 of 12 by Year 3 (basalt, Elium®, ZrB₂, 3Y-TZP, Si₃N₄, Ti₃AlC₂, KNbO₃-BaTiO₃).
