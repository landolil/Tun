The Forge violates all four: sensors are photonics in glass fiber, safety is optical E-stop loops, flow is recursive (EOL → feedstock), and every role must cross-train on the full CMF-RQT loop. The constraint is **physics** — an operator who cannot witness the FBG wavelength shift at 125 Hz and connect it to the thermal-admittance model running at 1 kHz on the Zone RTU will trip the SIF or miss the anomaly.

The CMF-RQT tensor (PROD-33) makes this recursive — training must close the loop.
