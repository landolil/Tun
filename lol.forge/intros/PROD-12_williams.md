### The Dielectric Citadel Principle

Conventional machines ground everything. The Aegis-Forge I grounds *nothing* inside the pressure vessel. The 18mm BFRP/Elium® vessel wall (Item 7) is 10¹⁰ Ω·m resistive. The 50mm aerogel gap (Item 8) is 10¹⁵ Ω. The PMMA POF control fibers (Item 17) are optical — infinite resistance. The PEX-a water circuit (Item 15) is 10¹² Ω. **Result:** No closed conductive loop exists within the process boundary. A Carrington event induces millivolts in internal conductors — but with no return path to earth, no current flows. No GIC damage. No transformer saturation. The machine simply *continues operating*.
