# Williams Heuristic — Constraint, Necessity, Commitment
## Document: PROD-07 — Gas Capture & Fume Solidification

---

### Constraint (The Lock)

The GFGS subsystem is forced by a single irreducible constraint: **Zero Emission as Thermodynamic Closure** — every gas molecule generated by the thermal cascade must be captured, classified, and converted to solid or liquid product. There is no vent to atmosphere path. The constraint is not environmental regulation; it is mass balance. The cascade processes 50 kg feedstock and generates ~15 kg gas-phase products per batch (water, organics, pyrolysis gas, acid gases, metal vapors, CO₂). If any fraction escapes, the system violates its own physics — an open system cannot claim complete elemental extraction. The machine must be a hermetically sealed dielectric citadel where gas phase is a transient state, not an exit state.

This constraint manifests as the **Closed-Loop N₂ Atmosphere Architecture**: nitrogen circulates continuously as carrier gas, sweeping each stage's exhaust through five stage-specific treatment trains before returning to the drum. The N₂ inventory (2-3 kg/cycle) is the thermal ballast and the material conveyor. No air ingress (oxygen = uncontrolled combustion). No gas egress (every species recovered). The positive pressure (1.2-2.5 bar) is the physical enforcement of closure — leakage is outward only.

The constraint writes the entire architecture: five treatment trains (A-E), PSA CO₂ separation, plasma torch polisher, three-layer pressure relief. Every parameter derives from the capture target: water at 80→5°C, organics at 80/25/5°C fractionation, pyrolysis gas at 18.5 MJ/m³ recovery, acid gases at <1 ppm outlet, metal vapors at >99% capture, dioxins at 6-log destruction. There are no free parameters — the periodic table's gas-phase species determine the trains.

---

### Necessity (The Key)

The lock this document addresses is the **emission-recovery gap** — every historical thermal process (smelting, pyrolysis, incineration, gasification) vents 10-40% of input mass as gas, losing value and creating liability. Smelters vent SO₂, CO₂, metal fumes. Pyrolysis vents tars, CO, CH₄. Incinerators vent dioxins, HCl, particulates. The empirical reality: no existing thermal process captures its own exhaust as product. The key is **stage-specific gas treatment as elemental recovery** — each train targets the exact species generated at that temperature, converting gas to solid/liquid product with >95% efficiency.

The necessity is proven by the closed-loop char-tar cycle: Stage 3 produces char → char activated → captures tar from pyrolysis gas → char-tar composite re-fed to Stage 8 → tar pyrolyzes to more char. Zero tar emission, zero activated carbon purchase, net char accumulation. This is not waste management — it is material stewardship where the process consumes its own contaminants.

The acid gas scrubber exemplifies the same principle: Ca(OH)₂ neutralizes HCl/SO₂/HF → produces CaCl₂ (de-icing salt), CaSO₄ (gypsum for construction), CaF₂ (metallurgical fluoride). Zero hazardous waste. Every scrubber product has beneficial use or zero-cost disposal.

The plasma torch is the regulatory safety net: 3000-8000°C plasma ensures >99.9999% dioxin/furan destruction. But it is not the primary capture — it is the final witness that the upstream trains achieved their targets. The lock opens when the outlet gas is >99.5% N₂ + CO₂ with <0.1 ppm total organics.

---

### Commitment (The Turn)

We commit to the following as non-negotiable:

1) **Zero atmospheric release** — <0.1 ppm total organics at vent. Not low emissions — zero. Every molecule accounted for.

2) **N₂ carrier gas recycling >99.5% per cycle** — make-up cost <$0.30/batch. The loop is closed.

3) **CO₂ capture 95-99% purity, 90% recovery** — reused as drum purge + geopolymer curing. CO₂ is product, not waste.

4) **Energy recovery from pyrolysis gas 18.5 MJ/m³** — supplements Stage 7-8 induction, reducing electricity cost.

5) **Acid gas neutralization HCl <1 ppm, SO₂ <1 ppm** — dry lime scrubber, solid byproducts utilized.

6) **Metal vapor capture Zn >99%, Pb >99.5%** — two-stage condenser, Zn as electrolytic-grade liquid.

7) **Dioxin/furan destruction >99.9999%** — plasma torch polisher as final guarantee.

8) **All-dielectric construction** — zero structural metals in gas path. BFRP, ZrO₂, PEEK CF40, ceramic valves only.

The GFGS does not treat emissions. The GFGS *witnesses the gas phase into solid products* — the final measurement in the cascade sequence.
