| Vessel | Scenario | Relief Device | Set Pressure | Capacity |

| **NH₃ Storage** | Fire exposure | Relief valve (SS) | 25 bar | 10,000 kg/hr NH₃ |
| **Cl₂ Storage** | Fire exposure | Relief valve (Hastelloy) | 10 bar | 2,000 kg/hr Cl₂ |
