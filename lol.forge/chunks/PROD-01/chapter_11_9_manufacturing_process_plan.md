## 9. Manufacturing Process Plan

### 9.1 Build Sequence

1. **Drum flash sintering** (Week 1-2): Powder prep → die fill → FAST cycle → FBG embed → machine ports
2. **Coil winding** (Week 2-3): Cu tube bend → Al₂O₃ sleeve → BFRP housing → water circuit → FBG embed
3. **Vessel filament winding** (Week 3-4): Mandrel prep → [±18°/90°]₄ layup → cure → hydrotest → glaze
4. **Frame fabrication** (Week 2-3): Pultrusion cut → machine → assemble → ZrO₂ pads
5. **Subsystem assembly** (Week 4-5): Gas system, acoustic pre-processor, shredder, cooling
6. **Integration** (Week 5-6): Drum in vessel → aerogel fill → coil bank install → flange seal
7. **Instrumentation** (Week 6): FBG interrogators, mass spec, gas analyzers, control wiring
8. **Commissioning** (Week 7-8): Cold spin → vacuum leak check → hot commissioning (ramp schedule)

### 9.2 Critical Tooling

- FAST sintering press: 1000 kN, 2000°C, 200 V/cm (in-house CSMFAB)
- Filament winding mandrel: 900 mm ID, collapsible (for vessel removal)
- Port valve assembly fixture: ZrO₂ piston + Ti₃AlC₂ actuator alignment jig
- FBG embedding tool: 48-channel fiber routing through drum wall during sintering
