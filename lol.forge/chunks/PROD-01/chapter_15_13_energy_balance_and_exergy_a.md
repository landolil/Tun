### 13.2 Exergy Analysis

Exergy destruction occurs primarily at:
1. **Stage 1 IR heating**: Low-grade heat (80-150°C) → high exergy destruction (Carnot efficiency ~20%)
2. **Stage 3 Pyrolysis**: Chemical exergy of organics partially destroyed (char retains ~30%)
3. **Induction gaps**: 15 mm air gap + alumina insulation → coupling losses ~5%
4. **Heat losses**: Aerogel insulation (λ=0.010 W/m·K) limits radial loss to <2 kW/m² at 2200°C

Exergy efficiency target: >45% (state-of-the-art for metallurgical processes: 25-35%)
