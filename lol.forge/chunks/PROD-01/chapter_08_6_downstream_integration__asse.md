## 6. Downstream Integration — Assembly Line Handoff

The Aegis-Forge I feeds 8 material streams directly to the CSMFAB Assembly Line (Documents 113+):

| Port | Output Stream | Downstream Process (CSMFAB) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purified water | Water recovery → process reuse |
| 2 | Mixed polymer slugs | Elium® reformulation / BFRP additive |
| 3 | Carbon char | Activated carbon / carburizing agent |
| 4 | In/Zn/Pb/Sn alloy | Indium electrolysis (FOLLOW-14) + Zn/Pb/Sn refining |
| 5 | Al alloy slugs | Commercial scrap / non-structural tooling |
| 6 | Cu + Au/Ag/Pd | Electrorefining (FOLLOW-19) → pure Cu, PM recovery |
| 7 | Fe + Nd/Sm/Co | Hydromet REE recovery (FOLLOW-15) + Fe casting |
| 8 | Zr/Ti/REE oxide slag | Hydrometallurgical refinery (FOLLOW-21) |
