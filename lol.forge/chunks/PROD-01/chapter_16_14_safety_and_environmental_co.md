## 14. Safety and Environmental Compliance

### 14.1 Carrington Event Hardening

**GIC Immunity Architecture:**
- Zero conductive paths to earth ground (all dielectric construction)
- PMMA optical fiber for all control signals (no copper wires)
- FBG sensors (optical) for all temperature/strain measurements
- GaN inverters with optical isolation (no conductive control lines)
- BFRP frame on ZrO₂ isolation pads (capacitive coupling only)
- DC ground monitor: alerts at >10 A, isolates at >50 A

**Validation:** Simulated 100 A/m geomagnetic field (IEEE C37.90.1) — full operation maintained.

### 14.2 Fire Protection

**Passive:** YInMn Blue + CoAl₂O₄ exterior glaze (SRI=115) reflects 95% of solar + radiated heat. BFRP Tg=80°C protected.
**Active:** Inert gas (N₂/Ar) atmosphere prevents combustion. Plasma torch at 2000°C destroys any organic residue.
**Suppression:** Emergency N₂ flood (300 bar reserve) — 30 second full vessel inerting.
