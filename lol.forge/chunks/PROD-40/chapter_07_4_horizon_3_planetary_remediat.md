## 4. Horizon 3: Planetary Remediation (2030-2040)

### 4.1 Nuclear Waste Vitrification & Actinide Separation

The Aegis-Forge thermal cascade + induction + centrifuge physics applies directly to **high-level nuclear waste (HLW)** processing.

#### 4.1.1 HLW Feedstock Characteristics

| Component | Typical Concentration | Form | Thermal Cascade Stage |
|-----------|----------------------|------|----------------------|
| Fission products (Cs, Sr, Tc, I) | 5-10% | Oxides, fluorides | Stage 1-3 (volatile) |
| Actinides (U, Pu, Np, Am, Cm) | 1-2% | Oxides, metals | Stage 6-8 (refractory) |
| Structural materials (Fe, Cr, Ni, Zr) | 20-30% | Metals, oxides | Stage 4-7 |
| Glass frit (borosilicate) | 40-50% | Pre-added | Stage 8 (vitrification) |

#### 4.1.2 Nuclear Waste Cascade (Adapted 10-Stage)

```
NUCLEAR AEGIS-FORGE — HLW VITRIFICATION + ACTINIDE RECOVERY

Stage 0: Acoustic — skip (liquid feed)
Stage 1 (150°C): Water removal, tritium capture (zeolite)
Stage 2 (300°C): Nitrate/organic decomposition → NOx capture
Stage 3 (500°C): Cs/Sr volatilization → selective capture (crown ether)
Stage 4 (700°C): I-129 capture (Ag-loaded zeolite)
Stage 5 (900°C): Tc/Ru volatility management → reductive capture
Stage 6 (1200°C): Fe/Ni/Cr/Zr metal separation → recycle
Stage 7 (1400°C): U/Pu/Np co-separation → metallic fuel feedstock
Stage 8 (1600°C): Am/Cm separation → heat source / transmutation target
Stage 9 (1800°C): Vitrification — borosilicate glass pour
Stage 10 (cool): Glass canister fill, weld, inspect
```

**Critical safety features:**
- **Criticality control:** Drum geometry (diameter < 300 mm for Pu-bearing stages), neutron poison (Gd₂O₃) in drum liner Zone C
- **Decay heat management:** Continuous N₂ cooling, drum rotation for uniform heat distribution
- **Off-gas treatment:** 4-stage (HEPA + charcoal + Ag-zeolite + catalytic recombiner) — PROD-07 heritage
- **Remote handling:** All operations behind 2 m concrete + 0.5 m Pb shielding

### 4.2 Ocean Polymetallic Nodule Processing

**Clarion-Clipperton Zone (CCZ) nodules:** 21 billion tons (wet) at 4000-5500m depth. Composition matches Aegis-Forge stages perfectly.

#### 4.2.1 Nodule Composition

| Element | Grade (dry wt%) | Aegis-Forge Stage | Recovery Target |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Mn | 24-30% | Stage 7 (Mn oxide reduction) | 95% |
| Ni | 1.0-1.5% | Stage 7 (co-reports with Fe) | 90% |
| Co | 0.2-0.3% | Stage 7 (co-reports with Ni) | 90% |
| Cu | 0.5-1.0% | Stage 6 (Cu window) | 95% |
| REE (Σ) | 0.1-0.3% | Stage 8 (oxide residue) | 80% |
| Mo, W, V, Te, Pb | trace | Stage 8 | 70% |
| Fe | 6-8% | Stage 7 | 95% |
| SiO₂, Al₂O₃ | 20-30% | Stage 8 (slag) | — |

#### 4.2.2 Nodule Processing Cascade

```
OCEAN AEGIS-FORGE — NODULE PROCESSING (Pre-dried, pre-crushed)

Stage 0: Acoustic — nodule disaggregation (Mn oxide layers)
Stage 1: Moisture removal (nodules arrive ~25% moisture)
Stage 2: Skip (no organics)
Stage 3: Organic carbon burnout (nodules contain ~2% organic C)
Stage 4: Zn/Pb/Tl volatilization (trace)
Stage 5: Skip (no Al)
Stage 6 (1085°C): Cu separation — native Cu + Cu-sulfides
Stage 7 (1300°C): Mn/Fe/Co/Ni reduction (H₂ or carbothermal) → metal separation
Stage 8 (1600°C): REE oxide concentration in slag
```

**Key adaptation:** **Pre-reduction at mine site** (seafloor or shipboard). Nodules roasted at 500°C to remove organics, convert MnO₂ → Mn₂O₃. Reduces Gen-II energy by 30%.

**Offshore deployment concept:**
- Shipboard Gen-II cluster (3 drums) on dynamically positioned vessel
- Nodules pumped as slurry → dewatered → fed to drums
- Metal slugs stored in holds, slag returned to seafloor (environmental permit)
- Power: Ship nuclear (SMR 50 MWe) or shore cable

### 4.4 Tailings Reprocessing — Legacy Mine Waste Valorization

Global tailings: **>50 billion tons** stored. Contains unrecovered value: Cu, Au, REE, Co, battery metals.

#### 4.4.1 Tailings Feedstock Variability

| Tailings Type | Typical Grade | Volume (Gt) | Aegis-Forge Suitability |
|---------------|---------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Porphyry Cu tailings | 0.1-0.3% Cu, 0.05% Mo, 0.1 g/t Au | 15 | High (Cu Stage 6, Au Stage 6) |
| Iron ore tailings | 10-20% Fe, 0.01% REE | 20 | High (Fe Stage 7, REE Stage 8) |
| Bauxite residue (red mud) | 30% Fe₂O₃, 10% Al₂O₃, 0.1% REE, 0.01% Sc | 4 | High (Fe Stage 7, Al Stage 5, Sc Stage 8) |
| Phosphate tailings | 5% REE, 1% U, 0.5% Th | 2 | High (REE Stage 8, actinide Stage 9) |
| Coal ash | 0.5% REE, 1% Ge, 0.1% Ga | 3 | Medium (low concentration) |

#### 4.4.2 Tailings Processing Adaptation

**Pre-beneficiation required:** Tailings too dilute for direct Aegis-Forge feed.
1. **Hydrocyclone classification** → remove <10 μm slimes (clay)
2. **Magnetic separation** → remove magnetite (feeds Stage 7 directly)
3. **Flotation/dense media** → concentrate target minerals 10-50×
4. **Aegis-Forge feed** → 5-20% target metal equivalent

**Mobile Aegis-Forge unit:** Containerized 500L drum (Gen-I scaled down) deployed at tailings facility. Processes 500 ton/yr concentrate. Avoids transporting bulk tailings.
