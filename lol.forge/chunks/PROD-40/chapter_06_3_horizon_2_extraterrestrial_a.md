### 3.1 Lunar Regolith Processing — ISRU Oxygen, Metals, Glass

The Aegis-Forge thermal cascade is **inherently suited** to lunar ISRU: no water, no organics, oxide feedstock, vacuum environment, solar thermal/electric power.

#### 3.1.1 Lunar Feedstock Characteristics

| Parameter | Lunar Mare Regolith | Lunar Highlands | Aegis-Forge Relevance |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Composition** | 45% SiO₂, 15% Al₂O₃, 12% FeO, 10% CaO, 8% MgO, 5% TiO₂ | 45% SiO₂, 25% Al₂O₃, 5% FeO, 15% CaO | Oxide mineralogy matches Stage 8 |
| **Ilmenite (FeTiO₃)** | 5-20% (mare) | <1% | Fe + TiO₂ recovery at Stage 7-8 |
| **Volatiles (H, C, N, He)** | 100-200 ppm (solar wind) | 50-100 ppm | Stage 1 capture — trace |
| **Particle size** | <1 mm (70%), <10 μm (20%) | Similar | No shredding needed |
| **Thermal conductivity** | 0.005 W/m·K (vacuum) | 0.005 W/m·K | Aerogel insulation critical |
| **Gravity** | 1.62 m/s² (1/6 G) | 1.62 m/s² | Centrifuge G-force scales |

#### 3.1.2 Adapted Thermal Cascade for Lunar ISRU

```
LUNAR AEGIS-FORGE — 6-STAGE CASCADE (No organics, no low-temp metals)

Stage 0: Acoustic Liberation (skip — regolith already disaggregated)
Stage 1: Volatile Release (150°C) → H₂, He, H₂O capture → life support
Stage 2: Skip (no polymers)
Stage 3: Skip (no organics)
Stage 4: FeO Reduction Window (800-1000°C) → H₂ reduction: FeO + H₂ → Fe + H₂O
Stage 5: Ilmenite Reduction (1000-1100°C) → FeTiO₃ + H₂ → Fe + TiO₂ + H₂O
Stage 6: Aluminum Extraction (1600-1800°C) → Al₂O₃ electrolysis (molten salt) OR carbothermal
Stage 7: Silicate Glass Formation (1400-1600°C) → Basalt fiber, structural glass
Stage 8: Residual Oxides (CaO, MgO) → Slag for radiation shielding bricks
```

**Key innovations for lunar:**
- **Hydrogen reduction loop:** H₂ recycled from water electrolysis (Stage 1 output). Closed H₂/O₂ cycle.
- **Solar thermal direct heating:** 100 kWth parabolic concentrator replaces induction for Stages 4-6. Induction retained for Stages 7-8 (higher temp).
- **Vacuum operation:** Drum operates at 10⁻⁶ mbar — eliminates convection losses, enables lower temp reduction.
- **Centrifuge at 1/6 G:** Drum speed increased to 1200 RPM (Gen-II) for equivalent 25 G separation.

### 3.2 Martian Atmosphere/Soil Co-Processing

Mars offers **CO₂ atmosphere (95%)** + **regolith (perchlorates, sulfates, Fe-oxides)**. Co-processing enables propellant + life support + metals.

#### 3.2.1 Martian Feedstock

| Component | Concentration | Processing |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| CO₂ (atmosphere) | 95% @ 6 mbar | Compression → Stage 1 capture |
| H₂O (regolith) | 1-5% (by mass) | Stage 1 drying |
| Perchlorates (ClO₄⁻) | 0.5-1% | Stage 3 decomposition (450°C) → Cl₂ capture |
| Sulfates (SO₄²⁻) | 5-10% | Stage 3-4 → SO₂ capture |
| Fe-oxides | 15-20% | Stage 7 reduction |

#### 3.2.2 Martian Cascade (8-Stage)

```
MARTIAN AEGIS-FORGE — CO₂ + REGOLITH CO-PROCESSING

Stage 1 (150°C): CO₂ compression/liquefaction + H₂O removal → LOX/LCH4 feedstock
Stage 2 (300°C): Perchlorate decomposition → Cl₂ capture (ZrO₂ scrubber) → O₂ release
Stage 3 (500°C): Sulfate decomposition → SO₂ capture → H₂SO₄ production
Stage 4 (800°C): Fe-oxide pre-reduction (H₂ from electrolysis)
Stage 5 (1000°C): Fe metal separation (centrifuge)
Stage 6 (1200°C): Silicate glass/basalt fiber
Stage 7 (1500°C): Al/Mg extraction (carbothermal)
Stage 8 (1700°C): Residual slag → construction material
```

**Outputs per ton regolith + 100 kg CO₂:**
- LOX: 200 kg, LCH₄: 75 kg (Starship propellant)
- Fe: 120 kg, TiO₂: 30 kg, Al: 40 kg
- Basalt fiber: 200 kg, Glass: 300 kg
- Sulfuric acid: 50 kg (ISRU chemical feedstock)

#### 3.3.2 Simplified Cascade (4-Stage)

```
ASTEROID AEGIS-FORGE — METALLIC SEPARATION ONLY

Stage 0: Acoustic (microgravity — capillary/acoustic hybrid)
Stage 1: Skip (no volatiles)
Stage 2: Skip (no organics)
Stage 3: Skip (no organics)
```

**Microgravity separation physics:**
In microgravity, centrifugal force IS the effective gravity. Drum at 500 RPM, r=0.6m → 167 G. Density stratification occurs radially:
- Inner radius: Fe (7.87 g/cm³)
- Mid radius: Ni-Co (8.9 g/cm³)
- Outer radius: PGM (12-22 g/cm³)

**Port design:** Radial ports at calculated density boundaries. No thermal actuators needed — ports opened by commanded drum position.

### 3.4 Microgravity Thermal Cascade — Capillary/Centrifugal Hybrid

**Challenge:** In microgravity, liquid metals don't pool at the bottom — they form spheres held by surface tension. Centrifugal force must overcome surface tension.

**Bond number criterion:**
Bo = ρ·ω²·r·L²/σ > 10 for reliable separation
- ρ = 7000 kg/m³ (Fe), σ = 1.8 N/m (Fe surface tension at 1600°C)
- L = characteristic length (drum radius = 0.6 m)
- Required ω: ω > √(10·σ/(ρ·r·L²)) = √(10×1.8/(7000×0.6×0.36)) = **0.11 rad/s = 1 RPM**

**At 100 RPM (104 rad/s): Bo = 10,000 >> 10. Separation is robust.**

**Capillary assist:** Helical grooves on drum inner surface (ZrB₂-SiC) guide liquid metal toward ports via capillary wicking (PROD-09 valve heritage). Groove geometry: 2 mm wide × 1 mm deep, 30° helix angle.
