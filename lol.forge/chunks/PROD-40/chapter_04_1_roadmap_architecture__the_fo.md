## 1. Roadmap Architecture — The Four Horizons

The Aegis-Forge evolution follows a **four-horizon framework** that maps technical capability to strategic necessity. Each horizon builds on the proven physics of the Gen-I machine (PROD-01 through PROD-39) while extending into new operational domains.

```
HORIZON 1: GEN-II EVOLUTION (2026-2029)
├── 2.1  2000L Scale-Up (9× volume, 3× throughput)
├── 2.2  Multi-Drum Parallel Architecture (3-drum cluster)
├── 2.3  Advanced Induction Control (SiC → GaN → Diamond)
├── 2.4  Autonomous Operations (Digital Twin closed-loop)
└── 2.5  All-Aegis Material Maturity (zero-metal supply chain)

HORIZON 2: EXTRATERRESTRIAL ADAPTATION (2028-2035)
├── 3.1  Lunar Regolith Processing (ISRU oxygen, metals, glass)
├── 3.2  Martian Atmosphere/Soil Co-Processing (CO₂ + regolith)
├── 3.3  Asteroid Metallic Concentrate Handling (Fe-Ni-Co-PGM)
├── 3.4  Microgravity Thermal Cascade (capillary/centrifugal hybrid)
└── 3.5  Radiation-Hardened All-Ceramic Architecture

HORIZON 3: PLANETARY REMEDIATION (2030-2040)
├── 4.1  Nuclear Waste Vitrification & Actinide Separation
├── 4.2  Ocean Polymetallic Nodule Processing (Mn, Ni, Co, Cu, REE)
├── 4.3  Urban Mining Logistics Network (distributed preprocessing)
├── 4.4  Tailings Reprocessing (legacy mine waste valorization)
└── 4.5  Carrington-Hardened Grid Integration (GIC immunity)

HORIZON 4: ECOSYSTEM MATURITY (2035-2050)
├── 5.1  Phoenix Protocol Full Closure (100% EOL return rate)
├── 5.2  Policy & Incentive Framework (critical mineral independence)
├── 5.3  Open Architecture Licensing (CSMFAB material specs)
├── 5.4  Global Deployment Network (regional hubs)
└── 5.5  Self-Replicating Fabrication (Aegis-Forge builds Aegis-Forge)
```

**Governance Principle:** Each horizon requires **validation gates** at TRL 6 (system demo in relevant environment) before committing to the next. The Digital Twin (PROD-37) provides continuous virtual validation; physical prototypes validate twin predictions.
