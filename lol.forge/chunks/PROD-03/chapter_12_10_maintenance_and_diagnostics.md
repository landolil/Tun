## 10. Maintenance and Diagnostics

### 10.1 Predictive Maintenance Indicators

| Indicator | Normal | Warning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coil resistance (20°C) | Baseline ±1% | +2% | Inspect for hot spots |
| Resonant frequency | Baseline ±0.5% | +1% | Check C_r degradation |
| FBG strain (steady) | <50 με | >100 με | Check drum alignment |
| Water ΔT | 15°C | >20°C | Clean filters, check flow |
| GaN junction temp | <85°C | >95°C | Derate, check heatsink |
| V/I phase at resonance | <2° | >5° | Check coupling, retune |

### 10.2 Replacement Intervals

| Component | Life | Replacement Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| GaN switches | 100,000 hrs | R_ds(on) +20% |
| Resonant capacitors | 50,000 hrs | tanδ > 0.005 |
| PEX-a water hose | 20,000 hrs | Pressure test fail |
| FBG interrogator | 50,000 hrs | Drift >1°C/yr |
| BFRP housing | 10,000 cycles | Visual delamination |
