## 11. Scale-Up: Gen-II (2000L Drum, 4 MW Induction)

### 11.1 Scaling Laws

| Parameter | Gen-I | Gen-II | Scaling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drum diameter | 600 mm | 1200 mm | 2× |
| Coil radius | 315 mm | 615 mm | 2× |
| Turns per zone | 12 | 12 | Same |
| Frequency | Same | Same | Physics unchanged |
| Current per turn | 500 A | 1000 A | 2× (area 4×, power 8×) |
| Voltage per turn | 50 V | 50 V | Same |
| Cooling flow/zone | 15 L/min | 120 L/min | 8× |
| GaN devices/zone | 4 | 16 (parallel) | 4× |

### 11.2 Gen-II Challenges

1. **Current handling:** 1000 A/turn → parallel GaN devices, current sharing
2. **Magnetic field:** 2× radius → 2× stored energy → larger forces
3. **Thermal management:** 8× power → 120 L/min water per zone
4. **Control bandwidth:** 2× thermal time constant → slower MPC
5. **Cost:** 4 MW GaN = $2M (vs $220k Gen-I)
