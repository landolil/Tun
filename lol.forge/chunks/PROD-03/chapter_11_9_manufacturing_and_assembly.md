## 9. Manufacturing and Assembly

### 9.1 Coil Winding Process

1. **Copper tube bending:** CNC mandrel bender, 12 turns per zone, 100 mm axial
2. **Alumina sleeve installation:** Automated wrapping machine, 3 mm overlap
3. **PEX-a water circuit insertion:** Push-fit through tube bore, pressure test 10 bar
4. **BFRP housing filament winding:** [±15°/90°]₃ on collapsible mandrel
5. **FBG embedding:** 4 sensors per housing (mid-wall) during winding
6. **Resonant capacitor bank:** NP0 ceramic, mounted on cold plate
7. **GaN inverter assembly:** Dielectric standoffs, optical fiber routing

### 9.2 Coil-to-Drum Assembly

1. Drum centered in vessel (laser alignment, ±0.1 mm)
2. Coil bank segments lowered over drum (crane, 8 segments)
3. Air gap verification: 15.0 ±0.5 mm (feeler gauges at 8 positions)
4. Water manifold connection: Quick-connect PEX-a fittings
5. Optical fiber routing: FBG interrogator to coil sensors
6. Power cable routing: Dielectric conduit (no metal conduit)

### 9.3 Commissioning Sequence

1. **Cold resistance check:** All zones, 4-wire Kelvin measurement
2. **Vacuum leak test:** Coil water circuit, helium, <1×10⁻⁹ mbar·L/s
3. **Resonance sweep:** Each zone, no load, measure f_r, Q
4. **Load test:** Steel dummy load, measure coupling, adjust C_r
5. **FBG calibration:** Ambient zero, reference sensor validation
6. **Integrated test:** Full 8-zone ramp to 500°C, verify thermal profile
