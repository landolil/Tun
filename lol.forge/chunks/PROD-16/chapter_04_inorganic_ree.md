## 6. Inorganic Transformation

### 6.1 Filler Mineral Decomposition & Transformation

During Stage 3, inorganic fillers from thermoset composites undergo thermal decomposition, forming reactive oxides that become part of the char/slag matrix.

| Filler Mineral | Formula | Decomposition Reaction | Onset (°C) | Completion (°C) | Product |
|----------------|---------|------------------------|------------|-----------------|---------|
| **Calcium carbonate** | CaCO₃ | CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ ↑ | 650 | 850 | CaO (quicklime) |
| **Magnesium carbonate** | MgCO₃ | MgCO₃ → MgO + CO₂ ↑ | 350 | 500 | MgO (periclase) |
| **Talc** | Mg₃Si₄O₁₀(OH)₂ | Mg₃Si₄O₁₀(OH)₂ → 3MgO + 4SiO₂ + H₂O ↑ | 850 | 1000 | MgO + SiO₂ |
| **Kaolin (clay)** | Al₂Si₂O₅(OH)₄ | Al₂Si₂O₅(OH)₄ → Al₂Si₂O₇ (metakaolin) + 2H₂O ↑ | 450 | 600 | Metakaolin → Mullite (>1000°C) |
| **Aluminum hydroxide** | Al(OH)₃ | 2Al(OH)₃ → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂O ↑ | 200 | 350 | Al₂O₃ (alumina) |
| **Magnesium hydroxide** | Mg(OH)₂ | Mg(OH)₂ → MgO + H₂O ↑ | 300 | 400 | MgO |
| **Barium sulfate** | BaSO₄ | Stable to >1400°C | — | — | BaSO₄ (inert) |
| **Glass fiber (E-glass)** | SiO₂-Al₂O₃-CaO-MgO-B₂O₃ | Softens 850°C, melts 1100°C | 850 | 1100 | Slag (CaO-MgO-Al₂O₃-SiO₂) |
| **Carbon fiber** | C | Oxidizes >450°C (if O₂ present) | 450 | 600 | CO/CO₂ (if oxidizing) |
| **Antimony trioxide** | Sb₂O₃ | Sb₂O₃ → 2SbO₂ + ½O₂ (sublimes) | 650 | 800 | Sb₂O₄ (vapor) |
| **Zinc borate** | 2ZnO·3B₂O₃·3.5H₂O | Dehydrates → 2ZnO + 3B₂O₃ | 300 | 500 | ZnO + B₂O₃ (flux) |

### 6.2 Slag Formation & Chemistry

**Slag sources in Stage 3:**
1. Glass fiber from FR4/PCB (major contributor: 40-60% of inorganic mass)
2. Filler minerals (CaCO₃, talc, kaolin) from polymer compounding
3. Metal oxides from Stage 2 filtration (ZnO, PbO, SnO₂ traces)
4. Ceramic liner wear particles (ZrO₂, SiC traces)

**Slag composition (typical, wt% of inorganic fraction):**

| Component | Range | Source |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| SiO₂ | 45-60% | Glass fiber, kaolin, talc |
| CaO | 10-20% | CaCO₃ decomposition, glass fiber |
| Al₂O₃ | 8-15% | Kaolin, glass fiber, fillers |
| MgO | 5-12% | Talc, Mg(OH)₂, glass fiber |
| Na₂O + K₂O | 2-5% | Glass fiber, fillers |
| Fe₂O₃ | 1-3% | Impurities, shredder wear |
| ZnO + PbO + SnO₂ | 1-5% | Stage 2 carryover |
| ZrO₂ + SiC | 0.5-2% | Liner wear |
| Unoxidized carbon | 2-8% | Char entrainment |

**Slag behavior at Stage 3 temperatures (450-700°C):**
- **Mostly solid** — liquidus of CaO-MgO-Al₂O₃-SiO₂ system >1100°C
- **Partial sintering** at 650-700°C forms friable agglomerates
- **No liquid slag phase** in Stage 3 — liquid slag forms in Stage 7 (1200-1600°C)

### 6.3 Glass Fiber Transformation

**E-glass fiber (from FR4 PCBs) — Major inorganic stream:**

| Stage 3 Process | Temperature | Transformation |
|-----------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Organic sizing burnout** | 300-450°C | Silane coupling agent pyrolyzes → clean fiber surface |
| **Boron volatilization** | 600-800°C | B₂O₃ from glass partially volatilizes (B₂O₃ vapor pressure significant) |
| **Surface reaction with char** | 650-700°C | C + SiO₂ → SiC (minor) + CO; CaO from fillers reacts with SiO₂ → CaSiO₃ |
| **Physical state at 700°C** | — | Solid fibers, sintered at contact points, friable mat |

**Mass balance for FR4 (typical 20% glass, 80% epoxy by weight):**
- 100 kg FR4 feed → 20 kg glass fiber + 80 kg epoxy
- Epoxy pyrolysis: 80 kg → 16 kg char + 64 kg gas
- Glass fiber: 20 kg → 20 kg (inert) + minor B loss
- **Stage 3 residue from FR4: ~36 kg (16 kg char + 20 kg glass)**

### 6.4 Metal Oxide Formation from Stage 2 Carryover

Stage 2 filtration solids (2-5 kg/batch) contain metal particles that oxidize in Stage 3:

| Metal | Stage 2 Form | Stage 3 Oxidation | Product | Fate |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|---------|------|
| **Zn** | Molten Zn (420°C) | Zn + ½O₂ → ZnO | ZnO (white powder) | Slag component, Stage 4 recovery |
| **Pb** | Molten Pb (327°C) | 2Pb + O₂ → 2PbO | PbO (yellow) | Slag, Stage 4 recovery |
| **Sn** | Molten Sn (232°C) | Sn + O₂ → SnO₂ | SnO₂ (white) | Slag, Stage 4 recovery |
| **Al** | Solid Al (660°C) | 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃ | Al₂O₃ | Slag, Stage 5 recovery |
| **Cu** | Solid Cu (1085°C) | 2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO | CuO (black) | Slag, Stage 6 recovery |
| **Fe** | Solid Fe (1538°C) | 3Fe + 2O₂ → Fe₃O₄ | Fe₃O₄ (magnetite) | Slag, Stage 7 recovery |

**Note:** N₂/CO₂ atmosphere maintains <2% O₂, limiting oxidation. Most metal oxidation occurs during brief air exposure at discharge or from residual H₂O/CO₂.

## 7. REE Partitioning — Critical Mineral Tracking

### 7.1 REE Sources in Stage 3 Feed

**Primary REE-bearing materials entering Stage 3:**

| Source | REE Content | Form | Mass/Batch (Cat A) | Stage 3 Fate |
|--------|-------------|------|-------------------|--------------|
| **NdFeB magnets** (from motors, HDDs) | 25-30% Nd, 5% Dy, 1% Pr | Metallic alloy | 0.5-2 kg | Stage 2 heavy fraction → Stage 7 |
| **SmCo magnets** | 25% Sm, 50% Co | Metallic alloy | 0.1-0.5 kg | Stage 2 heavy fraction → Stage 7 |
| **YInMn Blue glazes** (Aegis returns) | 10% Y, 2% In, 1% Mn | Ceramic pigment | 0.1-0.5 kg | Stage 2 heavy fraction → Stage 7/8 |
| **Zircon (ZrSiO₄)** | 0.1-1% HREE (Y, Dy, Er) | Mineral sand | 1-5 kg | **Stage 3 residue → Stage 8** |
| **Monazite (Ce,La,Nd,Th)PO₄** | 50% LREE | Mineral sand | 0.1-1 kg | **Stage 3 residue → Stage 8** |
| **Xenotime (YPO₄)** | 50% HREE (Y, Dy) | Mineral sand | 0.05-0.2 kg | **Stage 3 residue → Stage 8** |
| **Phosphor powders** (lamps, displays) | Eu, Tb, Y, Ce, La | Oxide phosphors | 0.01-0.1 kg | Stage 2 heavy fraction → Stage 7/8 |

### 7.2 Partitioning Behavior in Stage 3

**Stage 3 (450-700°C) is a CRITICAL partitioning point for REEs:**

| REE Group | Behavior at 450-700°C | Partitioning |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------|
| **LREE (La, Ce, Pr, Nd)** | Associated with phosphate/silicate minerals (monazite, zircon) | **Remain in solid residue** → Stage 8 |
| **HREE (Sm, Eu, Gd, Tb, Dy, Ho, Er, Yb, Lu, Y)** | In zircon (HREE-enriched) or oxide phosphors | **Remain in solid residue** → Stage 8 |
| **Magnet REEs (Nd, Sm, Dy, Pr)** | In metallic NdFeB/SmCo particles | **Do NOT melt in Stage 3** (Tm > 1000°C) → Stage 2 heavy fraction → **Stage 7** |
| **Indium** | In ITO (In₂O₃:SnO₂) coatings on glass | In₂O₃ stable to >1500°C | **Remains in glass/slag** → Stage 8 |
| **Yttrium** | In Y₂O₃ (phosphors, YSZ) or YInMn | Y₂O₃ stable | **Remains in residue** → Stage 8 |

### 7.3 Critical Partitioning Summary

| Element | Stage 3 Input Source | Stage 3 State at 700°C | Reports To | Downstream Recovery |
|---------|---------------------|------------------------|------------|---------------------|
| **Nd** | NdFeB magnets | Solid metal (Tm=1021°C) | Stage 7 slag/metal | FOLLOW-15 (Hydromet) |
| **Sm** | SmCo magnets | Solid metal (Tm=1072°C) | Stage 7 slag/metal | FOLLOW-15 (Hydromet) |
| **Dy** | NdFeB (1-5% Dy) | Solid solution in NdFeB | Stage 7 slag/metal | FOLLOW-15 (Hydromet) |
| **Y** | YSZ, YInMn, phosphors | Y₂O₃ ceramic | Stage 8 residue | FOLLOW-15 / FOLLOW-21 |
| **In** | ITO coatings, YInMn | In₂O₃ in glass matrix | Stage 8 residue | FOLLOW-14 (Electrolysis) |
| **Ce, La** | Monazite, phosphors | Phosphate/oxide | Stage 8 residue | FOLLOW-21 (Hydromet) |
| **Zr** | Zircon, YSZ | ZrSiO₄ / ZrO₂ | Stage 8 residue | FOLLOW-16 (Dissociation) |

**Key Insight:** Stage 3 does NOT recover REEs directly. It is the **partitioning gateway**:
- **Metallic REEs (magnets)** → survive Stage 3 intact → **Stage 7** (ferrous cascade)
- **Mineral REEs (zircon, monazite, phosphors)** → survive Stage 3 intact → **Stage 8** (mineral residue)
