## 15. Feedstock-Specific Cascade Profiles

### 15.1 Profile A: Mixed E-Waste (PCBs, Plastics, Metals)
- Stage 0: Extended (30 min) — high mineral content in PCBs
- Stage 1: Standard (20 min) — moderate moisture
- Stage 2: Extended (40 min) — high polymer content
- Stage 3: Extended (40 min) — high thermoset content (FR4)
- Stage 4: Standard (25 min) — moderate Sn/Pb/Zn
- Stage 5: Standard (25 min) — moderate Al
- Stage 6: Extended (40 min) — HIGH Cu + PM content
- Stage 7: Standard (35 min) — moderate Fe
- Stage 8: Standard (20 min) — moderate mineral
- **Total: ~4.5 hours**

### 15.2 Profile B: Auto Shredder Residue (ASR)
- Stage 0: Standard (15 min) — low mineral content
- Stage 1: Extended (30 min) — high moisture/dirt
- Stage 2: Extended (40 min) — high polymer (ASR is 30-40% plastic)
- Stage 3: Standard (30 min) — moderate thermoset
- Stage 4: Extended (35 min) — HIGH Zn (galvanized), Pb (batteries)
- Stage 5: Extended (35 min) — HIGH Al (engine blocks, wheels)
- Stage 6: Standard (30 min) — moderate Cu (wiring)
- Stage 7: Extended (45 min) — HIGH Fe (ferrous dominant)
- Stage 8: Short (15 min) — low mineral
- **Total: ~4.5 hours**

### 15.3 Profile C: Mineral Concentrates (Zircon, REE Ores)
- Stage 0: Extended (30 min) — PRIMARY liberation target
- Stage 1: Short (10 min) — low moisture (pre-dried ore)
- Stage 2: Short (10 min) — minimal polymer
- Stage 3: Short (10 min) — minimal organics
- Stage 4: Short (10 min) — minimal low-temp metals
- Stage 5: Short (10 min) — minimal Al
- Stage 6: Short (10 min) — minimal Cu
- Stage 7: Short (15 min) — minimal Fe
- Stage 8: Extended (40 min) — PRIMARY zircon dissociation target
- **Total: ~3.5 hours**

### 15.4 Profile D: Phoenix Protocol Returns (Aegis EOL)
- Stage 0: Extended (30 min) — ZrB₂-SiC, BFRP liberation
- Stage 1: Standard (20 min) — moderate moisture
- Stage 2: Standard (30 min) — BFRP matrix (Elium®) pyrolysis
- Stage 3: Extended (40 min) — high char yield from BFRP
- Stage 4: Short (15 min) — minimal low-temp metals
- Stage 5: Short (15 min) — minimal Al
- Stage 6: Short (15 min) — minimal Cu
- Stage 7: Standard (35 min) — Fe from MR fluid, magnets
- Stage 8: Extended (40 min) — ZrO₂, YInMn, KNbO₃ recovery
- **Total: ~4.5 hours**
