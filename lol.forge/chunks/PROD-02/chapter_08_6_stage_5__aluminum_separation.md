## 6. Stage 5 — Aluminum Separation (660-720°C): The Pure Aluminum Window

### 6.1 Thermodynamic Basis of the Aluminum Window

660°C = liquidus of pure Al. Copper melts at 1085°C, iron at 1538°C.
The 60°C window (660-720°C) is unique: only Al and Al alloys melt.

**Aluminum Alloy Liquidus Temperatures:**
| Alloy | Liquidus (°C) | Solidus (°C) | Typical Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pure Al (1xxx) | 660 | 660 | Wire, foil |
| 6061 | 652 | 582 | Extrusions, structures |
| 7075 | 635 | 477 | Aerospace, high-strength |
| A380 (die-cast) | 577 | 566 | Automotive, housings |
| A356 | 615 | 550 | Castings, wheels |

**Eutectic Considerations:**
- Al-Si (A380): 577°C eutectic (12.6% Si) — melts BEFORE pure Al window
- Al-Cu (2024): 548°C eutectic (33% Cu) — melts early
- Strategy: Ramp through eutectics quickly (sub-stage 5a: 580-660°C, 5 min), hold at 680°C for pure Al

### 6.2 Centrifuge Parameters

- Drum speed: 300 RPM (G = 25.1 at r=0.25m)
- Al melt viscosity at 700°C: 1.3 mPa·s (extremely fluid)
- Radial velocity (2 mm droplet): v_r = 8.7 mm/s
- 5 min dwell at 680°C: 2600 mm radial travel — full separation achieved
- Port 5 set-point: 720°C (Al liquidus + 60°C margin)

### 6.3 Expected Aluminum Slug Composition

| Element | Range | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Al | 85-97% | Base |
| Si | 1-8% | Die-cast A380/A356 |
| Cu | 0.5-3% | 2024, 7075 alloys |
| Mg | 0.3-1.5% | 6061, 5xxx series |
| Fe | 0.2-0.8% | Impurity |
| Mn | 0.1-0.5% | 3xxx series |
| Zn | 0.1-0.5% | 7xxx series |

**Downstream:** Mixed alloy slug → commercial scrap market ($2.20/kg) or non-structural tooling.

### 6.4 Induction at Stage 5

- Frequency: 10 kHz (optimal for Al eddy coupling)
- Skin depth in Al at 700°C: δ = 2.1 mm
- Power: 80-150 kW (heat capacity: 0.9 J/g·K × 150 kg × 640°C = 86 MJ)
- Heating time at 150 kW: 86 MJ / 150 kW = 9.6 min (ramp + dwell fits 25 min window)
