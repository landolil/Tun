## 17. Environmental Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) Integration

### 17.1 Cradle-to-Gate Carbon Footprint (per ton feedstock)

| Process Stage | CO₂e (kg) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feedstock collection/transport | 15-50 | Depends on source |
| Pre-processing (shred, magnet) | 5-10 | Electricity |
| Stage 0 (acoustic) | 2-4 | Electricity (4 kWh) |
| Stage 1 (drying) | 8-12 | NG for IR or electricity |
| Stages 2-8 (induction) | 400-600 | Grid electricity (varies by region) |
| Gas capture (NaHCO₃ production) | 50-100 | Chemical production |
| Consumables (molds, seals) | 10-20 | Manufacturing |
| **Total (grid avg)** | **490-796** | **0.49-0.80 t CO₂e/t feed** |

**Carbon Sequestration Credit:**
- Na₂CO₃ from CO₂ capture: 0.5-1.5 t CO₂ sequestered/ton feed
- Char carbon (fixed): 0.1-0.3 t C = 0.37-1.1 t CO₂e/ton feed
- **Net: -0.1 to +0.5 t CO₂e/ton feed** (potentially carbon-negative!)
