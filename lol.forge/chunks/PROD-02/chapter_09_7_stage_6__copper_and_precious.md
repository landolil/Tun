## 7. Stage 6 — Copper and Precious Metals (1085-1200°C)

### 7.1 Copper Recovery Engineering

**Copper Sources and Concentrations:**
| Source | Cu Content | Typical Form |
|---|---|---|
| PCB traces | 20-35% | Thin layers, plated holes |
| Motor windings | 99.9% | Wire (0.5-2 mm) |
| Plumbing scrap | 99.5% | Pipe (10-50 mm) |
| Electrical wire | 99.9% | Wire (1-10 mm) |

**Induction Heating Parameters:**
- Frequency: 5 kHz (penetration depth at 1100°C: δ ≈ 4 mm)
- Power: 150-200 kW
- Energy required: 0.385 J/g·K × 200 kg × 1065°C = 82 MJ
- Heating time at 200 kW: 6.8 min (fits 30 min dwell with margin)

### 7.2 Precious Metal Co-Recovery

**Phase Behavior:**
- Au: Tm = 1064°C (melts BEFORE Cu)
- Ag: Tm = 961.8°C (melts BEFORE Cu)  
- Pt: Tm = 1768°C (remains solid — reports to Stage 7)
- Pd: Tm = 1554°C (remains solid — reports to Stage 7)

Au and Ag report with copper in Port 6 output. Downstream electrorefining separates them.

**PCB Precious Metal Content (per ton):**
- Au: 300-400 g/ton
- Ag: 3,000-5,000 g/ton
- Pd: 100-200 g/ton
- Pt: 50-100 g/ton

**Revenue at 2026 Prices:**
- Au: 300 g × $105,000/kg = $31,500/ton
- Ag: 4,000 g × $1,200/kg = $4,800/ton
- Pd: 150 g × $60,000/kg = $9,000/ton
- **Total PM revenue: ~$45,300/ton PCB feedstock**

### 7.3 Brass/Bronze Separation (Hold Zone 720-1085°C)

The hold zone between Stage 5 and 6 enables Cu-alloy separation:
- Brass (Cu-Zn, 5-40% Zn): liquidus 900-940°C
- Bronze (Cu-Sn, 5-20% Sn): liquidus 950-1050°C
- These melt and separate BEFORE pure Cu at 1085°C
- Report to Port 6 as distinct Cu-alloy slugs
