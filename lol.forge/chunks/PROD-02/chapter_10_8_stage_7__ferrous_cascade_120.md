## 8. Stage 7 — Ferrous Cascade (1200-1600°C): Iron, Steel, and REE Magnets

### 8.1 Ferromagnetic Induction Coupling — The Physics

Ferrous metals couple to induction via **two mechanisms simultaneously**:
1. Eddy currents (conductivity): ρ_Fe ≈ 1.0×10⁻⁷ Ω·m
2. Hysteresis losses (ferromagnetism): μ_r = 100-5000 below Curie

**Total heating power enhancement:** 2-10× vs non-ferrous at same frequency.

**Curie Temperature Transitions:**
- Fe: 770°C (ferromagnetic → paramagnetic)
- Ni: 358°C
- Co: 1121°C
- Stainless 304: Austenitic, non-magnetic at all temperatures

At Stage 7 temperatures (1200-1600°C), pure Fe is paramagnetic. However, the **induction heating history** matters: below 770°C, ferromagnetic coupling rapidly heats Fe particles. By the time they reach 1200°C, they are already molten.

### 8.3 Rare Earth Magnet Recovery — Critical Value Stream

**Magnet Melting:**
- NdFeB: Tm = 1177°C (melts in Stage 7)
- SmCo: Tm = 1490°C (melts in Stage 7)
- Combined RE-Fe-B and RE-Co melts report to Port 7 with main ferrous stream

**REE Content in Ferrous Slug:**
- Nd: 2-5% of magnet mass (magnets are 2-5% of ferrous feed)
- Sm: 1-3% (SmCo magnets)
- Co: 5-15% (SmCo) + trace in NdFeB
- Dy/Tb: 0.1-0.5% (heavy REE additives)

**Downstream Hydrometallurgy (FOLLOW-15):**
Ferrous + REE slug → HCl/H₂SO₄ leach → solvent extraction (PC-88A, Cyanex 272) → separated REE oxides.

### 8.4 Stage 7 Centrifuge Parameters

- Drum speed: 350 RPM (G = 34.1 at r=0.25m)
- Fe melt viscosity at 1500°C: 5.5 mPa·s
- Dwell: 35 minutes (longest — high thermal mass, Curie transition kinetics)
- Port 7 set-point: 1600°C
