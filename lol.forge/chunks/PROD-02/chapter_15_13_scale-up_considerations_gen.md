## 13. Scale-Up Considerations (Gen-II: 2000L Drum)

### 13.1 Geometric Scaling Laws

| Parameter | Gen-I (226L) | Gen-II (2000L) | Scaling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drum diameter | 600 mm | 1200 mm | 2× |
| Drum length | 800 mm | 1600 mm | 2× |
| Volume | 226 L | 1800 L | 8× |
| Feed mass | 50 kg | 400 kg | 8× |
| Surface/Volume | 4.2 m²/m³ | 2.1 m²/m³ | 0.5× |
| Thermal time constant | 3.7 min | 7.4 min | 2× |
| Induction power | 500 kW | 4 MW | 8× |
| G-force at 300 RPM | 25 G | 50 G | 2× |

### 13.2 Scale-Up Challenges

1. **Thermal gradients:** Larger drum → longer heat penetration → slower ramps or higher power density
2. **Mechanical stress:** Centrifugal stress ∝ r² → 4× at 2× diameter (manageable)
3. **Port scaling:** 8 ports → 16 ports (2 per zone) or larger apertures
4. **Acoustic pre-processor:** 8× volume → 64 kW LF + 32 kW US
5. **Gas handling:** 8× flow → larger scrubbers, plasma torches
