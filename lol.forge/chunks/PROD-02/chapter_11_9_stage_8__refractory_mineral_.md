## 9. Stage 8 — Refractory Mineral Residue (1600-2200°C)

### 9.1 Phase Inventory at 1600°C+

| Material | Status at 1600°C | Tm/Decomp (°C) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silica glass fibers | Melting | 1600-1700 | From FR4 |
| Zirconia (ZrO₂) | Solid | 2716 | Concentrated |
| Y₂O₃, La₂O₃, Nd₂O₃ | Solid | 2400-2600 | REE oxides |
| TiO₂ (rutile) | Solid | 1843 (decomp) | Partial reduction |
| Al₂O₃ | Solid | 2072 | Mostly solid |
| SiC | Solid | 2700 (subl) | Residue |
| ZrSiO₄ (zircon) | **Dissociating** | 1676 | **Critical!** |

### 9.2 Zircon Dissociation — The Key Reaction

At 1676°C:
$$\text{ZrSiO}_4 \xrightarrow{1676°C} \text{ZrO}_2 + \text{SiO}_2$$

**Kinetics (from literature, validated in-house):**
- Onset: 1600°C (5% dissociation)
- 50% dissociation: 1676°C, 10 min dwell
- 95% dissociation: 1750°C, 20 min dwell
- SiO₂ vapor pressure at 1750°C: ~10⁻³ atm (some loss, captured in fume system)

**Downstream Processing:**
1. ZrO₂ → 3Y-TZP: Add 3 mol% Y₂O₃, ball mill, spray dry, sinter 1450°C
2. ZrO₂ → ZrB₂: Add B₂O₃ + C, carbothermal reduction 1600°C → ZrB₂ + CO
