## 18. Regulatory and Permitting Framework

### 18.1 Air Emissions Compliance

**Applicable Standards:**
- EPA MACT (Maximum Achievable Control Technology) for secondary metals
- EU IED (Industrial Emissions Directive) BAT-AELs
- Local air quality permits (PM, SO₂, NOx, HCl, HF, dioxins)

**Aegis-Forge Performance vs. Limits:**
| Pollutant | Limit (mg/Nm³) | Aegis-Forge Performance | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| PM (total) | 5 | <0.1 | 98% |
| SO₂ | 50 | <1 | 98% |
| HCl | 10 | <0.1 | 99% |
| HF | 1 | <0.01 | 99% |
| Dioxins (TEQ) | 0.1 ng | <0.001 ng | 99% |
| Hg | 0.05 | <0.005 | 90% |

### 18.2 Waste Classification

**Output Streams:**
- Metal slugs: Products (not waste) — commercial specifications
- Mineral concentrate: Byproduct — feedstock for hydromet refinery
- Char: Product — activated carbon, carburizing agent
- Water: Process water — recycle or discharge (permitted)
- Scrubber salts (Na₂CO₃, Na₂SO₄): Byproducts — chemical grade
- Plasma slag: Inert vitrified material — construction aggregate

**Zero hazardous waste to landfill** — all streams have defined valorization pathways.
