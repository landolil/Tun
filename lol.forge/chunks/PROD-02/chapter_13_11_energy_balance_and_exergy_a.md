### 11.2 Heat Recovery Systems

1. **Gas combustion heat** (Stages 3-8): Preheat N₂ inlet (Stage 1) — saves ~400 kWh/ton
2. **Char combustion** (excess char): Thermal oxidizer heat → steam → power — saves ~300 kWh/ton
3. **Condenser heat** (Stages 1, 4-5): Preheat feedwater — saves ~200 kWh/ton
4. **Drum cool-down:** Heat exchange to next batch preheat — saves ~500 kWh/ton
5. **Aerogel insulation:** Limits radial loss to <2 kW/m² — saves ~800 kWh/ton

**Net energy after recovery:** ~1,100 kWh/ton (meets <1,200 target)
