## 5. Stage 4 — Low-Temperature Metals (420-900°C): The Indium First Strategy

### 5.1 Multi-Metal Overlap Challenge

Stage 4 spans 480°C (420→900°C) with five target metals melting in overlapping ranges:
- In: 156.6°C (melts alone — unique window)
- Sn: 231.9°C
- Sn-Pb eutectic: 183°C
- Bi: 271.5°C
- Pb: 327.5°C
- Zn: 419.5°C
- Zamak: 380-390°C

**Indium First Strategy — The Key Innovation:**
At 156.6°C, ONLY indium melts. All other metals (Sn, Pb, Bi, Zn, Al, Cu, Fe) remain solid.
This single-stage selective extraction of a critical mineral from e-waste is impossible in any smelter or refinery.

### 5.3 Induction Selectivity Physics

At 50 kHz, 80 kW:
- Zn: ρ = 5.96×10⁻⁸ Ω·m → excellent coupling
- Pb: ρ = 2.0×10⁻⁷ Ω·m → good coupling
- Al (solid): ρ = 2.7×10⁻⁸ Ω·m but T < Tm — no melt
- Fe (solid): ferromagnetic but T < Curie (770°C) — some coupling but no melt

**Skin Depth at 50 kHz / 500°C:**
- Zn: δ = 0.38 mm
- Pb: δ = 0.67 mm
- Al: δ = 0.54 mm (but solid)
- Fe: δ = 0.15 mm (ferromagnetic)

The frequency selectively heats the low-melting metals within the solid Al/Cu/Fe matrix.

### 5.4 Indium Recovery Economics

**Indium in Feedstock (per ton mixed e-waste):**
- LCD panels: 50-200 g/ton In (ITO glass)
- Total recoverable: 100-300 g/ton
- At 99.9% purity via electrolysis: $580/kg
- Revenue: $58-174/ton feedstock

**Electrolysis Refining (Downstream, FOLLOW-14):**
- In-rich slug (Stage 4 output) → HCl leach → InCl₃ solution
- Electrodeposition: In³⁺ + 3e⁻ → In (cathode efficiency >95%)
- Product: 99.99% In, feeds YInMn Blue production (CSMFAB)
