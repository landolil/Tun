### 12.2 Export Controls Process

| Step | Action | Tool | Timeline |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| **1. Classify** | Determine ECCN/USML for each item | BIS CCA, supplier SDS | Design phase |
| **2. Screen** | Denied persons, entity list, OFAC | Descartes, Thomson Reuters | Per shipment |
| **3. License** | Apply if required (rare for Forge) | SNAP-R | 30-90 days |
| **4. Record** | AES filing, EAR retention | ACE, internal DB | 5 years |
| **5. Audit** | Annual self-audit + BIS audit | BIS guidelines | Annual |
