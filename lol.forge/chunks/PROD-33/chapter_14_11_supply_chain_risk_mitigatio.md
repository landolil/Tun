### 11.2 Mitigation Strategies Detail

**Dual-Source All Critical Materials:** No single-source for any Tier 1 material. Geographic diversification: US + Japan + EU + Canada + Australia.

**In-House Production Targets (Vertical Integration):**
- Basalt roving: 100% by Year 3 (domestic basalt rock → melting → roving)
- Elium® resin: 100% by Year 5 (bio-MMA → polymerization)
- ZrB₂ powder: 100% by Year 3 (flash sinter furnace + SHS ZrB₂)
- 3Y-TZP: 100% by Year 2 (co-precipitation from ZrOCl₂)
- Si₃N₄: 100% by Year 2 (direct nitridation of Si powder)
- Ti₃AlC₂: 100% by Year 2 (reactive sintering)
- KNbO₃-BaTiO₃: 100% by Year 2 (solid-state synthesis)

**Strategic Stockpile:** 90-day DPA Title III stockpile for In, REE, Co, Zr, Nb, W.

**Contractual:** 5-yr + 5-yr option LTAs, PPI+1% escalation, force majeure clauses, OFAC screening.
