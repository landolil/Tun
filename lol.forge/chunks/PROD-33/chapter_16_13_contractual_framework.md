## 13. Contractual Framework

### 13.1 Long-Term Agreement (LTA) Template

| Term | Standard | Forge-Specific |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Duration** | 5 years + 5-year option | 5+5 for Tier 1, 3+2 for Tier 2 |
| **Price Escalation** | PPI + 1% annually | PPI+1% (capped at 5%), In/REE: LME-linked ±10% collar |
| **Volume Commitment** | 80% of forecast | Take-or-pay 70% min, 120% max |
| **Quality** | ISO 9001/AS9100 | + CSMFAB spec, FA requirements, NCR 8D |
| **Delivery** | DDP Forge site | JIT (Tier 1), Kanban (Tier 2) |
| **Force Majeure** | Standard | Pandemic, war, gov't action, 90-day max before termination |
| **IP/Tech Transfer** | Supplier owns | CSMFAB owns process IP for in-house materials |
| **Dispute** | Mediation → Arbitration | JAMS, Texas law, English language |
| **Termination** | 90-day notice | 180-day for Tier 1 (critical) |
