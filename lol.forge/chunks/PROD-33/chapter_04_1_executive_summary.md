## 1. Executive Summary

The Aegis-Forge I supply chain is a **zero-metal, Carrington-immune, domestically sourced, Phoenix Protocol circular** logistics network. Unlike conventional industrial supply chains that optimize for cost, the Forge supply chain optimizes for four simultaneous objectives: (1) 100% domestic/zero-metal material sourcing for hot-zone components, (2) Phoenix Protocol EOL return logistics that close the material loop, (3) 90-day strategic stockpile for critical minerals under DPA Title III, (4) SIL-3/SIL-4 quality assurance on every component entering the hot zone.

**Key Supply Chain Metrics:**

| Metric | Target | Basis |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Domestic Hot-Zone Sourcing** | 100% | ZrB₂, SiC, basalt, Elium®, In, REE, Ti₃AlC₂, 3Y-TZP, Si₃N₄ |
| **Critical Mineral Stockpile** | 90 days | DPA Title III: In, REE, Co, Zr, Nb, W |
| **AVL Supplier Count** | 50 total (12 critical) | ISO 9001/AS9100/NQA-1 qualified |
| **Phoenix Depot Network** | 65 hubs (Year 3) | 3 Primary, 12 Secondary, 50 Tertiary |
| **Transport Mode** | Rail + Electric Truck | Zero-dunnage BFRP crate system |
| **Crate Turnaround** | 14 days | Return → clean → redeploy |
| **Supply Chain Carbon Intensity** | 0.08 kg CO₂e/ton-km | Electric Class 8 + rail |
