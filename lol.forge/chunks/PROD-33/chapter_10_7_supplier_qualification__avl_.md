### 7.3 Supplier Audit Checklist (Critical Elements)

| Category | Weight | Key Requirements |
|----------|--------|------------------|
| **Technical Capability** | 40% | Cpk >1.67 on critical dims, equipment adequacy, traceability to raw material, in-house testing (UT/CT/mech/thermal), R&D for new grades |
| **Quality System** | 30% | ISO 9001+AS9100 cert, 8D NCR/CAPA, Tier 2 flow-down, doc control, training records |
| **Financial/ESG** | 30% | D&B rating >3A, no OFAC sanctions, conflict minerals (CMRT), GHG reporting, labor compliance, BCP |

### 7.4 Supplier Quality Management

| Process | Frequency | Tool | Escalation |
|---------|-----------|------|------------|
| **Incoming Inspection** | 100% Critical, 10% Normal | LIBS, XRD, CMM, UT, visual | NCR → CAPA → Sort/Return |
| **Supplier Scorecard** | Monthly | On-time, quality, PPM, responsiveness, ESG | <80% → probation, <60% → disqualify |
| **Process Audit** | Annual (Tier 1) | CSMFAB-AUD-001..050 | Major finding → 30-day CAPA |
| **First Article** | New supplier/part | 3 lots, full data package | FAIR approval required before production |
| **Change Notification** | Mandatory | Form SCN-XXX, 90-day lead | No change without CSMFAB approval |
