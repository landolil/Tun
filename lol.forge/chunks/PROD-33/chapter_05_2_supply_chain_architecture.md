## 2. Supply Chain Architecture

### 2.1 Three-Layer Supply Chain Model

```
Layer 1 — Virgin Material Supply (Domestic/Allied)
    ↓ Critical Minerals: In (Teck/Indium Corp), REE (MP/Lynas), Zr (Tosoh/Chemat), Co (US/Allied)
    ↓ Composites: Basalt (Kamenny Vek/in-house), Elium® (Arkema/in-house MMA)
    ↓ Ceramics: SiC (Wolfspeed/Onsemi), 3Y-TZP (in-house), Si₃N₄ (in-house)
    ↓ MAX Phase: Ti₃AlC₂ (in-house synthesis)

Layer 2 — Forge In-House Production (Vertical Integration)
    ↓ Flash Sintering Furnace → ZrB₂-SiC drums, liners
    ↓ Filament Winding Cell → BFRP vessels, coil housings, frames
    ↓ MMA Polymerization → Elium® resin (from bio-MMA)
    ↓ Basalt Melting → Basalt roving (from domestic basalt)
    ↓ Powder Synthesis → Si₃N₄, 3Y-TZP, KNbO₃-BaTiO₃, Ti₃AlC₂

Layer 3 — Phoenix Protocol Circular Supply (Recursive)
    ↓ 65 Depot Network → EOL Aegis products → classified feedstock
    ↓ Disassembly → BFRP solvolysis → Elium® monomer + basalt fiber
    ↓ Ceramic cores → hydromet → Zr/Y/In/Co hydroxide gels
    ↓ Critical mineral glazes → ionic liquid extraction → 99.9% In, 98% Y/Co
    ↓ Output → Forge re-entry (blend ratio 40-60% EOL)
```

### 2.2 Supply Chain Governance

**Supply Chain Steering Committee (SCSC):**
- Chair: CSM VP Supply Chain
- Members: Procurement Director, Forge Operations, Materials Engineering, Phoenix Protocol Lead, Quality, Legal/Compliance, Digital Twin Architect
- Meets: Weekly (operational), Monthly (strategic)
- Authority: AVL changes, stockpile releases, depot expansions, sourcing strategy

**Supply Chain Risk Management (SCRM) Process:**
1. **Identify:** Quarterly risk assessment (geopolitical, market, regulatory, logistics, quality)
2. **Assess:** Likelihood × Impact × Detection (FMEA scoring)
3. **Mitigate:** Dual-source, stockpile, in-house capability, contractual, substitution
4. **Monitor:** Real-time (PRISM platform), Weekly (SCSC), Quarterly (Board)
