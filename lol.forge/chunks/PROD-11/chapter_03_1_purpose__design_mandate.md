## 1. Purpose & Design Mandate

The Feed Preparation & Shredding subsystem is the **gateway** of the Aegis-Forge I. Every kilogram of material entering the thermal cascade must first pass through this system: ferrous pre-separation, size reduction to <150 mm, acoustic pre-liberation, and moisture verification. The output of this subsystem directly determines the efficiency, purity, and throughput of all eight downstream thermal stages.

**Design Mandate:**
- Zero structural metals in the shredder chamber — BFRP blades, PEEK CF40 liners, ceramic bearings
- Permanent magnet ferrous separation (1.2T NdFeB belt) removing 40-60% ferrous mass pre-shredder
- Particle size control: Rosin-Rammler distribution targeting <150 mm maximum, optimal liberation
- Moisture gate: Capacitive sensing at >25% triggers 80°C pre-dry before drum entry
- Acoustic pre-chamber: 1-minute LF/ultrasonic mineral liberation at ambient conditions
- Throughput: 500 kg/hr continuous feed rate matched to drum batch cycle
- All-dielectric construction: No copper, no steel, no aluminum in process-contact zones
- Carrington immunity: No conductive paths, no ferromagnetic components

**The Irreducible Constraint:** *The Aegis-Forge I drum is a dielectric citadel — a rotating ZrB2-SiC ceramic pressure vessel at 2200°C. Any metallic contamination entering the drum (steel blade fragments, ferrous wire, copper shreds) becomes a permanent defect in the ceramic matrix, creates GIC coupling paths, or forms intermetallic compounds that poison downstream separation. The shredder must reduce heterogeneous waste to a controlled particle distribution without introducing a single gram of metallic debris.*

This constraint forces every design decision: BFRP composite blades (basalt fiber/Elium®) instead of hardened steel, PEEK CF40 chamber liners instead of manganese steel, ceramic hybrid bearings instead of steel rollers, and a permanent magnet separation belt upstream that removes the bulk ferrous fraction before it ever reaches the shredder.
