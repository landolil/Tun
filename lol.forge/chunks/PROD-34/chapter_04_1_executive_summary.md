## 1. Executive Summary

The Aegis-Forge I training and certification program is a **zero-metal, Carrington-immune, Phoenix Protocol recursive** competency framework. Unlike conventional industrial training that optimizes for throughput, the Forge program optimizes for four simultaneous objectives: (1) 100% operator competency on zero-metal, all-dielectric systems under GIC/EMP threat, (2) Phoenix Protocol cross-training so Forge operators can operate depot disassembly/solvolysis and vice versa, (3) SIL-3/SIL-4 safety instrumented function authorization competency, (4) digital twin integration where training scenarios are generated from fleet-wide anomaly data.

**Key Training Metrics:**

| Metric | Target | Basis |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Initial Certification (Apprentice)** | 400 hours | 5 role tracks × 80 hrs core + track |
| **Annual Maintenance Training** | 200 hours/yr | ISO 9001/AS9100/NQA-1 recertification |
| **Simulator Fidelity** | 95% physics | PROD-24 thermal model + PROD-37 digital twin |
| **Scenario Library** | 50+ scenarios | Normal, degraded, emergency, Carrington, Phoenix |
| **Phoenix Depot Cross-Training** | Mandatory | 40 hrs depot ops for Forge operators |
| **SIL-3 SIF Authorization Pass Rate** | 100% | CT-04 gate requirement (PROD-28) |
| **Certification Validity** | 2 years | Biennial recertification + annual refresh |
