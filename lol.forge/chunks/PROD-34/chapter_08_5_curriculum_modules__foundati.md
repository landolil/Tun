## 5. Curriculum Modules — Foundation (Safety, Materials, Physics)

### 5.1 Module F-01: Industrial Safety & Carrington Immunity (16 hrs)

**Topics:**
- Forge hazard identification: Thermal (2200°C), EMF (500 kW induction), Chemical (H₂, NaHCO₃, HCl), Pressure (10 bar), Radiation (XRF/LIBS)
- GIC/EMP threat: Carrington storm physics, 5000 nT/s → >100 A/km induction, Faraday cage integrity, LiFePO₄ UPS backup
- Zero-metal safety: No conductive tools in hot zone, PMMA POF handling, BFRP tooling, PEEK CF40 fasteners
- Emergency response: E-stop locations, evacuation routes, refuge stations, comms protocols

**Assessment**: Written (20 questions, 80% pass) + Practical (E-stop drill, evacuation <30 sec)

### 5.2 Module F-02: Zero-Metal Materials Science (16 hrs)

**Topics:**
- ZrB₂-SiC drum: Flash sintering, 3 liner zones, 8 ports, FBG embedding (PROD-04)
- BFRP vessel: Filament winding, Elium® resin, 50 mm aerogel, YInMn glaze (PROD-06)
- MAX Phase Ti₃AlC₂: Port actuators, EMI shields, thermal conductivity (PROD-09, FOLLOW-04)
- Basalt fiber/Elium®: Roving specs, sizing, solvolysis recovery (PROD-12, 33)
- Critical minerals: In, REE, Co, Zr, Nb — properties, handling, ITAR (PROD-12, 33)

**Assessment**: Written (25 questions) + Material identification lab (10 samples blind)

### 5.3 Module F-03: Thermal-Acoustic-Inductive Physics (24 hrs)

**Topics:**
- Stage 0: Acoustic liberation (KNbO₃-BaTiO₃ transducers, 20-200 Hz + 40 kHz, PROD-05)
- Stage 1: IR drying (90°C, moisture <1%, PROD-14)
- Stage 2: Polymer melt centrifuge (250°C, density stratification, PROD-15)
- Stage 3: Pyrolysis/char (450°C, thermoset decomposition, PROD-16)
- Stage 4: Indium-First (160°C, 400 RPM, centrifugal separation, PROD-17)
- Stage 5: Aluminum window (660-720°C, Mg removal flux, PROD-18)
- Stage 6: Copper/PM (1085-1200°C, anode casting, PROD-19)
- Stage 7: Ferrous/REE (1200-1600°C, dual-port REE, PROD-20)
- Stage 8: Zircon dissociation (1676°C, 3Y-TZP flash sinter, PROD-21)

**Assessment**: Written (30 questions) + Simulator: identify stage from thermal/profile data
