## 10. Certification Pathways

### 10.1 Four-Level Progression

| Level | Designation | Prerequisites | Hours | Valid | Authority |
|-------|-------------|---------------|-------|-------|-----------|
| **1** | **Apprentice** | High school + safety orientation | 400 | 1 yr | Supervised operation only |
| **2** | **Journeyman** | Apprentice + 2000 hrs OJT + all practicals | +800 | 2 yrs | Independent operation |
| **3** | **Master** | Journeyman + 4000 hrs + instructor cert + project | +400 | 2 yrs | Train others, authorize SL |
| **4** | **Instructor** | Master + adult learning cert + curriculum dev | +200 | 3 yrs | Develop curriculum, assess |

### 10.2 Certification Requirements Per Level

**Apprentice (400 hrs):**
- Core Knowledge: F-01, F-02, F-03 (56 hrs) — written 80% each
- Core Skills: S-01, S-02, S-03, S-04 (80 hrs) — practical sign-off each
- Operations: O-01, O-02, O-03, O-04 (72 hrs) — simulator + tabletop
- Maintenance: M-01, M-02, M-03 (48 hrs) — practical + written
- Phoenix Cross: P-01, P-02, P-03 (40 hrs) — practical at depot
- Assessment: O-01 cold startup sim, O-03 E-stop sim, S-03 oral board
- Behavioral: 90-day observation log, shift handover × 10

**Journeyman (+800 hrs = 1200 total):**
- Advanced Knowledge: PROD-24 thermal model, PROD-25 FEA, PROD-31 economics (80 hrs)
- Advanced Skills: PID tuning (S-01), cascade optimization (S-02), CBM interpretation (M-02) (120 hrs)
- Judgment: 50 authorization gates (FO-J-01 through J-04) logged, 3 SIF bypasses witnessed
- Emergency: 5 Carrington sim, 5 zone fault sim, 5 drum anomaly sim — all PASS
- Phoenix: 200 hrs depot OJT, LIBS classification 100 returns, solvolysis 10 batches
- Assessment: Full 8-hr simulator scenario (normal + 3 emergencies), oral board (3 examiners)

**Master (+400 hrs = 1600 total):**
- Instruction: Train 5 Apprentices to Journeyman (documented)
- Curriculum: Develop 1 new simulator scenario (validated by PROD-37 twin)
- Project: Lead 1 continuous improvement (Kaizen) with measurable result
- Cross-domain: Qualify in 2nd role track (DT, MT, QI, or SL)
- Assessment: Portfolio review + teach-back session + peer panel

**Instructor (+200 hrs = 1800 total):**
- Adult learning: Certified Technical Trainer (CTT+) or equivalent
- Curriculum: Own 2+ modules end-to-end (design → deliver → assess)
- Assessment: Validate all Apprentice/Journeyman practicals for assigned modules
