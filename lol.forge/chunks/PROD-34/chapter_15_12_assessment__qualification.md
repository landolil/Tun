## 12. Assessment & Qualification

### 12.1 Assessment Methods

| Method | Application | Standard |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Written Exam** | Knowledge (K) competencies | 80% pass, 2 hr max, proctored |
| **Practical Assessment** | Skill (S) competencies | Instructor sign-off, zero critical errors |
| **Simulator Scenario** | Skill + Judgment (S, J) | PASS/FAIL per scenario rubric |
| **Oral Board** | Judgment (J) competencies | 3 examiners (SL, Safety Eng, QA), 45 min |
| **Behavioral Observation** | Behavior (B) competencies | 90-day log, shift lead sign-off |
| **Cyber Range** | Digital (D) competencies | Phishing, USB, network scan defense |

### 12.2 Qualification Records

Each operator maintains a **Digital Qualification Record (DQR)** on Hyperledger Fabric (PROD-38):
- UUID: CSM-DQR-<Role>-<EmployeeID>-<CertLevel>
- Immutable: All assessments, hours, sign-offs, simulator scores
- Auditable: Annual internal, biennial ASME/OSHA, continuous twin sync
- Portable: Transfers with employee across Forge sites
