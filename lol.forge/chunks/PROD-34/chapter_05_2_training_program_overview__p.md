## 2. Training Program Overview & Philosophy

### 2.1 Training Philosophy: "Witness, Don't Monitor"

The Forge training philosophy derives from the El Segundo heuristic (PROD-23, PROD-28): **operators do not monitor — they witness the phenomenon into existence.** This requires training that develops sensory-motor coupling to the process, not dashboard interpretation skills.

**Three Training Pillars:**

1. **Material Intimacy**: Operators must *feel* the 25 kg ZrB₂ drum mass, *see* the Ar backfill pressure, *know* this powder becomes Drum Zone B at 1676°C. Training includes hands-on material handling, LIBS operation, crate cycle counting — direct sensory coupling to the CMF-RQT supply chain (PROD-33).

2. **Recursive Judgment**: Operators make authorization decisions at four cognitive checkpoints (PROD-28 Gates SG-2, SG-3, CT-04, SAT-05) where no algorithm accepts organizational risk. Training develops judgment through scenario repetition with expert debrief.

3. **Cross-Domain Fluency**: Forge operators train on Phoenix Depot operations (disassembly, solvolysis, LIBS classification). Depot techs train on Forge cascade control. This creates a single workforce that understands the full CMF-RQT loop from virgin feedstock → cascade → EOL return → feedstock.
